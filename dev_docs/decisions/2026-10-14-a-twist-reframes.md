# A twist changes how the earlier story reads; an echo is not one

## Context

Run-04 (branch `bestdan/story-run-04`, parked) planned its twist as the
robot arm in the firm's training clip tapping a fitting twice, which was
the narrator's own habit, planted early, beside her father's single tap.
The cold reader called it "the best moment for me". The human picked it up
and found it clearly spelled out, but said it "doesn't seem like a twist,
although it is a very nice echo or implication". Asked what they learned
late that changed how they saw the earlier part, they said: "I don't think
there were any big reveals towards the end."

The reveal confirmed what the reader already knew, that her staged mistakes
were going into the robots. It changed nothing about how the earlier
scenes read. The grader can't catch this: it grades Twist on whether the
reader can state the fact and its link, and a reader can state an echo
perfectly.

Run-03 is a softer case of the same. Its human read the twist as "an
inconsistency or oddity", not a reveal (`stories/run-03/debrief.md`).

## Decision

The story bible's Twist row names what the reader believed before the
reveal and what they see differently after it. A payoff the reader can see
coming, such as an echo of a planted habit, is not a twist, however well it
lands. It gets a traced row, and the Twist row says "none". Added to the
payoff ledger's rules in `templates/story-bible.md`.

## Consequences

- Good, because stage 4 has to say what the reveal overturns, which shows
  early when there is nothing to overturn.
- Good, because a story with a strong echo and no twist is graded as that,
  not as a twist the reader "missed".
- Bad, because the cold reader still can't measure surprise, so the gate
  still can't tell a reveal from an echo. This rule works at stage 4 or not
  at all.

## Revisit when

- A debrief again finds a planned twist read as expected, although the
  bible's Twist row named what it overturns: the check needs a lens at
  stage 4 or 5, not just a template rule.

## Confirmation

Nothing beyond review: the rule is in `templates/story-bible.md`, which the
stage 4 lenses read.

## Alternatives

- **Spell out the twist's meaning on the page:** the first version of this
  record. Run-04's twist was already spelled out, and the human got it; the
  problem was what it revealed, not how clearly.
