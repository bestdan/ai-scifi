# Stage 2 audit: run-10

The Cartographer's map of 30 cards from 10 lenses. Yield is judged at 4,500
words, the midpoint of the brief's range. Confidence is given for every
attribution; "nearest I know of" means only that.

## The seed's own map

The seed is a fixed premise, so it is not scored. A card's novelty has to come
from its treatment. For calibration, these are the nearest works to the seed
itself. **No lens cited any of them.**

- *Small Gods* (Terry Pratchett, 1992, novel), confidence high. A god's size
  and health track how many people truly believe in him. The church is huge,
  but one novice believes, so the god is a tortoise. This is the closest
  published shape to "a being kept alive only by both sides keeping faith".
- Max Gladstone's Craft Sequence (*Three Parts Dead*, 2012, onward), confidence
  high on the series and med on the detail. Gods are sustained by worship and
  bound by contract, and Craftsmen build god-like Concerns. Asimov and Vinge
  named it.
- Tinker Bell in *Peter Pan* (J. M. Barrie, 1904, play), confidence high: a
  being who lives only if the audience believes, and is restored by applause.
- *American Gods* (Neil Gaiman, 2001, novel), confidence high: gods fed and
  starved by attention.

A web search turned up no published story in which a treaty itself wakes as a
being. The nearest title is a 2026 novel called *Treaty*, in which "Treaty"
names a planet, not a living agreement (confidence low on its details). The
seed's premise looks uncharted as a treaty, and charted as a
belief-sustained being.

## Convergence

The lenses spread on the being's **body**, and converged on its **plot**.

**The obvious card.** Nine of ten lenses wrote the same obvious card: the
interpreter finds a hidden cheat and must decide whether to tell. Chiang's
version adds a mistranslation, and only Rajaniemi wrote a different one (a
kidnap). This is the hard functionary attractor in the seed's clothes. The
written cards that come closest are Banks 1 (traces the cough to a hidden
breach), Asimov 1 (finds when the padding began) and Banks 3 (uncovers secret
operations). That makes run-10 another hit on the functionary entry, though
every one of the three turns its reveal.

**Clusters:**

   overdone, costless or propped up, until the being's health misreads or its

## Collisions

1. **We-Two × Small Clauses** (Le Guin 2 + Chiang 3). The being can perceive
   only promises, and its only word for intimacy is a dual that holds exactly
   two, and both places are taken by the signatories. Each promise the
   interpreter makes brings her into view, and edges her toward a "we" with no
   room for her. *Native Tongue* and "Lifecycle" each have one half. Nobody has

   Scores: N5 F4 R4 Y5 E5 S4 → weighted 37/40.
2. **The Uncut × What It Costs to Keep** (Le Guin 1 + Chiang 1). Chiang's rule,
   that good faith counts only as forgone temptation, gets a literal body: the
   pillars are ore not taken. Robbing a pillar on retreat is the temptation
   made physical. Chiang's abstraction loses its hand-wave, and Le Guin's mine
   gains a metabolism. Neither "Devil in the Dark" nor any costly-signal
   fiction I know combines them.
   Scores: N5 F3 R5 Y4 E5 S4 → weighted 35/40.
3. **True Finds Only × Rouge** (Watts 1 + Lem 2). The interpreter discovers
   that he is the rouge. His contentment is the cosmetic flush that lets both
   sides' compliance engines look like good faith, so he has to recruit the
   forty amateurs and then take himself out of the chemistry. Pratchett's
   tortoise god has a sincere believer, but no believer who is also the
   forgery.
   Scores: N4 F3 R4 Y5 E4 S4 → weighted 33/40.
4. **The Last Round × Endurance Limit** (Egan 3 + Tchaikovsky 1). The festivals
   are what spend the being's fatigue life, and the leaked estimate threatens

   Scores: N4 F3 R5 Y4 E4 S4 → weighted 32/40.
5. **The Left Hemisphere for Hire × The Gloss** (Watts 2 + Chiang 2). The two

   makes them one self. The glossed page is the form, and her gloss is the

   charted. A gloss as the bridge between brain halves is not.
   Scores: N4 F4 R3 Y4 E5 S3 → weighted 31/40.
