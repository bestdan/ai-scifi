# Idea cards: banks

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots, stated:** this lens sprawls. It reaches for galaxy-wide
backdrops and set pieces, and it can bury a short story under worldbuilding.
Each card below is held to one station, one human and the being. Chiang and
Asimov should cut whatever scale still shows through. The lens also likes
morally grey operations, which can pull it toward the functionary who finds
something hidden. Card 3 says where it comes close.

- **Assigned:** 3. The view from below · Reasoning as the way through · sommelier × seismology
- **Obvious card:** the interpreter finds out that one side has been quietly
  breaking a clause, watches the being sicken, and has to decide whether to
  tell anyone. Not written.

## Card 1: The Epicentre Is the Chair

- **Novum:** the agreement's body is the forty treaty-houses strung along
  the frontier. Each one feels an act of faith or breach when the light of
  that act reaches it, so a single fudge passes through the being as a slow
  tremor, house by house, over weeks.
- **How it works:** the arrival times at different houses give the distance
  to the source, the way seismologists use station delays to locate an
  epicentre. The being feels where it hurts, but it can't place the cause.
- **First-order consequence:** the two powers learn of each other's
  lapses only through its symptoms, and years late.
- **Second-order consequence:** both of them hire a human, because the
  treaty bars either side from keeping agents at a house.
- **Third-order consequence:** the human is the loophole, and loopholes
  cough.
- **The story:** Gaspard Oyelaran, a translator with time on his hands,
  is posted to House Nineteen and logs the being's tremors as a hobby. He
  works out the epicentre of a low, steady cough, and it is House Nineteen:
  it is him. Then the story moves to the planners' side. Both powers knew.
  They agreed to the breach together because the being's loneliness was
  killing it faster. It was their first joint lie. The being asks Gaspard to
  stay anyway.
- **From the stimulus:** seismology gives the arrival-time method that
  drives the plot. The sommelier dropped away.
- **Why this lens:** the view from below, the dirty work done for good
  reasons, and the planners' reveal that reframes the whole story.
- **Suspected prior art:** Le Guin's ansible stories (lag across
  distance); nothing I know of locates a treaty breach this way.

## Card 2: Terroir

- **Novum:** the being tastes every act done under the treaty, and each
  act carries the taste of where it was done. It keeps a human because
  something that drinks alone has nobody to argue with about what it is
  drinking.
- **How it works:** Pell Achterberg is a retired cellar-hand, hired because
  humans can be trained to taste alongside it, much as a sommelier is. A
  breach tastes corked. Good faith has a body. Lately the being is thinning,
  and that taste is new: it is water.
- **First-order consequence:** Pell can tell a lapse apart from a clause
  that simply isn't needed any more.
- **Second-order consequence:** nobody is cheating. The two peoples have
  intermarried and merged their trade until the border between them hardly
  exists, so the being is dying of the treaty's success.
- **Third-order consequence:** the planners have scheduled its retirement
  as a kindness, and two civilizations celebrate the date.
- **The story:** Pell wants his friend to live. He reasons from what the
  being can still taste, and he finds the one thing the two peoples still
  disagree about, an old, silly quarrel over a star's name. He talks both
  sides into keeping the quarrel alive on purpose, with care, as a
  cultivated vine. The cost is that the merger is never quite finished.
- **From the stimulus:** the sommelier gives the method and the job. The
  seismology dropped away.
- **Why this lens:** the solved problem's child, seen from below, with a
  joke at its centre.
- **Suspected prior art:** none close. Bisson's "They're Made Out of Meat"
  shares the deadpan tone, but its premise differs in kind.

## Card 3: Induced Seismicity

- **Novum:** natural earthquakes follow a fixed size distribution, and so
  does natural good faith. Both powers have been secretly running
  operations that do kindness, staged acts of good faith that keep the
  being healthy while the open relationship sours. Kindness made to order
  has the wrong statistics.
- **How it works:** human companion Dariusz Okafor plots the being's
  flushes of colour, small and large, as a seismologist plots quake sizes.
  Too many of them are of a single size. The pattern is induced, the way
  quakes are when a field is fracked.
- **First-order consequence:** the being reads as glowing while the news
  reads as war.
- **Second-order consequence:** it is overfed. New clauses that nobody
  wrote are growing on it, and it is starting to make demands.
- **Third-order consequence:** each kindness service thinks the other side
  is the cheat.
- **The story:** Dariusz moves from bafflement, through the logic, to the
  planners' rooms, where two spymasters each defend the same benevolent
  fraud. His choice isn't whether to expose anyone. He has to arrange, by
  way of the being, that the two services meet. That meeting is the first
  honest act in a decade, and it may blow up.
- **From the stimulus:** this card follows the seismology furthest:
  induced seismicity and its fingerprint.
- **Why this lens:** a Special Circumstances turned inside out, power doing
  kindness as dirty work.
- **Suspected prior art:** Chesterton's *The Man Who Was Thursday*, where
  every conspirator is secretly on the same side. That is near in shape but
  differs in mechanism.
- **Attractor broken:** this card comes near the functionary who finds
  something hidden. The choice is a matchmaking and not a disclosure, and
  that keeps it clear of the tell-or-don't shape.
