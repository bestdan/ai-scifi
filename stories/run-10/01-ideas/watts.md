# Idea cards: watts

**Blind spots:** This lens runs bleak, and it treats meaning, ritual and joy as side effects rather than forces in their own right. It is also prone to case-study characters and to piling on detail. To push back I have aimed each card at warmth that has a mechanism under it. The drafter should still check that the people stay good company and the density stays readable.

- **Assigned:** 3. The body rebuilt · The same questions in nonfiction · search-and-rescue dog handler × a deep-space probe
- **Obvious card:** the interpreter finds that one side has been quietly cheating while the being sickens, and has to decide whether to tell the other side. Not written.

## Card 1: True Finds Only

- **Novum:** The treaty's body is an autocatalytic chemical network. It formed in the exchange chamber where the two species swap the industrial goods the treaty covers, and each species needs the other's waste as feedstock. The companion is a former search-and-rescue dog handler whose nose has been rebuilt to dog grade so he can read its moods.
- **How it works:** Every delivery under a clause is a reactant. A short delivery starves one loop of the network, and the being coughs. A surplus works as a catalyst, and it flushes. Autocatalytic sets are real origin-of-life chemistry (Kauffman; Hordijk & Steel 2004). Smell is the one sense that reaches the amygdala without passing through the thalamus first, so his rebuilt nose sends the being's state straight into his own feelings.
- **First-order consequence:** He smells its health before either side's sensors can measure it.
- **Second-order consequence:** The network starts using his sweat and breath as reactants (chemosignals: de Groot et al. 2012). When he is content, the being looks healthier than either side's deliveries can account for.
- **Third-order consequence:** Both sides read it as healthy and start cutting their deliveries, so his good mood is paying for their cheating.
- **The story:** Handlers know a dog rewarded for pleasing them will alert where there is nothing (Lit et al. 2011). Now he is the dog. He has to take himself out of the being's chemistry, even though he loves it, so that its cough can be honest again.
- **From the stimulus:** The handler's ethic and handler cueing come straight from it. The probe is dropped.
- **Why this lens:** a rebuilt sense remaking a mind, and the question of what a feeling is for.
- **Suspected prior art:** Butler's Oankali (bonding through chemistry); *Perfume*.
- **Attractor broken (only if one is):** It brushes against "the meddler's instrument manufactures the need", but in reverse: the reading isn't biased, his own body is propping the being up. It also touches "one person as the mind's whole humanity", which the seed's single interpreter makes hard to avoid.

## Card 2: The Left Hemisphere for Hire

- **Novum:** Neither species could survive in the other's environment, so to give the woken treaty a body each built half of it, from its own biology. The two halves are joined by a narrow channel through the vacuum between them. The rebuild split its mind in two, the way severing the corpus callosum splits a human brain.
- **How it works:** Each half feels only its own side's clauses. When one side cheats, its half acts on that and the other half has no idea why. Split-brain patients make up reasons for what the cut-off hemisphere does; Gazzaniga called the brain system that does this "the interpreter". Neither alien species makes up reasons for itself. Humans do, and are hired for it.
- **First-order consequence:** Her job is to supply the being with one story in which everything both halves do was intended by a single self.
- **Second-order consequence:** She gets good at reasons that both halves will accept, and the being holds together.
- **Third-order consequence:** Both species come to see the human talent for self-deception as a precision instrument, and start to envy it.
- **The story:** One half has acted on a breach by its side. She can tell the being a story in which it chose that, which keeps it one self, or she can let it become two treaties, two smaller peaces. Comedy runs through it: a professional confabulator being admired for the very thing her species is embarrassed by.
- **From the stimulus:** The stimulus moved a long way. What survives is that handlers narrate their dogs' motives for them.
- **Why this lens:** The self is a story the brain tells, here bought and sold as a service.
- **Suspected prior art:** *Blindsight* (Siri Keeton as a synthesist; the Gang of Four sharing one brain); Leckie's divided Anaander Mianaai.

## Card 3: Interbout

- **Novum:** The two civilizations are forty light-years apart and have never met. The treaty woke inside the error-checking software of the relay probe at the midpoint, which has to model both sides to check each against the terms. The companion's body has been rebuilt to hibernate (from the fat-tailed dwarf lemur, the only primate hibernator: Dausmann 2004), so he wakes for one day in every hundred.
- **How it works:** The signal takes twenty years to reach the probe from either side, so the being's health reflects good faith kept twenty years ago. In hibernating ground squirrels, synapses retract during torpor and regrow on waking (von der Ohe et al. 2006), and some learned tasks are lost (Millesi et al. 2001). He comes back from each sleep a little new.
- **First-order consequence:** The being remembers him better than he remembers himself.
- **Second-order consequence:** The two of them have a friendship of their own, and that is an agreement too. It wakes as a small third being.
- **Third-order consequence:** He keeps forgetting promises he made to the small being, and it coughs.
- **The story:** Hibernators outlive non-hibernators of the same size (Turbill et al. 2011). He can stay awake, remember everything, and die in decades, or keep sleeping, keep forgetting, and live for centuries alongside it. It is a hospice conversation (Gawande) held between two parties who are both mortal.
- **From the stimulus:** The probe is the treaty's home. The handler survives in the working rhythm of rest shifts.
- **Why this lens:** a body rebuilt for deep space, and memory as wet tissue that can be lost.
- **Suspected prior art:** Watts's *Freeze-Frame Revolution*, where the crew wake only in rare shifts; the hibernating spiders of Vinge's *Deepness*.
