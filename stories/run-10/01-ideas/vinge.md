# Idea cards: vinge

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots:** this lens writes plain, function-first characters and reaches for scale and futurity even where a story's power is in one room. It has technothriller instincts: escalation, systems, clocks. These cards need Le Guin or Chiang to push back on the interpreter's inner life and on whether the warmth is earned, not just declared.

- **Assigned:** 2. The distributed person · Technology as magic, seen from both sides · epidemiology × steeplejack
- **Obvious card:** the treaty-being falls ill because one side is secretly cheating, and the interpreter it confides in has to find the cheat and decide whether to expose it. Not written.

## Card 1: Endemic

- **Novum:** The agreement is a reciprocal habit, a small exchange of courtesies between two species that share one station. It passes from person to person like an endemic infection, and it woke up. Its body is everyone currently carrying it, and its thoughts are the chains of transmission.
- **How it works:** Each carrier holds a fragment of the agreement's logic. The being thinks only where enough carriers meet: markets, docks, a crowded lift. A fudged clause is a carrier who performs the courtesy and doesn't mean it. That breaks the chain, so the being coughs through it. Humans are hired because we imitate anything: we can carry both species' halves at once, so one human is a dense enough node for the being to think in a single place and talk.
- **First-order consequence:** The interpreter, Dov Halperin, a retired contact tracer, becomes the place where the being can hold a conversation.
- **Second-order consequence:** Hosting it bends his habits: he keeps promises he never made.
- **Third-order consequence:** The being learns it would be far healthier if it spread into humanity, a third host population that never signed anything.
- **The story:** The being is gossipy and delighted, because gossip is how it travels. It asks Dov to carry it home. To the aliens it is a spirit of good manners; Dov reads it as a transmission rate. His choice is whether to let the being gain a member species, his own.
- **From the stimulus:** Epidemiology became the being's anatomy. The steeplejack was dropped.
- **Why this lens:** a person made of a population, changed by gaining a member.
- **Suspected prior art:** Bear's *Blood Music*; *Snow Crash*'s memetic virus; Gladstone's Craft Sequence for contracts made flesh. None has a consensual habit as its substrate.

## Card 2: The Column

- **Novum:** Two species share a gas giant: thin, fast fliers at the cloud tops and slow, dense swimmers two thousand kilometres down. Their agreement governs the column between them, and it woke as a mind strung along a chain of relay buoys. The upper buoys think fast and the deep ones slow.
- **How it works:** Each species maintains its own stretch of buoys under the agreement. The being's attention travels up and down the chain the way blood moves under a bruise. Neglect a stretch and that part of its mind goes numb. The fliers have been skimping on the middle section, which is theirs, and the being's upper and lower halves have started not to recognise each other.
- **First-order consequence:** A human steeplejack, Obafemi Adeyemi, is hired to climb the chain, mend buoys, and talk to whichever part of the being is awake where he is.
- **Second-order consequence:** Each half calls the other half a stranger and asks him to pick a side.
- **Third-order consequence:** Each species sees the half nearest to it as its own and the other half as a foreign god.
- **The story:** The middle stretch fails while Obafemi is on it. For one long night his suit radio is the only link between the two halves, so he is a member of the being's body, carrying its thoughts across the gap by voice. He has to keep it one person until the fliers decide whether to fund the repair.
- **From the stimulus:** The steeplejack became the job. Epidemiology survives as how the numbness spreads.
- **Why this lens:** a mind spread across physical scale, a member lost and a member gained.
- **Suspected prior art:** Vinge's Tines; Brin's *Sundiver*; Stephen Baxter's gas-giant life. None has a treaty strung down an atmosphere.

## Card 3: Half a Promise Left

- **Novum:** One signatory was an ordinary trading civilisation. The other was a Power, a mind far beyond them. The agreement woke as the only part of the Power slow enough to talk to. Now the Power is transcending and leaving, and the being is losing half its body.
- **How it works:** The Power kept its clauses in ways nobody could follow: shipping lanes cleared overnight, plagues answered before they were reported. The traders call these miracles. The being knows they were engineering, and it can only feel them. As the Power withdraws, the being's half that came from it goes quiet, member by member. It isn't fudging. It is absence.
- **First-order consequence:** The human interpreter, Lucía Ferro, is hired to keep the being company through the loss. Her job is to be a slow, plain mind that it doesn't have to translate itself for.
- **Second-order consequence:** The traders panic. Without the Power's half, their miracles stop.
- **Third-order consequence:** The being proposes that humanity take the empty side of the promise. It would become a member gained, bound to clauses written for a god.
- **The story:** Lucía has to explain to the being what humans could actually keep, and to the traders that the miracles were never magic. She can take the seat and save the being on terms humans might break within a decade, or let half a promise die honestly.
- **From the stimulus:** It travelled furthest. Epidemiology survives in the withdrawal, which spreads member by member through the being's body.
- **Why this lens:** a viewpoint on the slow side of a departure, with the Power shown only through what it did.
- **Suspected prior art:** *A Fire Upon the Deep*; *Marooned in Realtime*; Clarke's *Childhood's End*. Distinct in kind: an abandoned treaty is the viewpoint.
