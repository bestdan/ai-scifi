# Names

- **Sione Havili** — the narrator; a Tongan court interpreter from Auckland, hired as the treaty's Listener.
- **Ennu** — the treaty's living word, the being Sione keeps company.
- **Vell** — Second Speaker of the Ashkari brood, a senior Hhoth delegate who proposes the trial pronoun.
- **Aaruum** — an Ammaru elder delegate who opposes the trial; Sione wrongly flags her.
- **Talek** — a junior Hhoth clerk at the register.
- **Tikk** — a Hhoth hatchling at the shelf school who flags Sione's own exclusive "we"; Vell's brood-nephew.
- **Ressa** — a Hhoth ice hauler whose contract keeps failing in the coughs.
- **Lesieli** — Sione's mother in Auckland.
- **Mele** — Sione's sister in Auckland.
