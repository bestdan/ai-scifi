# Graded ledger rows

| Row | The fact |
|---|---|
| Premise | Ennu is the treaty creole's inclusive dual ("you-and-I, both"), which woke; it perceives only promises and speaks only in promises, and Sione, hired to hear exclusive uses of ennu and keep it company, can be seen by it only by making it promises |
| Stakes | If the cough keeps rising, promises stop registering, the Table can't sit, and the Concord's word dies: the peace that ended the Drilling ends with it. Sione's own stake: the only friend who has needed him, and the job |
| Why | The coiners built ennu, and made the word itself the Concord's party, because the Drilling began with a "we" heard two ways (a brood's "we will not drill" heard as all Hhoth) |
| Twist | Before: the rising cough seems to be a delegate's or broker's cheating, which Sione is paid to catch. After: ennu has no "I", so every promise it makes to Sione is "ennu will", which seats him across from it and puts a whole people out of its second seat; his kindnesses (his open promises) are the illness |
| Ending | Ennu refuses to let the trial "ennu-sa" register, because every promise made with it in the hall seated two against one, and a word with three seats can leave someone out; instead it learns to promise Sione in "you", which displaces no people. He keeps only nine promises open, so it sees him less and coughs less; the delegates still play the dual; Vell loses his career's reform; Sione writes to his sister with kitautolu, "all of us" |
| Reach | Ennu's health decides whether every creole speaker on Ninefold can make a binding promise (contracts, haulage, insurance); careers ride on one word; a trial pronoun would make humanity a party to the treaty and a swing vote, moving power; the next Listeners are children in the shelf schools |
