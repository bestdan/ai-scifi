## The one thing
Cut the repeated "it's a cheat" beats (Ressa three times, the hitches "striking eight or nine times an hour", Sione "closing in on crooked glass-brokers"). The first reader guessed the cause at the lattice and waited for Sione to catch up. The novelty is the reversal that the cough is the being's own speech. A reader who is ahead of the narrator spends that reversal early.

## Notes
1. **Prior art, the core (structural, low risk).** Nearest I know of: *Embassytown* (Miéville, 2011), confidence high, for a language that is the substance of a mind. Also near: *Native Tongue* (Elgin, 1984) and *Babel-17* (Delany, 1966) on grammar shaping thought, and *Small Gods* (Pratchett, 1992) for a being fed by sincere promises. A pronoun that is the party to a peace, and whose only voice is a binding verb, I know no match for. The seed's cough-and-colour is inverted here: the cough is not a fudged clause but the being's own kindness. That inversion is the real distance. Keep it.
2. **The ending's drift toward *Embassytown* (structural).** I flagged in stage 3 that "change the language to admit the outsider and the thing changes" is that novel's shape. The draft mostly dodged it: the being refuses *ennu-sa* itself, and the fix is a postal header, not a new grammar. Good. But the plenary vote still runs three rehearsal clauses, one beat repeated. "In every sentence, the speakers had three seats to play with..." is the stock "three-way alliance politics" beat. One clause plus the narrator's flag would do.
3. **Stock beat: the protagonist's family wound mirrors the novum.** *Kimaua* in Mele's texts, then *kitautolu* at the end. It works on the reader ("It stung because it was about him"), but it is the standard "personal arc echoes the theme" move. It is charted, not wrong. Lines 107-109 hand it over explicitly ("will hear one in his own mother's house", then the texts). Let one of the two do it.
4. **Stock beat: the lonely machine that hurts by accident.** "I found myself feeling sorry for" it, said the reader. This is near "the AI that wants to be human", on the charted list. It escapes because the being never says *I*: "It hadn't learned to say *I*; it had solved a seating problem with a postal header." Protect that, and don't let the register annotations sound wistful. Two annotations ("The other half stands in the corridor and shakes the door") lean into feeling.
5. **Registry annotations (line-level).** The reader never settled whose voice they are. Novelty-wise they are the story's most original device (the being's own logbook), so make the voice consistent: clerk-flat, not lyrical.
6. **Hedged point.** I can't verify that no story has used inclusive/exclusive *we* as a treaty device. Linguists have discussed it, and I can't recall fiction. Nearest I know of: Elgin. Confidence low that it is uncharted in fiction.
7. **The "broken promises as scars" paragraph (line-level).** The reader lost the arithmetic (nine broken, two hundred and two kept). This is the one place where novum logic is unclear. The idea (a being that values proof of free choice) is fresh, so state it plainly once.

## What to protect
- "Hhoth can't hear the door close. Ammaru can't hear that there was a door. I was hired because I can hear it."
- "*Ennu* will keep the ones you broke... You could have broken all of them."
- The being's refusal of *ennu-sa* by conditional clause: "In this hall, every promise made with it seated two against one. *Ennu* can't be that."
- "It hadn't learned to say *I*; it had solved a seating problem with a postal header."
- The planetary cough and "Bless" to nobody.

## Blind spot disclosure
I judge distance from other works, not whether the story is good. I am blind to clarity, comedy and character depth. I have no reliable memory of work after mid-2026. My confidence that the core is uncharted rests on absence of recall, not on a search I could run to ground. I may over-penalise familiar emotional structures that readers enjoy: the family echo might be exactly what makes this story work.
