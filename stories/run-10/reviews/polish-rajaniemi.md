# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi lens

Blind spots: I favour opacity and density, and I can't judge reader fatigue. Defer to the First Reader on whether the vault scenes drag.

Glossary check: ennu, ennu-sa, the lattice, the register, the Gathering, brood and the Tongan terms are used consistently. The scene 1 and scene 2 splits between `the register` and `the lattice` hold. Payoff-ledger lines are all explicit and untouched. Two new glosses (`mantle`, `hogget`) are in apposition, which the style guide discourages, and neither is in the glossary. Both should be cut to plain words.

1. `a clause explaining ... An address isn't a seat inside *ennu*, so no people sits in it; it only routes the envelope, and *ennu* had only ever spoken from inside the word.` →
   `The address header isn't a seat inside *ennu*; it only routes the envelope. *Ennu* had only ever spoken from inside the word, and never to the outside of it.`
   The "so no people sits in it" clause is clumsy and repeats the next paragraph.

2. `a vault that saw me less: because I kept only nine promises open instead of forty, and none of them used *ennu*, the global coughs eased back` →
   `a vault that saw me less. With nine promises open instead of forty, and none of them spoken in *ennu*, the global coughs eased back`
   The "colon, because" construction is the polish's own addition and reads as clumsy.

3. `his mantle, a Hhoth's shoulder-cape of hide, pinned with the gold clasp` →
   `his hide shoulder-cape pinned with the gold clasp`
   This drops the undefined term and the apposition gloss. The scene 4 "flank" change then needs no further fix.

4. `three crates of Hawke's Bay hogget, young mutton from your own islands, requisitioned` →
   `three crates of Hawke's Bay mutton from your own islands, requisitioned`
   This touches protect-list #23, but it keeps the line's texture and drops an off-glossary word.

5. `The cough that hit the hall sounded like a spruce beam splitting down the grain.` →
   `The cough that hit the hall sounded like a kelp cable parting under load.`
   Spruce is a familiar Earth detail on a world with no taro, and kelp cable is already established in the story.
