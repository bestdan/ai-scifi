# Terms

- **ennu** — the creole's inclusive dual, "you and I, both of us, nobody else"; also the being.
- **ennu-sa** — the proposed trial pronoun, "we three".
- **the creole** — the treaty language both peoples speak through earpieces.
- **the lattice** — the glass-filament register that carries all creole speech and logs promises; ennu's substrate.
- **the register** — the treaty's log of binding promises; a promise binds only when it registers.
- **the Table** — the standing conference of both delegations.
- **the Listener** — Sione's office: flagging exclusive uses of ennu before a clause closes.
- **the Gathering** — the Ammaru spawning, every seven years.
- **the Drilling** — the disaster sixty-eight years ago and the war after it.
- **brood** — a Hhoth hatch-line, the group a Hhoth "we" means.
- **Hhoth / Ammaru** — the two alien peoples: ice-surface clickers and ocean-dwelling singers.
- **kitaua / kimaua / kitautolu** — Tongan: "you and I" / "she and I, not you" / "all of us, you included".
- **lu pulu** — a Tongan dish, corned beef wrapped in taro leaves.
- **"Bless"** — what people say when the cough hitches.
- **hogget** — young sheep meat (New Zealand).
- **Otahuhu, Hawke's Bay** — places in New Zealand.
