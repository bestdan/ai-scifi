## The one thing
Give the ones who pay a life, not a line. Muaru has one speech and Ressa
has "BLESS!". The Ammaru as a people still show up only as Aaruum and a chorus.
One scene at the grating, with the two of them mid-trade and no plot business,
would make the price of the cough a lived loss.

## Notes
1. **Scene 3, weeks six to ten (structural):** this stretch is still
   background. "Vell had told me why I won the audition over ten other human
   candidates..." is a recital, and "Still, I was only a stopgap. Fourteen
   years ago, the shelf schools..." jumps from Tongan to schools with no
   hinge. The first reader called it "background piled up". Fold the audition
   into one line and let the school be the scene.
2. **Scene 5, the mechanism paragraph (line-level):** "The mechanism was
   staring at me from my margin log..." restates the line before it, and the
   first reader noted this. Keep "Nobody was cheating. It hasn't got a word
   for I," and cut the paragraph.
3. **Scene 5, the broken promises (line-level):** "I thought it kept those
   nine broken vows because only a promise I could break proved the kept ones
   were choices" is still the instruction I warned about in myself, and the
   first reader couldn't follow it. The clerk-voice line plus Talek's
   "It keeps an unclosed line for good" is enough. Let the reader decide.
4. **Scene 6, the rehearsals (line-level):** the first rehearsal is
   dramatised and the second and third are one long sentence. That works. But
   Sione's flag speech then explains all three again, in the voice of a man
   delivering the theme. Cut it to the last clause: "Put me inside that word
   and you just teach the treaty how to leave somebody out."
5. **Scene 7, the cost to the being (structural, praise with a request):**
   "I sat on my stool invisible for forty minutes at a stretch" is the best
   cost in the story. Elsewhere, "Ressa... stared at the green registration
   light" and "Aaruum... logged her five-day buffer in silence" settle quickly.
   Aaruum's repayment is a sentence, and she should get a beat of
   acknowledgement or none. Silence suits her. Let it stand.
6. **Society (line-level):** the Hhoth home life is still absent. Talek's aunt
   and winch-grease kelp are the only hearth. Give one meal or one nap-nest
   in passing.

## What to protect
- "Hhoth can't hear the door close. Ammaru can't hear that there was a door."
- "Feeling like a barrister swearing an oath to a radiator."
- "A man who spends his life listening for a door shutting will hear one in
  his own mother's house," and the Mele reread left unsure.
- "You will come on the fourth day. You is the seat nobody else is in."
- Tikk's copper token, Vell's "astonishingly expensive employee," and the
  *kitautolu* message with the answer nine days off.
- The dimmer vault: the being is seen less, and the story admits it.

## Blind spot disclosure
I over-ask for slow scenes and I can't judge the seat mechanism's rigour, so
leave that to Egan and Lem. The note on Aaruum's silence is a preference for
restraint, which I am prone to. I lean toward moral explanation, as in notes 3
and 4, and that is a failing I recognise in my own register.
