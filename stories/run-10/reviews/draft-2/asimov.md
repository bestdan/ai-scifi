## The one thing
Make *ennu*'s refusal of *ennu-sa* a consequence of a rule the reader holds. Right now Vell's remedy is logically correct, and the refusal depends on delegates' behaviour.

## Notes
1. **Structural. The refusal is not fair play.** Vell's diagnosis is sound: a human seat inside *ennu-sa* means a promise to Sione displaces nobody. Ennu declines because "every promise made with it seated two against one." That describes delegates, and the Listener exists to flag exactly that. The reader can argue Sione's key should police a three-seat word as it polices a two-seat one. Say what is structural about three seats (a majority can always form, so exclusion can't be prevented), or ennu's reason reads as taste. The first reader's closing question ("protecting the treaty or Sione?") is the symptom.
2. **Structural. The register's seat.** Register 4,682 gives "Seats: you; the register." If the register is a legal seat, why couldn't ennu always say "the register will"? Plant that the register may enter a promise as itself but never had a reason to, or the fix looks like a cheat.
3. **Structural. Rehearsal clauses aren't logged,** and Talek says only logged vows bind. Yet the register "declined" a unison vow, citing coughs that "every *ennu-sa* promise coughed." Which promises did it judge? One line settles it.
4. **Structural. A clever man waits.** Scene 3: "each time its read-back answered, the channel hitched." His test is two days out of the vault, but read-backs reach him upstairs ("pulsing reminder vows into my private read-back... while I sat upstairs"). He would hear them landing on the hitches. Have him test the right variable: stop answering, or log the read-backs, so his wrong conclusion is informative.
5. **Structural. The nine new promises.** The *you* fix alone should stop the coughs. Why does it matter that he keeps only nine, "none of them used *ennu*"? And who says *you* in Sione's promises? The mechanism sentence at "A promise to *you* has two seats" is the one the reader called a "clever trick I half trust". Show one *you* promise cycle on the page, cough-free.
6. **Line-level. Register 4,520:** "one people of ennu has no seat in this clause" can't be parsed cold. Cut to "Outside: one people."
7. **Line-level. Aaruum's cost:** "a two-year buffer" (scene 4) versus "five days" (scenes 6, 7). Pick one.
8. **Line-level.** The paragraph "The mechanism was staring at me from my margin log" restates the line above it; the cold reader marked it as repetition. Cut it, or cut the dialogue.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weight rule-consistency over feeling, and I may be asking a lonely-pronoun story for a theorem. The cold reader was moved by the Mele thread and by the ungrammatical *you*, which I can't judge. Defer to Le Guin on whether the ending's weight survives a thin reason. Note 1 matters most to me, and a reader who wants the grace reading may not need it fixed.
