# Stage 3 critique: Cartographer (run-10)

Novelty only. I do not judge clarity, comedy, scale or whether the other mind
thinks; the Chiang and Banks lenses own those. I read the premises as now
written, including the folded-in twists. I also have no reliable memory of
works after mid-2026, so "uncharted" means "nearest I know of".

Two quick searches this round (an inclusive-dual treaty creole; a salt
mine's pillars as a mind's body) turned up nothing that matches either. The
search tool is weak at this, so that is absence of evidence, not proof.

Nearest to the seed overall, as the audit said and no premise cites:
*Small Gods* (Pratchett, 1992), confidence high.

## A. We-Two

Nearest prior art: *Embassytown* (Miéville, 2011), confidence high, for
language as the substance of a mind, and for the ending where a change in
grammar changes what the people are. *Babel-17* (Delany, 1966) and *Native
Tongue* (Elgin, 1984) are near on pronouns shaping thought, confidence high.
Leckie's *Ancillary Justice* (2013) for pronoun play, confidence high.

Distance: a pronoun that is itself the mind, built to make a treaty
signable, is real distance and I know no match. The climax is nearer the map.
"Change the language to admit the outsider, and the thing you changed isn't
the same" is the shape of *Embassytown*'s close. That is a shape, not a
copy: here the language is the being, and the change is chosen by its own
parties. The cure-would-rewrite-it beat also meets cluster 3 (companion
enrolled as a party) and the keeper's-one-switch entry.

Revised Novelty: 5 on the core, 4 as the plot plays out. Status: uncharted
(core), edge-of-map (the we-three vote). Weighted 36/40, down one from 37.
Push: skip the vote. The parties offer *we-three*, and the being declines the
offer without telling Sione why, so the refusal is its one private act.

## B. The Uncut

Nearest prior art: "The Devil in the Dark" (*Star Trek*, 1967), confidence
high, for miners and a mind in the rock. Zahavi's handicap principle for the
costly-signal rule, confidence high. Le Guin's gift economy in *The
Dispossessed* (1974), confidence high. Slow minds: *Dragon's Egg* (Forward,
1980), confidence high, and Vinge's slow-fast pairings, med.

Distance: a body made of what was left standing, with a town whose wealth is
tonnes not taken, is distance from all of them. The three-day sentence is the
charted part (Forward). The gift of a pillar to the outsider is the new twist
and holds up: nobody else has made the first gift in a culture of abstention.
It sits in cluster 1 (costly good faith) and cluster 6 (fast and slow), so
the plot, not the body, is where it needs care.

Revised Novelty: 4. Status: uncharted. Weighted 33/40, unchanged. Push: let
the being's offer be refused, and make the refusal the first thing the town
mines out of the being, so the town learns to take a no.

## C. True Finds Only

Nearest prior art: *Small Gods* (Pratchett, 1992), confidence high. This is
the one premise that has imported its charted neighbour wholesale: "forty
amateurs who mean it keep the god alive while the official rites starve it"
is Pratchett's shape. The Risks line concedes it. Also near: Clever Hans
(Pfungst, 1907) and Lit et al. (*Animal Cognition*, 2011), nonfiction;
*Dawn* (Butler, 1987) for chemical senses, confidence high.

Distance: the handler who is himself the false positive is real distance, and
the chemistry is fresh. But the recruit-a-few-sincere-people resolution is
the charted half, and the Decision should not count the nose or the network
as novelty when the plot is Pratchett's. It is also the cluster 1 reflex
(good faith gamed) and cluster 3.

Revised Novelty: 3 on the plot, 4 on the mechanism, overall 3. Status:
edge-of-map (the audit's collision 3 had the charted Rouge half in it).
Weighted 31/40, down from 33. Push: don't recruit amateurs. He teaches the
being to read him as noise, so the cure is the being's, not a crowd's.

## D. The Long Quiet

Nearest prior art: *A Deepness in the Sky* (Vinge, 1999), confidence high,
for a species that hibernates through a dark phase. Aldiss's Helliconia
(1982-85), confidence high. Selten's chain-store paradox and the
surprise-examination paradox, nonfiction. Graeber, *Debt* (2011), for the
relationship that ends when it is settled.

Distance: fatigue-life as illness, with kindness as the load that cracks it,
is distance and I know no fiction that does it. The leaked horizon and
backward induction are the audit's completion cluster (cluster 2), flagged
an attractor, and the premise stacks it on the festival cluster (cluster 1).
Those two clusters together are the run's most-repeated reflexes. The seed
mismatch (line 3 weak) is not my call, but the cough from cyclic load is also
a novelty, because it is what makes the card unlike the others.

Revised Novelty: 4. Status: edge-of-map (cluster). Weighted 31/40,
unchanged. Push: the being knows its own end date, keeps it from both
peoples, and its cough is the secret.

## Attractor clusters in the four

- Cluster 3 (companion enrolled as a party): A strongly, C, and B in the
  gift. D alone avoids it.
- Cluster 1 (good faith gamed or costly): B, C, D. A avoids it.
- Cluster 2 (completion): D only.
- Cluster 6 (why a human, fast and slow): B, D.
- Cluster 8 (benevolent deception): none, cleanly.

## Ranking (novelty alone)

1. **A, We-Two.** The only premise whose body is something I can't name a
   precedent for, and the only one clear of cluster 1.
2. **B, The Uncut.** An uncharted body with a genuinely new twist, though it
   sits in clusters 1 and 6.
3. **D, The Long Quiet.** Fresh illness, but it stacks two flagged clusters.
4. **C, True Finds Only.** A fresh mechanism under *Small Gods*' plot.

Why A should win on novelty: nothing in my map is a mind that is a part of
speech, and its sense organ turns every small domestic kindness into
something on the page. The *Embassytown* resemblance is real but sits in the
ending, so the fix is cheap: keep the being's refusal, not the parties'
vote, as the decision. B is the sound runner-up; it is the only other
premise whose body I'd call new.
