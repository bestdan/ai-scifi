# Stage 3 critique: Chiang lens

Blind spots: this lens is low on plot momentum and conflict, and it favours measured, polished pieces. It undervalues comic engines and antagonists, which the brief ("fun and warm, with friction") wants. I skimmed the audit and did not re-search prior art; that is the Cartographer's job. Read my rankings with Banks's pushback in mind.

## A. We-Two

**Question:** what does it cost to be included, and can you be let into a promise without changing the promise? That is a real question about belonging, and it is the concept's own, not borrowed. **Feeling:** it grows from the idea. The being cannot address Sione without using a form with two seats, so its love is a grammatical impossibility, and the cough is the grief. This is the one premise where the emotional climax is the mechanism. **Form:** the idea asks for a close first-person or close-third text with the dual marked on the page, so the reader can feel who is inside each "we". Any other form loses the lever. **Failure at length:** the dual must be demonstrated by scene, not announced. If the reader needs the pronoun explained, the story is a lecture. The "we-three" debate can also turn into a speech. **First page:** yes. A man who is party to nothing and is asked to be someone's "you" is immediately sympathetic. **Other mind:** it thinks. It parses who is inside each utterance and is wrong about its own love, which is genuine theory of mind.

## B. The Uncut

**Question:** does restraint count if it costs nothing, and can a gift be received without being taken? The costly-signal rule is Chiang-shaped and sound. **Feeling:** it grows from the concept: the offered pillar is the being's attempt to learn what taking is. The feeling is intellectual, though, and warmer toward the idea than toward Dawit. **Form:** a plain, physical, procedural telling suits it, with salt practice carrying the weight. It would also work as a mining report or safety manual, but that gives up the companionship. **Failure at length:** three-day sentences against 4,500 words. The being's slowness has to be shown, not asserted, or the pace dies. Line 4 is partial, and the town scenes crowd the companionship. **First page:** Dawit with a tapping hammer, waiting, is good. **Other mind:** it reasons about load and about what was possible to take. That is thinking. It would not read as a transcript.

## C. True Finds Only

**Question:** can an observer read a thing without feeding it? It is a Hawthorne and Clever Hans problem. The question is good and personal. **Feeling:** the twist, that his contentment is the rouge, is the best single image in the four. But the story spends its length on teaching dockhands, which is procedure, not feeling. **Form:** a handler's field notes or case log, which would sharpen the problem. **Failure at length:** two nova and a twist sit at 6,000 words, the top of the range, and the being is "more a patient than a mind". On the brief's strongest pull, a mind that thinks, this is the weakest. **First page:** a man with a rebuilt nose is vivid. **Other mind:** it would read as a patient, so it fails the gate as written.

## D. The Long Quiet

**Question:** what is a relationship worth if its end is known, and is love a festival or a habit? A fine question. **Feeling:** the rigger's widowhood runs beside the concept, not from it. The festival and the cable are about the treaty; the widowhood is attached. **Form:** a life told across wakings, one scene per cycle, would enact the eleven-year gap. It would be the best form in the set. **Failure at length:** two nova in 4,500 words, and the seed's line 3 is *weak* by the synthesis's own account. It turns the seed's mechanism over, which the gate rejects in proxy mode. **First page:** an old rigger is warm. **Other mind:** it reasons about tomorrow's worth, but it is mostly a stated preference.

## Ranking

1. **A.** The idea produces the feeling, the form is demanded, and the seed fit is strong on every line.
2. **B.** Rigorous and sound, but slower and weaker on companionship.
3. **C.** A great twist, weighed down by procedure and a thin mind.
4. **D.** The best form, but a weak seed fit disqualifies it in proxy mode.

**Why A should win.** Only A makes the being's whole perception equal to the novum, so the interpreter's job (company) and the plot (what company costs) are one thing. The ending can deepen the question: changing the grammar to admit Sione both gives him a place and ends the friend he would belong with. Do not let the story settle that. Keep the "we-three" outcome costly to both sides. Watch the attractor risk: the choice must not be a single keeper's switch, so let Sione's broken promises, which the being treasures, be what he can offer instead. Cut the elders-call-it-contamination subplot if length gets tight. It is probably the unnecessary scene.
