# Vinge lens: stage 3 critique, run-10

**Blind spots, stated.** I push futurity and favour structure over people. I
read plain prose and a human-scale payoff as secondary, so I may undervalue
A's warmth and D's comedy. Weigh Chiang and Banks over me on whether any of
these is fun. Note also that the brief floor is F3, and the seed itself is
the unwriteable-mind problem solved by the cheapest trick available: a being
made of a promise is already a mind we can read through its effects.

## A. We-Two

**Futurity reached:** F4. The main mind is a part of speech and it is not
single in the usual sense, it is a relation. **Too familiar:** Sione. A
Tongan-English translator who never belonged is a present-day person in
costume, and "I want to belong" is the oldest want in the genre. The
delegates are familiar too: they argue about a word in a chamber. **Viewpoint
trick:** it holds, and it is the best here. The being sees only promises
spoken in the creole, so the page can show it entirely through what it
notices. The risk is the twist: "it loves him, so it coughs" is the AI that
learns to love, from the style guide's banned list, and it must be shown as
grammar failing, not feeling. **One change:** make the two peoples' "we" not
just pronouns but the unit of personhood. Let the Hhoth's exclusive plurals
set who may own property or be tried, so the dual is a legal person and
"we-three" is a change in what a citizen is. That is F4 held at 4,500 words.

## B. The Uncut

**Futurity reached:** F4, brushing F5 on the being's tempo. **Too familiar:**
the town. Crew boss, shift meeting, laid-off miners: this is a labour-dispute
story with a crystal in it. Danakil salt cutting is a fine detail but
Dawit is a competent working man in a familiar frame. **Viewpoint trick:**
partly holds. Three-day sentences let a human act as the fast end of a slow
mind, which is a real Vinge-type trick (the Zones of Thought are a speed
cap). But it costs pace, and the being's reasoning is shown by what it does
to the pillars, not on the page. **One change:** cut the slowness from the
dialogue and keep it in the economy. Make surface wealth literally
denominated in tonnes not taken, so the town's markets, mortgages and
marriages are priced in restraint, and the reader meets the novum at the
grocery counter. That pushes Reach far further than the shift meeting does.

## C. True Finds Only

**Futurity reached:** F3 to F4. The rebuilt nose is a body modification in a
familiar frame, and the chemical being is the thinnest mind of the four, a
patient. **Too familiar:** the compliance offices, the dock crews, the
inspector. A rebuilt-human specialist who is the fragile sensor is a standard
shape, and it is *Small Gods* in a lab coat. **Viewpoint trick:** fails at
this length. The being "doesn't know it is being propped up", so there is no
mind to show, and the reader gets a man sniffing a vat. The true Vinge
question, how a feedback loop between a mind and its reader looks from
outside, is the interesting part and it gets one scene. **One change:** drop
the nose. Let the interpreter's sweat be the only thing, and let the being
slowly learn to use him on purpose. Then it is a mind that plays the human,
which is the stronger twist and removes a novum.

## D. The Long Quiet

**Futurity reached:** F4 to F5. A species that dries into dormancy for
eleven years, a treaty that lives in a cable, a known last day by induction:
this is the one with real deep time in it. **Too familiar:** the festival
committee, the widowed rigger, "love loudly versus love quietly". The human
frame is the most conventional of the four. **Viewpoint trick:** holds. The
rigger has watched across cycles, so he is the one narrator slow enough for
the being, and the Tun's dormancy gives a clean horizon. **Seed fit** is the
real problem and I will not argue past it: the cough comes from fatigue, not
fudging, and the brief says proxy mode may not spend seed fit to buy scale.
**One change:** if it is narrowed, make the leaked estimate the fudge. The
cough starts when one delegation quietly hedges against the known last day,
so line 3 is served directly and the fatigue is the cause behind the fudge.

## Ranking

1. **A, We-Two.** The viewpoint trick is the strongest and every seed line is
   strong.
2. **B, The Uncut.** The most structurally rigorous, and its economy of
   restraint is a real F4 idea, but the seed's line 4 is partial.
3. **D, The Long Quiet.** The most futurity, but line 3 is weak.
4. **C, True Finds Only.** The thinnest being and the weakest viewpoint trick.

## Why A should win

A is the only premise where the novum is the viewpoint. We cannot see inside
a mind made of a pronoun, but we do not have to: its sense organ is the set of
promises people say aloud, so every scene is already a scene from inside it,
and the reader reads the being by what it can and cannot hear. That is the
Vinge move at short length, a cap on what the mind perceives that makes it
writeable, and it is the only option in which "other mind" reasoning arrives
through the plot with no memo or log to carry it. It is also the only one with
four strong seed lines, so the proxy gate does not cost anything. The two
dangers are real and named: the love twist must stay grammatical and not
turn into an AI that feels, and Sione needs a trade and a daily routine
beyond belonging. Give the dual legal force, as above, and the premise reaches
the top of F4 without leaving the page.
