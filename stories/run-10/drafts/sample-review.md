Lens: banks. Blind spots: I favour sprawl and set pieces, and I'm not a natural short-story reader. I have not judged length or pacing of the whole story, and I have not checked the glossary or payoff ledger.

## Evidence

The cold reader's three words are "dry, wry, warm". The brief asks for "fun and warm, with friction". The bible's Register is "wry, warm, a working interpreter's exactness about words, quick to the joke, slow to the feeling". Two of the three words match the Register directly. "Dry" is the nearest to a gap, and I read it as the narrator's exactness, not a lack of fun. The cold reader's note says the brother-in-law joke "told me not to expect gloom", which is the tone charter working.

## Where the fun lives

- The scale is felt, not stated. "Forty million earpieces across Ninefold gave the same cough", then a hauler on the ice saying "Bless" to nobody, puts the planet and one woman in the same beat. That is the scale-against-the-small-person move I'd ask for.
- The harm is friction and not menace. "Ammaru harbour-master sang it back as eight hundred tonnes and a compliment to the hauler's aunt" is the kind of joke the world makes itself.
- The sample has friction as well as warmth. Vell is liked and nearly commits a fraud by accident. The junior attaché really does cheat, and the cough follows.

## Watch for the rest of the draft (not misses)

These do not fail the match. They are what could thin the fun later.

1. The paragraph beginning "Then Second Speaker Vell rose" carries most of the exposition: the Hhoth and Ammaru *we*, the creole, and why Sione was hired. It is funny ("every Ammaru promise like a claim on the furniture"), but the jokes are all in the narrator's explaining voice. Later scenes need jokes that come out of people talking past each other, as the style guide's rule 4 asks. The cold reader's line came from the narrator and not from a character.
2. "I drank my dead kelp tea feeling thoroughly worth my freight from Earth" is the last self-satisfied beat before the cough. If the narrator stays this pleased with himself in the cough-keeping scenes, the warmth will go smug. He needs at least one thing he gets wrong on the page.
3. The sample ends on a promise of the cough scene ("going downstairs to keep the cough company"). The tone then has to carry the first scene with the being itself. That scene should be where the "fun" sits, because the being, not the narrator, is the comic and warm centre.

No rewrite is needed. Resume the drafter for the whole draft.
