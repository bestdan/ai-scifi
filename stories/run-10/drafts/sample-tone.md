Tone: dry, wry, warm.

The line that set it:

"Fourteen years of interpreting at the Auckland District Court had taught me that when a man says *we* under oath, he usually means himself and the brother-in-law who owns the truck. On Ninefold, the heating was worse, though my job was the same."

(Opening lines. The joke about the brother-in-law told me not to expect gloom, and the complaint about the heating confirmed it.)
