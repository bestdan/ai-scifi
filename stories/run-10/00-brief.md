# Brief: run-10

- **Form and length range:** short story, 3,000 to 6,000 words. The range's
  top is the drafter's hard ceiling, so leave room for what the premise
  turns out to carry.
- **Gates:** proxy. The orchestrator decides every gate after this brief
  and the human reads the story blind. The human knows the seed, so
  "blind" here means the angle, the characters and the ending.
- **Futurity floor:** F3 (see `process/rubric.md`)
- **Tone:** fun and warm, with friction. The tone charter in
  `process/style-guide.md` always applies.
- **Seed (from issue #51, verbatim):** Two alien civilizations once signed
  an agreement, and the agreement woke up. It's a being made entirely of
  the promise between them, and it stays alive only as long as both sides
  keep it. Every fudged clause gives it a cough, and every quiet act of
  good faith puts colour in its cheeks. A human interpreter is hired to
  keep it company.
- **Seed adherence:** every card keeps the seed: a living being made of an
  agreement between two alien civilizations, whose health tracks how well
  both sides keep it, and a human interpreter hired to keep it company.
  Lenses may wander in what follows from it: what the treaty covers, how
  it woke, what its body and senses are, who pays for its health, and why
  a human is the one hired.
- **Off-limits:** nothing beyond the style guide.
- **Must include:** nothing.
- **Idea cards per lens:** 3.
- **Panel changes:** defaults.
- **Notes:**
  - **The drafter is Gemini, not the `drafter` role.** At the human's
    request, stage 6, every stage 8 revision and the stage 9 polish are
    written by Gemini 4 Argon (`gemini-4-argon-high`) through `agy`, in
    one resumed conversation, per `dev_docs/external-drafter.md`. It is
    the model run-08 and run-09 used, and the human rated its voice

  - **The run is unattended.** The orchestrator logs each gate decision,
    with its runner-up, in `STATUS.md`. Nothing the human sees before
    reading reveals the angle.
