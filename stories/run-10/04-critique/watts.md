# Watts critique: 04-bible.md (run-10)

Blind spots: I underrate ritual and joy, and I lean cold. The tone charter
and the brief ("fun and warm") overrule my instincts here. I aim these notes
at strangeness, not misery. I haven't checked the arithmetic past one row,
because Egan owns that.

1. **[must-fix] The being's love has no selective logic.** Ask what ennu's
   affection is *for*. The bible gives it a "want" (to be said to) with no
   mechanism. Its body is the register of kept promises, so its reward
   gradient is already there: kept promises warm it, and Sione is the one
   creature who produces a steady supply of them. Say that on the page. It
   loves him the way a gut loves a reliable food source, and it doesn't
   know that. Then its address to him costs the signatories because the
   lattice has two seats per utterance and he is now sitting in one. Put
   that line in scene 5, beside the log.

2. **[must-fix] Sione's ear is too reliable.** "Hears who is inside a 'we'
   without trying" and the Vell scene, where Vell is "genuinely surprised,
   rephrases", give one mind more clean self-knowledge than the evidence
   supports. Clusivity in Tongan is grammar. In the creole, with its single
   word, Sione is inferring intent from prosody and context, and people
   confabulate intent constantly. Let him flag an exclusion that wasn't
   there, in scene 1 or 3, because he has heard doors shut in Mele's
   *kimaua* for thirty years. That is the same error ennu makes ("a seat
   can be made by wanting it"), and the story can let the reader see the
   rhyme without anyone saying it.

3. **[must-fix] "Why a human" fails the why-not-just test.** Tongan is not
   special: te reo Māori has *tāua* and *māua*, so Auckland holds many
   Listeners with the same ear, and Fijian and Samoan have it too. The
   reader will ask why not a Hhoth-Ammaru child from the shelf schools,
   who grew up in the creole. Answer both on the page, in scene 1. The
   shelf-school children hear ennu used badly and don't flinch, because
   the habit is already in them. Sione is chosen because he is outside
   both peoples and knows what exclusion costs.

4. **[must-fix] The numbers contradict.** The Drilling fell "in a Gathering
   year" 63 years ago, which is nine Gatherings at seven-year intervals,
   so the next Gathering is now, not "in 2 years". Change one of the two
   figures. Check that the memorial anniversary lands in the Gathering
   cycle, since the Ammaru sing there.

5. **[should] Name the enforcement mechanism neither people evolved.** Hhoth
   trust is kin, and a promise to non-kin is the puzzle. Ammaru "we" means
   everyone alive, with no kin discrimination, which is exactly why they
   are exploitable. Neither species could police a cheater. The cough is a
   punishment signal that works for them where biology can't, and that is
   the better reason the lattice matters. Show it in one small scene: a
   hauler says ennu to a buyer, means only her brood, and the whole street
   stops and says "bless". Everyone has been caught, publicly, and the
   shame is the enforcement.

6. **[should] The ending flinches twice.** "The cough eases but doesn't end"
   is a soft landing. Sione's *kitaua* to Mele resolves a thirty-year wound in
   one message, which the style guide calls a one-paragraph epiphany. Make
   *kitaua* a promise, the 212nd on his list, one he can break. Then the
   truth is unsettling and warm together: ennu now lives partly off his
   fidelity to his sister, and ennu itself has never been allowed to lose
   him. Leave Mele's reply undecided.

7. **[could, but I'm at six so fold into 5]** Hauling ice for deuterium needs
   a reason: ordinary ocean-world ice is barely enriched. Name a source
   (cometary-rich ice) or change the cargo.

Must-fix: (1) give ennu's love a selective logic, (2) let Sione's ear be
fallible, (3) fix the "why a human" answer, (4) the Gathering arithmetic
(63 years vs "next in 2 years") contradicts.
