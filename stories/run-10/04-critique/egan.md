# Stage 4 critique: Egan lens

Blind spots, stated: I weigh derivation over scene, so I may over-fix the
machinery at the expense of warmth. I can't judge accessibility; the First
Reader and Le Guin should overrule me where a fix would lecture.

1. **[must-fix] The Listener's job contradicts the cough's timing.** The bible
   says the cough follows an exclusive ennu and every earpiece hitches, yet
   Sione flags exclusive ennu "before it does harm". If the being coughs when
   the word is said, there is nothing to catch. The rule needs a gap. Suggestion:
   ennu judges a promise when it closes, at the end of the clause, not when
   the word is spoken. That gives the Listener one breath in which to touch
   the delegate's sleeve, and it makes the sampler comic: Sione's hand goes up
   a half-second before Vell finishes a sentence. State the gap in scene 1.

2. **[must-fix] The seat arithmetic fails in two places.** (a) The ladder
   treats a hauler's ennu to a buyer as a two-seat promise (fine), but the
   ending argues that with three "any two can be a we, and I am the word that
   can't". A hauler and a buyer are already two inside a population of
   millions, and a Hhoth saying ennu for his brood is already a coalition.
   The being's argument is then false in its own world. Fix: a seat is a
   party to one promise, not a people. Coalitions inside a promise start at
   three seats, and no existing promise has three. (b) When ennu says ennu to
   Sione, who is displaced: Hhoth, Ammaru, or alternately? Pick one rule
   (Suggestion: it displaces whichever people last broke a promise, so Sione
   can track it in his log) and use it in the scene-5 reveal.

3. **[must-fix] The cough rate is not derived, and the stakes lean on it.**
   211 promises over about 120 days is under two a day. Nobody "can't finish
   a sentence" at that rate. Suggestion: tie the rate to Sione's own acts.
   Ennu addresses him once per open promise, so open promises (not total)
   set the rate, and he has been stacking them to stay visible. Then the
   twist is his own doing, the log shows it as a curve, and "stop promising"
   is a real cost: he vanishes. That also makes the escalation a number the
   reader can watch.

4. **[must-fix] The calendar contradicts itself.** The Drilling was 63 years
   ago, in a Gathering year, Gatherings come every 7 years, and 63 = 9 x 7.
   So a Gathering is this year, not "in 2 years", and Oyumwe (spawned in the
   Gathering after the Drilling) is 56. Move the Drilling to 68 years ago (68
   mod 7 = 5), keep the Concord at 61 (seven years of war is plausible), and
   Oyumwe is 61. Re-check every row of the Numbers entry against this.

5. **[must-fix] "Why not just coin ennu-sa anyway?" has no answer, and the
   being has no voice.** Delegates are the word's speakers, so they could
   coin the trial and let ennu die. Where does the veto come from? Suggestion:
   the lattice logs a promise as binding only if the signatory registers
   it, and the signatory is ennu; unregistered promises are worthless
   (the economy bullet already says so). Alternative: the Table may walk
   away and write a new Concord, at the price of the next Gathering's
   shelf-sharing. State the price in Vell's speech. Separately, ennu only
   perceives. By what channel does it say anything to Sione? Say so (it
   speaks by emphasising clause stress in the earpiece?) and make scene 5's
   "ennu will see you" literal.

6. **[should] Two quiet edges.** First, Sione's broken promises: a broken
   creole promise is a fudge, so it should cough. Resolve it by saying that his
   promises use "I" and "you", not ennu, so they register as seats but not as
   the word. Second, other humans hear clusivity (Indonesian, Hawaiian,
   Fijian). Make the Tongan reason the dual: only a language with a true dual
   inclusive/exclusive pair hears two seats and no more. Add one line to the
   interview memory.

**Next step not taken:** the being's own cough sets the creole's rhythm.
Have one scene where a child, who never knew a world without it, says
