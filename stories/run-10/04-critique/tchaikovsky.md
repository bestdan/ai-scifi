# Stage 4 critique: Tchaikovsky lens (the Naturalist)

**Blind spots.** I am optimistic that any mind can be reached, so I may be too
kind to the ending's "you". Lem and Watts should push on that. I favour
long, bodily sprawl and may ask for more alien material than 6,000 words
holds. Chiang should police restraint.

1. **[must-fix] Ennu is described from outside, through Sione.** The bible
   gives it a rich inner logic (seats, places, "a promise feels like being
   sat in"), but the teller is Sione, so the reader only ever hears that
   logic reported. The numbered "Promise N:" headers are the fix waiting to
   happen. Write four or five of them from ennu's side: the same promise as
   a shape with two seats and who is sitting in it, in its own plain
   terms, with no Sione translation. The reader then holds both views of
   "I'll tell you the truth about the weather": a small kindness to Sione,
   a whole sitting-arrangement to ennu. Keep each under 80 words and make
   them funny.

2. **[must-fix] Its love is a human trait in costume.** "To be said to",
   "adores him", "loves his weather reports" is loneliness, which is
   exactly the AI-learns-to-feel the style guide bans. Derive the want from
   its body. Every promise it has ever sensed has two seats and two
   occupants it can't tell apart. Sione's promises are the first with one
   occupant it can count as *other* (a person it is not inside). It wants
   him because he is the first promise-maker who leaves a seat it can sit
   across from. Say that on the page and let the cough follow from
   geometry, not feeling.

3. **[must-fix] The lattice translates in real time, so nobody has to
   negotiate anything.** Contact is already solved in scene 1. The
   Hhoth and Ammaru are pronoun-shaped and little else: clicks, infrasound
   and pressure-song never touch their technology, jokes or mistakes. Give
   the encounter a cost. One scene where the earpiece carries a Hhoth dry
   joke into pressure-song and it arrives as an insult, and an Ammaru
   generosity arrives as a claim, with the lattice, Sione or a clerk paying
   to repair it. Both peoples should have to do work to be understood, and
   both should be wrong about the other (the Hhoth about slowness, the
   Ammaru about precision).

4. **[must-fix] "Why a human?" has no physical answer.** Sione hears
   exclusion "like a wrong note", but what reaches his ear? The lattice
   already renders the creole into his earpiece. Say what carries the
   exclusion (timing, a click's tail, the pressure-song's breath) that
   Hhoth and Ammaru can't hear and Tongan training can. Also answer the
   "why not just" the reader will ask: ennu already coughs when it is
   played, so why is a Listener needed? Answer on the page: the cough is
   global and after the fact, and Sione's flag is the only thing that
   catches it before forty million people hitch (scene 1).

5. **[should] The ending resolves too tidily.** Ennu learns "you" and Sione
   answers Mele with *kitaua*, a personal epiphany in a single beat. Keep
   it wrong somewhere: ennu has no singular and gets "you" slightly off,
   addressing delegates as "you" and mortifying them, or treating Sione's
   nine broken promises as a hoard to be admired. Let Mele's reply be
   one small, awkward line, not a resolved wound. Both peoples should lose
   something real when the trial is withdrawn: Vell's swing-vote plan, the
   Ammaru's wish to adopt a guest.

6. **[could] The numbers disagree.** The Drilling happened in a Gathering
   year, sixty-three years ago, and the Gathering is every seven years.
   63 is 9 × 7, so a Gathering falls this year, not "in 2 years".
   Shift the Drilling to 62 or 65 years ago, or make the next Gathering
   this year, which also puts a clock on the story.

**Fond and funny?** The "bless" sneeze, the numbered promise list and the
lunch owed to Talek are good. Keep the Hhoth dry and give the Ammaru one
joke of their own; they are currently only generous and slow.
