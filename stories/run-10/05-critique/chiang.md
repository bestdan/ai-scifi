# Stage 5 critique: Chiang lens

Blind spots: I am low on plot momentum and conflict. The panel's Asimov and Banks should judge pace and wit; I judge whether the form and ending come from the idea.

The idea is strong and the form fits it. The register entries are the only thing ennu can see, so the reader sits in its seat. Notes follow.

1. **[must-fix] The ending's mechanism is unstated.** Scene 7 says "You is the seat nobody else is in," but the story never says why a promise to Sione that uses "you" displaces no one, when "ennu will" displaced someone each time. Scene 5 defines the harm as Sione sitting across from ennu in a dual. Say on the page what "you" does to the seats. A cough measured before and after, shown in one register entry, would do it. Otherwise the climax runs beside the idea instead of growing from it. This is also where the page lets the AI-learns-to-be-human reading in. State plainly that "you" is a grammar fix, with the seat still wrong.

2. **[must-fix] Scenes 5 to 7 repeat the same tradeoff.** In scene 5 Sione learns that each kindness costs a stranger. The obvious fix is to close his promises and fade from ennu. Scene 7 then does exactly that, after a detour through ennu-sa. End scene 5 on the real dilemma, stated aloud: stop promising and be unseen, or keep promising and keep paying. Then ennu-sa answers a different question, which is whether anyone should reshape the word for him. At present Part IV's question is Part III's with a vote attached.

3. **[must-fix] Scene 1 carries too much.** It has 750 words, seven new names or terms (Sione, Vell, ennu, the Table, the Listener, the register, kitaua/kimaua), all five world facts, a flag, a hitch and a memory. Move the test memory and kitaua/kimaua into scene 3, where Tikk flags Sione. Keep scene 1 to Sione, Vell, the clause, the hitch and "bless."

4. **[should] Show the cost, don't state it.** Ressa pays on the page and Sione's cost is only "sees him less." Put it in a register entry. Ennu's annotation could note that the far seat is empty for three days and that it keeps the scar. The entries are the one place a cost can land without Sione explaining it. Also, "I'll come on the fourth day" returns in the final entry, which is the banned echo of the first line. Name it in a waiver, or have the last entry carry a different promise.

5. **[could] Cut or fold scene 4's Mele beat, or scene 3's hunt for the cheating delegate.** Scene 4 has the misflag, the bore and Mele in 750 words, and scene 3 holds six items in 900. The misflag does the work on his certainty, and the Mele reread can sit in scene 7 as the lead-in to kitautolu. A light extra device: let the entry's "Seats:" line list who was displaced, blank until scene 5, so the reader can solve the twist from the entries.

**Ending deepens or answers?** It mostly deepens: ennu still gets "you" wrong, and Mele's answer is nine days away. The risk is note 1.
