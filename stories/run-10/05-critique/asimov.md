# Stage 5 critique: Asimov lens (Logician)

Blind spots: I read the outline as a system of rules and clues. I am weak on
interiority, the body, and whether the Mele thread and the song at the bore
will land emotionally. Let Chiang and Banks judge those.

1. **[must-fix] The cough has two mechanisms, and the clue depends on telling
   them apart.** World fact 4 says a hitch fails every promise in mid-air.
   The novum says only the promise ennu displaced fails (whoever was
   "promised to" at that moment). Ressa's contract failing four times needs
   one rule. State it in one sentence in scene 2 or 3: a cough is a seat
   emptied, and the earpiece hitch is how the lattice announces it. Then
   separate the two causes on the page. A delegate's played ennu coughs at
   clause close. Ennu's reply coughs in the gap after Sione's promise is
   answered. A reader can then time the hitches and beat Sione to it.

2. **[must-fix] Someone has to be stupid until scene 5.** The clerks log
   every hitch, Talek holds the register, and Sione is a trained interpreter
   who hunts a "cheating delegate" for weeks without checking whether hitches
   follow ennu's own replies. Talek's "shift change" is the only cover.
   Give the clerks a reason not to see it: ennu's replies run through the
   read-back channel the clerks were built never to read (privacy, which both
   peoples insisted on). Make Sione's theory a reasonable one, with a
   delegate who really is cheating in scene 3, so the hunt finds something
   and still misses the cause.

3. **[must-fix] Scene 6 is a detour unless the simple fix fails first.**
   Once the log shows the cause, Sione can close his open promises, which is
   exactly what he does in scene 7. Part IV's question ("can the peoples
   make room?") is only a question if closing them costs him ennu. Make
   scene 5's turn an attempt: he closes three, ennu goes dark to him for an
   hour, and the cost lands. Then the trial is an offer to avoid that cost,
   and scene 7 is the same cost accepted.

4. **[must-fix] The climax's reason is not planted.** Ennu declines because
   every rehearsal promise "seated two against one", but scene 6 only shows
   delegates saying the word in unison. Plant three or four rehearsal
   promises with their seat-counts on the page ("ennu-sa will outvote the
   shelf"), at least one in the register entry before 7. A reader who counts
   can predict the refusal. Without them the refusal looks like ennu's mood.

5. **[should] Overloaded scenes break the length budget and the
   introductions rule.** Scene 1 carries all five world facts, seven names or
   terms, a flag and a test memory in 750 words. Scene 3 carries Tikk,
   Lesieli, lu pulu, brood, the weather and the hunt in 900. Scene 7 carries
   the refusal, the closing, Vell's drink, Mele and an entry in 700. Move
   Lesieli's message to scene 4 beside Mele and cut Vell's drink to a line.
   Introduce *kitaua/kimaua* in scene 1 and defer *brood* to 3. The numbers
   also need one fix: 40 open, 31 closed, 9 kept reads as the nine broken
   ones. Say "nine promises left open" or keep the counts apart.

Rules are statable by the midpoint (scenes 1, 2, 4). Stakes (scene 5) comes
before the climax, and Why, Reach and Legal party all have explicit scenes.
