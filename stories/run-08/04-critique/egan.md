# Stage 4 critique: Egan lens (run-08, "Forty")

Blind spots: I weight derivation over scene and can under-read the comedy. Let the First Reader and Le Guin push back on anything below that costs warmth.

The arithmetic holds where it is stated (0.01 a year is 0.0000274 a day; every heading figure rounds correctly; 40 - 45 = -5). The breaks are elsewhere.

1. **[must-fix] The table is impossible as written.** "Every element through 117 in pure form" can't be. Past about element 99 the half-lives run from days to milliseconds, and astatine and the heavier elements can't be held as a macroscopic sample at all. A reader who knows this stops believing the climax. Either (a) say the table is atoms made and counted, with everything heavy than about 94 made live and witnessed, so 118 to 120 are just the last three of many; or (b) move the island of stability so the heavy elements last. Option (a) is cheaper and makes Cistern's missing witness matter for dozens of entries, not three. State the choice in scene 2 or 3.

2. **[must-fix] The twist has no observable difference.** The installation logs what her eyes see, so hiding from it and hiding from her look identical to the birds, and to her. The bible never says how they found a watcher on day nine, or what behaviour differs. Give them both. Detection: the implant's uplink gives off ultraviolet, which they see and she can't. Difference: they show things to her only when her eyes are shut or her back is turned, so hands learn and the log doesn't. A blindfolded Lusk handling the elements in scene 7 would be the slapstick and the proof of trust in one scene.

3. **[must-fix] Scene 7 contradicts the edge case.** The Twist row has Cistern showing Lusk the cache in scene 7. The installation is her eyes, so a shown cache there is already spent. Split it: scene 7 is touch only (blindfold, hands), scene 8 is the one sighted showing, the deliberate spend. The ledger's "Shown in" cells need to say so.

4. **[must-fix] "I had never been uncertain" is wrong at -5.** Log-odds of -5 is near-certainty the world is fine, the same installed certainty with the sign flipped. The line should be the joke: she is as sure of "fine" as she was of "dying", and from inside it feels the same. That also finishes Lusk's arc about the Office manufacturing certainty.

5. **[should] Why 40, and why this observation?** "As high as possible short of 1" has no upper bound, so 40 is arbitrary. Make it the actuaries' stated ceiling of what any one observation could ever carry (their largest on record is about 38). Then 45 beats it only because this world is off every table, and Gennet, her wound, is a world where nothing came close. Also say why a spent workshop or furnace doesn't carry enough: show a lesser cache worth about 12 and have Lusk do the sum.

6. **[should] The Concern already holds the footage.** The log goes home, so the table is seen by the Office the moment it is made. Say what stays unspent: the Concern's archive is one watcher in cold storage, and no ten thousand staff, no survey of the vents. Orbital heat and radiation would show furnaces and an accelerator; give one line (geothermal vents under compost, heat read as rot).

Next step not taken: if every future agent's prior is lowered, the Office also loses its only way to judge a world fine. Let the Desk's message name that cost.
