## The one thing
Cut the Day 4 lecture back and give the cache the page. The stage 3 warning was that the story must live in the cost of the cache, not the log-odds, or it becomes Egan again. Day 4 is where it slips.

## Notes

1. **Day 4, the "base-ten log of the odds" paragraphs, the Hessary Inquiry, and the table speech (structural).** This is the draft's drift. The installed-certainty, Bayes-readout material is the territory nearest Egan, "Axiomatic" (1990), high confidence. It is also the attractor cluster 1 the synthesis flagged. Cistern's "Somebody has to remember where I put things" is the uncharted part, and the first reader says it is the best line. Yet the reader reports "drag in the middle, where the story explains rules at length", and "The arithmetic ... went past me". Halve the arithmetic. Let the Day 37 and Day 114 headers carry the rate. The Hessary notebooks image can stay, since it is concrete, but the "doubt kills" lesson is a stock origin for a bad institution, so one sentence is enough.

2. **Day 118, the proof (structural).** The climax is the 120 synthesis, and the page spends its length on equipment ("capacitor banks", "californium-249 target drum", "scintillator crystal"). The first reader called it "a thick wall of equipment" and couldn't picture it. This is the fan-pleasing island-of-stability beat, charted in the sense that hard SF always ends on the big experiment. What is uncharted is the cost, which is four hundred birds walking out and Cistern not closing the shutters. Move the length there. The line "Her table was complete, and her table was spent, in the same forty microseconds" is the real climax and is a single sentence.

3. **Day 118 to the end, the "same machine, other way" turn.** The −5.0000 reversal ("I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way") is the fresh move. The nearest I know of is Keyes, *Flowers for Algernon* (1959), in a different sense, and I know of no direct neighbour for it, so confidence is medium. The paragraph after it ("The same unaudited model that had watched two hundred birds...") restates it. Cut the restatement.

4. **Section 8, Harriet Baines, the coffee and the refit (structural, stock beat).** "Your addendum triggered a service-wide review" followed by a fix that makes the machine worse ("sub-dermal tactile sensors in all ten fingertips") is the bureaucracy-absorbs-the-protest ending. Chiang's "Liking What You See" (2002) and any number of Banks scenes have it. It does earn something, because the birds' last unwatched channel is closed. But the draft makes it a pleasant office scene with real coffee. The reach is explicit, which is good. The push off the map: let the refit be the reason the box can never be unspent. At the moment she says "I declined the refit" and the cost lands on her, not on the birds. Make the cost land on a bird, such as a new survey that names the flock.

5. **The box, "Don't look" (mysterious final image).** The style guide asks for two specific readings. The reader asked only "what is in the box?" and "I think I'm meant to leave alone." That suggests one reading (a gift she keeps unseen), and the other (what it contains) cannot be answered. It is acceptable because the story's logic is that not looking is the gift. Name the second reading once on the page, for example that the box is a cache that only stays unspent while the Office can't log touch, which the refit now threatens. As written the box and the refit never touch.

6. **Prior art for the hiding-corvid culture.** Tchaikovsky, *Children of Memory* (2022), high confidence, has corvids with their own cognition. Watts, *Blindsight* (2006), and "Blink" (2007) have things that move when unobserved. The draft's join (hoard that only counts if witnessed live, the glint as the thing they hide from) has no neighbour I know of. Distance is real. Keep the birds' reasoning about her gaze front and centre.

7. **Stock beats, line-level.** Hands in the dark, blindfold-as-trust, "walks me through by touch" is a familiar intimacy scene. The ratchet is the physical specifics (two forward, two back toes). Keep those. The "forty-two winters of purified elements sitting under the green light like cracked pots in a mud yard" echo is good, since it closes the Day 1 image.
