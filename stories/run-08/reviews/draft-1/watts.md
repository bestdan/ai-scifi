## The one thing

Repair the physics and the power budget in Days 117 and 118, then cut that catalogue prose to one scene's worth. Cistern's table is the story's rigor claim, and right now it fails a check a reader could run in a minute.

## Notes

1. **Day 118, "calcium-50 ions ... into the californium-249 target drum" (structural).** Ca (Z=20) plus Cf (Z=98) makes 118, not 120. Element 120 needs Ti-50 on Cf-249, or Cr-54 on Cm-248, and you already have curium and californium on the bench. The bigger problem is rate: the cross-section is expected in femtobarns, and Og took months of beam per event. "Two seconds later the scintillator crystal flashed" is the eye-roll line. Have the beam run for weeks, with silent birds on watch and a bell wired to the chain trigger. That fits "I have made 118, 119, and 120 three times each since he died", and it is more tender.
2. **Day 117, "ninety-nine pure samples" (line-level).** Astatine (85) and francium (87) have never existed in visible quantity, so a biologist or chemist will see the cut at 99 break at two entries. Give those two to the "made live" list. Also, "warm curium and californium at ninety-seven and ninety-eight" is wrong, because 97 is berkelium.
3. **Day 117, treadle power (structural).** Forty birds treading a bellows give hundreds of watts. A cryogenic Nb₃Sn ring, liquid hydrogen and capacitor banks need orders of magnitude more. Day 4 says "power" is a by-product, but the page never names the source. Name it, perhaps karst hydro on the aquifers it vents into, in one concrete line.
4. **Day 118, "I recognized the exact texture of that relief."** On Gennet she says she could only see the prior's work "after the prior came out of my head on the ship home." Here she catches it instantly. Let the relief be sincere for a paragraph and the recognition come late, which is truer to confabulation. "Exactly as sure as I had been" also conflicts with 40 versus −5.
5. **Closing paragraph, "the only thing in my head I know is mine" (flinch).** She has just shown that a felt certainty can be installed. Her authentic doubt is another feeling she can't audit. One clause of suspicion would keep the ending honest.
6. **Day 1 and Day 117 (structural).** Cistern could patch the left eye and tour the hall unspent, so the whole blindfold route looks avoidable. One line of the birds trying and failing would close it. The installation also hears, which may be the answer, so say so.
7. **Day 114 (structural).** Ten to the fortieth is justified only by the Hessary trauma. Who benefits from an unfalsifiable prior? Remediation has ten thousand staff, so say what the Office gains.
8. **Day 9, "six light-hours back" with a four-hour reply.** The timing is inconsistent. Line-level.
9. **Day 118 (structural).** The ring hall's spectrum and footage "went home". Say that nobody reads it, because only the number is audited. Otherwise the birds are spent to the whole archive.
10. **Day 4 (Character).** Both Perpetua's Hessary speech and Cistern's elements speech are lectures. The first reader skimmed them. Stage one as friction, with Cistern changing the subject.

Real science worth citing in the bible: scrub jays re-cache when watched (Dally, Emery and Clayton 2006), Clark's nutcrackers recall about 30,000 caches, and corvid gaze-following. These ground the premise.

## What to protect

- "If I trust you, what you know is mine. That's what trust is for. Somebody has to remember where I put things."
- Cistern addressing her remarks to the weeds.
- The jigsaw piece hidden to make the board safe.
- "My number said −5.0000. I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way, on the word of the same machine."
- The Harriet coda, where the fix is to log touch. It is the best consequence in the story.
- The alcove 115 flicker, with the Mc chain correct (Ca-48 on Am-243).

## Blind spot disclosure

I over-weight physical accuracy and under-weight ritual and warmth, so the first reader's delight in the puzzle may matter more than my rigor notes. Notes 1 to 3 are fixable cheaply.
