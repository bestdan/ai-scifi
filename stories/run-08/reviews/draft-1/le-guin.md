## The one thing

Put more of the society into scenes and cut the briefings that stand in for it. The society is the story, and the best of it comes through gesture: the claws prying a fist open, the stolen puzzle piece. Day 4's guild-and-table speech and Day 118's equipment paragraph tell the reader what those gestures already show.

## Notes

1. **Day 4, "I am an assayer of the guild. Under the marl our deep workshops…" (structural):** Cistern delivers the economy as a paragraph, and the first reader skimmed it. A bird who has lived with this wouldn't explain it unprompted. Give the table a scene earlier, or let Perpetua find the flux and ask. Day 117's tour then pays it off instead of repeating it.
2. **Day 118, the hall paragraph (structural):** The reader was "waiting for the experiment to happen." This is the story's one gathering, the table complete, and it reads as a parts list. Cut to what Cistern's feet do, and let the birds turning outward carry the image.
3. **Who pays (structural):** The cost is visible and I'd keep it: Seven-Holes' grandmother's transit, "Seven-Holes never forgave me," the flock's four-hundred-year works. But the vote on Day 114 is settled before the count, which the first reader felt. Seven-Holes's dissent is the only real friction. Let someone besides Cistern argue for her side, or let the vote be close.
4. **Day 4, how the birds learn Concern standard:** Cistern speaks "stiff Concern standard" and Under-the-Steps "trade-speech." Where does a flock that hides from outsiders get a stranger's tongue? One line on the language, or on who traded, would make the world's edges feel real.
5. **Names (line-level):** "Under-the-Cistern," "Seven-Holes" and "Under-the-Steps" come from the world, since each is named for a decoy. That is the best invented-word work in the draft. Cistern's own explanation is fine ("your mouth lacks the click in the middle"). The glossary check still needs to cover "moot," "assayer" and "the table."
6. **Day 9, "six light-hours back" (line-level):** She waits six hours, but the reply comes four hours later. The first reader read it as a slip.
7. **Day 118 to the final section (structural):** The Harriet Baines scene is cheerful ("said brightly") and briefly makes the Concern a mild, tidy institution. The irony works: the reform fixes the leak and ignores the cost. But a more ambiguous utopia would let Baines mean it, and let Perpetua gain something from it. The birds are never mentioned in it. Does the Office ask whether the table was ever rebuilt? Leave that unanswered, but let Perpetua not ask.
8. **Gennet (line-level):** Keep it. It is the one place the number's cost to her is personal, and it comes late. A line of it earlier would give the reader a reason to care about Perpetua before Day 37.

## What to protect

- "If I trust you, what you know is mine. That's what trust is for. Somebody has to remember where I put things."
- Cistern addressing the weeds, and "I am forgetting my corners, Perpetua Lusk."
- The jigsaw: a bird steals one piece "so no passing stranger can ever have a look at the whole harbour," and the empty dent at the end.
- "I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way, on the word of the same machine."
- "A doubt about Gennet slipped right past the gate, cold and sharp and entirely mine."
- The structure of trust as a gift pressed in from behind, which reads as a carrier-bag act and not a thrust.
- The sensor refit: "No future officer will be teachable by hand behind her installation's back."

## Blind spot disclosure

I favour social texture and am slow on mechanism. I may undervalue the physics scenes and the arithmetic, which the Banks, Egan and Watts lenses will judge better, and I may overvalue the Baines irony because it carries a moral. My pace preference may also be pushing the cuts in notes 1 and 2 harder than a hard-SF reader would want.
