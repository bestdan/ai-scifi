# Graded ledger rows

| Row | The fact |
|---|---|
| Premise | A Concern officer issued near-certainty that a world is in crisis is sent to birds who hide whatever is watched; only a cache they show her can change her mind, and showing her spends it |
| Stakes | If her number is above zero on day 120, Remediation comes and every cache on the world is spent; if the proof is shown, Cistern's life's work is spent |
| Why | The Concern issues near-certainty because Hessary's open-minded assessor took 31 years while two billion starved; the 120-day window and the audit-only (camera and microphone) evidence rule come from the same Inquiry |
| Twist | Before: the birds hide from Lusk by instinct, Cistern is exasperated with her, and the fledgling's eyes-shut games are a child's games. After: from day nine the birds, having seen the installation's ultraviolet glint and tested it, have been hiding from the installation, not from her, and teaching her to know things by touch, which it can't log; the behind-the-back handovers were trust being made |
| Ending | Cistern lets the installation watch element 120 made live, the last square of her table. It is the observation that swamps the prior (40 − 45 = −5), Remediation is cancelled, and the table is completed (Lusk, a trusted one, witnessed it) and spent (the installation, an outsider, saw it) in the same moment. Lusk notices she is now exactly as certain the other way, by the same unaudited model. She reports that the prior was wrong, ending her promotion. Cistern gives her a cache of her own, pressed into her hand from behind, which she must never look at; she has kept it unlooked-at since |
| Reach | The issued prior staffs every Concern mission, so this world's proof makes the Actuarial Office review the prior for every future agent, and it also closes the birds' trick, because the Office starts logging touch; on the birds' world, Remediation would end their art and the industry that is its by-product |
