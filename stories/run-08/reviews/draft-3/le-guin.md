## The one thing

Resolve who the second memory is. Cistern's creed is that a trusted one's knowing is hers, and Lusk was made that one. The coda now says the archive is "a witness that will outlive Cistern as she chose and never trusted". The sentence is tangled, and it still has Cistern choosing the outsider's archive. Lusk is the one who remembers, so let the coda say that Lusk holds the three silver notes for her, and let the archive be what Cistern paid, not what she chose.

## Notes

1. **Coda, P225 (structural):** "as she chose and never trusted" reads two ways and the first reader did not mention it, so it is not landing. Say plainly: the archive keeps the recording, Lusk keeps the memory, and only one of them is Cistern's.
2. **The birds' fate (structural):** Item 4 admits Seven-Holes was right ("my look had taken in the whole hall"), but the coda says only that the world was struck off the list. Do the furnaces go cold? One domestic detail from a later survey or a retrieved letter would do. The first reader's last question is still open.
3. **Day 114 moot (structural, carried):** "Nearly three hundred pairs of rear claws struck the basalt twice." One dissent. It was logged as declined; I carry it to the debrief. The gathering is the story's one communal scene and it is narrated, not argued.
4. **Day 4 opening (structural, carried):** the first reader was bored by the Hessary block. It is the cost of the setup and the gate may need it, so I do not press it.
5. **Prose (line-level):** the reflex triplets are still there ("hot slate, clean oil, and dried clover"; "wet feathers, hot tallow, and crushed mint"). Adverb openers still crowd the action ("Frantically", "Instantly", "Suddenly"). Day 118's hall paragraph is one long inventory.
6. **Day 37 speech (line-level, carried):** Cistern's californium and half-life lecture is delivered while she presses an ampoule into a blind hand. Keep "I am forgetting my corners."
7. **Harriet Baines (line-level):** her speech states the theme ("No future officer will learn one thing with her hands while her head logs another"). Let the refit be a memo line and let Lusk's dock post carry it.

## What to protect

- "If I trust you, what you know is mine. That's what trust is for. Somebody has to remember where I put things."
- "Keep your hands in your lap or she will spend your boots."
- Under-the-Steps and the salt-barrel game, with twenty adults listening in dead silence.
- The jigsaw, the stolen piece, and the thumb in the empty dent.
- "I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way, on the word of the same machine."
- "It's yours," ... "Don't look." Trust as a gift pressed in from behind is the carrier-bag act, and the climax is a vigil, not a fight.

## Blind spot disclosure

I favour social texture and distrust exposition, so I may undervalue the Day 4 and Day 117 explanations the comprehension gate needs; the gate outranks me there. I read slowly, and may overvalue the moot and the coda's moral irony. I have no standing on the physics of element 120.
