# Comprehension answers: draft 3 (from memory, no reread)

1. A field officer for a service that "fixes worlds" lands on a planet with an implanted certainty that it is dying. She finds a society of birds who hide everything they value, because anything an outsider sees is "spent". She ends up giving up her promotion, and the birds give up their workshop, to prove the world is fine.

2. She stands to gain a promotion to the Actuarial Office if she finishes three clean missions. If she is still above zero on day 120, a Remediation force of about ten thousand staff with cameras arrives. As I understood it, that would spend every cache the birds have and probably shut down their workshops and medicines. She also risks being voided if she edits her own number.

3. The Concern set it up after the Hessary disaster. There an assessor spent thirty-one years being careful while two billion people starved, so they decided doubt kills. They issued agents near-certainty (forty), a 120-day window, and audit-only evidence from cameras and microphones. I think the idea was to make agents act, not dither. The story presents it as a mistake, because the machine is never checked.

4. Late on, I learned that the implant glows violet behind her left eye when her eyes are open, and the birds could see it. The vanishing carts and caches were the birds hiding from the thing in her eye, not from her. The pebbles and the eye game were them teaching her hands the table in the dark so she could be a "trusted one". That made the early "starving savages" reading look like her own implant's bias.

5. She opened her eyes at the detector and watched element 120 being made. The machine logged it, and her number swung to −5.0039. The world was cleared and the task force stood down. But the machine also logged and spent Cistern's whole table and workshop, and the birds walked out. Cistern had chosen this, so that a witness would outlive her. Perpetua then wrote a report saying the issued certainty was defective. She was reassigned to dock duty, and the Office added touch sensors to the implants. She kept Cistern's last gift welded in a lead box, unopened. The jigsaw is on her shelf with one piece missing.

6. Perpetua Lusk: narrator, field officer on her third mission, qualified in wheels, sister sent the puzzle.
   Under-the-Cistern (Cistern): grey heron-sized bird, assayer of the guild, keeper of the "table", old, mate dead nine winters.
   Under-the-Steps: young fledgling who plays the eye game and hides the puzzle piece.
   Seven-Holes: the flock's eldest, opposes showing the ring hall, never forgives Perpetua.
   Harriet Baines: clerk in the Actuarial Office who announces the touch sensors.
   The Desk: the dispatcher sending the replies. I'm not sure whether it is a person.
   Cistern's mate: dead; he witnessed the short-lived elements with her up to 117.

7. I'm unsure how the numbers work: why 45 points were subtracted and how that gave −5.0039. I'm unsure why the birds' talk moved the number so little but the decay numbers moved it a bit. I'm unsure what the six-sided gift is. I'm unsure whether the bird's culture or the birds' hiding is meant as a real or a lasting thing, and whether the birds ever forgive her. I'm unsure about the "unlogged channel" and what exactly the Office does next.

8. "Spent": I got it from Cistern's explanation on Day 1. "Zygodactyl": a description of toes, clear from "two forward, two back". "Cache": clear from context as a hidden hoard. "The table": I only got it fully on Day 37 and Day 117, as the collection of every element. "Assayer": guessed, from her work with flux and samples. "Remediation": explained by Perpetua. "Hessary Inquiry": explained on Day 4. "Californium" and "titanium-50": I took these as real elements and guessed. "Element 120": I took it as an undiscovered superheavy element. "Moot": explained as the district assembly. "Mercer's Ring" and "Actuarial Office": picked up from context. "Attached number": the log-odds reading, partly.

9. The change is that the Concern's certainty rule gets reviewed, and the Office adds touch sensors to every implant. For other agents, that means the birds' trick is closed, and the gap that let her learn things unlogged disappears. For the birds, their world is struck from the crisis list and the probes stop. But their one big workshop was spent, and the story seems to say that their way of keeping things is harder from now on. For ordinary people elsewhere, I think the story suggests the Concern may have been wrong on other worlds, as with Gennet, and nobody will ever know.
