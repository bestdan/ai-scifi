# Terms

From the bible's Glossary:
- **the Concern:** the interstellar service that fixes worlds, which Lusk works for.
- **the installation:** the implant behind Lusk's left eye that holds her issued prior (her certainty) and logs what she sees and hears.
- **spent:** a cache an outsider has seen, which its owner then abandons.
- **Remediation:** the Concern's full intervention on a world: ten thousand staff with cameras.
- **the Hessary Inquiry:** the investigation, after a famine on the world Hessary, that made the Concern's rules.

Used by the draft, not in the Glossary:
- **the Desk:** the Concern's sector dispatcher Lusk reports to.
- **the Actuarial Office:** the part of the Concern that sets the priors.
- **Concern standard / trade-speech:** the language the birds speak to Lusk.
- **californium, titanium-50, astatine, francium, einsteinium:** chemical elements named in the table and the accelerator run.
- **zygodactyl:** feet with two toes forward and two back (a real biological term).
