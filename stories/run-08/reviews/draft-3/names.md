# Names

- **Perpetua Lusk:** a Concern field officer on her third mission, the narrator.
- **Under-the-Cistern ("Cistern"):** an old bird, an assayer, assigned by her flock to deal with Lusk.
- **Under-the-Steps:** a fledgling of the flock, about two years old.
- **Seven-Holes:** the flock's eldest.
- **Harriet Baines:** a clerk in the Concern's Actuarial Office.
