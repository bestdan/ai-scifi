## The one thing
Cut the paragraph after the minus-five line: "The same unaudited model that had watched two hundred birds scatter chaff... handing me a fresh certainty just as smooth and impervious to doubt as the old one." It explains the irony that "I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way, on the word of the same machine" had just landed. The first reader "sat up" at that line, which means it needs no help. The round-3 fixes (whisper, evidence, element 120) work as repairs. Even so, this paragraph and the one before it do the idea's emotional work twice, and the second copy is the one a reader feels as lecturing.

## Notes

1. **Day 114, the gold-ingot exchange (structural):** My draft-2 note, still open. "What if we carry up two hundred ingots of pure gold" exists to let Lusk explain the forty-five-point model a second time. The first reader skimmed the moot. 2. **Day 4 / Day 114, Hessary and "ten thousand staff, all of them with cameras" (structural):** The same threat is stated twice, once to a reader who hasn't yet understood the number and once to the birds. The first reader was bored at the Hessary block. Trim Day 4's to the notebooks in the glass room, and let the moot carry the rest.
3. **Day 117, the whisper (line):** It now works: "ordinary talk is testimony and moves nothing, while those decay numbers... a ruin's old records could hold them too." The first reader still wrote "I didn't really follow", on the "ruin's records" clause. Say it concretely: a number can be looked up, while a claim cannot. That is a one-clause change. It is a fix of phrasing, not of rule.

"Frantically it hooked the wire and beads back out..." is the same swell. Day 1's first paragraph is the scale to match. Stage 9 should take these.
5. **The coda (line):** "I noted that the Office had learned how to close the birds' trick much faster than it had learned to doubt itself" states the theme. Cut it. The Baines scene already shows it. Keep the missing piece and the thumb in the dent; the first reader called the close "the circle".
