# Comprehension answers (from memory, no reread)

1. Perpetua Lusk, a field officer for an organisation that fixes failing worlds, is sent to a planet of birds who hide everything of value from anyone who sees it. Her implanted certainty that the world is dying blinds her, until the bird Cistern makes her a trusted keeper of a secret and the world is proved alive and gifted, at the cost of the very thing it was hiding.

2. She stood to gain a promotion to the Actuarial Office after three clean missions. She stood to lose, or to cause, the arrival of a task force of ten thousand people with cameras on day 120 if her number was still above zero. That would spend every hidden cache the birds have, and I understood it would end their workshops and medicines. In the end she lost the promotion by indicting the system in her report.

3. The Concern set the number at forty after the Hessary Inquiry. On Hessary an assessor was careful for thirty-one years while two billion people starved, so they decided doubt kills and made agents near-certain, time-limited and audit-only (cameras and microphones). They also set it just short of the maximum so it could still move. I'm not sure it was a wise reason, and the story seems to say it wasn't.

4. Late on, I learned the implant glows violet behind her left eye when her eyelids are open. The birds had been hiding from the machine, not from her, and had stopped hiding from her on day nine. That changed the "Shut. Open. Shut." game on the salt-barrel, which I'd taken for play. It also changed the objects pressed into her hand in the dark, which I now saw as a way of making her a trusted one. I also learned late that "the table" is the periodic table.

5. On day 118 she kept her eyes open eleven hours and saw an atom of element 120 made and decay. The machine logged it, and her number dropped by forty-five points to minus five. The birds walked out and left the workshop, because it was now seen and so spent. She felt relief, then saw she was just as falsely certain in the other direction. Cistern gave her one last object in the dark. She welded it into a lead steel box by touch. Her report called the issued certainty defective. The world was struck from the crisis list. The Office planned fingertip sensors so touch would be logged too, and she took a dock instructor's post.

6. Perpetua Lusk: the narrator, a field officer. Cistern (Under-the-Cistern): an old grey bird, an assayer, who becomes Perpetua's trusted one. Under-the-Steps: a young fledgling who plays the eye game and hides a puzzle piece. Seven-Holes: the flock's eldest, who opposes showing Perpetua the ring hall and never forgives her. Harriet Baines: an Actuarial Office clerk who tells her about the review and the fingertip sensors. The Desk: the dispatcher, which sends terse replies. Cistern's mate: dead nine winters, who witnessed the elements with her. Hessary's assessor: the careful one who let two billion starve.

7. I'm unsure why her number moved when Cistern whispered the decay energies with her eyes shut, since the machine seemed to log only sight and hearing. I'm unsure what the six-sided gift is. I'm unsure whether the birds' furnaces and medicines survived. I'm unsure how the flock could keep making things if they can't show any. I'm unsure whether the story means the Concern was wrong everywhere, or only here.

8. Zygodactyl: I took it from context to mean the toes two forward, two back. Spent: explained by Cistern as a cache that has been seen. Cache: clear from context. Assayer: she explained it as someone who tests metals, roughly. Californium, astatine, francium and element 120: I knew californium and the periodic table from outside the story. Remediation: explained as the task force. Actuarial: I know the word from outside; the story used it for number-setters. Moot: she explained it as the assembly. Trusted one: explained by Cistern as a second memory. Titanium-50: not explained, and I skipped it.

9. For the birds, the change means the machine and the Office will stop watching them, and the survey probes stop coming. Their work is struck from the crisis list, but the workshop was seen and abandoned, so they lose what they had made. For other worlds, it means a service-wide review of the starting number. The future officers will have touch logged too, which closes the birds' trick. The story suggests that the Office learned to close the loophole faster than it learned to doubt itself. Gennet, and anywhere else the number was set at forty, may have been damaged for nothing.
