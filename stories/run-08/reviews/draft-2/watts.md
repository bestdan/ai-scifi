## The one thing

Make the physics and the scoring rule agree. The story's rigor claim is that the number moves on hard-to-fake measurement. The 115 whisper, the "made 120 three times" line and the "few days" beam rate contradict that.

## Notes

1. **Day 117, the alcove 115 whisper (structural).** A decay chain scored "hard to fake unless you had actually run the element" should move the number by many points, as the 120 atom does. It moves 0.0007. Either scale it up and let Cistern's whisper cost her, or make her whisper something the model scores as weak, such as a half-life without a witnessed run. Also see note 2: a reader checking the arithmetic will find the contradiction.
2. **Day 37, "I have made 118, 119, and 120 three times each since he died."** Day 118 then treats the first 120 atom as the unprecedented event, and the stall is "empty". Both can't hold. Cut it to "I have run the beam for 118 and 119 alone". Also 119 is itself unmade in reality, so the birds are ahead of the field, which is fine if said once.
3. **Day 37, "everything heavier than einsteinium ... last seconds."** Fm-257 lasts about 100 days and Md-258 about 51. The short-lived cut really falls near rutherfordium and up. Say "the heaviest ones", or move the cut. Astatine and francium are right. Californium's "few centuries" is fine (Cf-249, 351 years).
4. **Day 118, the self-audit.** "I recognized the exact texture of what I was feeling" comes three steps late, which is better. But Lusk narrates every confabulation in real time ("my certainty insisted a starving child had dug..."). A confabulation the narrator can name as it happens is not working. Let one of the early readings go unmarked, so the reader catches it before she does. The scrub-jay and nutcracker work (Dally, Emery and Clayton 2006) is a good model: the bird doesn't know why it re-caches.
5. **Day 118, "exactly as sure as I had been."** −5 against 40 is not exact. The next paragraph's "just as smooth and impervious" carries it. Drop "exactly" or say "as sure in kind".
6. **Throughout, "warm, scaly forward toes."** Bird feet run cool by countercurrent exchange. A biologist will notice it six times. Use "dry" or "hard".
7. **Zygodactyl feet (Day 1).** That is a parrot, woodpecker or cuckoo arrangement. Corvids are anisodactyl. If the birds are an invented clade it needs no fix, but "zygodactyl" invites the check. Line-level.
8. **Day 117, the UV lens.** Why would an implant emit UV when the eye is open? One clause of mechanism (it is a rangefinder on the optic tap, say) closes it. The birds noticing it by day 9 is plausible given UV vision.
9. **Coda.** The footage goes to an archive. Say once that it is the Concern's log, which the Office reads, so the table is spent to them and she knows it. That would be the cost of Cistern's choice, and the story hasn't paid it yet.
10. **Prose (line-level).** Day 117's "the smell of ... ozone, hot beeswax insulation, and deep limestone water" and Day 9's wet feathers, hot tallow and mint are sense-lists. Keep one smell per scene.

## What to protect

## Blind spot disclosure

I over-weight accuracy and under-weight ritual. The cold reader loved the puzzle and the pebble, which are not rigor, and notes 6, 7 and 10 are fussy next to that. Notes 1, 2 and 4 are the ones that matter.
