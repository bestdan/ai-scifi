## The one thing

Let the birds argue the moot. Day 114 is the story's one gathering, and the stakes are narrated into it ("Nobody contradicted her on what the furnaces were for") instead of fought over. A society that settles a question this large should split on it, and the vote should not be near-unanimous.

## Notes

1. **Day 114 vote (structural):** "Nearly three hundred pairs of rear claws struck the basalt twice" against one dissent. Item 11 reordered the scene but did not make it close. Give Seven-Holes a few allies, or give the flock a real reason to fear Lusk's eye, so that "Seven-Holes never forgave me" costs something.
2. **Who is the second memory (structural):** Cistern's creed is that a trusted one's knowing is hers. Then at the moot she says "Let the machine's archive outlive me", and the coda calls the Concern's archive "the one witness that will outlive Cistern as she wanted". The archive is an outsider by her own definition. Lusk is the second memory. The coda should say so, and let the contradiction stand as Cistern's price.
3. **The birds' fate (structural):** The first reader ends asking whether the furnaces went cold. The coda says only that the world was struck off the list. Cistern argued only the ring hall and her bench would be spent, but Lusk saw the whole hall. Either answer is fine; the page should support both.
4. **Day 4 and Day 9 openings (structural):** Both were skimmed. Day 4's number speech and the Hessary paragraph stack up before anything happens to anyone. Day 9's jar-thread and anchovy catalogue delays the fledgling.
5. **Prose (line-level):** Stacked participles and reflex triplets ("wet feathers, hot tallow, and crushed mint"; "ozone, hot beeswax insulation, and deep limestone water"; "hot slate, clean oil, and dried clover"). Adverb openers ("Frantically", "Instantly", "Suddenly") crowd the action. Day 118's hall paragraph is still one 130-word inventory. Cut every third modifier.
6. **Cistern's Day 37 speech (line-level):** Eleven clauses of californium and half-lives delivered while pressing an ampoule into a blindfolded hand. Keep "I am forgetting my corners" and the three wrong flagstones, and move the rest to a second, shorter beat.
7. **Everyone articulate (line-level):** Cistern's and Seven-Holes's long set-piece speeches are fluent to the point of oratory, though Concern standard is a language learned from probe loops. Let one of them fumble a word.

## What to protect

- "If I trust you, what you know is mine. That's what trust is for. Somebody has to remember where I put things."
- "Keep your hands in your lap or she will spend your boots."
- The jigsaw, the stolen piece, and the thumb in the empty dent.
- "...cold and sharp and entirely mine."
- "I was exactly as sure as I had been, the other way, on the word of the same machine."
- "It's yours... Don't look." Trust as a gift pressed in from behind is the carrier-bag act, and the climax is a vigil, not a fight.

## Blind spot disclosure

I favour social texture and distrust exposition, so I may be cutting setup that the Understood axes need; the comprehension gate outranks me there. I also read slowly, and may overvalue the moot as drama and the coda's moral irony.
