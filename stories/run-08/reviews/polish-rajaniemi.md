# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi lens (density and texture)

Blind spots: I favour opacity and can mistake density for depth, and I did not check arithmetic or rulings (Asimov's job). I am also reading a story whose opening has already been made plainer on purpose, so I held back from asking for more strangeness there.

**Overall.** The polish kept the texture. The explanations are mostly present, not clumsy: the table, the Desk, californium and Baines each carry their role in the sentence. The invented terms (the Concern, the installation, spent, Remediation, the Hessary rules) are used consistently with the glossary. The Desk and "the table" are in the ledger or the amendments but not in the glossary; that is a bible gap, not a story fault.

**Suggestions**

1. L125, duplicated gloss. The moot is glossed here and again at the moot itself (L135).
   `The flock is calling a moot, a full meeting of the district underground."` → `The flock is calling a moot."`

2. L5, an overloaded sentence on the first page.
   `forty being as sure as they make anyone, and while I carried it I took` → `forty is as sure as they make anyone. While I carried it, I took`

3. L191, "snatched" is the wrong verb for a thing hidden from a lens.
   `had been snatched from the glowing violet lens in my left socket` → `had been hidden from the glowing violet lens in my left socket`

4. L207, a stacked, stock relief paragraph. This is the one place the polish left a cliché.
   `A sudden, flooding tide of physical relief washed through my neck and chest, so clean it almost buckled my knees:` → `Relief went through my neck and chest so clean it almost buckled my knees:`

5. L163, a human weapon imported into a bird hall.
   `a single pistol-crack as Seven-Holes drove her beak` → `a single flint-crack as Seven-Holes drove her beak`

The payoff-ledger lines (L181, L199, L203, L223) are explicit and clear. I did not ask for any change to them.
