# Stage 3 critique: Banks lens, run-08

**Blind spots, stated.** This lens sprawls, loves set pieces and is bad at
the short form. It tends to reward scale and to forgive load. I have tried
to discount for that: where I praise a premise's reach, I also say whether
it fits 7,500 words. Premise C is a collision built partly on my own
Once-Fired card, so treat my verdict on it as the most suspect. I have not
checked prior art; that is the Cartographer's job.

## A. The Prior

**Plain pitch:** holds. "Certain people get things done" is a joke an
outsider gets at once, and the birds-hide-what-you-look-at half is
graspable without coined words. The two halves still need a join on the
page: why this service would rather staff on false certainty than on
doubt.

**Moral friction:** the best here. The service has knowingly installed a
falsehood in its own people, and the actuaries signed it off as
efficiency. That is dirty work with a paper trail. The bird's choice
(spend a lifetime's cache) is a real price, and the agent's alternative
edit also costs her her standing. Nobody is clean.

**Humour:** lives in the mechanism. An agent who must not look at things,
a bird she has exasperated for weeks, her own log-odds read aloud like a
weather report. That is wry without a narrator stating that she is wry.

**Scale and intimacy:** the strongest pairing on the table. Staffing
policy across the whole service is the vastness, one bird and one cache
the closeness. The guild debate is a genuine scene of weighing.

**Enjoyable to inhabit:** yes. Corvids, hoards, a periodic table as
treasure. Risk is the Bayes arithmetic turning into a lecture.

## B. The Ending Technician

**Plain pitch:** holds best of all five. A child could repeat it.

**Moral friction:** moderate. The service's belief is wrong in a gentle,
well-meant way, and the town's cold minute is wiser than it. Nobody does
real harm; the dirty work is the technician's own kindness. That is
cleaner than I like.

**Humour:** thin on the page. A man whose job is pleasant goodbyes among
people who refuse them is a good comic setup, but the endgame is a
retirement walk to a hatch, and that road runs straight to sentiment. The
brief wants funny; this one risks landing as touching.

**Scale and intimacy:** intimacy is excellent, scale is mostly stated.
The reach line (every client world's medicine, labour and mourning) is
asserted in one clause. It needs one concrete image of a service-softened
deathbed to feel real.

**Enjoyable to inhabit:** very, and it is the safest. It is also the
smallest, and the gate says clarity is no reason to pick the smaller
premise.

## C. The Signatory

**Plain pitch:** holds, barely. "Minds that can always be undone need one
member who can't" is clear. The kiln and the slowing are two more
inventions the pitch quietly leaves out.

**Moral friction:** high and well-aimed. A benevolent hegemon needing the
one thing its power rules out is my home ground, and a charter that
demands a signature nobody can give is good bureaucratic cruelty. Who
signed off on sending the agent to be fired? That question has an answer
here.

**Humour:** a sardonic kiln-master against an affronted agent is a real
pair. But the agent being slowed and fixed sits on the cluster the audit
flagged as a model attractor.

**Scale and intimacy:** scale is felt through the million-fold speed;
intimacy is thin until the firing.

**Enjoyable to inhabit:** the premise is three ideas. This is exactly the
sprawl I warned about in myself. I would not choose it at 7,500 words.

## D. The Fee

**Plain pitch:** holds. Fostering paid in help is clear, and the trap
(too well fixed to pay) is an elegant irony an outsider gets.

**Moral friction:** good. A mother forging a need to let her son go is
love as fraud, and the agent must accept or refuse what she can see
through. The service's two-million-year child trade is cold and
benevolent at once.

**Humour:** a dumpling stall, a daughter sent home to find something
broken. The comedy is domestic and could be warm. Risk: the audit's
"obvious card" match, and tenderness crowding out the wry.

**Scale and intimacy:** scale rests on the fee economy; the market
council weighing whether to let a thing break is the vast moment, and it
is a good one. Reach clearly leaves the household.

**Enjoyable to inhabit:** yes, with the family triangle as the engine.

## E. North for Lunch

**Plain pitch:** holds, and it is the funniest of the five. A brilliant
salesman losing brilliance every mile on a train is a gag that explains
itself.

**Moral friction:** soft. The gift is a mind, and the question of whether
it is good or merely persuasive is philosophical, not costly. Nobody pays
anything.

**Humour:** best on the table, and it is mechanical comedy, not narrator
adjectives. The audit also flags it as squarely on the "agent made
smaller" attractor, and that is a fair warning.

**Scale and intimacy:** the zone as a geographic institution is scale
felt through a railway line. Intimacy is a lunch.

**Enjoyable to inhabit:** very, but the stake is the weakest here, and
the gate wants consequence followed further.

## Ranking

1. **A, The Prior.** Moral friction, humour and the scale-to-intimacy
   pairing all land, and the birds are a mind the reader would want to
   watch think.
2. **D, The Fee.** Strong friction and a real Reach, held back by the
   obvious-card shadow and the risk that tenderness drowns the wry.
3. **C, The Signatory.** The best moral ground and the worst fit for
   length.
4. **B, The Ending Technician.** Clear and lovely, but small and too
   likely to end soft.
5. **E, North for Lunch.** The funniest and the lightest in stakes.

**Why A should win.** The seed is about a benevolent service meddling
where nobody asked, and A is the only premise where the service's
benevolence is the wound. It issues its agents a deliberate falsehood
because doubt is inefficient, and then the story makes one agent pay for
seeing through it. The bird's cache is the best single image on the
table: proof that costs the giver everything, so rigour and intimacy are
the same act. The comedy comes from the mechanism, an agent who cannot
look and a bird who is hiding on purpose, so the tone charter's
friction-without-gloom is met without anyone telling the reader she is
dry. My caution, as the lens that over-builds: A carries two nova and a
turn, and the Bayes arithmetic must stay a few lines and a gag, not a
set piece. At about 6,000 words it sits inside the 7,500 ceiling. Keep
the guild debate as the one scale scene, cut the rest of the service's
world to what the bird can see of it, and A holds scale and intimacy
together without burying the short story.
