# Stage 3 critique: Cartographer

Lens: cartographer. Ranks on novelty alone.

**Blind spots.** I judge novelty and nothing else here. I can't say whether a
premise is funny, whether its other mind reads as thinking, or whether it
fits the word count. I did no new web search this round. Every attribution
below is from memory, and the audit's checked ones (Sheckley, Dick, Brin,
Sterling) are not repeated. Where I'm unsure I say "nearest I know of".

## A. The Prior

Revised Novelty: N5 for the combination. Status: uncharted.

Nearest I know of, high confidence: Egan, "Axiomatic" (1990), for an
installed belief. Tchaikovsky, *Children of Memory* (2022) for corvids, and
Watts, *Blindsight* (2006) plus *Doctor Who*, "Blink" (2007), for things that
move only when unwatched. Each is only a part. A hoard that counts only if
witnessed live, with the proof priced in the bird's lifetime, has no neighbour
I know of. The new twist (the cache shows her the service) is the card's
earlier push, and it adds distance.

Attractors: this is **cluster 1**, the service's instrument manufacturing the
need, and the synthesis's escape is only half true. The prior *is* the
instrument. It stays off the map because the story lives in the cost of the
cache, as the audit advised. Mild paperwork (cluster 6) from the log-odds
readout. Not cluster 5.

## B. The Ending Technician

Revised Novelty: N4 (down from 5). Status: edge-of-map.

The peak-end service is still uncharted. I found no fiction on it, confidence
medium. Chiang, "Liking What You See: A Documentary" (2002) is the nearest for
a bias made policy. The new frame is what costs it. A retiring worker's last
shift, made gentle at the end, is the stock "last day" scene, and the warm
ending is the obvious landing. The risks line already says so. The town's
counter-custom (the remembering self as a second person) is the fresh part.

Attractors: cluster 1 in softened form (a service model fails on a healthy
town). The cut report removes cluster 6, which is the one thing that helps.

Push: the ending he builds works, and next shift's carelessness proves the
town right.

## C. The Signatory

Revised Novelty: N4. Status: edge-of-map.

Nearest, high: Brin, *Kiln People* (2002); Egan, "Learning to Be Me" (1990).
For the slowing, Forward, *Dragon's Egg* (1980). A hegemon short of one
irreversible act is the uncharted part. The folded push (permanently committed
to revisability) is a good paradox and adds real distance. It also now needs
three devices on a 7,500-word ceiling, so the slowing probably costs it.

Attractors: **cluster 2** (agent made smaller, here slowed and fixed) and the
law/consent and reversible-minds entries, named in the audit. This premise
hits more attractors than any other.

## D. The Fee

Revised Novelty: N3.5, scored N4 on the fee and N3 on the forgery. Status:
edge-of-map.

Nearest, high: Strugatskys, *Beetle in the Anthill* (1979-80), on planted
children. Clarke, *Childhood's End* (1953) also has a service taking a world's
children. Fosterage as payment is new to me. The mother's forgery of a need
is the "fake a crisis" card five lenses wrote blind, moved off the agent.
That is real distance in who fakes, and none in what is faked.

Attractors: **cluster 3** exactly, plus the law attractor. This is the most
reader-visible match in the set: someone who has read the seed will have
thought "she'll fake the problem" within a minute.

## E. North for Lunch

Revised Novelty: N3 (down from 4). Status: edge-of-map.

Vinge's Zones of Thought in *A Fire Upon the Deep* (1992) is the scaffold,
and the card names it. A narrator whose reports degrade as he goes is
Keyes, *Flowers for Algernon* (1959), high confidence. The geographic cap
used as a custom is new, and the audit's check against Chiang and
Vonnegut still holds. The lunch scene is new. The mechanism of the loss is
not.

Attractors: **cluster 2** squarely (the synthesis says so). The gift-is-a-mind
turn is a small variant of the Zones.

Push: the dye-works owner's own case is the one that gets better as it
goes north, which would invert the Keyes arc.

## Ranking (novelty only)

1. **A.** The only premise whose two nova have no joint neighbour.
2. **B.** The novum is uncharted, but the ending trades distance for the stock last-day scene.
3. **C.** Real core, but three devices and the most attractors.
4. **D.** The fee is new, the forgery is the obvious card.
5. **E.** Zones plus Algernon, on the diminished-agent attractor.

**Why A should win.** Cached is the only card with Novelty 5 as a pair, and the
collision is built so the answer to the seed's "why does the service think it
is needed" is also what the story pays for. Both halves are charted
separately: installed belief by Egan, hiding corvids by Tchaikovsky,
unwatched motion by Watts. The join is uncharted, because the proof must be
given at a cost. A is cluster 1, so the draft must not spend its pages on the
prior's arithmetic. If the page lives in the cache and the guild's quarrel, it
stays off the map. If it lives in the log-odds, it becomes Egan again.
