1. **The whisper and the glint** (consensus: Chiang's one thing, Asimov,
   Watts; lost-list 9). Separate the two rules. The installation's ears
   never close; shutting her eyes shuts only its sight, and only the sight
   channel glints. Talk is testimony and moves nothing. Cistern's whisper
   was numbers, which the model trusts a hair more than talk, because a
   ruin's records could hold them too: so it moved 0.0007, not 45. Fix
   Lusk's "Don't speak" paragraph to say so, and Cistern's "It shines when
   it looks" paragraph so her mistake is plain: they thought shutting her
   eyes shut all of it, and that talk was safe because talk never moved
   it. Add one clause on its cost to Cistern (Asimov): she has spent a
   little of 115 by saying it. Rows served: Twist (the line "We stopped
   hiding from you on day nine. We hide from the thing that looks out of
   you" is elsewhere and stays word for word) and the Glint traced row.
2. **What evidence counts** (Asimov's one thing). Day 4 has Lusk say "a
   cache of work ... shown openly to my eyes" would move her; Day 114 says
   only a live atom can; Day 118 she watches a working hall for eleven
   hours and the number doesn't move. Reword Day 4's line to agree with
   Day 114 (something only a living, working people could make, made in
   front of her eyes, not dug up). On Day 118, add one sentence: the hall
   itself moved nothing, because a ruin can have a hall and survivors to
   work it; the installation wanted the atom. Row served: Premise (keep
   Cistern's reply, "Anything shown to an outsider's eyes is spent").
3. **Element 120's history** (Watts). Day 37 says Cistern made 118, 119

   37: she has made 118 and 119 alone, which don't count, and nobody has
   ever made 120. Also fix "everything heavier than einsteinium last
   seconds or less" to "most of the elements past einsteinium".
4. **Seven-Holes was right, and the archive is not trusted** (Asimov, Le
