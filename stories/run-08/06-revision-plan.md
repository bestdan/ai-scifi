## Consensus (fix these)

1. **The hearing rule** (Asimov's one thing; Chiang). The installation
   hears everything, yet Cistern's talk on Days 4, 37 and 114 moves the
   number by nothing while one whisper on Day 117 moves it 0.0007. Make
   the rule statable. On Day 4 Lusk says it plainly: talk is testimony,
   and testimony moves her almost nothing, because a dying world can say
   anything about itself. What moved it at alcove 115 was not a claim but
   a measurement: an exact decay chain with its numbers, specific enough
   that the installation's physics model scored it as hard to fake. Rewrite
   Cistern's Day 117 line to match (they thought talk was safe because
   talk had never moved it; they didn't know numbers were different). Keep
   every cache's *location* out of what Cistern says aloud anywhere (no
   "third gallery", no "fourth gallery"), so talk spends nothing. Rows:
   Twist (its explicit line, "We stopped hiding from you on day nine. We
   hide from the thing that looks out of you", stays word for word).
2. **The light-lag** (Asimov, Chiang, Le Guin, Watts). Day 9 says the Desk
   is six light-hours away and the reply comes in four. Make the Desk
   three light-hours away, reply six hours later, before dawn.
3. **The lectures and inventories** (Le Guin's one thing; the
   Cartographer's; Chiang; the First Reader was bored on Days 117 and 118
   and nearly lost on Day 4).
   - Day 4: cut the explanation to what a reader must hold. Keep the
     number's meaning, four thousand years, why not exactly one, the
     window and Remediation, and Hessary with its notebooks. Cut the
     decimal rate from dialogue (the headings carry it). Give the table
     two sentences, not a paragraph; its detail moves to Day 37.
   - Day 114: don't repeat Day 4's explanation. Lusk gives only the
     stakes and the proof's shape (item 12).
   - Day 117: the table by touch keeps four or five cells, not a tour.
   - Day 118: one paragraph of the hall at full scale (Banks's stage 5
     note), half its current length, then straight to the detector.
4. **Days 117 and 118 repeat each other** (Asimov, Chiang). The bellows
   and ring are described in both. Day 117 is sound, smell and hands only;
   Day 118 is the only sight of it.
5. **The box must follow the story's rules** (Chiang; the Cartographer;
   lost-list 11). As written, the prior is gone and she is a trusted one,
   so opening the box would spend nothing. Fix: only the prior comes out
   on Mercer's Ring. The recording installation stays, because every
   officer carries one, so anything she looks at still goes into the
   Concern's log, and a look would spend the gift. After the touch refit,
   even handling it would log it. So she welded it into a box by touch
   *before* the refit and hasn't touched it since. The box and the refit
   are one thought.
6. **The coda's tone** (Le Guin; the Cartographer: "the bureaucracy
   absorbing the protest" is stock). Keep the Reach content (item 13),
   but Baines is dry, not bright, and the scene is short. No numbers for
   the reform. The irony is stated once, by Lusk, not underlined.
7. **The narration should enact the certainty** (Chiang's one thing;
   Watts). Through Day 37, every marvel Lusk finds, her reasoning bends
   toward a crisis reading, and the reader sees the bend. Day 9's report to
   the Desk can't say "this world was doing completely fine": she files
   the facts (the vacuum threads) in plain words and adds, in good faith,
   that they are "consistent with a collapsing society hoarding salvage".
   The Desk still refuses it as narrative. At −5, the relief arrives, and
   she recognises its texture only a beat later, not instantly.

## Adopted single-lens notes

8. **Physics** (Watts).
   - Element 120 is made from titanium-50 on a californium-249 target, not
     calcium-50 (which gives 118).
   - A single atom of 120 comes at a rate of maybe one every few days.
     So the beam runs for days: Cistern started the run on day 110. On day
     118 Lusk sits at the detector with her eyes open for hours, waiting,
     not knowing whether it will come before the window closes. That gives
     her the climax's act: she chooses to keep looking. When it comes, a
     bell on the detector rings the decay chain.
   - Astatine and francium can't be held as samples. Change the split: the
     long-lived elements are held as samples; the short-lived ones,
     astatine, francium and everything heavier than einsteinium, only as
     atoms made live and witnessed. Don't give a count like "one through
     ninety-nine".
   - Element 97 is berkelium, not curium or californium.
   - The bellows-treading birds work the furnaces and vacuum pumps. The
     ring runs on water turbines in the deep aquifer.
9. **The left-eye loophole** (Watts). The draft lets her see with an
   unlogged right eye. Fix: the installation taps the optic nerve and logs
   both eyes; only its lens behind the left pupil glints. So shutting both
   eyes is the only way not to be logged. Cut "I saw it with my right eye
   as the trusted one".
10. **How the birds speak Concern standard** (Le Guin). One line: they
    learned it from survey probes broadcasting it for sixty years.
11. **The vote** (Le Guin). On Day 114 the count of what they'd lose comes
    first, then Seven-Holes, then the vote. As written the vote is
    effectively settled before the count.
12. **The arithmetic** (Asimov). 39.9961 − 45 = −5.0039, not −5.0000. The
    heading of the coda stays "—". Change the number in the Ending line to
    −5.0039; the rest of the line stays word for word. Rows: Ending (still
    reads), the proof.
13. **The birds' side of Reach and their fate** (Chiang, Watts; grader:
    Reach partial). At the moot, make it concrete that the furnaces, glass
    and medicine exist only because of the caches: with every cache spent,
    the flock would have no reason to keep any of it, and that is the end
    of their medicine as well as their art. In the coda, one or two lines
    on what became of them: the footage of the ring hall went into the
    Concern's archive (the only witness that outlives Cistern, as she
    wanted); the world was struck from the flagged list, and the probes
    stopped coming.
14. **The protagonist in the climax** (Watts: passive). Handled by item 8's
    waiting at the detector.

## Comprehension items (structural)

15. **Premise, partial.** The reader never said that only a cache shown to
    her can change her mind. On Day 4, after "unless you show me something
    that can't happen on a dying world", have Cistern ask what that would
    be, and Lusk answer: something only a working people could make,
    shown to her eyes, and Cistern answer that anything shown to her eyes
    is spent. One exchange, plain.
16. **Ending, partial.** The reader missed that Cistern chose to let the
    installation watch, and that the table was completed and spent in the
    same moment, and why. On Day 118, before the run's atom arrives,
    Cistern says it in plain words, facing away: Lusk's seeing completes
    the table, because she is a trusted one; the thing behind her eye
    seeing it spends the table, because it is an outsider; it is the same
    look, and she has chosen it. Keep "Her table was complete, and her
    table was spent" after the event.
17. **Reach, partial.** Item 13.
18. **Terms, missed.**
    - "prior", "posterior": don't use them. Say "my certainty" or "my
      number". The Desk's messages say "your attached number".
    - "Hessary Schedule C": cut.
    - "californium", "curium", element names generally: keep at most
      three, each with its plain role ("a heavy metal you can't buy
      anywhere") at first use, or cut them.
    - "witness-cradle", "coincidence plate": use "the detector".
    - "windlass", "plumb-bob", "shim", "fathom", "marl", "chalk", "moot":

19. **Lost-list 1 (the number on page 1).** In the first section, one plain

20. **Lost-list 2 (spent).** Day 1 is right in substance; make the bird's

22. **Lost-list 7 (what the gifts were for).** On Day 117 Cistern says
