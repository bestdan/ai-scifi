# Stage 5 critique: Asimov lens (the Logician)

Blind spots: I read for rules, clue order and who knows what. I under-weight
voice, the birds' bodies, and whether the jokes land. Defer to Banks and
Chiang on those.

1. **[must-fix] The "it hears" clue contradicts scene 2.** Fact 4 says
   everything she sees and hears is logged. Scene 2 has Lusk explain her number
   to Cistern. If she says "sees and hears", Cistern knows on day 4, and the
   whisper in scene 7 teaches nothing. If she leaves sound out, a certain,
   competent officer withholds the one fact that would help her hosts, and she
   is made stupid. It also breaks the glint row ("so we thought it was deaf").
   Fix: Lusk says plainly in scene 2 that it records both. The birds' mistake
   is then the wrong model of *what it does with them* (they think it judges
   gaze only). The whisper shows that one spoken element name moves the number
   0.0007, about 25 days of ordinary evidence, so the installation reads
   content. The reader learns it from the number.

2. **[must-fix] Part II and the middle repeat each other.** Scenes 3, 4 and 5
   each ask the same question: her hands know something her number won't
   count. Scene 5 then restates the stakes and proof that scene 2's Premise
   line ("something that can't happen on a dying world") already gave. Days
   37 to 110 are also skipped with no turn. Fix: cut scene 4 to the
   jigsaw-theft and the Gennet doubt (700 words), and move the proof's
   threshold into it (gold moves her 3, she needs more than 40). Make scene 5
   turn on a new fact, namely that Cistern volunteers 118 to 120 and what
   forgetting costs her.

3. **[must-fix] World facts 2 and trusted sight miss the first third.** The
   scenes run 850, 900 and 950, so the first third (about 2,270 words) ends
   inside scene 3, and the pebble comes late in that scene ("that night"). Fix:
   move the pebble and Cistern's "what trust is for" line to the end of scene
   2 and let scene 3 carry only Hessary, the Desk and the eyes-shut games.

4. **[must-fix] Scene 8 is overloaded.** In 800 words it holds the live
   synthesis, the flip to -5, the self-noticing, the report, the gift and a
   coda of five beats (Desk, refit, box, jigsaw, Gennet). The scene table
   also sums to 7,000, not the stated 6,800. Fix: make the coda its own
   short section (about 350 words, no number) and take the words from scenes
   3 and 4 (around 100 and 250). Total stays under 7,300.

5. **[should] Fair play on the proof.** Say before scene 8 why 120 and not
   118 or 119: those are made, unwitnessed, and equally "live". Say which
   one the installation will count. Plant the grading ("its own model gives
   a working civilization's live synthesis one in 10^45") in scene 5 as
   Lusk's reading of the installation's rules, not as a reveal in 8. An
   attentive reader can then compute 40 - 45 before she does. Also have the
   narrator mention, in scene 3, that the fledgling keeps staring at her left
   eye. That is the planted glint clue.

**Answers:** can the reader state the rules by midpoint? Yes, once 1 and 3
are fixed. Is anyone required to be stupid? Lusk, in note 1, and both
parties waiting 100 days in note 2. The information order is right except
for note 3.
