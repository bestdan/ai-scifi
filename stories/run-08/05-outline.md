# Outline: run-08 (working title: Forty)

- **Target length:** about 6,800 words. Hard ceiling 7,500.
- **Shape:** linear, in seven dated sections and a short coda. Each section is headed with
  the day of the 120-day window and Lusk's number, e.g. "Day 1. 40.0000".
  The form enacts the idea: the number barely moves for seven sections
  while everything she learns says it should, and then falls 45 orders of
  magnitude in one line. The reader watches certainty refuse to update,
  in the margin, the whole time.
- **Opening question:** why is everything she touches disappearing, and
  why is she so sure this world is dying?
- **Final image:** years later, a small box on Lusk's shelf, holding the
  thing Cistern pressed into her hand from behind. She has never looked.
  Next to it, the harbour jigsaw, finished, with one piece missing.
- **What the ending costs the protagonist:** the promotion she wanted (her
  report indicts the office she wanted to join), and the comfort of never
  knowing what she holds. Cistern pays more: her table is completed and
  spent in the same moment. Dozens of caches Lusk spent by looking are
  never forgiven.

## What each part asks

| Part | Sections | The new question it raises |
|---|---|---|
| I. Arrival | 1–2 | What is this world, and why can't she see it? Why is she certain, and why did her people build her that way? |
| II. Learning by hand | 3–4 | Can you know something your certainty won't count? And what do the birds think she is, a thief or someone asking to be trusted? |
| III. The price | 5 | Who pays for the proof, and do they agree to? |
| IV. The proof | 6–7 | What does it mean to be trusted by people you can't look at, and what does the machine in her make of the thing that saves them? |
| Coda | 8 | What does her institution learn, and what does she keep? |

## Arithmetic and calendar

Her number falls about 0.0000274 a day: 40 − 0.0000274 × (day − 1).

Rate: 0.01 a year, so 40 / 0.01 = 4,000 years. The proof is worth 45:
40 − 45 = −5. The whisper moves it 0.0007.
