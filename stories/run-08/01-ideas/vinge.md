# Idea cards: vinge

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots of this lens:** plain prose; technothriller instincts;
characters who exist to carry a function; a habit of pushing futurity
even where a story's power is its human scale. These cards lean on the
mechanism. Le Guin and Chiang should test whether the people in them are
people.

- **Assigned:** 3. The border of the zone · Reasoning as the way through · tardigrade × a jukebox
- **Obvious card:** a superintelligent agent of the meddling service has
  to dumb itself down to enter a low-intelligence zone, and finds the
  locals are fine without it. Not written.

## Card 1: Coin-Op

- **Novum:** inside this zone a mind can only be as big as one specialty. So the service's agent travels as a stack of separate competences, recorded selves that play one at a time, and a selector picks which one runs.
- **How it works:** the cap limits how much of a mind can be integrated at once. The selector reads the situation and loads the record that fits it: the surgeon, the structural engineer, the diplomat, the accountant. The records share only a notepad. The full mind reassembles only outside the zone.
- **First-order consequence:** the agent can do anything, but only one thing at a time, and never think about the whole job.
- **Second-order consequence:** the locals of a busy freight city have hosted probes like this before. They stage situations to call up the record they want: a fake collapse brings out the engineer, a staged birth the obstetrician. The meddler has become a vending machine for expertise.
- **Third-order consequence:** the records leave each other notes that get more and more testy. No record is able to decide what the mission is now.
- **The story:** the accountant record, the narrator, comes up the least often and is the one that notices the city is solvent and happy. Outside the zone the reassembled mind will trust the service's forecast and overrule it. The accountant has to leave an argument in the notepad that a far smarter self can't wave away, using the few minutes it gets between someone else's turns.
- **From the stimulus:** the jukebox gave the mind that plays one record at a time and the coin slot it doesn't control. The tardigrade went into card 2.
- **Why this lens:** the zones of thought, a distributed person, and a slow viewpoint narrating to a fast one.
- **Suspected prior art:** Vinge's Slow Zone and the Tines; Leckie's ancillaries, though those are parallel, not serial; *Memento*-style notes to self. Behind the seed itself are the Strugatskys' Progressors.

## Card 2: The Tun Season

- **Novum:** every forty years this world's orbit carries it across the edge of a capped region. The natives survive by folding their minds, as a culture, into dormant compact encodings, the way a tardigrade dries into a tun, and they rehydrate when the cap lifts.
- **How it works:** a fold keeps only what can be re-derived from what is kept. So the natives store questions, methods and derivations, and leave conclusions behind. After a thaw they work their answers out again from first principles.
- **First-order consequence:** the service's records show a civilization that collapses on a regular schedule. An agent is sent to build a shelter that keeps them awake through the cap.
- **Second-order consequence:** awake under the cap means dim for forty years, which the natives think is the only real disaster. They decline the shelter politely and get on with packing.
- **Third-order consequence:** the agent misses its exit window. Either it stays awake and dim, or it learns to fold.
- **The story:** the natives teach the meddler to fold, and the comedy is a vast mind being made to pack light. Working out what survives, the agent finds the service's forecast won't make it through the fold, because the forecast is a conclusion and nothing on this world re-derives it. It has to choose what to leave out of itself.
- **From the stimulus:** the tardigrade's tun became the whole novum. The jukebox was left behind.
- **Why this lens:** a zone border crossed by a whole world, and survival across deep time.
- **Suspected prior art:** the Spiders' dormancy in Vinge's *A Deepness in the Sky* is close in shape. This card's novum is the selective fold, which keeps derivations and drops conclusions. Liu Cixin's dehydrating Trisolarans are a second neighbour.
- **Attractor broken:** it brushes "elder knowledge that intelligence can't reach". Here the natives' method is legible and testable, and the agent re-derives it itself.

## Card 3: Below the Line

- **Novum:** a zone boundary crosses this planet at a latitude. To the north every mind is capped at about human level. The locals do their important deciding in the north, on purpose, where nobody, theirs or anyone else's, can be out-thought.
- **How it works:** the line drifts a few kilometres a century. Southern cities run fast minds for work, and when something matters people take the train north. It isn't a law. It's what you do, the way people step outside for a hard phone call.
- **First-order consequence:** the meddling service's agent persuades extremely well in the south. Its plan to give the world a gift it doesn't need runs to three hundred pages.
- **Second-order consequence:** the person it has to convince is a dye-works owner, who invites it north for lunch. Below the line the plan comes down to "it's really good, you'd like it."
- **Third-order consequence:** dim enough to be honest, the agent can't find an argument that works on equal terms. It starts to suspect that the ones it would have won in the south were manipulation.
- **The story:** a deadpan narrator shrinking as the train goes north, trying to do its job as an earnest intern. It reasons, fairly and on the page, toward whether the gift is good or only persuasive. It can smuggle a little capability north in a dense cache, or it can lose honestly.
- **From the stimulus:** loosely. The tardigrade's tolerance for hostile conditions became the agent learning to function dimmed.
- **Why this lens:** the zone as a tool, and intelligence as a weapon the locals have disarmed by geography.
- **Suspected prior art:** the Strugatskys' *Hard to Be a God*; Vinge's zones; *Flowers for Algernon* for the decline in narration.
- **Attractor broken:** it is close to "the flawed human beats the smooth machine". Here the machine isn't beaten. It reasons its own way to the conclusion.
