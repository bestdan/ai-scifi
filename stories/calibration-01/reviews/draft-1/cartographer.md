## The one thing
Don't let the heron be erased. Your own push for B was a clause-compliant gift that persuades anyway. Scene 6 builds exactly that, then Oyelaran erases it and the story returns to compliance. The draft ends on the charted answer (the self leaves faint traces; love and ritual carry across) instead of the uncharted one (the note that isn't argument). Let one thing survive that is not argument and does the work of argument.

## Notes

1. **Drift (mixed):** The Swordholder never shows up, and that is a point for the draft. The sealed self in § 14 and Hanne's "other reading" keep the Determination from becoming a Cixin vote. But the Dworkin/Dresser question is staged with the answer built in: the past self may not bind the new one, so she erases. The draft dramatises the ban and never tests it.
2. **Scene 5, Pell:** "your marginalia are the Keeper's own apparatus, like your spectacles". This is the official-certifying-a-reading attractor, in its purest form. Pell supplies the legal interpretation and the scene turns on his ruling. Give Oyelaran the move: she finds the loophole, or Pell is wrong, or he has no answer. Cut his hedge about the stove.
3. **Stock beats:**
   - The child who says the forbidden thing ("What do you think about *that* one?", Mina's red ears).
   - The old woman who states the theme: "So we don't set. Otherwise we'd all be our grandparents forever." This breaks rule 4, because the theme is delivered out loud.
   - "Why does everybody have to?" as an innocent-question setup.
   - The kitchen-table last-night farewell with food, the loyal spouse, "twice-met": the bereavement-and-ritual skeleton from my stage 3 note, cluster 1.
   - The joke that returns and breaks the stoic woman. "She did not laugh. She never had." then she laughs. The payoff is competent and familiar.
4. **Ending:** "In the raking light her hand stood up out of it anyway." That is the "traces persist" move, and it also approaches the banned "affirms resilience by default". The drafter's note says the closing image was chosen for "the deepest grooves". It is a lovely image on well-charted ground.
5. **Banned-list pass (mechanical):**
   - Em-dashes: zero (grep). Credit.
   - "Not X. Y.": one, "That's not argument. That's a heron." Within quota. Near-miss: "She did not laugh. She never had." and "Bisi did not laugh." (a repeat of the shape across scenes).
   - Phrases ("testament", "weight of", "something shifted", "flicker", "hum of"): none.
   - Senses in a row: none.
   - Names: clean. "Mina", "Hanne" and "Ros" sit in the Nordic register I flagged (cluster 8); a mild hit.
   - Triplets: "Hanne groaned. Ros put her face in her floury hands. Oyelaran waited" (three reaction beats). Also "washed it and put it on the shelf". Near-miss: "what she noticed, what made her pencil move" is a pair. The four-item day list is deliberate.
   - One-line zinger endings (the habit is not cured, and the drafter admits it): "which was worse." / "She put it back in the box." / "Wasted on her." / "It had been drafted by minds that did not leave words out by accident." / "They had built the term around the latest she could possibly be." / "because the letter was hers." / "She could not stop." / "She had always written lightly." I count eight or nine in about 2,800 words. "He said the term the way some people say a psalm, with the commas in" is a good one, but it is also a closer.
6. **Therapy-speak and articulacy:** Everyone, Pell especially, speaks in complete, lawyerly, self-aware paragraphs. Bisi's "That's courting, not arguing" is also fluent in the same way.

## What to protect
- The term arithmetic and the "write to her next, hedge across every woman Oyelaran might turn out to be" passage. It is the best new idea in the draft.
- "You came back from the last one liking eels." This is the novum surfacing sideways.
- Hanne's "other reading", which keeps the term unresolved.
- Erasing by margin, face-down, eyes soft.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh prior art over felt effect. Echoes of stock beats may land as ritual rather than cliché for a reader outside the genre. I judged the ending's image by its lineage, not its sensory work. My knowledge of prior art is from memory, and "I know of no work" is not a claim that none exists.
