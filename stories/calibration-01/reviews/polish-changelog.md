## Judgment calls for the human

If you would rather the illegibility read as physics (pressure without shape), "every stroke she had ever pressed there, and not a word of it legible" is the alternative. Check that the clause doesn't crowd the snail simile.
2. **Last line kept.** Chiang suggested ending on "*Retained.*" alone. I kept "*Unedited.*" because the brief and Watts protect it, and because the unreadable grooves now give it an ironic edge rather than a verdict of survival. This is worth one more read aloud.
3. **Merged arithmetic paragraph.** Folding "There was no door…" into the sum paragraph makes one longer paragraph at the hinge of scene 4. It keeps "She had wanted the seat." mid-paragraph, which was also Watts's note 2. If the 3 a.m. scene now feels dense, you could split it again after "She had wanted the seat."
4. **Pell's "you, as you are this morning".** This is plainer than Asimov's "you, by then the one before". It loses the faint archival joke in "the one before". Check that it still sounds like Pell.
