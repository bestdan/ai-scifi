## The one thing
Decide whether the grooves are legible to her next. The ending shows an erasure that fails, which is the "traces persist" answer again. The § 14 echo gives it an ironic second reading, but only if the failure is a cost, not a consolation.

## Notes

1. **Ending (structural):** The echo does real work. "Retained. Unedited." turns the image from sentimental continuity into the Parties' own warranty, applied to her. That is the story's own plant, not a generic palimpsest, and it moves me from 2 to 3. It does not reach 4. The image is still faint-traces-survive-erasure (lineage: the palimpsest trope, and the "Lethe" type of memory-wipe stories). Worse, it breaks logic the plan itself defended. Declining the heron because survival "would break § 9" is inconsistent with a margin that survives in full, legible under raking light. Either raking light is available to her next, and Pell's "pass intact" and the erasing night are void, or it is not, and the last image is decoration. The page gives no answer. Pick one, in a clause.
2. **Pell's ruling (structural, declined):** "Your marginalia are the Keeper's own apparatus, like your spectacles" is still an official certifying a reading. I accept the plan's decision. It stays short and comic, and I record it as live.
3. **Stock beats (line-level):** Mina's forbidden question with the reddened ears, the last-night kitchen farewell, and the joke payoff remain. The joke now lands without "could not stop", which is a gain. Hanne's "other reading" is still the best friction in the story.
4. **Zingers (line-level):** The drafter says three remain. I count about eight paragraph closers: "which was the nearest he came to saying anything", "because Bisi was watching her from the stove", "which was worse", "because the letter was hers", "They had built the term around the latest she could possibly be", "and she put it back in the box", "Oyelaran said it was very good", "and nobody asked her to". "She had wanted the seat." is mid-paragraph, not a closer.
5. **Banned-list pass (mechanical):**
   - Em-dashes: zero. Credit.
   - "Not X. Y.": "That's not argument. That's a heron." (the one allowed). Near-misses: "She did not laugh. She never had." and "There was no door. There was the term". That makes a second shape, unused.
   - Phrases ("testament", "weight of", "something shifted", "flicker", "hum of"): none.
   - Senses in a row: none.
   - Names: clean (Nordic register logged for calibration-02).
   - Triplets: "Three people came" followed by three parallel arrivals. Minor, and deliberate.
   - Tics not on the list but worth a look: "the way X does Y" appears about seven times, and "her face did nothing" three times (lines 9, 47, 105).
6. **Articulacy (line-level):** Pell's dozen-clause sentences are in character. Bisi's "That's courting, not arguing" is still tidy.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh lineage over felt effect, and the closing echo may land harder with a general reader than my 3 admits. My prior-art memory is unchecked, and "palimpsest trope" is a category, not a citation.
