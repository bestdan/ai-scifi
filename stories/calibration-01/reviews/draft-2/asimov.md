## The one thing
Make the scene-4 inference say which woman the Parties can hedge against. "which was the one woman the Parties could hedge against" has a loose "which". It should name the generic next self, so the reader sees that erasing, which § 9 forces, also hands the Parties their target.

## Round-1 fixes: verified

- **Pell, "your next" and "the one before":** landed. The Keeper on the date is her next, and the presenter is the one before. "The one before" is a little clipped, so a reader may take it as her next's predecessor in the archive sense. This is line-level.
- **"Intact" versus graphite:** landed. "Every page of every account in the order received, though not the graphite, which is yours" removes the clash with scene 6. It also quietly plants the grooves, since the paper passes and the pencil does not. That makes the ending fair play.
- **"When you signed":** landed, in scenes 1 and 4, and it agrees with the 32 months.
- **"To the month":** landed.
- **Hanne's three turns:** landed. Three turns fit a 25-year cycle and her eighties.
- **Arithmetic re-checked:** 2y 8m + 22y 7m is 25y 3m. The taste goes on day 9, and a week to settle leaves a margin of two days. The scene headings Nine, Eight, Eight agree with "Yesterday. The tea."
- **§ 14:** now used in the last line, so the asymmetry pays off.

## Remaining notes (none structural)

1. **Scene 4, "which was the one woman…":** line-level, as above.
2. **Scene 5, "will be the one before":** line-level. Consider "will be you, by then the one before", so the antecedent can't slip.
3. **Scene 1, "for longer than she had been herself":** line-level. It implies the joke predates her present self, but no earlier turn is established at that point. It also sits against "She never had" laughed. Say "for as long as she had been herself", or drop the clause.
4. **Scene 6, the grooves:** line-level. The ending leaves open whether grooves in passed paper count as argument under § 9. That is a fair open question, so keep it open. Don't add a gloss.

## Blind spot disclosure
This lens wants every fact solvable and treats the sum as the story. It has no standing on the bodily and comic texture (the eraser, the ramp) and may overprice precision in Pell's ruling at the expense of his fog.
