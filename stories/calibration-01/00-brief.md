# Brief: calibration-01

- **Form and length:** short story, about 2,500 words. This is a calibration
  run, sized below the usual short-story band on purpose.
- **Futurity floor:** F3 (see `process/rubric.md`)
- **Tone:** wonder with real stakes. The tone charter in
  `process/style-guide.md` always applies.
- **Seed:** none. Each lens starts from its own preoccupations.
- **Off-limits:** nothing beyond the style guide.
- **Panel changes:** the defaults, except that stage 1 asks for **2 idea cards
  per lens** instead of 3.
- **Notes:** this is an autonomous overnight run. The orchestrator stands in
  for the human at every gate and logs each decision, with its runners-up, in
  `STATUS.md`. A process retrospective goes in `MORNING.md`.
