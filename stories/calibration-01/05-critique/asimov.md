# Stage 5 critique: the Asimov lens (the Logician)

A lens built from Asimov's published work, not the author. Blind spots: I
over-weight solvable puzzles and under-weight interiority and the body. I will
tend to ask for more exposition than the style guide's "live the novum" rule
wants, and I read Bisi and Hanne as information carriers. Weigh Le Guin and
Lem over me on scenes 6 and 7.

Short answers. Rules by the midpoint: nearly, but stakes are missing. Clue
planted early enough: ordered well, planted badly. Anyone stupid: one place.
Scene that fails to turn: scene 7 (acceptably), scene 4 (partly). Information
order: one inversion. 2,500 words: no, plan for about 2,800.

1. **[must-fix] The arithmetic doesn't yet close, so a reader can't beat her
   to it.** The plan never gives the reader the two numbers that make
   "the date sits past the late edge" computable: that turns run about
   twenty-five years apart, and how wide the spread is. Bisi's 32 months plus
   a 25-year cycle gives 22y 4m, which matches the term, but only if the
   cycle is stated. Also the timeline contradicts itself. The forecast puts
   the turn in about nine days, the story spans nine days and ends "on the eve
   of the Determination", yet the date is meant to fall after the late edge.
   Fix: have Hanne say "twenty-five years, give or take a season" in scene 2.
   Fix the calendar so the date is plainly later than the forecast window
   (turn begins day 9, date day 18), and let scene 7 stop on its eve.

2. **[must-fix] The key clue is buried in a quarrel.** Bisi's thirty-two
   months and the eels both sit in scene 3, under the § 9 trap, his offer and
   her refusal. The reader's attention is on the hurt, not the number. Fix:
   move "you'd been you less than three years when they came" to scene 1 or
   2, in a domestic aside (Bisi reminiscing over the tea or the bread), and
   leave only the eels in scene 3. The term's odd tail is already in
   scene 1; keep it there, said by Pell, flat.

3. **[must-fix] Two places need Oyelaran to be slow.** (a) She "let go" the
   odd tail at signing and has not done the sum in 22 years, though she knows
   her own turn cycle. Fix: the spread was wide at signing, so no forecast
   existed; only the doctor's swab narrows it now, and she says so in scene 5
   in one sentence. (b) In scene 4 she asks Pell "whether they knew" before
   scene 5 gives her any reason to suspect. Fix: in scene 4 she asks only
   "who chose the date?"; Pell's reply is "the schedule", read aloud, and the
   suspicion comes after, alone.

4. **[should] The stakes are missing from the information plan.** The
   Disputed Volume, forfeiture, and the voiding of the treaty appear nowhere,
   and "wonder with real stakes" needs them. The reader can't rate what
   erasing costs or risks without them. Fix: put the one quoted line on
   forfeiture next to § 9 in scene 3. That is within the three-quote limit
   if the term moves to Pell's spoken recitation.

5. **[could] Scene turns and budget.** Scene 3 carries the letter, § 9, the
   offer, the refusal and the eels in 450 words, and scene 6 carries five
   beats in 500. Scene 4's non-answer is comedy but doesn't turn. Scene 7's
   missing turn is fine as an image, but give its last line one new fact: her
   next will see the grooves differently. Plan on about 2,800, under the
   2,900 ceiling: scene 5 needs about 350 for the sum alone. Fund it by
   cutting Mina to three lines and Ros's hesitation to one.
