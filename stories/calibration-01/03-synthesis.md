# Synthesis: calibration-01

The stage 2 shortlist was chosen by the orchestrator standing in for the human
at the gate (see STATUS.md). It holds four options with distinct cores, drawn
from the audit's Top 5 and the next options in line. Each premise below folds
in the Cartographer's "push past the map" note where one was given.

## A. The Unthinking (Egan) — audit 36/40

The citizens of a heat-starved habitat run as software on reversible logic. To
spend nothing on erasure, they reason, keep only the conclusion, and run the
reasoning back to nothing. Senna's ledger holds one conclusion from four months
ago: *don't let him board the sail.* Her son leaves at the next launch window,
in eleven hours, and demands the reason. At her budgeted pace, re-deriving it
would take three days. The only heat she can draw on in time is his. If he
lends it, he must keep everything he'd budgeted to forget, including this
night. She has to choose. She can trust a past self she can no longer
question. Or she can rebuild the reasoning, knowing her present self may reach
a different answer, or may learn why she chose not to keep it.

**Strengths:** the highest novelty score in the audit. The physics produces
the feeling. The story fits one night and two minds.
**Risks:** lecturing on Landauer. Posthuman minds behaving like humans in
costume.

## B. The Keeper's Successor (Vinge × Chiang) — audit est. 35/40

Two posthuman polities, which can rewrite their own values and so can't make a
credible promise, have escrowed their treaty in Oyelaran. She is a baseline
human whose craft is being unpersuadable. On a fixed date she must make one
choice, and if anyone is caught trying to persuade her, the treaty is void.
Her rebase is due before that date: the scheduled procedure that keeps her
memories but re-forms her values. Whoever chooses on the day will be someone
new. The obvious answer is a letter to her successor, but the treaty's own
clause catches it: a letter arguing for a choice *is* persuasion, and her
former self is not exempt. To keep the promise legally, she has to make sure
the next Oyelaran is someone who will choose rightly, while saying nothing
that argues for anything.

**Strengths:** an Asimov-style edge case grows straight out of the novum, and
it has a fair-play solution. The scale is F4, but the viewpoint is the slow
mind, so the story stays writeable.
**Risks:** two nova to establish in 2,500 words. Treaty mechanics could crowd
out the person.

## C. The One Day (Le Guin × Watts) — audit est. 33/40

In Marrit's valley the dying are not dead. They are wintering: put into a
torpor in the back room, fed, turned and set a place at table, and they can be
woken once, for about a day, which is what kills them. Marrit has tended her
grandfather for eleven years. He asked to be woken at her wedding, and she has
never married. Her brothers are waking him now, because only he knows where a
contested boundary runs. Someone has to tell him eleven years in a day, and
Marrit is the one who knows him. What she tells him decides who he dies as:
the marriage that didn't happen, the land, the brother he'd be ashamed of.

**Strengths:** the warmest option, with the clearest emotional engine and the
smallest cast.
**Risks:** F3 floor only. It sits nearest the convergence clusters the audit
flagged as a model attractor (the dead, the valley, the boundary vote).

## D. Release (Rajaniemi) — audit 32/40

Here the dead are kept as threshold shares: eight friends each carry a shard
(*sirp*) of Aunt Marjatta, and five must gather to wake her. Ilmi inherited her
mother's sirp, and she wants to emigrate down a light-lagged relay. Leaving
would put the quorum one short forever, which in this culture is killing. At
the pominki, Marjatta wakes and asks the keepers to stop gathering. Ilmi's
leaving is the release she's been waiting for. The keepers who won't let go
become the antagonists, and Ilmi's departure becomes the mercy.

**Strengths:** information-theoretic rigor, and a reversal of the moral
polarity that's good for this tone.
**Risks:** six keepers is a large cast for 2,500 words. The audit names *Coco*
as the nearest neighbor in emotional shape. It also sits in the "the dead"
cluster.

## Decision

**B, The Keeper's Successor**, revised to answer the panel's two warnings:

- **Two nova** (Banks, Chiang). The turn, the rebase, is made *ordinary and
  involuntary*: a biological season every adult goes through, so it costs the
  reader no more than puberty would. The polities appear only through the
  treaty text and the escrow clerk.
- **A solved puzzle with a stated cost** (Chiang). The solution turns the
  premise over. The polities foresaw the turn: the treaty's oddly exact date was
  set to fall just after it. A judge known for twenty-two years can be gamed,
  and an unknown judge can't, so the only way to be found faithful by her is
  to have been faithful. Her successor was the mechanism all along. The cost
  is paid on stage. Every margin note she has written in twenty-two years of
  reports is an argument, and the clause forbids passing arguments on. She
  erases them.

**Declined:**
- Vinge's drift in the meaning of "persuasion". It's a third layer that 2,500
  words can't carry.
- Chiang's documentary form. Prose scenes give the erasure its physical weight.

**Seed stock:** A, The Unthinking (Chiang's first choice, novelty N4) is the
strongest runner-up and a natural core for calibration-02.
