# Outline: The Keeper's Margins (working title)

- **Target length:** about 2,500 words, with a hard ceiling of 2,900.
- **Shape:** linear, seven scenes over nine days, with time skipped between
  them. The shape is a funnel. It opens on the wide treaty (polities, the
  town) and narrows to one table, one pencil sketch and one groove.
- **Opening question:** why does her tea taste of nothing, and what is this
  woman whose job is to have no reaction?
- **Final image:** dawn on the day of the Determination, before Pell's boat.
  She holds an erased page up to the window, and in the raking light her own
  hand is still there as grooves. She squares the stack. (Chiang could: no
  wink about who might read them.)
- **What the ending costs her:** twenty-two years of reasons, the authority
  of her own conscience, and the last nights of her present self. She spends
  them erasing herself instead of saying anything. For Bisi, it costs any
  certainty that her next will want him.

## Calendar and arithmetic (fair-play clue, Asimov must-fix 1)

All four inputs are planted before scene 4, so an attentive reader can do the
sum first.

- **Cycle** (Hanne, scene 2): "Twenty-five years, never more than a season
  late."
- **Duration** (the doctor's forecast, scene 2): the turn settles over about a
  week once the taste goes.
- **Since her last turn** (Bisi, scene 1, as a domestic aside): "You'd been you
  thirty-two months when they came."
- **Term** (Pell, scene 1): "twenty-two years, seven months and nine days."
- **The sum:** 2 years 8 months + 22 years 7 months = 25 years 3 months, which
  is the cycle at its latest. The extra nine days let a turn that starts at
  the late edge finish settling before the Determination.
- **Her turn is late,** right at the edge. The tea goes flat nine days before
  the date.
- **Why she never did the sum** (Asimov must-fix 3, Banks should): she saw the
  odd tail at signing and chose not to work it out, because she wanted the
  seat. She admits this to herself in scene 4.

## Scenes

The scenes are headed by a day count running down from nine; the last
heading is left blank (Chiang could). Scenes 4 and 5 are swapped (Chiang
must-fix, Banks should).

## Notes for the drafter

- **Mr. Pell is the comic engine.** Give him one gloriously exact sentence in
  each of his scenes.
- **The treaty is quoted at most three times:** the term, § 9 and § 14.
  Quote the schedule definition once. Each quotation is short.
- **Don't explain the turn.** Let Hanne, Bisi and the tea carry it.
- **Never state the verdict or what the notes argued.** Not even a hint about
  which way she leans, beyond "the Settled Question's accounts were too
  perfect", which is allowed once, in scene 3, before she stops writing.
- **Watch the attractors the audit flagged.** These include an official
  certifying a reading, and Nordic and Baltic names. The bible's names avoid
  them, so don't drift back toward them.
