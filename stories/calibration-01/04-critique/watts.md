# Stage 4 stress test: Watts lens (calibration-01)

This is a critique through a lens built from Watts's preoccupations. It is not Watts and does not speak for him.

**Blind spots:** I over-weight mechanism and underrate ritual, so the erasing night may work better than I credit. I lean bleak. Citations are from memory and need checking.

**Verdict on the turn:** the broad direction is real, and the unusual part (memory surviving) is the weak point. Adult critical-period reopening has been shown in animals. Chondroitinase digests perineuronal nets and restores plasticity (Pizzorusso 2002). Fluoxetine reopens visual-cortex plasticity (Maya Vetencourt 2008). Psychedelics reopen a social-reward window in mice (Nardou 2023). Every one of these works by loosening existing circuitry, and that is the problem for "memory intact". Reopened plasticity overwrites. Reconsolidation also makes each recalled memory labile and rewritten by the current self. So the strongest true detail is that Oyelaran's *remembered* twenty-two years will drift toward the new self. Use it.

1. **[must-fix] Memory is argument too.** The successor keeps twenty-two years of memory, including the old conclusion that the Settled Question kept the letter, not the faith. Erasing the margins then looks pointless, and it weakens the polities' game theory, since an unknowable judge still has this judge's memories. Close it on the page. One option is that the clerk or the clause treats the notes as presented argument while memory is not, and the old self knows this is a distinction without comfort. A better option is that she finds her own recollection of the conclusion has already gone strange: reconsolidation turns a verdict into a story about a woman who held one.

2. **[must-fix] She is too lucid mid-prodrome.** "Acutely aware that her certainty is a property of a self that is ending" is textbook self-knowledge. People can't introspect on value change. Choice-blindness work (Johansson and Hall) shows we confabulate reasons for choices we didn't make. Let her feel her certainty as plain fact, and let the awareness arrive only as small failures. She reaches for an old opinion and it is cold. She defends the erasure with a reason she'd have rejected in March.

3. **[should] The prodrome is too tidy.** Taste first is defensible: chemosensory turnover is fast, and flat food is also what depression and anosmia feel like. Real broad plasticity would be messier. Expect broken sleep, affective lability, sensory over-brightness, hunger changes, and puberty-like or perimenopausal weather. Keep the tea but add one embarrassing body detail, say crying at the bakery, so the turn isn't only a refined palate.

4. **[should] Bisi knows too much.** "Wrong about nothing" and "treats it as weather" make him the wise spouse, and the guide bans the always-articulate character. Attachment is strongly biased toward the familiar. He should be wrong about something small and physical, such as cooking for the woman he expects rather than the one in front of him. The neighbours who never ask are too kind too: let one want to peek.

5. **[should] Who made the turn, and what is it for?** "Engineered", with no selective logic, is the biologist's eye-roll. Don't explain it, but show one thing the culture takes for granted because of it, such as child-rearing timed around turns.

6. **[could] The story flinches at its own conclusion.** The logic says Oyelaran was a consumable instrument, and the ending lets her redeem it by faithful erasure. Consider leaving the successor's verdict dangling, with the groove ambiguity left unresolved and unrewarded. Wonder comes from her complicity in her own replacement being an act of love that she can't confirm was the choice she made.
