# Stage 4 stress test: Egan lens (logic)

This is the Egan lens, not the author. Blind spots: I let derivation outrank the scene and under-weigh the emotional beat of the erasure night. Weigh this against the First Reader and Chiang.

Verdict: the game theory holds only in a weaker form than the bible claims.

1. [must-fix] The date arithmetic fails. The second turn came 18 months before signing and turns run about 25 years apart, so a 22y 4m 9d term ends about 1.2 years before the expected third turn, not nine days after. Fix: make the second turn about 32 months before signing (2y8m + 22y4m9d is roughly 25y), planted in one line of Bisi's. Say the polities set the date at the late edge of the spread. A tongue swab gives days of warning only at prodrome, never 22 years out. Nine days is where her turn actually fell.

2. [must-fix] Erasure has two holes. The bible forbids destroying the reports as treaty property, yet erasing margins alters them. And the successor remembers what the notes said; § 9 bars presenting argument, not remembering it. Fix: the clerk rules in one fussy sentence that marginalia are "the Keeper's own apparatus", not treaty text, but become argument when the archive passes "to the Keeper". Oyelaran then sees that erasure stops the paper presenting what memory already holds. It is not secrecy.

3. [must-fix] "Unknown judge forces genuine compliance" overclaims. A vast polity needs only the distribution of post-turn Oyelarans, and can be robustly faithful across it. That is hedging, not virtue, and "too perfect" accounts are the hedge's signature. The distribution is also narrow, since she keeps 22 years of memory. Fix: let her conclude the judge cannot be gamed but can be hedged against. Add one piece of divergence evidence, such as Bisi recalling a food she loved before her second turn and can't now stand.

4. [should] Random draw, and why a human? Answer in the clerk's mouth. A random stranger must be briefed, and briefing is argument under § 9; the turn alone gives an informed judge with unforeseeable values. A sealed fixed-value agent can't judge "faith" because both parties authored it. Also state that neither polity can influence the turn. The bible says "engineered"; say it predates the treaty.

5. [should] Erasure leaks. The successor remembers erasing. Eraser wear and page order show which pages mattered. Grooves persist, and the neighbours read the notes. The next step the story hasn't taken: a successor with no notes is the prior mean, so perfect protection makes her more predictable. Don't resolve it. Let grooves be the ending, with Bisi asked to leave one page unerased without saying which.

6. [could] The polities are humans in costume only partly. A well-timed date is human cleverness, and a value-editor would build a sealed precommitment before recruiting a mortal. Fix: one treaty clause only a self-editor would write, such as each polity confessing, with a stated probability, that it will come to want the Volume.
