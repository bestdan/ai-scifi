# Outline: Foreseeable (working title)

- **Length:** about 6,000 words, within the brief's 4,000 to 7,000.
- **Shape:** linear, eight scenes from Tuesday 3 June to a Sunday in late
  July 2059, told by Tamsin a few weeks after the hearing. The bracketed
  prediction log runs through it: it is how the reader watches her outthink
  her mother, and in scene 7 it becomes evidence.
- **Opening question:** what did Tamsin predict about Friday, and why won't
  she say it?
- **Final image:** Tamsin and her mother high on the viaduct scaffold on a
  Sunday, the city below, and a bracketed prediction Tamsin leaves unscored.
- **What the ending costs the protagonist:** her mother's fine and six
  months off work, her own term off checking, and the house rule, because
  the log is now public.
