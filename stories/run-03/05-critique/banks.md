# Banks lens: stage 5 critique of the outline

Blind spots: I favour sprawl and set pieces, and short stories aren't my form. Treat every "add" below as a swap for something else, not an addition. Let Asimov and Chiang cut.

1. **[must-fix] Scale is stated, never felt.** The world's vastness is "nearly every baby since 2043 is treated", and the outline only ever shows one desk and one kitchen. Scene 1 has the one chance for a concrete moment. Fix: in scene 1, give a single physical image of the handover, for example the office floor: forty teenagers at desks, two old teachers in the corner using the one slow terminal the section kept for them, and Tamsin's minute-long catch set against the week they would have spent on it. Keep it to one paragraph. Scene 8 has the vertical scale, the city from the viaduct. Plant in scene 1 what she can see from that height, so the scene 8 view pays off.

2. **[must-fix] Wit is thin and mostly unscheduled.** The bible gives Priya's badly predicted cricket scores, Kyle's leeks and Mum's "weather report", but the outline uses almost none of it. Scenes 3 to 5 (collapse, Kofi, form, letter) run four scenes of procedure and dread in a row. Fix: schedule one joke per scene in the outline. Use Priya's cricket forecasts in scene 4 as her tell. Give Kyle and Bev in scene 3 the leek photographs. Let Tamsin score her bracketed predictions about Gran's Sunday call with deadpan precision in scene 6. Humour next to the injury is what stops the middle reading as self-pitying.

3. **[should] Scene 6 is overloaded and scene 4 lectures.** Scene 6 holds the darts, Gran's call, the father's card, the hospital, the log reread and the walk out, in 1,200 words. The twist lands at the end of a pile. Fix: move the Fat Cat darts and Gran's call up into the gap between scenes 4 and 5. They then become the "gathering" that makes the Stakes visible. Scene 6 becomes the hospital, the reread and the viaduct only. Scene 4 carries Kofi, the form, Priya's bent rule and the law explained. Move Kofi's visit after the form so the exposition has a person in front of it.

4. **[should] Who is missing: the crew, and the judge.** The untreated appear only as Mum, Kyle in a bed, and a judge who delivers a ruling. The view from below is Mum's whole crew, three tired people in the rain, which Kofi's schedule ignored. Fix: in scene 2 or 3, give the crew one line each, quoted in Mum's account (names optional, at most two). In scene 7, give the untreated judge two sentences of her own reasoning aloud. She is the benevolent power doing the dirty work. She rules for mercy-adjacent reasons that are thin, and she knows it. Right now only Tamsin knows.

5. **[could] Try a different ordering for the reveal.** The structure is linear, so the twist (Tamsin's silence) arrives in scene 6 and the opening question is "why won't she say it?" A Use of Weapons move: open with about 150 words of the cross-examination ("Who scored these?" "I did."), then run scenes 1 to 6 as the lead-up. The reader meets the log as evidence first and as a diary second, so each bracketed entry is read as potentially damning. The cost is that the scene 7 reveal is partly spent. If the panel thinks that is too high a price, keep the order linear and drop this.

Also check, for Asimov: scene 8 has Freya suspended for six months and then on a scaffold. Say whose site it is, and that suspension covers signing, not standing on one. Otherwise a careful reader will ask whether the last scene is lawful.
