# Stage 5 critique: Asimov (the Logician)

Blind spots: I weigh rules and fair play over interiority, texture and the mother-daughter feeling. Le Guin and Banks should overrule me there.

1. **[must-fix] The statement's logic is not on the outline's page.** Tamsin files "could have chosen otherwise" with a log whose 3 June entry predicts this very choice at eighty. A reader will ask how that supports her statement. The bible has the answer (the hit rate falls on rare choices under pressure, and the misses are evidence), but the outline plants it nowhere before scene 7. Fix: in scene 2, put the rule in Tamsin's own words ("cheap hits are tea; pressure is where I miss"). In scene 5, set her reasoning beside the stakes. Then the judge's ruling in scene 7 is something an attentive reader could predict, not a surprise.

2. **[must-fix] The Twist is "shown" by a walk.** Scene 6 ends with her walking to the viaduct, which is a mood, not a consequence. The twist is Tamsin's silence, so show it costing her. Make the cross-examination in scene 7 read the 3 June entry aloud, and have the office's term off checking follow from it. Also plant in scene 2 that she had Tuesday to Friday, and that one earlier unspoken entry exists. Otherwise "two earlier entries" in scene 6 comes from nowhere.

3. **[must-fix] The word budget is wrong for the scene count.** Scene 2 carries about nine beats in 1,000 words: log, tea, stair fault, a miss, Kofi's schedule, rain, ties, the colleague's father, the Friday prediction. Scene 6 carries five in 1,200: darts, Gran, card, Kyle, log reread. The climax in scene 7 gets 900. Fix: cut Gran and the card to one line each. Give scene 6 about 900 words, scene 7 about 1,400, and move the colleague's father to scene 1 if it fits. Recount the total.

4. **[should] Kofi's thread dangles, and who-knows-what is unclear.** He asks in scene 4 whether the statement will blame him and is never answered. The outline also doesn't say how the court learns that only one set of ties was fixed (an inspector, a crew member). Fix: say in scene 4 what each outcome does to the firm and to Kofi, who is thirteen and can't be blamed anyway. Have one line in scene 7 settle it. Name the source of the court's knowledge in scene 3.

5. **[should] Scene 3 doesn't turn.** "Her prediction came true" confirms what the reader already suspects from the ties and the rain, so it shouldn't carry a scene. Give it a choice: Mum confesses, and Tamsin could say "I knew" but keeps the rule again. That makes the silence an active second choice and sharpens the turn in scene 6. Make sure the rain delay, the one-day schedule and "two levels, two sets of ties" are all on the page in scene 2, so the reader beats Tamsin to the shortcut fairly.

Checked and fine: the world facts are all in the first third. Stakes (scene 5) come before the climax (scene 7). Premise and Why each have a stated and a shown scene.
