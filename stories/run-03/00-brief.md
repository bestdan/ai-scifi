# Brief: run-03

- **Form and length range:** short story, 4,000 to 7,000 words. The range's
  top is the drafter's hard ceiling, so leave room for what the premise
  turns out to carry.
- **Gates:** proxy. The orchestrator decides every gate after this brief and
  the human reads the story blind. The human knows the seed, so "blind" here
  means the angle, the characters and the ending.
- **Futurity floor:** F2 (see `process/rubric.md`). The seed lives one
  generation out, in a society reorganizing around the change, which is F2's
  shape. A card that reaches F3, changing what parent, child or elder means,
  is welcome but not required.
- **Tone:** honest but optimistic; a coming-of-age story. The tone charter in
  `process/style-guide.md` always applies.
- **Seed (fixed):** a treatment given in utero or at birth makes children
  dramatically smarter, on the order of 60 IQ points, and they learn much
  faster. Every card keeps this premise. Lenses may wander in what follows
  from it: mechanism, rollout, who gets it, what the world does next.
- **Direction:** the generational timeline. The treated children grow up and
  replace the adults they are already smarter than: parents, teachers,
  bosses, institutions. The story lives in that handover, two or three
  steps out.
- **Off-limits:** nothing beyond the style guide. In particular the
  eugenics-horror default and the hidden-side-effect twist count as the
  stock darkness the tone charter rules out, unless a card makes one of them
  its uncharted novum.
- **Novelty:** the human has waived novelty for the seed itself. The
  Cartographer will mark the bare premise charted; that does not
  disqualify it as the core here. Novelty is scored on the angle each card
  takes on it.
- **Panel changes:** defaults. 3 idea cards per lens.
- **Notes:** an unattended proxy run. The orchestrator logs each gate
  decision, with its runner-up, in `STATUS.md`. Nothing the human sees
  before reading reveals the angle.
- **Seed adherence (the human, after run-02):** run-02 drifted from the
  seed: its treatment came late, and its children didn't surpass their
  parents. This run keeps the seed as written. The children are treated in
  utero or at birth, they are far smarter and faster-learning than the
  untreated adults around them, and the story shows that gap on the page:
  the children outthink their parents, teachers and bosses. Stage 3 rejects
  any premise that weakens, delays or inverts this.
- **Rerun:** this is a second run on run-02's brief and seed, under the
  process as changed after run-02's debrief. Run-02's premise (the
  "Setting Day" bell-founding angle) is charted for this run: the audit
  treats it as prior art, and no card may reuse it as its core.
