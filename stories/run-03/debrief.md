## 5. Process takeaways

- **[process: stage 7] Twist is graded on recall, not on whether the motive
  carried.** The cold reader repeated the Tuesday entry and passed; the human
  saw it and read it as an oddity. A comprehension question could ask why the
  protagonist acted as they did at the turn, and grade the answer.
- **[process: stage 7] The lost-list didn't predict the human.** Run-03's four
  reports listed 7, 6, 5 and 8 entries; the human snagged on none of them. The
  over-three rule failed the gate four times on confusions the human never had.
  Candidate: drop the count from the gate, or raise it, and keep the
  graded-row test.
- **[process: stage 4] Accelerated children still need to be children.** The
  bible and the persona panel asked what treated minds can do, never what they
  still lack at fifteen. Run-02 and run-03 both drew this note in different
  forms.
- **[process: stages 2–4] The world beyond the story's one question.** "The
  topic was very narrow (legality etc) but good." Atmosphere needs a few
  details of the treatment's other effects, so the world reaches past the
  plot.
