# Terms

From the bible's Glossary:
- **treated / untreated:** born with the prenatal treatment that makes children far smarter, or born before it existed (before 2043).
- **ties:** the steel bolts and tubes that fix a scaffold to the wall it stands against; two sets a level near the top.
- **limited:** the court's finding that a person couldn't have chosen otherwise, which bars them from ever again signing for safety work.
- **statement (foreseeability statement):** the written answer a treated relative gives the court on whether the person could have chosen otherwise.

Other words the draft uses that a reader might not know:
- **the log / weather report:** Tamsin's record of predictions about her mother, each with a confidence figure, scored Hit or Miss with a running tally.
- **repointing:** replacing the mortar between bricks.
- **parapet:** the low wall along the top edge of the viaduct.
- **level (of a scaffold):** one storey of boards, about two metres.
- **sliding bearing:** a support that lets one end of a landing slide as the building moves.
- **double sixteen, treble twenty:** darts targets; finishing on a double is the rule.
- **the second read:** the adults' re-check of plans the teenagers have already passed.
