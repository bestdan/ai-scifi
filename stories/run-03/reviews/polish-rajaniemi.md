Blind spots: I favour opacity and can read plain explanation as clumsy when it is the point. This story is deliberately plain, so I held back. Asimov and the First Reader should overrule me.

**Verdict:** the polish is clean. The added explanations (year, darts, Why chain, tally, unscored entry) read as present rather than clumsy, and each stays in Tamsin's voice. Glossary check: treated/untreated, ties, limited, statement and "foreseeability statement" (once, in the letter) match the bible. No stray coinages. "Weather report" and "second read" are plain compounds, not terms needing entries. All payoff-ledger lines are explicit.

Suggestions (3, all optional):

   "Everyone born since 2043 is treated. Since that year nearly every pregnant woman in the country has been given an injection, which makes the baby..." →
   "Everyone born since 2043 is treated. Nearly every pregnant woman since then has been given an injection, which makes the baby..."

2. Scene 2, the tally line restates the line above it.
   "That's my score: I've been right about her 4,388 times out of 4,871." →

   "Thirty-eight of them have teenagers at them" →
   "Thirty-eight of them have children at them"
   (A "children" count against "school ends at about eleven" is odder and truer. Decline it if the First Reader found "teenagers" easier to follow.)

Otherwise none. The log's brackets, the gas bill, the Friday biscuit tin and the "fish" line carry the texture without help.
