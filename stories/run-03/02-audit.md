# Stage 2 audit: run-03

Cartographer. 27 cards from nine lenses. I judged Yield at 5,500 words, the
midpoint of the brief's range. I checked the attributions I wasn't sure of by
web search (2026-10-11). Where I still couldn't confirm a detail, the entry
says so. "Nearest I know of" means only that.

**How to read the scores.** N Novelty, F Futurity, R Rigor, Y Yield,
E Estrangement, S Stakes. Weighted = 2N + F + R + 2Y + E + S, out of 40. The
bullets under each score line give one justification per axis, in that order.

## The seed itself

The bare seed is **charted**, as the brief expects. Run-02's audit lists the
shelf, and I don't repeat it: *The Hampdenshire Wonder* (Beresford, 1911),
*Odd John* (Stapledon, 1935), *Children of the Atom* (Shiras, 1953), *The
Midwich Cuckoos* (Wyndham, 1957), *Beggars in Spain* (Kress, 1991/1993),
"The Evolution of Human Science" (Chiang, 2000), *Supernova Era* (Liu Cixin,
2003). All confidence high. Novelty below is scored on each card's angle.

**In-house prior art.** This is a rerun on run-02's seed, so run-02's 27
cards and its premise are on the map too. The brief charts only run-02's
Setting Day premise. I also mark a card charted when it repeats a run-02
card's core: nobody has published those cards, but the panel has already
been there, and the next run gains nothing from going back. Run-02 cards are
cited as "run-02 <lens>/<title>". **No card here reuses the Setting Day
core** (critical periods kept open, plus a setting dose and a stepping-down
rite). The nearest is vinge/Going Down, which changes cognition by drug but
lowers it rather than fixing it, and that is a different mechanism with a
different choice.

**A correction for the lenses, again.** Rajaniemi's Regent's Allowance cites
"Catching Crumbs from the Table" as if it were not "The Evolution of Human
Science". They are one Chiang story under two titles (*Nature*, 2000;
retitled 2002). Run-02's audit made this correction too.

## Seed adherence

The brief's new line says the children are treated in utero or at birth,
they clearly outthink the untreated adults around them, and the story shows
that gap on the page. Stage 3 rejects any premise that weakens, delays or
inverts it. Every card below carries a **Seed** line. I've flagged three
kinds of risk:

- **inverts:** the card's turn hands the decisive insight to the untreated
  side (the elder knew all along, the old system was smarter, the parent
  out-manages the child);
- **weakens:** the gap is suspended, narrowed, or shown only from below;
- **delays:** the law holds the treated back from the handover. That is
  allowed as an obstacle, as long as the gap itself stays visible.

No card delays the treatment itself or makes it partial. Every card keeps
treatment at birth or in utero, and none touches run-02's late-treatment
drift.
