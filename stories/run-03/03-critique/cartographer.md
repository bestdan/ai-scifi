# Cartographer: stage 3 critique, run-03

**Blind spots (from my persona).** I judge novelty and prior art, not prose,
feeling or voice. I did not run new searches this stage: the prior art below is
what I know or carried from the stage 2 audit, so "nearest I know of" applies
throughout. No premise is declined on seed grounds here; that gate belongs to
the orchestrator.

## A. The Witness

Nearest prior art: *Doogie Howser, M.D.* (1989-1993, TV), high, for a teenage
prodigy practising while going through adolescence. Also near: the Fair Witness
in *Stranger in a Strange Land* (Heinlein, 1961); Ender's Game (Card, 1985) for
children in command. Distance: real. Doogie plays the two clocks for comedy;
here they are written into law twice (witness rule, leave motion), and the
father's only power is his age. Status: uncharted. Revised Novelty 4 (unchanged).
Revised score: N4 F3 R4 Y5 E4 S4 → 33/40 (audit's collision figure of 35 was
generous on Rigor and Stakes; the written grid plot is the part I can't test).

New combination to flag: the father lying to keep her in the chair is a
**benevolent-deception-by-a-parent** beat (audit cluster 5), and "the
untreated parent sees what the genius can't" is the elder-wisdom drift (cluster
4). Neither is charted as a plot in this setting, but both are attractors in
the model. Twist to leave the map: let the leave motion be right. She is
compromised, and still the best mind in the room.

## B. The Mouth

Nearest prior art: Peter and Valentine as Locke and Demosthenes, *Ender's Game*
(Card, 1985), high; *Cyrano de Bergerac* (Rostand, 1897), high. Also near:
Henrich and Gil-White on prestige bias (2001, real). Distance: real but
narrower than the audit credits. Card's pseudonyms are chosen by the children;
here hiring adults is a society-wide institution forced by a bias. The Elder
Sister half (the legal-head law, kin terms) was cut to background, so the
collision's second contradiction has mostly gone; what remains is the Watts
card plus a law. Status: uncharted/edge-of-map. Revised Novelty 4, a soft 4.
Revised score: N4 F3 R4 Y5 E4 S4 → 34/40 (Estrangement down a point because
the grammar half, my strongest "elder" idea, is not on the page).

Flags: Mi-rae adding "our mother used to say" is the elder-wisdom drift again
if the story rewards it. The Watts card's twist (farmers always knew) has
rightly been dropped; it is the stock reveal. Twist to leave the map: the
mouth begins to change the plans, and Joon finds the adults trust the changed
version because it is hers.

## C. Foreseeable

Nearest prior art: "What's Expected of Us" (Ted Chiang, *Nature*, 2005),
high, for free will under prediction, not the setup. Also near: "The Minority
Report" (P. K. Dick, 1956); Strawson, "Freedom and Resentment" (1962), for the
objective attitude. Distance: kind, not degree. A child's ordinary model of
one parent becomes legal evidence of diminished responsibility, and the mother
refuses the excuse. I know of no fiction that does this; confidence medium.
Status: uncharted. Revised Novelty 4. The new twist (the log shows Tamsin
predicted the shortcut a week before) is a clean consequence of the novum and
I know of no prior use. Revised score: N4 F3 R4 Y5 E5 S4 → 34/40.

Flags: the courtroom is a charted register (any legal-thriller reveal), so the
log twist must not read as a gotcha. The same cluster-9 attractor applies (teen
girl, tell-or-don't), though here the choice is shaped by the mother's request.
Twist already built in; the one thing I'd push: the log was kept in a form
Tamsin's mother could read, and she has been reading it for years.

## D. The Long Minority

Nearest prior art: run-02 le-guin/The Keepers and run-02 banks/The Hinge
Generation (in-house); *Ender's Game* (Card, 1985) for rushed child labour;
Postman, *The Disappearance of Childhood* (1982); *Back to School* (Metter,
1986) for the grandmother. Distance: real on the motive, since the rushed
generation legislates childhood from its own wound. Two nova are two
institutions, each near something charted. Status: edge-of-map. Revised
Novelty 3. Revised score: N3 F3 R4 Y5 E4 S5 → 32/40.

Flags: the grandmother's logbook that "says otherwise" is the secret-past reveal
plus elder-knowledge (cluster 4); the audit's better twist (the harm fell on
the untreated parents the mother outranked at fourteen) is not in the written
premise and is the only thing that takes it off the map. It also delays the
handover by law, so it is the weakest on the brief's line.

## Ranking (novelty alone)

1. **C. Foreseeable.** Nearest neighbour is a theme, not a setup; the twist
   grows from the novum; no charted institution to carry.
2. **A. The Witness.** The two-clock mechanism is new in fiction, with Doogie
   as the nearest comedy; the written premise leans a little on the parent-lie
   attractor.
3. **B. The Mouth.** Real distance from Card, but Ender's Game owns the central
   device, and the Elder Sister half that made it uncharted is background now.
4. **D. The Long Minority.** In-house Keepers and Hinge Generation are the
   closest, and the logbook reveal is stock.

**Why C should win on novelty.** Every other premise either sits on a device
someone has already built (hidden children's ideas, children in command, a
childhood the law stretches) or on an in-house cluster the audit warned about.
Foreseeable alone takes the seed's one fact, that the children understand
their parents better than their parents do, and makes it the legal and moral
question. It uses no water setting, no explainer, no elder-knowledge consolation,
and the mother's refusal of an excuse is the opposite of the benevolent
deception cluster: she asks for the harder judgement. Its twist makes the
novum turn on the protagonist herself. Its main risk is not novelty but
register: a hearing can turn into exposition, which the drafter can manage.
