## The one thing
Arvo's 2 October remark is the idea's best moment, and the story misses what it does. He pastes "From this morning I am writing to you" into Annex A "so that his remarks would sit openly on the public record." He chooses to be read. Desmond did not. That difference between a sentence you put in the open and one that was taken is what the title asks about, and the draft never lets Arvo or anyone see it. One sentence at the 2 October scene would do it. He knows what Desmond lost and publishes anyway, because a letter that can't be read isn't a letter.

## Notes
1. **The circularity is told three times (structural):** Hamid's "It was right that a request might come, and wrong that one was coming anyway!" appears verbatim in Arvo's head after the Hum's late reply. The Hum has just stated the loop itself ("it was the arrival of the page in Tharsis... that caused the inner ring to file"). Cut Arvo's restatement. Let the Hum's paragraph stand, and leave Arvo with "nobody could ever prove" alone.
2. **Teresa at the plaque (structural):** This is the one unnecessary scene, or its first two thirds are. The Quick Years history is Nominal's 6 March argument told again at length. Keep Mele's "isn't it lonely out there?" and Teresa's "even a mirror might get tired of never putting a word in." Cut the Oslo vote and the hoarding to a paragraph.
3. **Huda's Babylon letter (line-level):** The Hum's one oracle voice comes back at the end: "the wish at the foot of the pillar was the part that lasted." Item 4 asked for cold fragments. The last one in the story consoles again, and Graça's lemons "without a trace of bitterness" close the same way. A literal sentence here, like Mele's 3,020 turns, would make Huda's blank TO: harder.
4. **Hamid's mechanism speeches (line-level):** "A station's diagnostic box is read-only silicon from 2059..." is still a scientist explaining to listeners. Nikolasi's objection that this is a convict tapping on a pipe never came. Give it to him.
5. **The chord paragraph in "Lap 13,492" (line-level):** It does the idea's work in the Hum's own register, which is right, but "From inside the long ring, the chord is like breathing with someone else" arrives after the reader has met the chord in Arvo's head. Order it so the Lap section comes first.
6. **"neither was the man on Mars" (line-level):** The cold reader misread Desmond as the chess partner. Say "the man in Tharsis."
7. **Chess numbering (line-level):** "once a week since 2117" does not reach move 24.

## What to protect

- Lap 13,820. The Hum lets the general chapters fade and keeps "the notes from the desk under the forty numbers." It is the first thing it chooses to forget, and it needs no explanation.

- "You did not write to me. I read it." and Desmond's December fragment, which heals nothing.

- "Put me down as my own residual addressee until further notice, young man."
- "waited for the backwash of a seventh wave to clear the weed from the stone, and went in flat under the foam."

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh the idea over momentum, and I read the Part II procedure more charitably than the cold reader did (who was bored by 3 March to mid-May). I am harder on tidy endings than a general reader will be. I have not scored Character.
