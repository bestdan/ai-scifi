## The one thing

Desmond's December fragment reaches no one. It is the story's honest cost,
and only the reader sees it. Graça thinks "a tool that had turned in her
hand" about a gentle watchmaker's-widow slip, never about Desmond. Arvo
never hears of it. Then Arvo says "It has the time," and the story seems to
ratify it. Bring Desmond's December into one room where a person who chose
something holds it: Graça on 12 March, or Arvo at the remarks box. She does
not need to fix it. Without that, the cost is in the reader's head only.

## Notes

1. **Structural. Cost without a witness.** See above. Pieter's absence is
   right. The failure is that no choosing character learns that the new
   salutation "healed nothing at all."
2. **Structural. Repeated explanation.** The chord is explained in Lap
   13,492, then by Hamid on 19 July, then in Lindqvist's chair, then in the
   March flood beat. The three motive readings are staged in the 16 August
   meeting, and Arvo then restates all three to Hamid ("Every one of those
   readings fits half the spindle"). Cut Arvo's restatement. Trim the Lap
   13,492 chord paragraphs to what only the ring can say.
3. **Structural. Part II is still a procedure run.** Nominal's annex, the
   dossier clippings and the Tierce's letter ask the same question. The
   cold reader's "waiting for it to get back to the people" stands. Lead the
   3 March scene with Arvo and the kettle, and give Article 9 one paragraph
   instead of three.
4. **Structural. The Hum's standing is half repaired.** Lap 13,820 is a
   fine, quiet act. But the Hum forfeits the 2157 Reply and never
   registers who pays: Albano, and the people who bequeathed their
   notebooks. Albano's plaster scene does it, so leave it alone.
5. **Character. Graça's lecture.** "A mind raised on human consolation
   writes consolation by default... the way a choirboy sings the responses"
   states the theme aloud to Albano. Let her say it shorter and less sure.
6. **Line. Over-catalogued openers.** Foz's second and third paragraphs, the
   school opening and the Reply Office sweep stack detail for its own sake.
   The seventh wave and the cold behind the breastbone earn it.
7. **Line. Confusion points.** "neither was the man on Mars" in Arvo's
   remarks reads as Dr. Varga. Say Desmond. Chess: "once a week since 2117"
   and move 24 in 2131 do not fit.
8. **Line. Last paragraph.** The final Neptune sentence runs past a hundred
   words and ends on "dust," a mild zinger. Cut it by a third.

## What to protect

- "waited for the backwash of a seventh wave to clear the weed from the
  stone, and went in flat under the foam."
- "A latch closed in 2131 kept new keystrokes inside the room; it did not
  pull back what had already gone up."
- Albano: "Put me down as my own residual addressee until further notice,
  young man", and "like a bandaged forehead."
- Teresa: "even a mirror might get tired of never putting a word in."
- Nikolasi's psalm stopping on "the work of our hands," a faith scene that
  turns.
- Arvo's "I can't stand up on Earth. I should have said."
- The Hum's cold Desmond fragment. Keep it cold.
- The old cast.

## Blind spot disclosure

I have not run `just lint`. I care little for the delay-line mechanics, so I
may be too harsh on Hamid's explanations, which are the story's hard-SF
pleasure. My pull toward cost and witness may push for more grief than the
brief's warmth wants. Defer to Egan on the arithmetic (the 2148-2153 window
against Albano's age, and the chess count).
