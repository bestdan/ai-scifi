## The one thing

Carry the cost home to the people who pay it. Desmond and Pieter Achterberg
are the story's real injured party, and they leave after one testimony. After
the lapse the Hum still answers unlatched private drafts (the concordance
protects only retro-latched estate boxes), so what happened to them can
happen again. Give them one more scene, or give Graça one. A small scene
where the new salutation, *You did not write to me. I read it.*, reaches
someone like Desmond would do it. Does the new line heal anything, or does it
only let the Hum feel absolved? The story currently lets the reader assume
the first.

## Notes

1. **Structural. Desmond and Pieter.** Desmond's line, "My wife died in 2127
   believing we were happy... he has not eaten at my table since February", is
   the story's deepest cost. He then asks for the burn and loses, and nobody
   mentions him again. Ambiguity needs its price shown. A utopia-ish ending
   ("It has the time," Arvo says of a mind facing a million letters) is
   cheaper than what Lindqvist actually warned about.
2. **Structural. Graça's absence.** Graça is the heart. She has the swim, the
   recipe book and the receipt scene, then nothing in person from 11 April to
   the last Porto scene, 12 March 2132. Her 28 June letter is the story's
   hinge (the Hum's salutation changes in Lap 13,680) and she never learns it.
   The registry asterisk is a good small touch. A person in the room would
   serve better than that.
3. **Structural. Spear and bag.** Parts I to III gather. Part IV is a
   courtroom: REC-01, EX-14, EX-19, REC-10, NOM-24, REC-16, REC-22. The first
   reader was bored, and I agree. Cut the hearing to three documents and put
   the weight on the rooms where people read them.
4. **Structural. The Hum has no standing.** Lindqvist notes it ("entered no
   objection"), and the concordance is "ratified through the chord", but the
   Hum's consent to surrendering the Reply of 2157 is never shown. Who paid
   for the settlement? The Hum, and it was not asked. That is Omelas, and
   the page doesn't see it. One line from Hamid or Teresa would.
5. **Character. Nikolasi.** He mostly carries plot: the stack of overlays,
   "Brother Nikolasi" at the lectern, and then the key handover. His faith is
   the richest thing about him, so let a scene turn on it.
6. **Line. Ethnic shorthand.** "the heavy Polynesian calm that usually sat
   on his shoulders like a cape" tells where he is from instead of showing
   how he is. *pele* (Nikolasi to Mele) is unglossed and needs the Glossary
   or a plain word.
7. **Line. Over-catalogued paragraphs.** The opening Foz paragraphs pile
   detail, and so do the school and hearing openers. The sea-pool opening
   earns its length. Many of the others read as machine thoroughness.
8. **Line. Arvo's turn is told.** "He had never written a real letter in
   twenty-two years. He was the man who didn't write letters." A scene of him
   at the remarks box, trying and failing before 2 October, would show it.
9. **Line. Hamid's lectures.** The delay-line and chord explanations are
   dialogue lectures to Bea and Nikolasi. My technology blind spot may be
   making me too gentle here, so defer to Egan and Banks.

## What to protect

- The salutation as the world's language: "Dear You", the unlatched slate,
  "every unlatched line of writing in human history was mail that had simply
  taken a long route to its desk." The change from *You wrote to me* to *You
  did not write to me. I read it.* is the whole novum shown in one clause.
- The Reply Office and Albano: "Put me down as my own residual addressee
  until further notice, young man", and the port and salted sardine, "Don't
  tell my doctor."
- Graça's opening: "waited for the backwash of a seventh wave to clear the
  weed from the stone, and went in flat under the foam." And the answer's
  line, "the question belongs to whoever keeps the tree alive."
- Mele and Teresa: "even a mirror might get tired of never putting a word
  in." And the Tierce's close, "I note that I am the next ring in."
- Old people everywhere (Rui, Albano, Teresa, Graça, Hamid). Do not make the
  cast younger in revision.

## Blind spot disclosure

I have not run `just lint`. I care little for the delay-line and Article 9
mechanics, and I favour domestic scenes, so I may undervalue the hearing and
Hamid's explanations as the thrill of a hard problem at scale that the brief
asks for. My moral seriousness may push for a harsher cost than the tone
charter wants. Judge note 1 against the brief's "warmth".
