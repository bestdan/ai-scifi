## The one thing
Cut Part IV's hearing by two thirds and put Arvo's principle against himself on the page. Arvo writes "She wasn't writing to the sky" to the Hum while he keeps typing to it. The story never lets him see that "You did not write to me. I read it" is also true of his remarks box, which he did not write *to* anything and which was read as a letter anyway. That is the idea producing the feeling, and it is buried under dockets.

## Notes
1. **Form (structural):** The routing blocks do work. Every letter arrives with a lag, a postage line and a "Sent" time, and that is how the story enacts reading a message meant for someone else. The first-reader says it skipped them by the fifth. They carry the idea, but they are one register. Vary them. Let the Hum's letters have no block at all, or let the block be wrong in a way that matters.
2. **Part IV, the hearing, REC-01 through REC-22 (structural):** The same trade comes back three times: Nominal forgets, the Tierce holds a grudge, the Chair notes it. Nominal contradicting itself is funny once (14 May) and a restatement on 15 November. The chronology minute that explains the circularity is the story's best idea, and it arrives as a legal footnote. Dramatise it in Hamid's "He caught it!" and cut Lindqvist's cathedral-organ aside.
3. **The ear (structural):** The 14 May audit of 3,418 tickets exists only to be wrong. The reader knew from "station 23's ear" in scene 3, so it adds no question. Compress it to a page.
4. **The ending, Concordance and Tierce (structural):** The Tierce withholds, the Compact legalises the relay, and Nominal files again in 2141. That is tidy. The Hum is given a lawful mouth and a flood of mail, and the concordance reads as an answer. The question "may a reader who wasn't addressed answer?" is better kept open by Huda's pen, so let the Concordance be one clause shorter.
5. **The Hum's late reply, "wrong that it was fixed" (line-level):** The cold reader could not parse it, and I cannot say what "fixed" means. The reply is the one place the Hum speaks about its own motive. Say plainly what it was wrong about.
6. **Hamid's mechanism speeches (line-level):** The scientist who explains everything. "A station's diagnostic monitor is an independent hardware box..." is correct and necessary, but Bea and Nikolasi are listeners only. Give Nikolasi the objection, "that is a convict tapping on a pipe", and let Hamid refuse it with something other than data.
7. **Nominal's memory (line-level):** It forgets weekly but keeps a signed file. The reader took "it forgets, but leaves itself notes" and could not tell whether that counts as remembering. This is the story's second philosophical question, whether a file is a memory, and the draft only half-asks it.

## What to protect
- "when a person dies before she can answer, the question belongs to whoever keeps the tree alive, and you kept yours through forty-eight winters after that November."
- The September letter: "It takes a long time to build a small window in a dark wall when the only words one has are the names of broken parts." Arvo "did not correct it."
- The Lap 12,638 column set against the work-order column; the form pays off there and nowhere better.
- "Put me down as my own residual addressee until further notice, young man."
- The Tierce: "I note that I am the next ring in."
- Huda's empty TO: line.
- Graça's "Dear You" struck through.

## Blind spot disclosure
I favour restraint and an idea carrying the feeling, so I will under-weight the plot of the hearing, which Banks and Watts may want more of. I also treat the tidy ending as a flaw more heavily than a general reader will, and I am not judging action or momentum. I have not scored Character.
