## The one thing
Take the Hum's fragments out of the sage register, or make the page pay for
it. Every fragment (Leonor, Mele, Arvo, Hammurabi, Peralta) is the same
consoling oracle. That drifts to the banned beat: the machine that turns out
to be the most human voice in the room. The novum is uncharted, but the voice
is stock.

## Notes
1. **Fragment voice (structural).** "The light does not grow tired, because
   each mirror catches the pulse before it fails", "the wish at the foot of
   the pillar was the part that lasted", "the metal has kept warm". These are
   aphorisms with a closing consolation. Nearest I know of: the benevolent
   oracle mind in the vein of Lem's *Golem XIV* (1981), confidence medium on
   the voice only. The Hum is supposed to be a century-average that thinks in
   26-hour laps and holds grief. Give it one fragment that is wrong, cold or
   oddly literal, so Pieter's "every word of it was true" isn't the Hum's
   only mode. Nominal is the cold bureaucrat and the Hum the warm one,
   which sorts the minds too neatly.
2. **Huda's blank "TO:" (structural).** The style guide bans a final image
   the page gives no way to answer, and the cold reader asks "Whose letter...
   what does she say?" That is the "who sent it?" signal. The page supports
   one reading (to the Hum), not two. Either give the line a second candidate
   (a clerk writing to the dead scribe, versus writing to the Hum), or
   address it.
3. **Estranged-daughter letter (line-level).** Retiring keeper, apple trees,
   unanswered letter eleven years late, answered at the last gate: charted
   ground in mainstream and SF literary fiction. It is saved by the specific
   cause (0.08 g body, "I can't stand up on Earth") and by Liv's own life in
   Nominal's postscript. Keep the gravity, trim the apple-tree ritual.
4. **Denial-before-reveal (structural).** Arvo declares "His ring was clean"
   on 14 May and is overturned on 18 June. The "ear" joke and the Lap
   sections already tell the reader. The first reader says: "I knew he would
   find nothing." Cut the May audit to a paragraph, or have him find the
   pattern and misread it.
5. **Prior-art echoes the page should own.** The reply-or-withhold shape
   recalls *The Three-Body Problem* (Liu, 2008), confidence high, but the
   direction is reversed here, so the distance is real. Readers of *His
   Master's Voice* (Lem, 1968) will see the three rival readings in the hearing
   as its argument. The Quick Years and the distance-enforced patience echo
   the Long Now Clock project (real-world, Hillis/Brand, confidence high).
   None of these is a match. I did not search for a mind speaking through
   maintenance faults, so the symptom-as-mouth mechanism is "none I know of",
   low-to-medium confidence.
6. **Surveillance beat (line-level).** Nominal's postscript to Arvo ("forty-one
   unlatched letters") flirts with the excluded surveillance register. It
   is saved because Nominal declines to read them. Keep that restraint
   explicit.

## What to protect
- The two-column scene (Item 412 beside Lap 12,638): the one device here with
  no neighbour I can name.
- "We asked the stations how they felt. We never asked who was answering."
- The Tierce's "I note that I am the next ring in", with the self-interest
  showing under the reason.
- "...a Reply... answered one at a time, so most will be answered after
  their writers are dead", and Albano's "Put me down as my own residual
  addressee."
- Nominal arguing against its own paragraph without knowing it.

## Blind spot disclosure
I judge novelty and stock beats from memory and from a thin search, not
prose or pace. My reflex marks any warm AI voice as the banned beat, which
may be too harsh for a mind the story builds as a century-average, and I
likely under-credit how well documents can carry feeling. I did not check
every number in the lag tables.
