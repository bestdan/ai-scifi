## The one thing
Fix the causal chain behind the ending. The Tierce's whole vote turns on "it changed after being answered", and the draft dates that change before its cause.

## Notes

1. **Structural. The Tierce's letter (21 Feb 2132) vs Lap 13,680.** The opening changed on 26 Sept, "following ... ticket `LSL-WO-2131-0614` (Halme)". That ticket is dated 2 Oct. Lap 13,680 credits only Graça. Either move the change after 2 Oct or drop Arvo from the Tierce's list. Related: "Since June ... two hundred times" is about 82 laps, not 200.
2. **Structural. The chord rule is broken.** Nikolasi says "The Tierce read your remarks field when the two rings pooled in the chord." Hamid's rule is that nothing the Hum holds alone passes. The remarks field rides the outer ring's housekeeping channel and is Hum-only. Route it through Arvo's copy in the Chancery annex, or lose the line.
3. **Structural. The reveal is told three times.** Hamid on 18 June ("The bus measures the light passing through the bench"), the two-column page, and Hamid again on 19 July ("It holds its own gain profile..."). The bible wanted the reader to hold the two columns before any character does. Cut 18 June to "It *is* the ring", and let the two-column scene carry the mechanism.
4. **Structural. Two rules the plot needs are never stated.** (a) Why 5:4 and 31.25 AU, rather than any move? Nobody says the slow pair must keep a simple ratio to pool. The First Reader never understood it. (b) The strip clause, "the chief must state whether stripping ... restores the hull", first appears on 16 Aug, at the moment it traps Arvo. Plant it on 3 March or 14 May. The Hum's "surrender" of the Reply is a list item in the concordance, with no act by the Hum.
5. **Line. Count the numbers.**
   - The Hum's "three thousand four hundred and twelve mornings" does not fit "every day for twenty-two years" (about 8,000).
   - "typed six words" against "his usual four words".
   - "Two hundred and ninety thousand" pages on 3 March, against 114,600 on 26 Feb at about 10,000 a day.
   - "circumference shifts by meters inside the first hour", for a 1.25 AU move.
   - Hamid's "Mele's homework was ordinary" is patched by "a note the ring had already been holding", which breaks the fade rule.
6. **Line. Who knows what.** On 26 Feb and 3 Mar Nominal and the Tierce treat the Hum as the sender, with no evidence on the page. One line about style or the chord would do. The Hum's late answer addresses why the pages were early but not why the collectors were widened. "wrong that it was fixed" lost the First Reader.
7. **Line. Exposition in speeches.** Hamid, Teresa and Kalle deliver mechanism and history in long turns. Teresa's is earned (she has a grievance). Part III's three-theories debate reads as speakers assigned to ideas, and the First Reader could not tell who held which.
8. **Line. The opening.** "relay dishes south of Coimbra, bound for the inner ring at Mercury's distance" and Nominal's "the chord did not send it ... three selves have not come into step" stack three coined terms in a clause. Say "the Bell" and "Nominal" first. Hold "chord" until Lap 13,492.

## What to protect
- The fair-play seed: *Morning, 23. Keep warm.* planted in Arvo's first scene, then turned in the 14 Sept letter. The lap-date arithmetic is exact (Lap 13,722 to 13,843 is 131 days).
- The Tierce's "I note that I am the next ring in", with its undeclared stake visible.
- Chair Lindqvist's REC-16 note catching Nominal quoting itself, and Hamid's "He caught it!".
- Graça's two-column answer: "reading a letter that fell out of somebody else's pocket does not make you the one she loved."
- Albano's "Put me down as my own residual addressee until further notice, young man."

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh rules and sequence over feeling, and I like the explaining scenes the First Reader found slow. Discount note 7 if Le Guin or Lem defend them. I did not run the linter. I score no interiority beyond construction, and I may undervalue Graça's and Arvo's inner life.
