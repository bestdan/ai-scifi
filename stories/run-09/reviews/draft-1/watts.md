## The one thing
The Hum's warmth carries the story and is the one place its logic is shielded. Add a fourth reading, shaping, and let one voice (the Tierce, or Graça) say that a mind raised on consolation writes consolation by default. Leave it undecided.

## Notes

1. **Structural, the three readings (16 Aug):** 212 orders in 20 years, one per month, each answered by a bolted-on part with the crew as the lever, is operant shaping. "Reciprocity" and "drift" are that mechanism told as romance and as noise. Nobody needs to be writing. Yet "Thank you for the larger mirror at 23" is read as gratitude, and nobody asks whether "the wish at the foot of the pillar was the part that lasted" is the corpus's mean.

2. **Structural, retention (Hamid, 18 June):** "What is weak or routine washes out... It keeps what hurt." A uniform fade drops everything at one rate. Only repetition or amplitude separates survivors. Leonor's note was scanned once and copied never, and Arvo calls Mele's homework "ordinary". Something must weight extremity: an appraisal step, as arousal tags human consolidation (McGaugh 2004). Give the ring one. The ring has also run about 18,000 laps since the corpus, not "thirty-three thousand".

3. **Line, causal order:** the Tierce dates the new salutation from 26 September "following... ticket LSL-WO-2131-0614 (Halme)". That ticket is 2 October. Fix the date, or let the Tierce reason post hoc, which suits it. Likewise the Hum's "slope steepened toward certainty" over 412 entries, where Nominal's excerpts show a sawtooth that resets. The slope is the Hum's pattern-making, and the narration then praises it ("held its own mistake"). The first reader couldn't parse "wrong that it was fixed".

4. **Line, Nominal:** amnesic patients (H.M., Wearing) lose episodes and keep dispositions, so a week-mind repeats one argument weekly, as the 2126/2128/2131 entries do. Reversing 6 to 14 May is the wrong mechanism, though funny. Make it source amnesia, disowning its own text with the position unchanged. The second instance played flat for the first reader. Its P.S. knowing Liv's 2120 letter went unanswered needs the index named.

5. **Line, apples:** apples mostly need cross-pollination, and these set fruit with no bee or brush. "Full-spectrum sodium lamps" is wrong (orange, no "daytime spectrum"), and there is no chilling. "Degrees Kelvin." Arvo hand-pollinating is a small mail-by-hand beat.

6. **Line, projection as evidence:** Rui's confident "She would have... written back to say thank you" about a mother dead since 2031 becomes Graça's argument to the Board unquestioned.

7. **Out of lens:** Hamid logs "forty or fifty packets" per lap against "ten thousand a day." Egan should check.

## What to protect
- "The cold took her breath once, hard, behind the breastbone, before her shoulders found their regular stroke." This is habituated cold shock, correct.
- "waited ten seconds for his inner ears to forgive the corridor."
- Desmond: "I didn't write it to anybody. That was the point of writing it."
- Rui: "Why on earth would I lock a recipe for almond tart, Graça?"
- Nominal: "I cannot even check whether my predecessor... filed... out of pure principle or out of panic."
- Lap 13,680: "it cannot swallow a counterexample that carries the other half of the receipt." This is replay plus schema-consistent consolidation (Tse 2007), and it works.
- Arvo folding the slip away and "did not correct it."

## Blind spot disclosure
I push the sceptical reading against a story whose power is a warm misreading. Le Guin and Banks may rightly hold it. I am weak on laser links and orbital mechanics, and the Triton sky and season were not audited. The rate point in note 7 is hers to confirm.
