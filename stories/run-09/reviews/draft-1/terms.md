# Terms: run-09, draft 1

From the bible's Glossary:
- **the Bell**: the whole three-ring mind everyone writes to.
- **Nominal**: the inner, fast self; answers within the hour, remembers a week.
- **the Tierce**: the middle self; answers in days, remembers a decade.
- **the Hum**: the outer, slow self; answers no one directly, remembers a century.
- **lap**: one trip of light around a ring; one step of the Bell's thought.
- **the chord**: the Bell's selves pooling and sounding together; its considered view.
- **the Reply**: the Hum's promised answer to humanity, due 2157.
- **Dear You**: how letters to the Bell begin.

Used in the draft but missing from the Glossary:
- **slate**: a personal writing and messaging device.
- **latch / closed**: marking writing so the Bell won't read it.
- **spin hour**: daily time in a centrifuge to resist low gravity.
- **long ring / week-mind / decade-mind / century-mind**: informal names for the outer ring and for the three selves.
- **pele**: a Tongan term of endearment a father uses for his daughter.
- **Doutora / Senhor / Senhora / Mãe / Vovó / Pai**: Portuguese forms of address (Doctor, Mr, Mrs, Mother, Grandma, Dad).
- **folar**: a Portuguese Easter bread.
