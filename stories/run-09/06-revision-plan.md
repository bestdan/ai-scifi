## Consensus (fixed)

1. **The causal chain behind the ending (Asimov, Watts).** The Tierce's last
   letter says the salutation changed from 26 September "following" Graça's
   packet and ticket `LSL-WO-2131-0614`, but that ticket is dated 2 October.
   Credit the September change to Graça's letter alone, as Lap 13,680
   already does. The Tierce adds that from October the Hum's fragments to
   Lassell also answered Arvo's remarks. Asimov's companion fix: Nikolasi's
   line "The Tierce read your remarks field when the two rings pooled"
   breaks the chord rule, because the remarks are Hum-only. The Tierce read
   them because Arvo put his 2 October remarks into the Chancery record
   himself, as part of his full disclosure. Say so in scene 20 and at the
   Tierce's letter. Also fix Lap 13,680's "Since June ... two hundred
   times": from 28 June to 25 September is about 82 laps.
2. **Cut the hearing to its bones and put its weight in rooms (Chiang, Le
   Guin, the cold reader).** Draft 1's hearing (2,082 words, seven docket
   documents) runs the same trade three times. Draft 2 keeps it to about
   1,100 words and at most three documents:
   - Desmond's testimony, with "I didn't write it to anybody. That was the
     point of writing it.";
   - Lindqvist's minute naming the circularity, with the Reach line;
   - the vote, 3–2.

   Cut NOM-24, Nominal's second self-contradiction: it repeats 14 May. Cut
   REC-01's procedural rules, keeping only the query to the Hum, in a
   sentence. Cut the cathedral-organ aside. Arvo's sworn answer becomes one
   line in the record ("The first burn is the irreversible act."), not a
   document. The circularity is dramatised in the Lassell trim room, where
   Hamid reads it off the platen ("He caught it!") and says it plainly (see
   item 11). Graça's testimony moves into her own scene (item 6).
3. **The reveal is told three times (Asimov, Chiang, Cartographer).**
   - Scene 13 (18 June) keeps Hamid's timing and "It *is* the ring", the
     drone footage with Arvo's stencilled name, and "Fault codes. That's all
     it's ever said to us." Cut Hamid's mechanism lines there ("The bus
     measures the light passing through the bench" and the air-gap
     exchange). End the scene on the stencil and Arvo's silence.
   - The two-column scene carries the mechanism for the reader.
   - Scene 16 (19 July) keeps Hamid's explanation of the symptom, trimmed
     by about a third.
   - Compress the 14 May audit (scene 12) to about 600 words. Keep the
     inspection rule (each order against its own log, never the sum), the
     2128 aperture ticket checked and found honest, the strip clause (item
     10), and Nominal's 6 May/14 May contradiction with "was in a hurry". Cut
     the ticket-by-ticket walk and the "His ring was clean" coda. The
     Cartographer's alternative (have him see a pattern and misread it) is
     taken in small: he notices that the sunward arc ate three times the
     parts, and puts it down to solar wind, as Bea's canteen joke did.
4. **The Hum speaks in one consoling-oracle voice (Cartographer, Watts).**
   Every fragment ends on a consolation ("The light does not grow tired",
   "the metal has kept warm", "the wish at the foot of the pillar was the
   part that lasted"). Give the Hum at least two fragments that are wrong,
   cold or oddly literal.
   - The new fragment to Desmond (item 7) is one.
   - Mele's fragment gets one literal, unsettling sentence. Keep the 38
     kelvin and the mirrors, and cut "nobody answers them for a hundred
     years" as too knowing.
   Then let one character say what this means, and leave it undecided. The
   Tierce, in its last letter, or Graça, in her scene: a mind raised on
   consolation writes consolation by default, so its kindness proves
   nothing about whether it understands.
5. **Huda's blank TO: must support two specific readings (Cartographer,
   the cold reader's "who is Huda writing to?").** Before she uncaps the
   pen, put both candidates on the table in front of her, concretely:
   - the Box 4 basket and its Babylon I label for the dead drafter, a clerk
     writing back across thirty-eight centuries to another clerk who set
     tariffs;
   - the open outbound header to the outer ring.

   Give her one line of thought for each, a sentence each, with no
   resolution. The routing line stays empty. Don't add a third.
6. **Graça is absent from April to March, and never learns her letter
   mattered (Le Guin; the cold reader cared most about her and waited for
   her).** Add a Porto scene in November, about 700 words, in place of her
   docket testimony. She is at her kitchen table, or at the Reply Office
   with Albano, when the first fragment with the new salutation reaches
   Porto, or when the papers report that the change followed "a letter
   from Porto". She writes her short testimony to the Board there, and the
   key lines of draft 1's EX-19 go into her mouth or onto her slate:
   "When an old patient in my dental surgery misheard a question, I did not
   extract his tongue; I told him he had misheard." She knows that she was
   the counterexample. She is not sure whether that was a kindness. Keep it
   small.
7. **The people who paid most vanish (Le Guin).** Add a short Tharsis scene
   in December, about 600 words: Desmond receives a second fragment, now
   opening "You did not write to me. I read it." It is the cold, literal
   one of item 4. It doesn't console; it may be accurate. Pieter is or
   isn't at the table. The scene shows that the new salutation heals
   nothing by itself, and that the harm can recur under the concordance.
   No reconciliation.
8. **Comprehension: Terms missed on "the chord" (grader; lost-list 2).**
   Make the chord plain at its first real use, in scene 7. Say it in one
   plain sentence, in narration or in Arvo's head, not a gloss in
   apposition: every five and a half days the middle and outer rings come
   into step and pool what they hold, what both hold goes inward as the
   Bell's considered voice, and people call that the chord. Re-touch it
   once, in plain words, at the hearing.
9. **Comprehension: why 5:4, and what the "thirds" are (Asimov 4a; grader's
   Terms note; lost-list 5).** In scene 7, Nominal's letter or Arvo's reply
   says once, plainly:
   - The slow pair can only pool if their laps keep a simple ratio, so the
     founders' rules allow a retune only to another simple ratio.
   - 6:5 is what bellfounders call a minor third. The nearest other simple
     ratio the sealed reserve can reach is 5:4, the major third, which
     means moving the outer ring to 31.25 AU.
   - Any move at all ends the Hum, because its memory is the timing.

   Keep the arithmetic out of dialogue beyond that.
10. **Plant the strip clause early (Asimov 4b).** Under Subsection 4, a
    chief who finds a hull out of its mass envelope because of depot
    modifications must state whether stripping them restores flight trim.
    Plant it in scene 7, among the conditions Arvo reads, or in the
    compressed scene 12. In August it then springs as a trap the reader
    already holds.
11. **Comprehension: the circularity, and "right that a request would come,
    wrong that it was fixed" (lost-list 9, 10).** Say it plainly once, in
    Hamid's mouth in the trim room after Lindqvist's minute: the Hum sent
    the letters early to beat a petition, and the letters are what made
    Nominal file; it was right that a request might come, and wrong that one
    was coming anyway. In scene 26, Arvo's reading of the Hum's late answer
    uses the same plain words, and cuts "the story an old mind tells
    itself" sentence to one clause.
12. **Comprehension: Premise partial (the addressee belief, and whether a
    non-addressee may answer) — and Chiang's one thing.** In scene 20, when
    Arvo writes "She wasn't writing to the sky" in the remarks field, he sees

## Adopted single-lens notes

18. **Retention needs a weight on extremity (Watts).** A uniform fade can't
    select "what hurt". Leonor's note was scanned once and never copied, so
    something must have weighted it. Add one plain line from Hamid in scene
    13: when the founders loaded the corpus, they tagged each text by how
    much it had cost its writer, so grief, rage and prayer were stored
    louder and fade slower. The Quick Years mind had kept what was useful;
    the founders wanted the Hum to keep what mattered. Also fix "What
    survives thirty-three thousand laps across a hundred years": the ring
    has run about 17,000 laps since the corpus was fed, so say "what will
    survive".
19. **The motive readings: drift becomes shaping (Watts).** Make the
    Tierce's reading, as Kalle quotes it, sharper: each ache the ring made
    was rewarded with a part, so the manual trained it, the way you teach a
    dog. That is drift with a mechanism. Keep three readings, each with a
    holder.
20. **Hamid's packet count (Watts, for Egan).** "forty or fifty packets per
    lap" contradicts ten thousand a day. Make it about ten thousand packets
    per lap's eleven-minute window.
21. **The apple trees (Watts).** Fruit with no pollinator: Arvo
    hand-pollinates with a paintbrush in March (one sentence, in scene 3 or
    12). "Full-spectrum sodium lamps" is wrong; use "grow lamps", and keep
    sodium only for the streetlamps.
22. **Nikolasi's faith turns a scene (Le Guin 5).** After the triangulation,
    give him one short beat, about 300 words: the Thursday chapel, or Mele.
    In it he says something about the fragments now that they come from the
    stores, and it is not despair. It can be small: he reads Psalm 90 again
    and stops on a different verse. Cut "the heavy Polynesian calm that
    usually sat on his shoulders like a cape" (ethnic shorthand) and show
    how he is instead.
23. **"pele" (Le Guin 6; Terms).** Use the plain word, or make it clear from
    context in the same line ("his Tongan pet name for her"). The
    Portuguese forms of address (Doutora, Senhor, Mãe, Vovó, Pai) are clear
    from context and stay. Cut "folar", or let the sentence say it is Easter
    bread.
24. **Routing blocks are one register (Chiang).** The cold reader skipped
    them by the fifth. Keep full blocks on the Hum's fragments, on Graça's
    and Pieter's letters, and on Huda's final dispatch. For the
    institutional letters (Nominal, the Tierce, the Chancery), cut each
    block to two lines (FROM/TO, and RECEIVED with the lag). That matters
    more after item 2's cuts.
