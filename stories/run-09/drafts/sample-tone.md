# Tone of sample.md

The line that set it:

> The trade had left her fond of one-sided conversations and permanently unsentimental about what they were worth.

It was the first place I felt the voice smile at her. It is dry and affectionate at once, and it made me trust that the strange mail further down would be met with a shrug and then a pang, not alarm.
