## Evidence

The brief's Tone is "procedural and wondering, with warmth: an institution's working voice, the thrill of a hard problem at scale, and people who care how it turns out." The bible's Register line adds that the humour is "the humour of mail".

The cold reader gave three words: **wry, tender, uncanny**. Set against the brief:

- *Warmth* lands as "tender". The reader named the line about dentistry and "one-sided conversations", which is warmth with a smile in it.
- *Wondering* lands as "uncanny". A letter four light-hours out, addressed to a dead woman, answering a question about lemons and a house in Viseu, produces the uncanny directly. The idea makes the feeling, which is what I look for.
- *Procedural* does not appear in the three words. It is on the page in the docket blocks, the postage line and the light-lag clocks. It is only the third thing the reader noticed.
- "Wry" is not in the brief, but the bible's "humour of mail" covers it. I count it as in register rather than a drift.

The form is doing work. The forms and headers are the institution speaking, and the human scene around them is the warmth. I would not tell a conventional narrator to take over.

## Where it could slip, no more than three lines

1. **"Since the 2080s the Bell had treated every unlatched page in human history as mail that had simply taken a long route to its desk."** This is the best line in the sample for procedure and wonder together. Narration then spends the "wry" voice on the dentist and the glazier. As the story goes on, the institutional voice has to come from the Bell's side too: forms, rulings and the working habits of the people who run the relay. A narrator who is only wry will not carry the thrill of a problem at scale.

2. **Nominal's closing paragraph: "our three rings have not come into step to pool an answer since Tuesday evening… the chord did not send it either… a ring with no mouth just mailed your grandmother."** The ring, chord and "no mouth" terms arrive in one paragraph, and the reader has had no scene that explains any of them. Procedure here reads as jargon rather than a working voice. The situation needs one plain statement of what a ring is and what the chord does, in terms of what Graça can see on her slate. Then the seven-day memory line, which is the wonder, will land as a fact and not a puzzle. This is a clarity risk under style-guide rules 3 and 9, not a tone miss.

3. **"…far beyond the inner ring where daily answers came from."** The sentence is mild and mixes two distances. The lag is the story's sublime. The scene needs one figure Graça can feel: how long a letter to the outer ring should take, against the four hours this one did. As written, the reader has to do the sums, and the wonder gets delayed.

## One cut

The glazier and the skylight carry the "street writes to the sky" image, and that image is useful. The sour-starter and pleurisy list that follows is the one cuttable stretch in the opening paragraphs. It lists everyday uses, and the reader already has the image.

## Verdict

The tone is on brief. Proceed to the full draft. Carry notes 2 and 3 to the drafter as clarity points. The risk to watch at stage 7 is the wry narrator crowding out the institutional voice in the middle sections.
