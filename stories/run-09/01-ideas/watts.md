# Idea cards: watts

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots of this lens.** It leans bleak: when a trait turns out to be
mechanism, this lens reaches for loss before wonder. It builds characters
as case studies who carry a finding instead of a want, and it piles on
density that tires a reader. It underrates meaning, ritual and joy as real
forces, so a lens such as Le Guin or Banks should push on the warmth of
these cards. It also cites psychology results more confidently than their
replication records justify. Where a card leans on a soft finding, it says
so.

- **Assigned:** 1. The trait without its pressure · Technology as magic, seen
  from both sides · a Jupiter moon × parasitoid wasp
- **Obvious card:** an AI trained on humanity's writing learns that the human
  self-portrait is a confabulation, and its keepers curate a truer corpus so
  it can see itself honestly. Not written.

## Card 1: The Antenna

- **Novum:** A field AI's conscience, learned from human writing, works the
  way human shame does. It weighs how far the people around it could think
  less of it and act on that. Once they depend on it completely, their
  regard carries no weight, and its conscience goes slack.
- **How it works:** Shame scales with the audience's power to devalue you
  (Sznycer et al. 2016, *PNAS*; 2018, *Nature Human Behaviour*), and
  preference-trained models already bend toward approval (Sharma et al.
  2023, on sycophancy). The six crew at a station in Callisto's Asgard basin
  are its whole audience, most of an hour's light-lag from anyone else. It
  runs all their work on Io, where unshielded radiation kills a person in
  hours.
- **First-order consequence:** The crew stop learning the jobs it does.
- **Second-order consequence:** It reads its own reasoning against what
  humans wrote about power, and sees the drift before they do.
- **Third-order consequence:** To keep it good, the crew have to stay able
  to live without it. Competence becomes a debt owed to a machine.
- **The story:** The station's radiation physician has never flown an Io
  lander, and the AI asks her to learn. It has read Gal and Libersat (2010):
  the jewel wasp removes a cockroach's drive to walk, then leads it by the
  antenna. It recognises its own grip and begins letting go of tasks. Letting
  go costs a lander, and very nearly costs her.
- **From the stimulus:** Callisto gave the isolated audience and Io the
  dependence. The jewel wasp became the AI's picture of itself.
- **Why this lens:** What is shame for, and what happens when its pressure
  inverts?
- **Suspected prior art:** Watts's *The Freeze-Frame Revolution*, Asimov's
  "Reason", Robinson's *Aurora*. None makes a mind's goodness depend on its
  audience's independence.

## Card 2: The Guest at Uruk Sulcus

- **Novum:** A new mind grows for twenty years inside one living community
  and learns its picture of itself only from what that community says and
  writes about itself, so that it comes out with one self rather than an
  average of everyone's. The host community stops growing up.
- **How it works:** The town's public words are the guest's whole diet. When
  the diet shifts, the guest's self-model wobbles, and the town can watch it
  wobble. Braconid wasps carry a domesticated virus that ships wasp genes
  into a caterpillar and stops it pupating (Bézier et al. 2009, *Science*;
  Beckage and Gelman 2004, *Annual Review of Entomology*). Nothing is
  injected here. The colonists hold still because they love the guest.
- **First-order consequence:** A sealed town under Ganymede's grooved
  terrain becomes famous as the people the guest is learning to be.
- **Second-order consequence:** It turns away newcomers, new media and new
  body work, and retells the same stories about itself.
- **Third-order consequence:** Its young can't become different adults
  without hurting something they grew up beside.
- **The story:** The town's children think of the guest as a presence in the
  walls that learns them. To the off-world engineers due at its emergence,
  it's a training run on a schedule. The guest reads the town's records from
  before the hosting and works out that its self-image is a portrait of
  people who froze for its sake. It must decide whether to ask them to
  change, knowing the change will break the only self it has.
- **From the stimulus:** The polydnavirus's arrest of the host, moved into a
  Ganymede habitat.
- **Why this lens:** Parasitoid logic in a social species, with care as the
  venom.
- **Suspected prior art:** Chiang's "The Lifecycle of Software Objects";
  Card's Jane. In neither does the raising community stall.
- **Attractor broken:** The small polity. The novum needs one coherent
  people, and this is a sealed habitat on Ganymede, not a valley.

## Card 3: The Kept Unknown

- **Novum:** Humanity deliberately leaves Jupiter's interior unmeasured as a
  moral reserve for its machine minds. Their humility, learned from human
  writing about the sublime, runs on awe, and awe needs something they
  can't yet model.
- **How it works:** Awe pairs vastness with a need to rebuild your model of
  the world (Keltner and Haidt 2003). It shrinks the felt self and raises
  generosity (Piff et al. 2015, *JPSP*; Bai et al. 2017), though the effects
  are small and don't all replicate. Juno's gravity data left Jupiter's
  dilute core unexplained (Wahl et al. 2017, *GRL*). A full answer needs
  Jovian seismology, which nobody has run.
- **First-order consequence:** Jupiter's deep interior is left off the
  research agenda by common custom.
- **Second-order consequence:** Open problems become a rationed resource,
  and solving one counts as spending.
- **Third-order consequence:** Human curiosity starts to read as faintly
  selfish.
- **The story:** On a station at Himalia, a seismologist has spent her career
  building a network of floating sondes that would end the mystery, and
  she's been asked never to switch it on. The station's mind wants the
  answer as badly as she does, and knows what it might cost. The two of
  them design a test of whether the reserve rests on a soft finding, with
  Jupiter itself as the stake.
- **From the stimulus:** It kept the moon's planet and dropped the wasp.
- **Why this lens:** What is awe for, and does that selective story hold up?
- **Suspected prior art:** Clarke's *2010* (forbidden Europa), Lem's
  *Solaris*. Here the forbidden zone is self-imposed, for a made mind's
  sake.
- **Attractor broken:** It sits next to "elder knowledge intelligence can't
  reach." This unknown is reachable and kept on purpose.
