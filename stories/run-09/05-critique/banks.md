# Stage 5 critique: Banks lens (run-09, outline)

**Blind spots, stated:** I favour set pieces and scale, and I'm the lens most
likely to bury a novella under them. Chiang and Asimov should cut anything I
add below. I can't judge fair-play clue order well; that's Asimov's. Every fix
here swaps for an existing scene's content, not an extra scene.

1. **[must-fix] Scale is stated, never stood in.** The outline has numbers
   (a day and two hours, 188 AU, eight and a half hours' lag) but no scene
   where a body meets the vastness. The bible's best image, "a station's mirror
   the size of a house that nobody has seen in thirty years", appears only in
   the interlude (scene 2), where it is the Hum's sense, not a human's. Bea
   "flew most of the relay installations" and is never shown flying one. Fix:
   in scene 13, send Bea and a drone to station 23 (a multi-day flight, so the
   waiting is part of it). Arvo watches the feed. The mirror arrives out of the
   dark, a new 2.4 m aperture bolted to a thirty-year-old frame, with his own
   handwriting on the stores tag. That is the scale moment, and it plants the
   twist physically before scene 14.

2. **[must-fix] Part II's middle repeats one mechanism.** Scenes 7, 9 and 12
   are each Arvo reading a letter from a self that argues the case (Nominal,
   the Tierce, Nominal again). Scene 12 only restates 7 with "argues with its
   last Monday". Arvo only receives. The part's question (may a non-addressee
   answer) is already carried by 7, 10 and 11. Fix: merge scene 12's
   dossier-argument and Liv material into scene 7, and drop the daily letters.
   Use the freed ~1,200 words so that Arvo acts: he pulls the 2109-2129 logs
   himself in Part II, so the triangulation is something he half-avoids rather
   than something Nikolasi hands him.

3. **[should] The wit lives only at the edges.** Reply Office and Basra are
   funny; scenes 7, 9, 16 and 20, nearly 9,000 words, are procedure with
   dread. Nominal ("fast, warm") and the Tierce (grudging about a 2126 refused
   order) are natural comic voices, and Lindqvist's "game with rules" is
   unused. Fix: give the Tierce a postage-due pettiness in scene 9, give
   Nominal one cheerful self-ambush in scene 7, and put a misdelivery in the
   hearing (a Triton-bound question reaching Ceres a lag early).

4. **[should] Who's missing: the people below.** Graça vanishes from scene 11
   to scene 22, and Bea and Mele go after Part I and III. The Basra clerk
   whose reading ends the story appears nowhere before the last page, so the
   final image lands cold. Fix: give the clerk a line in scenes 4, 13 and 21,
   each a new misdelivered fragment crossing her desk, so that scene 24's
   reading closes a thread. Give Graça's posted testimony one beat in Part III
   (her draft, torn up).

5. **[could] Ordering after the climax.** Scenes 21 to 24 hold four endings
   in about 4,600 words, and 23 and 24 both answer someone late. Reordering:
   put Arvo's letter to Liv (23) before the ruling, so the ruling is the cost
   he pays after his private act, and end on 22 then 24. The twist's
   reveal (15) is also told thrice (14, 15, 17). Let 14 carry the mechanics and
   trim 15 to Nikolasi's dated list and Hamid's line.
