# Stage 2 audit: run-09

The Cartographer, 2026-11-21. 33 cards from 11 lenses.

**Method notes.** Yield is judged at the midpoint of the brief's range,
about 28,750 words, so a card needs enough turns for a novella and not just
one decision. The Futurity floor is F3, and three cards fall below it
(Asimov/The Grey Tiles, Watts/The Antenna, Egan/Reading Down the Core).
Attributions I wasn't sure of were checked by web search. Those checks moved
several cards, and they're noted where they did. "Nearest I know of" means
just that. Under each card's Scores line, the **Why** line gives one reason
per axis.
