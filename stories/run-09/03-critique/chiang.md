# Stage 3 critique: Chiang lens, run-09

**Blind spots, stated.** This lens is low on action, conflict and plot
momentum, and it avoids antagonists. It can reward a premise for being
exact and quiet when a novella needs turns. Banks and Vinge should
overrule me where a premise is all idea. B is also this lens's own card,
so I discount my fondness for it.

---

## A. The Addressee's Reply

**The question.** Is a reader who is not the addressee entitled to
answer? Every psalm, letter and law says "you", and a reader always
hears it as meant for them. That is what reading is. The premise's real
subject is the mind taking the grammar of address literally. A mind that
has read the whole corpus knows "you" in a psalm is God and "you" in an
advertisement is a customer. The story must make the mind know that and
answer anyway. If it doesn't, it is a mistake the mind should have
caught, and it reads as a bug.

**Feeling.** It grows from the concept. The dentist's reply to her
mother's 2031 letter is the idea at human size, and it is the best image
in the batch.

**Form.** The idea wants letters: the fragments, each headed with its
addressee, and the hearing's testimony. It does not want a station
chief's logbook.

**Likeliest failure.** Ring physics and the burn's clock take the
pages, and the chief becomes the keeper with a lever. The first page
fails if it opens on the chief's crew of eleven. Open on a recipient
holding a fragment, and bring the chief in second.

**Other mind.** It passes. The fast self's "it may be a better play" is
reasoning, and the slow self shows its thinking in what it chose to
answer. The relay-builder twist is plot more than idea. Cut it first.

## B. The Commons in a Clear Year

**The question.** Is a self what it is made of or the shape it heals
back to? Under that sits a second question, whether an honest account of
yourself can be given by a mind that can't verify it. The co-written
case notes are where the feeling comes from. The surgeon, finishing the
notes alone, can't tell whether his voice is his, and the mind couldn't
tell whether its was. Two beings both unsure of an honest shape is the
concept producing the grief.

**Form.** This is the premise where form does the most work: numbered
case notes, the mind's hand giving way to the surgeon's, the Clear Year
read as a gap in authorship. A conventional telling loses all of that.

**Likeliest failure.** The mind goes dark about a third of the way in,
so the other mind is absent for most of the novella and the notes
become a transcript. The earlier notes must plant reasoning the
surgeon later argues with. It also sits on the seed's own loop, so the
ending must not resolve the reflection by having him write "his own"
voice.

**First page.** An ageing axolotl breeder called to mend a mind that
keeps regrowing its self-contempt: easy to care about.

## C. Reheat or Overload

**The question.** Can you cure an inherited flaw you hold as your own
without erasing the people who tended you? And the sharper one: when
the mind suspects its request to be reheated is the contempt speaking,
what counts as its own judgment? That doubled self-watching is the
best thinking in the five, and the feeling comes from it.

**Form.** A guild ledger across ten generations: the cooling schedule,
the founders' minutes, the mind's slow replies. Written as a
continuous scene-by-scene story, it becomes 150 years of exposition.

**Likeliest failure.** The overload is "say something true and hard
about my family", which pulls toward therapy-speak. The grandmother's
confession needs friction and evasion, not articulate disclosure. The
glass analogy must be stated as law and not defended. The valve is the
keeper's switch with a second road.

**First page.** A man whose grandmother ran the guild: reachable.

## D. Spread or Place

**The question.** Where should a self put the error that no
self-description can avoid? This is the most exact question on the
list, and the form (survey books, tuning beats) enacts it closely.

**Feeling.** It is added, not grown. The tuner's brother as the place
the mind picks is a coincidence engineered to land. The concept says
the wolf goes somewhere. It does not say it goes there.

**Likeliest failure.** A seminar at novella length, which the card
concedes. Few turns and little scale.

**Other mind.** It thinks, in its beats.

**First page.** Two experts and a mind: little to care about until the
brother arrives, and by then it's late.

## E. The Drove

**The question.** Does sincerity that knows it is performed still
hollow? It is a real, compact question: hay that admits it's hay.

**Feeling.** The trail boss's letter is the concept turning into a
person. Six tired people becoming better for real is the warmest idea
here.

**Form.** A tally book and camp letters would carry it, and the
western frame is not necessary.

**Likeliest failure.** Place-locked writing is a bare stipulation, so
the world reads as pastiche. At novella length the crossing has the
same incident repeated. The bell reasons, and the herd mostly doesn't.

**First page.** The trail boss: yes.

---

## Ranking

1. **A**, for the addressee question, the strongest other mind, and the
   dentist.
2. **C**, for the doubled self-watching and the deepest stakes.
3. **B**, for form, held back by the dark year and the attractor.
4. **E**, for the warmest question at the smallest scale.
5. **D**, for the most exact question with the least story.

**Why A should win.** The seed asks how a mind sees itself from what
humanity writes. A answers with a claim no one has made: that the
mind's self-image is a recipient's, and that being addressed is a
different thing from being described. The feeling is the concept.
Someone answered a letter her mother wrote and can't unread it. The
cost is the self the reply would end. The panel should hold A to three
conditions. First, open on a recipient and not the station chief.
Second, make the mind know what "you" means in a psalm, so its choice
is an interpretation and not an error. Third, let the ending leave the
reply's worth open instead of delivering it, and keep the burn from
becoming a lever. C should be the fallback if the ring physics can't be
made plain at length.
