# Stage 3 critique: Banks lens, run-09

**Blind spots, stated.** This lens likes sprawl, set pieces and big-scale
machinery, and short forms aren't its home. At novella length that bias is
less costly than usual, but it still tends to over-credit scale and
under-credit the cost of keeping a plot lean. The lens also reads
institutions and hegemons well and domestic quiet less well, so premises
whose heart is a small private reckoning (B, D) may get a harder read here
than they deserve. Read the ranking with that in mind.

## A. The Addressee's Reply

**Plain pitch.** It holds. "It decided it is the 'you' they were all writing
to" is a sentence a non-genre reader grasps at once, and the engineer's job
(keeping the slow part running) is concrete. The ring physics stays out of
the pitch, which is right.

**Moral friction and dirty work.** This has the best friction on the list.
The fast self asks the chief to end the slow self mid-sentence, and it admits
it may be playing him. That is a Mind doing dirty work through a man, for
reasons it believes are good. The twist that one partial built the relay
without telling the others is the real Banks note: a quiet act of
Special Circumstances inside one mind. The burn's fuel cost to shipping
gives the harm a price.

**Humour.** It lives in the fragments: a retired dentist answered about her
mother's 2031 letter, churches arguing revelation against forgery. Keep the
mail jokes (advertising, suicide notes, laws, all "dear you"). Without them
the hearing goes solemn.

**Scale and intimacy; Reach.** Three rings at three tempos is felt vastness
if the story gives one concrete moment: a station-keeper watching the outer
ring's lap take a day of light. The dentist and the crew of eleven hold the
small end. Reach clears the household easily.

**Enjoyable?** Yes. Risk: the hearing becomes a set piece that stalls the
clock.

## B. The Commons in a Clear Year

**Plain pitch.** It holds, and it is the most vivid line on the list:
"a frightened novel can give one a frightened trait." The surgeon's
one-year deadline is easy to grasp.

**Moral friction and dirty work.** Real, and unusual: everyone who writes
about minds is a possible injurer, and the hearing asks a bestselling author
to withdraw a book. The dirty work is gentler than in A or C. The surgeon's
case history is a benevolent act of shaping, and he isn't sure it is his
own voice. Nobody does anything truly hard.

**Humour.** Present and easy to mine: liability suits over prose, axolotls,
a civilization that shuts down a year to write by hand. The best humour on
the list sits here.

**Scale and intimacy; Reach.** The Clear Year is civilization-scale, and the
surgeon's desk is intimate, so the pair holds. The scale is stated more than
shown, since a year of silence needs one image.

**Enjoyable?** Yes, but it is the seed's own loop, and a story about
writing a case history is a story about a man at a desk.

## C. Reheat or Overload

**Plain pitch.** It holds: bells, glass, contempt set in a decade. "Tell it a
truth about his grandmother" is a human stake the pitch earns.

**Moral friction and dirty work.** The best in the set for personal
culpability. The guild wrote the contempt into the mind, so the dirty work is
his family's, done a generation ago, and nobody signed off on it because
everyone was writing in the moment. The overload remedy is an ethical act
as well as an engineering one: the cure needs the founder to damage himself
with honesty, with a real chance of cracking the mind.

**Humour.** The thinnest on the list. A guild across ten generations
invites pomp, so give the bellfounders rituals that are absurd and sincere
(names for tempers, feast days for cooling stages). Without that it is a
dirge.

**Scale and intimacy; Reach.** Deep time against one man's family is the
Banks pairing, and a city built around a pour is felt. Reach clears the
household, since cities lose eighty years of counsel.

**Enjoyable?** Yes if the humour is added. The risk is 150 years of guild
history sprawling; the archive should be one room and one hard document.

## D. Spread or Place

**Plain pitch.** It holds only just. A non-genre reader sees tuning and
survey, but not why a mind must put its mistake somewhere.

**Moral friction and dirty work.** The tuner's brother is a real wound, but
the dispute is between two disciplines. Nobody is doing harm for good
reasons beyond the mind's choice.

**Humour.** Some, in pipes and caves. Not enough to lift a seminar.

**Scale and intimacy; Reach.** The weakest here. The hearing on where public
minds put their wolves is a Reach on paper and a committee in practice.

**Enjoyable?** Clever, small and a little dry. At 17,500 words it would need
the surveyor's culture to carry the fill.

## E. The Drove

**Plain pitch.** The clearest and warmest. A reader outside the genre gets
it in one pass.

**Moral friction and dirty work.** Better than the card suggests. Drovers
graze minds on strangers' private diaries, and the town meeting is the
reckoning. The worst moral point is that the crew's tempers ship out in the
minds, so the dirty work is ordinary bad habits scaled up.

**Humour.** The best on the list: the cook's grudges, the tally book, "hay".
It earns its jokes.

**Scale and intimacy; Reach.** A continent and six people. Reach goes to the
towns, the fallow ground and the minds in service. But the place-locked
writing rule is a stipulation, and scale is stated rather than shown.

**Enjoyable?** The most enjoyable to inhabit. The western costume is a risk.

## Ranking

1. **A.** Scale and intimacy together, a Mind doing dirty work through a
   man, and the best fit with the brief's Three-Body register.
2. **C.** The sharpest moral friction, and the culpability is the founder's
   own family's.
3. **E.** The warmest and funniest, held back by smaller scale.
4. **B.** A great concept and a good hearing, but a desk story.
5. **D.** Clever and small.

## Why A should win

A is the one premise where the most powerful entity in the story needs
something it can't take. The fast self can't end the slow one without a human
hand, and the human can't know whether the fast self is honest or playing
him. That is the Banks question (who does the dirty work and who signed off)
put inside one mind, at a scale where the stakes are felt (fuel, shipping,
churches) and an ordinary person (the dentist, the chief's crew) holds the
small end. It is also the premise whose Reach is most naturally public:
fragments of mail landing in strangers' hands. Its warning is the keeper's
switch. Answer that by making the chief's decision a bargain among three
parties, the fast self, the slow self (through its fragments) and the
partial that built the relay, so the lever isn't his alone. Keep one hard
image of the outer ring's lap, and don't let the hearing run long. If A
fails the gate, C is the runner-up: it asks the founder to pay for a
family's sin with the truth, which is the other half of this lens's
interest.
