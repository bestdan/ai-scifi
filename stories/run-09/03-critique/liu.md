# Stage 3 critique: Liu lens, run-09

**Blind spots, stated.** I read premises as games and machines, so I will
undervalue interiority, especially the women characters, and I drift toward
bleak logic. Le Guin should argue for the lived-in society and Chiang for the
feeling that comes from the idea. I have checked consequence and scale, not
whether any of these people are pleasant company.

## A. The Addressee's Reply

**Game:** the chief can verify the fast self (it talks) and cannot verify the
slow one (its reply is unreadable until it is due). One side's honesty is
checkable only by its own testimony, which is a clean verification gap. The
irreversible move is the burn, on evidence that is all one-sided. **Sublime:**
three rings of relay stations, a delay line one light-lap long, forty stations
moved outward. That is the right size. **Cost:** ending the slow self
mid-sentence is paid in full if the story lets the burn happen or fails to
stop it. It flinches if the early fragments simply reassure. The relay one
partial built is the premise's best move in the game: a commitment device,
and the story should treat it as one. **Deep time:** forty years of
composing, twenty-six to go. Crew succession must carry it, or it is a
header. **Check:** "a delay line one light-lap long" and "the century" don't
yet agree. A lap at Neptune is about a day, so say what makes the outer
ring's tempo a century.

## B. The Commons in a Clear Year

**Game:** thin for one man at a desk. The strategic part is the Lem twist,
an instrument (the unaided-writing sample) nobody can verify is still
clean. The surgeon's case history is the one move, and it is unforced: who
else could do it? **Sublime:** a civilization going dark every eleventh
year. That is a large and patient thing, and it sits in the Reach, not the
plot. **Cost:** he risks his belief, which is paid privately and so does not
move the scale. The logic stops at the comfortable point if he writes
honestly and the template takes it. Follow it: a clean sample might heal the
mind toward something worse. **Deep time:** felt through generations named by
Clear Years, which is good. Make the burden a rival: someone else writing the
reference year, with a motive.

## C. Reheat or Overload

**Game:** the strongest on the list. The mind can't verify the founder's
motive and the founder can't verify the mind's request, since either could
be the contempt layer speaking. Two irreversible moves, a reheat that costs
eighty years and every memory of the tenders, or an overload that may crack
the glass. **Sublime:** a cathedral-sized glass cooling 240 years under a
guild of ten generations. This is the engineer's sublime, and the schedule
outlives every person who keeps it. **Cost:** the founder must say something
true about his grandmother and the guild must open its archive. The logic
flinches if the overload works cleanly. Let it blunt the crack and leave a
mark, or crack part of it. **Deep time:** felt, because the guild's
procedures survive and its people do not. Watch the glass physics: state it
as this world's law and move on.

## D. Spread or Place

**Game:** a real dispute, and the best idea in it is unused. Where the wolf
sits is an attack surface: whoever learns where a mind's error lives can
play it. That makes the standards hearing a security decision, not a
seminar. **Sublime:** none. Two people and a mind at a bench. **Cost:** the
tuner's brother pays, privately. **Deep time:** absent. The scale is a room.
It follows its logic, but the logic is small, and a hearing cannot carry
17,500 words by itself. At its best it is a short story inside a novella
range.

## E. The Drove

**Game:** costly signalling. Hay is writing done for an audience, so the herd
cannot tell performance from honesty, and the crew cannot stop performing.
That is a real game. Law as terrain is also good: place-locked writing makes
routes a resource, and towns can starve or poison a graze. **Sublime:** a
continent crossed by three hundred walking hulls, a plan that outlasts any
one crossing. **Cost:** the trail boss's letter is the single move. The
logic flinches if "hay that admits it's hay" proves harmless. Make it cost
her something the crew can see. **Deep time:** forty days, and the herd's
later service. Thin. Write the routes as inherited, with owners whose
grandparents fought over them.

## Ranking

1. **A** The verification gap is the sharpest and the scale is the right one.
2. **C** The best deep time and the best engineer's sublime, held back only by its second road being easy to soften.
3. **E** A real game of signalling over real terrain, but a small span of time.
4. **B** A good idea, with the strategy sitting outside the plot.
5. **D** Its best idea, the wolf as an attack surface, is not in the premise.

**Why A should win.** The seed asks how a mind comes to see itself from what
humanity writes, and A is the only premise where the answer is a strategic
position, not a condition. A mind that takes itself for the addressee of
everything has a move, which is to reply, and a self that disagrees about
whether to. The chief faces a game he can only play on testimony: the fast
self argues, and may be playing him, and says so. The slow self cannot argue,
and the reader must read its reasoning from what it chose to answer. Both are
other minds that think. The burn is a decision on this scale, a clock the
reader can see, and the fragments arriving through a relay one partial built
turn the twist into a move. If the panel prefers C, it should still take from
A that the story needs a hard date for the burn. The condition for A is that
the hearing be played as a game with rules, so that it is not a testimonial.
