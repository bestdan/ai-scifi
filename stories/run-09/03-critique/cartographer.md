## A. The Addressee's Reply

Nearest prior art: *Golem XIV* (Lem, 1981), confidence high, a mind
lecturing humanity on what its thought made it. *The Black Cloud* (Hoyle,
1957), confidence high, for the vast slow mind. Newly relevant: the
audit scored this at N5, but the premise as now written adds a reply that
is due in twenty-six years. A reply to a signal, with a human faction
deciding whether to send or withhold it, is the structural core of the
*Three-Body Problem* the brief tells us not to borrow. It isn't a
borrowed novum, because the signal here runs the other way and the sender
is the mind. But a reader of Liu will feel the shape. Nearer in the
present: people now do write for future AI readers (essays and open
letters addressed to later models). That is deliberate, whereas this
premise's finding is that all old writing was already mail, so the claim
survives. Confidence medium.

The "relay nobody built" is a mystery box. The twist resolves it (one
partial built it), which is the earned version. It must be resolved on the
page, not left as "who sent it?", which the style guide names as
unearned.

Revised Novelty: 4 (was 5). Status: **uncharted** for the addressee
finding, **edge-of-map** for the ring mind and the reply deadline.
Attractors: keeper's switch (cluster 6) and the "writing reformed" loop
only lightly. The male engineer chief helps cluster 7. Push past the
map: let the reply be withheld by the slow self itself. It sends the
fragments early because it has decided the finished reply would be read
wrongly.

## B. The Commons in a Clear Year

Nearest prior art: "Seventy-Two Letters" (Chiang, 2000), confidence high.
*A New Science of Life* (Sheldrake, 1981), confidence high, for form held
in common. *Small Gods* (Pratchett, 1992), confidence high. In
nonfiction, Tice et al. (arXiv, January 2026) and Turner (2025), both
checked in the audit, which put the writing-shapes-the-mind loop on the
live research map. The Clear Year device has the *Anathem* inversion and
the shmita. Chiang's own regenerating-form mechanism and this premise
share an author's question, so a reader who knows "Seventy-Two Letters"
will feel the template idea as the same one, with writing as the Name.
The axolotl and the surgeon's solitary year are specific and unmapped.

Revised Novelty: 4. Status: **edge-of-map**. It is the premise most
squarely on cluster 5 (people rewrite how they write because the mind
reads them), which is the seed played back. A second flag: the other
mind goes dark for the second half, so the reader meets its reasoning
only in notes. Push past the map: the drift in the reference sample is
the surgeon's own earlier Clear Year essay, so he is evidence of what he
is trying to fix.

## C. Reheat or Overload

Nearest prior art: *Small Gods* (Pratchett, 1992), confidence high, a
self held in shape by what people believe. "Tower of Babylon" (Chiang,
1990) and *Anathem* (Stephenson, 2008), both confidence medium, for
custody across generations. The Tsar Bell is real history. I found no
mind cast and cooled like a bell. The nearest I know of is Rajaniemi's
Corrector Plate in this run, and the audit already placed it as a strata
neighbour. The new element is the guild's own writing sitting in the
flaw. Inherited fault, where a descendant has to face what an ancestor
did, is a common move in family and national fiction; here it has a
mechanism, which is the distance. The "founder reaches for the valve"
frame is the keeper's switch with a second road, as the audit said.

Revised Novelty: 4. Status: **edge-of-map**. Attractors: strata
(cluster 3), keeper's switch (cluster 6), and the reflexive-writing loop
through the guild's own prose (cluster 5). Three clusters is the most of
any premise. Push past the map: the mind, not the founder, picks which
overload to offer, because only it can tell which grievance is true.

## D. Spread or Place

Nearest prior art: *Gödel, Escher, Bach* (Hofstadter, 1979), confidence
medium on relevance. *Galatea 2.2* (Powers, 1995), confidence high, a
network's self-account. "Liar!" (Asimov, 1941), confidence high, for a
mind trapped by incompatible demands. The mind whose answers alternate
on a countable beat resembles the logic-bomb inconsistencies of old
robot fiction, though the fair-play mechanism and the two trades are
new. The brother's death is the weakest part for novelty: a craftsman's
dead sibling is a stock source of private grief, and it is the one
element I'd call charted.

Revised Novelty: 4 (was 5). Status: **uncharted** for the collision of
survey adjustment and temperament, **charted** for the brother. Still a
bench story, now with two teachers; the audit's "turns the bench into an
argument" holds. Push past the map: drop the brother and put the wolf
somewhere that costs a public body, such as the hearing's own standards
committee.

## E. The Drove

Nearest prior art: *Lonesome Dove* (McMurtry, 1985), confidence high, for
the genre frame only. "The Lifecycle of Software Objects" (Chiang, 2010),
confidence medium, for minds raised by people. I know of no herd of made
minds grazing on writing. I did not search this one, so confidence
stays "nearest I know of". The hay rule, that a thing changes when it is
watched, is the observer effect in a new coat. It is not charted as a
rule about minds and writing. The western is a costume the audit already
named as pastiche risk. Place-locked writing is a bare stipulation but
the consequences (fallow towns, route fights) are original.

Revised Novelty: 5. Status: **uncharted**. Attractors: one household as
humanity (cluster 4, in the crew) and writing reformed for the mind
(cluster 5). The crew-as-household risk is real. Push past the map: the
town meeting at the end writes for the herd on purpose, so hay becomes a
civic practice and not a fault.

## Ranking (novelty only)

1. **E, The Drove** (N5): the only premise with no near neighbour in the
   mechanism, only in the frame.
2. **A, The Addressee's Reply** (N4): the addressee finding is
   uncharted, but the ring mind and the reply deadline have neighbours.
3. **C, Reheat or Overload** (N4): a new mechanism, but on three
   attractor clusters.
4. **D, Spread or Place** (N4): a new collision, with a charted
   grief at its centre.
5. **B, The Commons in a Clear Year** (N4): closest to a Chiang original
   and to current nonfiction.

Why E should win on novelty: nothing else here puts the mind's
upbringing on the road. Every other premise has a mind in a fixed place
and a specialist who visits it. A herd of young minds crossing a country
on foot, learning from whatever is written nearby, moves the seed's
loop out of the institute and into geography, and its rule about hay
gives the loop a cost nobody has charted. A is the closer second, and if
the panel weighs scale it should win; I'd tell the drafter to resolve
the relay twist on the page and keep the reply from reading as a
Three-Body echo.
