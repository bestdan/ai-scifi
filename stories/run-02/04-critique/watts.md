# Watts lens: bible stress test (run-02)

Blind spots: I underrate ritual and joy, and I push toward bleakness. Weigh
the Le Guin note against mine on the rite. I can't judge Minas bell-founding
craft beyond the physics.

1. **[must-fix] The dose misses the rite.** *Firma* ends on the morning of the
   7th and the nets cure through the 14th. The Entrega is on the 7th and "the
   seat goes only to an heir who has set." At the rite Davi is still open, and
   the law the story rests on is broken by its own hero. Move the first tablet
   to 4 October so the curing week spans the rite, or change the law to "has
   begun firma". Either way, fix the ledger's Number row, and say on the page
   that the town knows he is mid-cure.

2. **[must-fix] Open drift is not what the nets do, and he shouldn't see it.**
   Perineuronal nets (Pizzorusso et al. 2002; Hensch 2005) close sensory and
   motor critical periods. They don't make tastes or loyalties suggestible.
   That is a second mechanism, so name it as the treatment's other effect, or
   the counsellor's speech will contradict itself. The bigger flinch is that
   Davi "notices it only afterwards." Choice-blindness work (Johansson and
   Hall 2005) says people confabulate ownership of swapped preferences
   without noticing. The honest open mind never feels the drift. Let the
   narration drift while Davi insists the new slang is his, and let the
   reader catch it first.

3. **[must-fix] The ending protects the reader from its own logic.** Davi
   chooses to stand by his mother all week, and the narration "holds hers."
   But a boy whose loyalties follow whoever is nearest has no vantage from
   which to choose a nearest person. The choice is the drift. Let his
   reasons for the choice already arrive in her cadence, and leave it
   undecided whether he picked her or she was the closest person to pick.
   That is the unsettling truth the novum supplies. Don't give a clean
   affirmative.

4. **[must-fix] The hands premise contradicts itself.** An open brain at
   sixteen has had sixteen years at a child's learning rate and still tears
   the mould. Learning speed isn't practice hours, and loam work needs
   thousands of them; the bible never says Davi has had them. State why: he
   spent them on theory and camps, and his mother kept him at the board only
   for weekends. Also, "motor skill learned while open stays" means three
   weeks of drilling before the dose banks real gain. And an adult doesn't
   lose motor learning, he slows. Ceição "couldn't" learn the accordion; say
   she wouldn't stay with it. Cite Newport (1990) on a declining curve, not
   a cliff.

5. **[should] The stakes are cheaper than the scene wants.** Delaying a year
   costs his mother some shame. The seat can wait, since the open already
   hold votes. Raise the price of staying open: who else is being set around
   him, what his pack will be by Christmas, and what the foundry loses if
   the licence lapses. Or let Seu Tonho show the cost of the delay in
   people's lives, not just in butcher's cuts nobody buys.

6. **[should] "No reopening dose" is too confident.** Chondroitinase ABC
   degrades nets and restores adult plasticity in rodents (Pizzorusso 2002;
   Gogolla 2009). Rafaela, who is a counsellor and honest, would say "none
   yet" and the town would know someone is working on it. That makes
   setting a bet, which is better than a law of nature. Leave a rumour in
   scene 3 and don't resolve it. Rafaela's voice-stuck-in-2049 detail is
   solid: Labov's adolescent peak for dialect is real. Keep it.
   [could] On bell physics: removing metal lowers a partial, but the
   position matters, since a cut near the lip lowers the nominal and a cut
   elsewhere can raise another partial. "You can only take away" is true;
   let Ceição add where, because that is the hands' knowledge Davi lacks.

Payoff ledger: the Mechanism rows can be made explicit in scene 2 if note 2
is taken. Otherwise Rafaela's speech has to defend two mechanisms.
