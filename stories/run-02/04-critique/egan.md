# Egan lens: stress test of the bible, run-02

Blind spots: I weight the derivation over the scene, and I haven't judged
whether any of this can be dramatised warmly. Defer to the First Reader and
Le Guin on that.

1. **[must-fix] The dates contradict the ending.** *Firma* finishes on the
   morning of the 7th, and the curing week "runs to the 14th". But the
   Entrega is on the 7th, and the seat passes only to "someone who has
   set". On the day, Davi is mid-cure and not legally set. The ledger also
   has him set "in her voice" and the narration fixing at the rite, which
   is a week early. Fix: take the tablets 28-30 Sep, so the cure ends on the
   6th or 7th. Then the week beside her is 30 Sep to 7 Oct, scene 4's offer
   falls in the 12 days from 16 to 28 Sep, and the rite is where the
   narration has already fixed. Recount "twenty-one days" against that.

2. **[must-fix] The hands arithmetic doesn't support the stakes.** He has
   been open for sixteen years with a child's learning speed, and his hands
   still tear the mould. A child who learns in weeks should have been
   casting competently for years. Ceição needed five years (9 to 14). So
   delaying a year buys one-fifth of an apprenticeship, not the trade. State
   what limits him. The only rate I can derive is feedback per pour: a bell
   is cast a few times a year, and each cut is irreversible, so
   practice is scarce however fast the brain is. Put the pours per year on
   the page (a number), and say the delay is a year of about N bells.

3. **[must-fix] Why not a machine?** The open built robots at camp. A
   fourteen-year-old with these minds would have an instrumented cutter
   closing on the target partials in a weekend. The bible says only that
   the craft would live "in a file". Give a reason inside the world: a
   licence clause requiring the titular founder's own hand, or the town's
   ear rejecting machine-cut bells. Ceição should state it in one line. The
   answer also tells us why the heir is not simply exempt from the hands.

4. **[should] Two escape hatches the open would find in an afternoon.**
   (a) "Setting can't be undone." In real work, enzymatic digestion of the
   nets reopens plasticity in animals, and it costs the memories the nets
   hold. Make that the in-world fact: a reopening exists, it unmakes
   what you set, so it is not a way back. (b) The Why row says open
   councils flip-flopped. Open people would try binding pledges first. One
   line from Rafaela: pledges signed Monday were renounced Thursday, and then
   the renunciation was renounced. That is the evidence the rite is the
   only fixed point.

5. **[should] The decision is made by the faculty it is about.** The open
   "argue themselves into anything a clever friend believes", and three
   weeks in the foundry beside Ceição will drift him toward her anyway. So
   his choice to "stand beside her all week" cannot be told apart from
   drift, and Ceição's offer of a year of shame is itself *pregar*. This is
   the next step the bible doesn't take. Let Davi notice it. His check is
   a decision recorded before the foundry weeks (a bell-code message he
   rings or writes to himself), and the ending is that he cannot prove it
   was his, and sets anyway. This also keeps "the narration holds her
   register" from reading as a trick.

6. **[could] Two details.** Slang is socially updated by adults and is not
   a plasticity effect; what nets plausibly lock is accent, prosody and what
   sounds right. Date Rafaela by her vowels and her cadence, with a
   word or two. Second, "you can only take away" holds for metal and not
   for pitch: cutting at different heights lowers some partials and
   raises others, so a skilled cut can correct a flat partial. Check this
   before writing scene 4. And if the bell hangs flat, say who pays
   (Ceição's reputation, or a recast).

**Ledger check.** Scene 2 carries Premise, Stakes, Why, both mechanism rows
and the dates. That is a lecture. Let Davi's impatience cut it, and give
the dates to him as a countdown, so Rafaela states only the nets and
the cost.
