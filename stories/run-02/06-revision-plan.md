## Comprehension item (structural)

1. **Premise (partial).** The reader wrote: "He stays open and works at
   the board beside his mother, and is set that way." They knew the outcome
   (answer 5 has the tablets on the 28th–30th), but the delay branch and
   the set branch share one picture, *at the board, beside her*. That
   picture came in with the round 2 fix (draft-2 line ~143, "Stay open one
   year, at the board, beside her", and ~227, "You stay open a year, here,
   at the board"). Both readers also left the town's shaming out of the
   premise.
   - **Separate the branches.** Describe the delay branch by *no tablets*,
     not by the board. For example: "stay open a year, no tablets, and
     learn it with a child's hands while the town watches her hold on". The
     28 Sep choice then reads as *taking the tablets*, with the board as
     where he cures.
   - **Put the shaming in the premise statement itself.** On the clinic
     steps (19 Sep), replace "The town would talk. Let it." with the word
     *agarrada* and what it does to his mother, in one or two plain
     sentences. Mention it again at the decision moment on 28 Sep, briefly.
   - Keep "I'm not taking your year". The ambiguity is about *why* he
     chose, never about *what* he did.

## Adopted lens notes

2. **The misreading must follow the rule** (Asimov, structural). As drawn,
   all nine feast strokes fall on the new, flat bell, so by Vô Zé's rule
   they would be nine lows, yet the relay is `high ••• · low • · high •••`.
   Fix it with a planted feast pattern: in scene 1, Vô Zé or Lia says a
   feast is rung three on the small high bell, one on the big high bell
   (the Virgin's stroke), three on the small high bell. The new bell is the
   big high bell, so exactly one stroke sounds sour, which an old ear hears
   as low. Redraw the 7 Oct marks to match. Keep "a sour high's a low"
   and the crack-is-only-a-buzz line.
3. **The hands arithmetic** (Asimov and Watts, structural). One or two
   lines on 16 Sep or 26 Sep. He has never been allowed the cutter, because
   metal can't go back, so the 26 Sep cut is his first real one. At the
   board he has worked Saturdays and holidays, and she was rarely beside
   him. Open hands learn fast *with a master beside them every day*, which
   is what her year would give him.
4. **Davi forecasts his own drift too fluently on 26 Sep** (Watts). Cut or
   blunt the line where he names it. The open don't hear their drift.
5. **"About six days"** (Watts). One clause from Rafaela: the tablets
   force the cure faster than nature would. That is their trick.
6. **The last line** (Chiang). "Pitch before number" was already his habit
   in scene 1, so "heard the low ... before he had counted" reads as habit,
   not change. Make what he hears concrete, for example whose bell it is or
   whose ear he hears it with, and leave its meaning unsaid. Don't add a
   line saying whether he chose (Le Guin's one thing).
7. **Line trims** (Le Guin, and the First Reader was bored by the Congado):
   break the single Congado sentence into two or three; drop the third
   "curl on his boots"; reorder the 28 Sep notebook paragraph if it reads
   better; cut the clause where Lia is wrong.
8. **Remove the drafter's appendix from the story text.** Keep
   `## Drafter's notes` at the end as usual, but nothing else after the
   story.
