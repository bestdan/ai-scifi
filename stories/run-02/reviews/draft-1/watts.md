## The one thing
The story's own logic says staying open is the better trade for the hands, and the ending protects the reader from that. Ceiça's argument (26 Sept) is that open hands "learn a cut in a bell or two", so a year open is eight bells of fast learning. Set hands "learn slow". Davi sets in her cadence, after she has quietly arranged for him to cure beside her. Make that arrangement knowing, and pay for it on the page.

## Notes

1. **Hands logic, 19 and 26 Sept (structural):** Rafaela says "your hands learn like mine. Like anyone's." The ledger says setting locks the hands. If so, the best master is one who stays open, learns the most, and then sets. Yet Davi's open hands botch the cut. The reader needs to see why plasticity didn't help (no practice? the pull scattering his attention?). Otherwise the one-year wait looks almost free, and his "obvious" reasoning at the notebook is the weaker one.
2. **The untreated parent as circumventer, 28 Sept (structural):** The Lei's preamble says untreated parents were "circumvented". Here the untreated parent sets the conditions of the cure ("where I can see your hands", the bass line he catches himself humming). Ceiça is the last and loudest speaker, and she says she is acting against herself. That irony goes unspent. Either she knows the mechanism and chooses her words, or she doesn't, and Rafaela notices. Right now the story's logic points at "he drifted toward his mother" and the prose tells us "he chose."
3. **Where it flinches, 22 and 7 Oct:** The pack is a single voice, all "ceiling". The open's real advantages (they can change their minds, learn a language in a month) never show. So closing costs him only a bus ride. Give one open person something worth envying, perhaps Lia, who at nine is already a fluent adult-mimic.
4. **Continuity, 19 Sept:** Rafaela set four years ago "beside my boyfriend", but he has been in Recife "six years". Fix the arithmetic.
5. **Biology, Annex Two and the Campinas rats (line-level):** The real basis is the perineuronal net (Pizzorusso et al. 2002, *Science*: chondroitinase reopens adult plasticity; Gogolla et al. 2009: the same enzyme erases fear memory; Beurdeley et al. 2012 on Otx2). "Rats forget the maze" is good. The pull sits on adolescent peer sensitivity (Gardner and Steinberg 2005). Two things a biologist would query: nets mature over weeks, not "six or seven days" (acceptable novum, but name it as the tablet's trick), and an adult failing the bass side of an accordion for a year is more fringe than "the nets locked my hands". Make it Ceiça's folk theory, which the draft half-does.
6. **Bell physics, 26 Sept (line-level):** Verify that a cut "near the lip" drops the strike note while the hum is tuned from the waist. I am not certain a bell founder wouldn't call this backwards.
7. **2 Oct, Davi's denial:** Right idea, since the open don't hear the drift, but the speech is a mash of three registers. It confused the First Reader. Keep two registers and let the third surface later.

## What to protect

- Seu Tonho: "I haven't played it. Then I'd know what she sounds like now." This is the cleanest statement of what the novum costs.
- "He could have reproduced every sentence, and none of the sentences was the one where it happened." The best account of confabulated choice in the draft.
- "Dadá," he said, kindly. The grandfather sees the drift and Davi can't.
- Ceiça's "Me, out of that chair. This one's got cinnamon in." Sorrow and joke in one line, and it shows the ringers' code working as a mis-signal.
- The 28 Sept sentence beginning "It was only that the feast doesn't move", whose long single run is itself the drift made audible.

## Blind spot disclosure
I read mechanisms, not ritual, and I may be underweighting how much the Congado, the bells and the shared canjica are real forces that Davi is choosing, and not just pressures acting on him. I also treat the ending's warmth as a flinch when the other lenses may rightly call it earned. I have no standing on character or prose.
