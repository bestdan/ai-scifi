# Names and roles

- **Davi Ribeiro (Dadá):** 16, treated, still open; heir to the family bell foundry.
- **Ceiça (Conceição Ribeiro):** his mother, untreated, master bell founder.
- **Vô Zé:** his grandfather, untreated, bell-ringer for the Rosário brotherhood; handed the foundry to Ceiça informally in 2038.
- **Lia:** 9, his cousin, treated and open.
- **Rafaela Brum:** 24, the town's setting counsellor, treated and set at 16.
- **Seu Tonho:** the butcher, untreated, the town's one agarrado.
- **Kátia:** Tonho's daughter, who never set (a cata-vento).
- **The pack:** Davi's open schoolfriends.
- **firma:** the three-tablet dose that sets (closes) a treated brain.
- **open / set:** treated and still plastic / closed by firma.
- **the nets, the pull:** the two things the treatment prevents or keeps.
- **the curing week:** the week after firma; whoever you're beside then is who you sound like for life.
- **Entrega:** the yearly handover rite on the feast day.
- **agarrado/agarrada:** a parent still holding the licence after the heir's feast.
- **cata-vento:** a person who never sets.
- **sino de mão:** the hand-made-bell licence.
