# Critique of draft 1: chiang

## The one thing
Make the narration do what the premise says. The question is whether a mind that drifts can tell it has chosen. The draft answers it with an omniscient-feeling clarity: motive after motive, then "I'm not taking the year." The page should leave the reader unable to say, as Davi is. Let the narration take on the nearest person's diction, scene by scene, unmarked, so that the 28 September decision arrives already in Ceiça's cadence and the reader hears it before Davi can.

## Notes

1. **Form (structural):** The register hardly moves. "Bricked", "ceiling" and "Zero chance" are in Davi's dialogue and thought, but the narration around Rafaela stays the same as the narration around the pack. After the cure, the outline says the narration should hold one register, hers. In the final scene the voice is the same plain voice as at the start ("the curl came off the edge of the profile long and whole"). The only proof of drift is told by Vô Zé ("Last month you talked like that camp"). Drift is the one thing only this form can show, and the story tells it in dialogue instead.
2. **28 September, the notebook paragraph (structural):** "the feast doesn't move, and a bell goes up when it goes up, and you hang what you've got and you ring it ... like the loam." The cadence is already Ceiça's, and I protect it. But the reasons are all good ones: the flat bell, the accordion, the feast date. A reader can conclude "he chose, sensibly". The ambiguity the bible wants needs one reason that sounds like his own and doesn't survive being said aloud, or one that is plainly the previous speaker's.
3. **Rafaela, 19 September (line-level):** "I was sixteen and open. I chose whatever he'd said last." This states the theme and hands it to the reader before Davi faces it. She is the one character allowed to know, so keep the cello, her "He had heard how it was going", and cut this line.
4. **2 October, the Vô Zé exchange (line-level):** "Zero chance. Sound like me. Always have. Uai, I don't," Davi said, "I never did..." Three markers are packed into one line, and the reader is left to count them. Let two be enough, or let the narration show it with no marker.
5. **The one unnecessary scene (structural):** the 7 October pack crossing, "He followed one word in three. When they laughed he laughed late." The first reader found it echoes the untreated boy at the fountain, but the echo is the whole job, and it explains the loss that the flat bell already carries. The ending is stronger if Davi's loss of the pack is a gap and not a scene.
6. **Last line, "and he counted" (structural):** Vô Zé's rule is "Pitch before number." If Davi now counts, the reversal is the question enacted: a man who heard pitch first now counts, so something in him moved. If that is not intended, the line is a flat note. I think it is intended, and it should be made unmistakable, since the first reader's report doesn't register it.
7. **Fountain paragraph, 22 September (line-level):** the first reader skimmed the middle. The single run-on is right. Cut the Christmas logic by half and keep "that's actually ceiling" and the moment he hears himself say it.

## What to protect

- "He could have reproduced every sentence, and none of the sentences was the one where it happened." This is the story's question in one line, and it comes from the idea.
- Seu Tonho: "I haven't played it. Then I'd know what she sounds like now."
- The flat bell as an error that the code turns into a message, and Ceiça's "Somebody is going, uai. Me, out of that chair. This one's got cinnamon in."
- "Dadá," he said, kindly.
- "Loam you can put back. Nobody puts metal back."
- The cello that misses the note and stops when he knocks.

## Blind spot disclosure
I favour the idea over momentum, so I may over-weight the narration form. The first reader found the draft moving as it stands, and Banks or Watts may say the plain voice is what lets the ending land. I also have no standing on the Understood axes, and the "he chose, sensibly" worry is an insider's: the cold reader still came away unsure whether he chose.
