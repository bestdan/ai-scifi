# Graded ledger rows

| Row | The fact |
|---|---|
| Premise | A treated boy must decide whether to set his still-open brain in time for his rite day, taking his mother's seat as a fixed adult, or stay open and keep learning her trade at a child's speed while the town shames her. |
| Stakes | If he sets: his mother steps down with honour and he takes the seat, but his hands learn the craft only at an adult's pace, maybe ten years to match her. If he delays: about eight more bells at a child's speed, but his mother becomes an agarrado (a parent clinging to the licence), and he keeps drifting, maybe with his friends to Belo Horizonte at Christmas. |
| Why | The seat goes only to someone set because councils of open members reversed themselves with every speaker, and even their pledges didn't hold (2049–2052); the rite lets untreated parents step down on a fixed day with dignity instead of clinging and being bypassed. |
| Twist | none |
| Ending | Davi declines his mother's offer, takes firma on 28 September and cures beside her at the foundry. He can't tell whether he chose her or she was simply nearest; his reasons, by then, are in her cadence. At the Entrega the bell he tuned rings slightly flat, and the town confidently misreads it as a message. The next morning, formally his junior, she puts her hand over his and slows it. |
