## The one thing
Fix the learning arithmetic. The engine of the choice is "a child's hands learn a cut in a bell or two", but 16 Sep says Davi has had the arm "a bell or two a season" for sixteen years. That is twenty-odd bells, and he still tears the waist and cuts low. As written, open plasticity looks overrated, which undercuts the year Ceiça offers. State what the open need and he lacks (a master beside them daily, not Saturdays), or the offer reads as sentiment.

## Notes

1. **16 Sep vs 26 Sep (structural):** see above. Ceiça herself "learned" in forty-some bells, so the numbers say Davi is on schedule and the year buys little.
2. **28 Sep, "Whose board?" / "Ours." (structural, mostly resolved):** the draft now lets Rafaela see the setter. The remaining flinch is small: Ceiça never shows she knows. I would leave her unreadable, but a single beat of her choosing where to stand (she "moved over") already does it. Do not add more.
3. **26 Sep, "The pull went to whoever had spoken last... the pack would have spoken last":** Davi states the mechanism of his own drift fluently. A drifting mind rationalises, it does not forecast. Have him believe it as a fact about the pack ("they'd talk him round"), not as the rule.
4. **Annex Two, curing (line-level):** nets mature over weeks. "About six days" is an acceptable novum, but Rafaela should flag it as the tablet's trick. Citations for the record: Pizzorusso et al. 2002 (chondroitinase reopens plasticity), Gogolla et al. 2009 (the same enzyme erases fear memory), Gardner and Steinberg 2005 (peer sensitivity).
5. **Accordion, 26 Sep (line-level):** untreated adult learning slowly at thirty is right. Keep it as her folk theory, which it already is.
6. **7 Oct, the relay:** a sour high heard as a low is a good selective story for a signalling code. Fine as is.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh mechanism over ritual. The hand-over-hand close reads to me as mild softening, though the first reader and other lenses may rightly call it earned: she is formally his apprentice, which is a cost to her. I have no standing on character or prose, and I may over-weight the arithmetic of a story whose power is the Congado and the kitchen.
