# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi (density, texture, glossary)

Blind spots: I favour opacity and can mistake density for depth. I did not check arithmetic or logic (Asimov's job) or whether a cold reader is lost (First Reader's).

Overall: the polish left the texture intact. The tower code, `brabo`, `agarrada`, `cata-vento`, `bricked`, `ceiling` and `Advice is one real` all carry meaning by context. The Annex is the one place an explanation is meant to be explicit, and the cut version reads as a bored clerk's recital, not as clumsy. The payoff-ledger lines are all still explicit. No fix needed there.

Glossary check: `firma`, `pregar`, `agarrado`, `cata-vento`, `sino de mão`, `Entrega` and the nets and the pull are all used as the bible has them. Two slips:

1. Term drift. The Annex says "hereafter the curing period"; the glossary and Rafaela say "curing week". The curing week is the word the town uses, so it should be the defined one.
2. `strickle` appears once, as an unglossed tool word, while everywhere else the tool is "the board" or "the arm".

Suggestions:

1. `hereafter the curing period, and the subject's state during that period is retained` → `hereafter the curing week, and the subject's state during that week is retained`
2. `his couldn't strickle a waist` → `his couldn't walk a board round a waist`
3. `chalky on his tongue and tasted of nothing` → `chalky on his tongue and tasted of the paper cup`

   Reason: "tasted of nothing" is the near-twin of the line the style guide protects from calibration-01. The substitute is a small, specific, odd detail.
4. `Fountain at dusk, and by the time the light went it was all planned` → `Fountain at dusk, and by the time the streetlamps came on it was all planned`

   Reason: this swaps a generic image for a local one. It is optional.

Nothing else. The tablet, the clinic and the lathe are already concrete; I would not make them stranger.
