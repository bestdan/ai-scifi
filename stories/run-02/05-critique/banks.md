# Stage 5 critique: Banks lens

Blind spot, stated: I favour set pieces and sprawl, and short stories are not
my form. Every fix below folds into an existing scene. None adds one. Chiang
and Asimov should cut anything that costs words.

1. **[must-fix] Scale is stated, never felt.** The outline has a country of
   treated children (Brazil, since 2033) and a town of 9,000. On the page
   there is a foundry, a clinic and a square. Scene 3 labels the pack "the
   open generation" but shows nine kids at a fountain. Fix: spend the scale
   once, in scene 6. The flat bell is misread by the town, and the misreading
   then travels. Vô Zé's tower is answered from the next town's tower, and
   that one from the one beyond, a wrong message going down the valley in
   bronze by afternoon. Nobody checks it. Add one line of Davi hearing it
   cross the hills in the bell code, which is the one register he can trust.
   That is vastness at human speed, and it comes straight from the novum.

2. **[should] The wit is thin, and the outline reads solemn.** The only
   comedy flagged is Seu Tonho, "sad and a little ridiculous". Put the jokes
   where the machinery is. The 2052 consent sheet is bureaucratic prose
   read flat by a woman who hates it, so give it one deadpan clause (a
   cooling-off period for the already-adolescent). Give the town's
   committee argument over the flat bell some Tonho-grade pomposity. Let
   Lia price her advice, since advice is worthless in this economy. Let the
   Entrega's junior's-words be a little absurd, because the formula was
   written by a council of drifting teenagers. Joy and absurdity should
   sit beside the cost, not after it.

3. **[should] Swap scenes 2 and 3.** The sheet currently lands on a boy who
   has seen only a torn mould, so the clinic carries all the law and the
   stakes as a briefing. Put the square first: the pack's decision, then
   Tonho and the unplayed message. Then the sheet reads as a document
   confirming what he has just watched, and the note in bell code ("stay
   open") follows Tonho's face rather than Rafaela's speech. The 22nd
   becomes the 19th and the clinic the 22nd; the 28th deadline is
   untouched. The Why row's hint in scene 3 becomes a hint in scene 1 only.

4. **[should] No one does dirty work for good reasons, and Rafaela is
   positioned to.** She is the state's counsellor, sets the 28th, and
   certifies the cure on the 6th, so she holds the whole machine. She also
   sees the vowels she is certifying. Fix: in scene 2 she shades the rule,
   an extra day on her own authority, and in scene 5 she hears Ceiça's
   cadence in the boy and certifies anyway, with one beat of hesitation.
   She is kind, the system is kind, and the child is fixed. Keep it to
   a gesture.

5. **[could] Who is missing from the view.** Everyone is the Ribeiros'
   neighbour. Absent: an untreated child (the watched minority), and
   Kátia, who is only a voice message. A *cata-vento* passing through on
   feast day, in a different accent each time Davi sees her, would give the
   pack's future a face without a new scene. Cut Kátia's message before
   Tonho ever plays it, to keep it unplayed.

Scene 5 is the climax and is also pure summary. Give it one concrete
physical anchor, such as the week's pour or Ceiça's hands on the strickle,
so it is enjoyable to inhabit rather than reported.
