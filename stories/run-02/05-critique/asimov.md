# Stage 5 critique: Asimov (the Logician)

Blind spots: I weigh rules and fair play, not interiority, texture or the
body. The drift-in-narration device is a sensory and emotional matter I can't
judge. Defer to Le Guin and Banks on whether the register shifts will land.

The rules are statable by the midpoint: scene 2 gives them, scene 4 adds the
stakes. The order is sound. The problems are one missing rule, one missing
plant, and load.

1. **[must-fix] The cost of delay is undefined.** The bible says the seat
   goes to an heir who has set "in the year" he turns sixteen. Ceiça offers
   "a year" and calls it agarrada for it. Nowhere does the outline say whether
   delay means the Entrega happens next feast, or whether the window closes.
   A reader will ask this in scene 2 and, unanswered, the whole dilemma looks
   optional. Fix: Rafaela's summary in scene 2 states that the rite is held
   yearly, that a delayed heir takes the next feast, and that the town counts
   the year as the parent's refusal. Then "a year" in scene 4 is arithmetic
   the reader already holds.

2. **[must-fix] The flat bell's misreading has no plant.** Scene 6 needs the
   town to decode a flat strike as a message. Scene 1 only has Davi decoding
   a death. Fix: in scene 1, show that the code distinguishes toll by pitch
   as well as pattern (the sharp bell for a death, the lower for a marriage,
   say, or a named interval), so the reader can predict the misreading, and
   Davi can see it coming before the town does. Also plant why Ceiça hangs a
   flawed bell (the feast cannot move, and the loam needs a year to recast),
   or she is the one stupid person in the plot.

3. **[must-fix] The ambiguity needs countable inputs.** The climax asks the
   reader whether Davi chose or drifted. Fair play means the reader can tally
   the evidence. Fix: scene 4 should end with Davi knowing Rafaela's rule
   (stand where you want to be set), and scene 5 should open with his two
   days (26 to 28) split visibly: hours beside Ceiça, hours with the pack's
   messages. Then the reader can weigh it, and the note in bell code is a
   clue they can read against the first tablet.

4. **[should] Scenes 2 and 4 are overloaded; scene 5 is starved.** Scene 2
   at 1,000 words carries the consent sheet, pledges, nets, pull, Campinas,
   boyfriend, cello, the 28th, the note. Scene 4 carries the cut, machine,
   licence, accordion, numbers and offer. Scene 5 spans nine days and the
   climax in 1,100. The ceiling has 900 words of slack. Move 300 to scene 2,
   200 to scene 4, 400 to scene 5. Let Davi count the dates himself and
   shorten Rafaela's lines.

5. **[could] Make scene 3's turn do ledger work.** Tonho's "will she make
   me wait" is the only visible cost of delay. Let Tonho state the town's
   rule (the yearly rite, the shame), which backs note 1 from an
   independent source.

Nobody else needs to be stupid once 2 is fixed. The Stakes sit before the
climax, in scene 4.
