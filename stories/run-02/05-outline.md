# Outline: The Setting Day (working title)

- **Target length:** about 6,000 words (hard ceiling 7,000).
- **Shape:** linear, a countdown from 21 days before the feast to the
  morning after it, in six dated scenes. The form enacts the idea through
  the narration's register: it drifts with whoever Davi stands beside for
  four scenes, settles during the curing week in scene 5, and holds one
  voice, his mother's, in scene 6. The bell code is the one register that
  never moves, and it is set on the page as a fixed typographic device:
  strikes written as counted marks on two bells, high and low, for
  example `low ••• · high •• · low •`. It is used three times: the noon
  death in scene 1, Davi's note to himself in scene 2, and the town's
  misreading in scene 6, so the reader can check the ending.
- **Opening question:** what is this boy three weeks away from, and why
  can't his hands do what his head can?
- **Final image:** the morning after the Entrega, at six, in the foundry.
  Ceiça, now formally his junior, puts her hand over his on the strickle
  board and slows it down. The town's bells are talking, and he counts
  them. The narration doesn't say whose voice he counts in.
- **What the ending costs the protagonist:** the hands at a child's
  speed (a year, about eight bells, gone for good); the pack and the city,
  whose fast talk he can no longer follow; and the certainty that the
  choice was his.

## Scenes

| # | Scene | Purpose | Turn (what changes) | Reveals | Introduces | ~Words |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The board.** 16 Sep, 6 a.m. to lunch. Ceiça strickles loam for the Rosário bell's mould. Davi, back from camp and talking like it, rams the mould and tears it; she fixes it in two strokes. The noon bells, written as marks: a death, on the low bell, which is how deaths go; Vô Zé says so in passing (plant: the code reads by pitch as well as count). Lunch at the family table: Vô Zé, Lia, beans. Vô Zé handed over "in a kitchen" in 2038. Lia corrects his sums, then charges Davi a real for advice and copies his slang. Davi counts: twenty-one days. | Ground the foundry, the family, head vs hands, the bell code, the rite. | From "he'll take the seat, obviously" to the torn mould sitting between them unspoken. | That his hands can't do it; that the rite is soon; that the open copy; that the code reads pitch. | Davi, Ceiça, Vô Zé, Lia; the Entrega; the Rosário bell; "the open" | 1,000 |
| 2 | **The consent sheet.** 19 Sep, the clinic. Cello through the wall, stopped. Rafaela reads the 2052 sheet aloud in its committee prose (comedy): the nets, the pull, the weathervane years. She abandons it on page two and initials that she read it all (she bends the rule). In her own words: the pledges; "none yet" and the Campinas rats; "After, your hands learn like mine"; "I set beside my boyfriend. Listen to me. Brabo." Explicit: whoever you are beside in the curing week is who you keep; the last day to start for this feast is the 28th; the rite is yearly, a delayed heir takes the next feast, and until then the town counts the parent as refusing. Leaving, in her vowels, he writes his note in bell marks: stay open. | Make the Premise, Why and mechanisms explicit through a document and a person who paid. | He leaves with a decision, written in the one register that is his. | The law, the mechanism, the yearly rule, the curing-week rule. | Rafaela; *firma*; the nets; the pull; the 28th | 1,200 |
| 3 | **The square.** 22 Sep, lunch. The pack at the fountain: fast, overlapping talk, and somehow they've decided on Belo Horizonte at Christmas, and he's in. Alone after, he can't reconstruct how. Seu Tonho's shop, the good cuts unsold, Kátia's voice message unplayed; Tonho, sour and funny, states the town's view: "The boy waits, the mother's agarrada. Ask me." Walking home Davi thinks his model on a cutter would tune truer than any hand. Nobody answers. | The cost of delay in a person; the open's alien edge. | The note gets a destination (the city) and a cost with a face. | What staying open becomes; what an agarrado is. | the pack; Seu Tonho; Kátia (name only); *agarrado*; *cata-vento* | 600 |
| 4 | **The lathe.** 26 Sep. The bell, poured mango-coloured the week before, on the lathe. "You can only take away. Where is the whole trade." He cuts near the lip; the strike note falls flat, a few cents, for good. He says the machine thing aloud; she answers with the licence. She will hang it anyway: the feast doesn't move, and a recast is three weeks. He asks her which to do. The accordion; "I never knew it was a price." Her offer: eight bells a year; ten years to match her once he's set; "Let them call me agarrada. A year." He doesn't answer. | Stakes explicit before the climax; the irreversible cut as the shape of the choice. | His failure becomes permanent in metal; her offer makes delay a gift he'd have to accept. | Stakes, the bells number, why not a machine, tuning, why the flat bell hangs. | *sino de mão* | 1,200 |
| 5 | **The curing week (staged).** Three beats, all in scene. **(a) 27 Sep evening:** with the pack, planning the city; the narration in their speed. **(b) 28 Sep:** six to ten at the board beside Ceiça; then the clinic. He takes out the notebook, reads his marks, and takes the first tablet. The narration gives no reason, and the reasons it gives are in her cadence. Countable: he was with the pack last night and with her this morning, and he knows the rule. **(c) 2 Oct, the board:** drilling the strickle, the physical anchor: loam under the nails, the strickle's pivot squeaking. Vô Zé: "You talk like your mother." Davi denies it ("Uai, I don't") in her voice, and the drift stops audibly mid-sentence: the sentence begins in camp clip and ends in her long cadence, and from there it stays. Close on the 6th: Rafaela certifies, hears whose vowels he has, says nothing but "Brabo." | The climax: the choice made without a chooser we can be sure of. | He sets; the narration fixes. | That the open don't feel drift; that he can't prove the choice was his. | — | 1,500 |
| 6 | **The feast.** 7 Oct, then six a.m. on the 8th. Congado; Vô Zé rings; the new bell is flat, low, and the town reads it, by the code, as a death not yet announced, or Ceiça mourning her seat. The wrong message goes tower to tower down the valley as the next villages' ringers repeat it (the scale, felt once). The Entrega: Ceiça hands over the licence and the trade vote and says the junior's words plainly; Seu Tonho watches from his door. Next morning at six, she puts her hand over his on the strickle and slows it. The bells; he counts them. | Close on cost and gain, unresolved. | The handover happens; the teaching carries on the other way. | The ending. | Congado | 800 |

**Total:** about 6,300 words.
