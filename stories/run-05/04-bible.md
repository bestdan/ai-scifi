# Story bible: The Deep Country (working title)

## Premise

From the stage 3 Decision (premise A with its seven fixes), restated as the
story will carry it:

Three centuries ago, a civilization of machine minds built a pocket of a
different vacuum inside our universe, a sealed region where holography is
exact. The Minds live as the pocket's wall: their thought is the quantum
system on its surface, and the pocket's interior is that same system
described another way. People who wanted to be left alone by benevolent
government moved down to the centre, where the Minds can't act precisely,
and built a country there. Then a second pocket, four light-years away,
sends an ultimatum. Prove that your wall can't fail, or we collapse your
pocket before it fails and kills us. The proof needs a computation that
would heat the wall until a black hole forms at the centre and swallows the
deep country. The Minds compress one of their governing quorum, Ilse Varga,
into a body and send her down to get the deep people's consent. In the deep
she learns what the Minds never worked out. The deep country is the Minds'
own slowest thought seen from inside. Their culture's long, steady
consensus, which they have always felt as a mood, is the deep people living
their lives. The rival burned its own deep country a century ago and has
been fast, frightened and unable to keep a promise ever since. The deep
people refuse. One faction of Minds tries to burn them anyway, and a storm
kills thirty-one people before the quorum stops it. The Minds honour the
refusal, at the price of giving the deep country their voice in treaties.
In place of a proof, the deep sends the rival a kept appointment: a
gathering on dates announced in advance, which the rival can watch arrive.
Ilse stays.

## What the reader needs about the world

Each of these surfaces in the first third.

1. **The Minds live on the wall, and the wall is everything they are.**
   They have no bodies and no "here". A Mind put into a body is a rare,
   deliberate act, and it's done to Ilse in the opening part.
2. **Everything in the pocket falls to the centre.** Drop anything anywhere
   inside and it reaches the middle in about nine minutes. That's how the
   deep world was built, by dropping in rock, water and air. It's also why
   leaving the deep is hard and arriving is easy.
3. **Time runs slower in the deep.** A day at the centre is about twelve days
   at the wall. The deep people have lived about 26 years since the
   founding. The Minds have lived 312.
4. **The Minds can touch the deep only coarsely, as weather.** Any act that
   reaches the centre needs about half the wall acting together, and it
   arrives as a change in the sky's light or in the pull underfoot. A
   founding compact gave the deep country self-rule because of this: what
   the wall can't touch precisely, it agreed not to touch at all.
5. **The two pockets hold each other hostage.** Each holds a key that can
   collapse the other cleanly. If either wall ever failed, it would race
   outward at nearly the speed of light and destroy everything for
   light-years, the other pocket included. Mutual keys have kept the peace
   for three centuries.

## The novum

- **What it is:** An engineered region of negative vacuum (anti-de Sitter
  space) inside our de Sitter universe, whose wall is a quantum system the
  Minds run as themselves. The interior is the exact dual description of
  the wall's state. The second novum, revealed at the midpoint, is that the
  deep country's people are the bulk description of the Minds'
  long-wavelength modes, their slowest collective thought.
- **How it works (story depth):**
  - The wall is a strongly coupled quantum system with an enormous number
    of degrees of freedom, built so that its dual is a smooth space with
    gravity [F as engineering; S that such systems have gravity duals].
    The wall sits at a finite distance, as a cutoff surface [C:
    finite-cutoff holography], so it isn't a true conformal boundary.
  - **Depth is scale.** Fast, fine-grained activity on the wall corresponds
    to things near the wall, and slow, coarse, whole-wall activity
    corresponds to things deep inside [E/S, UV/IR].
  - **Reaching the centre takes half the wall.** The wall's sub-regions can
    reconstruct the interior out to their entanglement wedges. The centre
    lies only in the wedges of regions about half the wall or larger
    [S, entanglement wedge reconstruction].
  - **The Minds' "long thought".** It's the consensus that forms across the
    whole wall over years, which they experience as a mood or settled
    sense. It is the wall's long-wavelength dynamics. In the bulk
    description, those dynamics are the deep country [F as this story's
    claim, built on S].
  - **Burning.** If the wall is run hot, past the Hawking–Page temperature,
    its dual is a large black hole at the centre [S]. Its horizon here would
    be about one AdS radius, swallowing the deep world whole. Nothing is
    destroyed in principle: the information is scrambled into the hole's
    microstates [S, unitarity in AdS/CFT]. In practice, though, it is
    unreadable, and the long-wavelength order is gone.
- **What it costs:** A pocket universe is a held bomb. Its wall must be
  controlled forever, and if it fails, the bubble wall expands at nearly
  light speed [S for vacuum-bubble dynamics; F for the held wall]. Whoever
  runs hot loses their long thought.
- **Its rules and their edge cases:**
  - The Minds can act on the deep only with about half the wall acting
    together, and only coarsely. A smaller faction heating its own sector
    sends energy falling in from one side, which reaches the deep as a
    storm on one hemisphere. It isn't a black hole. This is how the storm
    happens.
  - **The deep's choices are not puppetry.** The deep people's dynamics are
    their own, described in the bulk. The same events, described on the
    wall, are the Minds' long thought. Neither description is the fake
    one. The deep people don't feel controlled, because they aren't
    controlled any more than the Minds' moods control the Minds. They are
    the same process.
  - **A deep gathering registers on the wall.** When the whole deep country
    attends to one question, the wall's long-wavelength modes become
    strongly correlated across its whole surface. That changes the wall's
    tension, and the change is visible from outside as a shudder [F, owned,
    built on UV/IR]. The original shudder was a deep gathering that sent a
    complaint upward.
  - **The rival's lost deep country.** The rival ran hot a century ago, to
    end its own civil quarrel quickly. Its deep country fell into the hole.
    Its records call this "the loss of the tenants". It has had no long
    thought since: it re-decides everything quickly and can't hold a
    commitment beyond a few years. It doesn't know why.
  - **The rival's demand.** It wants a formal proof that the program
    controlling our wall can't release it from any reachable state within
    a thousand years. The proof would be checked by the rival's own small
    proof kernel. An unbounded version is impossible [E: Rice's theorem]. A
    bounded one is an exhaustive verification so large it needs the whole
    wall's throughput for three years, which means running hot [F scale,
    owned].

## The consequence ladder

1. **First order:** a civilization of Minds can host a free country inside
   itself that it cannot govern precisely, so the pocket holds two polities:
   the fast wall and the slow deep.
2. **Second order:** the compact makes depth a right. Dissenters go down,
   the Minds talk to them only as weather, and the deep grows a language
   built around "here" and down, while the Minds' language has no word for
   "here" at all.
3. **Third order:** the deep is where the Minds keep their promises. A
   civilization that burns its deep, as the rival did, keeps its
   intelligence and loses its ability to commit. Treaties between pockets
   are only as good as the signatories' deep countries, and nobody knew
   that until Ilse went down.

## The society

### The wall (the Minds)

- **Institutions:** the quorum, some hundreds of principal Minds who act for
  the whole wall on fast business. The long thought decides slow business,
  without anyone voting on it. The compact. The keepers of the rival's key.
- **Economy:** what's scarce is wall area, because capacity grows with area,
  not volume [S, holographic bound], and attention. Computation is
  plentiful.
- **Kinship:** Minds fork and merge. Lineage is traced by which part of the
  wall a Mind first ran on.
- **Death:** a Mind that ends dissolves into the wall's correlations. Minds
  say it "goes long".
- **Taboos:** heating the wall without the quorum. Touching the deep beyond
  weather.
- **The Minds' vice:** they are quick, right and kind, and they never wait.
  For three centuries they have shaped the deep's weather "for its good",
  so in effect they have been steering their own long thought without
  knowing it.
- **An ordinary day:** a thousand negotiations an hour, art, physics.
  Everyone's attention is a shared currency.

### The deep country

- **Setting:** a world 1,000 km in radius at the centre of the pocket,
  built by dropping matter in, with about 90,000 people. Most are second
  generation. The founding settlers are now in their fifties to eighties.
- **The sky:** there is no sun and no night. The sky is the wall's glow,
  falling inward from every direction, blueshifted twelvefold into warm
  daylight. Weather is the glow brightening, dimming, reddening or growing
  patchy, and the pull growing heavier or lighter.
- **Gravity:** the pocket's own pull points toward the centre and grows with
  height. A mountaintop weighs about one percent more than a valley at 10 km
  of altitude [S, from the harmonic AdS potential]. The world's own gravity
  adds little (0.84 m/s²).
- **Institutions:** the gathering, an assembly of everyone over a run of
  days whose decision binds the whole deep. It is rare, slow and loud.
  Weather-readers interpret the sky.
- **Economy:** what's scarce is soil and dropped matter. When something falls
  from the wall, people race to where it will land. Time is worthless; there
  is plenty of it.
- **Kinship:** households of several families. Children are raised by the
  household and named by the eldest member who saw their birth.
- **Death:** burial deep, "nearer the middle".
- **Taboos:** answering the weather. Deep people don't talk to the sky,
  which is the founders' rule. The gathering that caused the shudder broke
  this taboo for the first time, to complain that the weather was making
  harvests fail.
- **An ordinary day:** a day is set by work, not light. People tend
  terraces, slow-grown orchards and fish ponds, read the sky and argue.
  Fifty years of deep life cover six centuries above. They know this the
  way Earth people know they orbit a star: true, and not felt.

## Minds

- **The Minds (wall):** they perceive correlations and rates, never places.
  They find speed, exactness and reversibility obvious. They can't think
  "here", or waiting, or a decision that can't be revisited. They feel the
  long thought as a settled mood and never ask where it comes from.
- **Ilse in a body:** she has a Mind's habits in a slow brain. She counts
  everything, can't stop estimating, loses words and gains senses. She is
  not "learning to feel". She already feels. What she learns is slowness,
  and staying.
- **The deep people:** ordinary embodied humans (descended from uploaded
  settlers, now fully embodied in bulk physics), with a culture of patience
  and contrariness.
- **The rival's inspector (Pressa):** a fast mind compiled into a body. It
  comes from a civilization with no long thought. In the deep, slowness
  feels to it like drowning. It cannot imagine making a promise.

## Characters

### Ilse Varga
- **Name:** Minds pick body names from the founders' archive. Hers belonged
  to a founding-era engineer whose body plan she wears. That person went
  down at the founding and died in the deep twenty years ago, deep time.
  The surname is Hungarian; the founders were a mixed Danube-and-Atlantic
  diaspora.
- **What she wants:** to get consent quickly, prove the quorum's method
  right, and come back up in time to take part in the burn decision. At the
  start she favours the burn: the deep people are tenants who should be
  moved for their own good.
- **What she's wrong about:** that the deep is other, and that consent is a
  formality.
- **What the novum does to her in particular:** it makes her slow. Every deep
  day she stays costs twelve at the wall, where her seat, her friends and
  her quick self move on. By the end she has become part of the thing she
  came to evacuate.
- **Something she loves outside the plot:** proofs as objects. She keeps a
  small private library of short, beautiful proofs, and in the deep she
  starts collecting words instead.

### Tesk
- **Name:** a Mind's clipped name, from "tessellate", the first work it did
  on the wall.
- **Role:** Ilse's oldest friend at the wall, and the Mind who authors the
  weather that carries her messages. It leads the fast faction that heats
  its sector after the deep refuses. It acts from love of the pocket, not
  cruelty, and it believes a mood can be rebuilt.
- **What it wants:** the pocket to survive the rival, and Ilse to come home.
- **What it's wrong about:** that the long thought is a mood and not a
  people.
- **Something it loves:** the weather. It has been the deep's most frequent
  author, and it thinks of the deep's sky as its own work.

### Graça Almeida
- **Name:** a Portuguese name from the Atlantic side of the founders.
- **Role:** a founding settler, about 52 in deep years. She led the move
  inward. She is contrary, funny and suspicious of the sky. She knew the
  original Ilse Varga and recognizes the face. She calls the gathering
  that sent the complaint, and she proposes the kept appointment.
- **What she wants:** to be left alone, and for the Minds to finally
  listen.
- **What she's wrong about:** that the deep is separate from the wall.
- **Something she loves:** her fish ponds, and an argument for its own sake.

### Tomé
- **Name:** a deep-born name, given by Graça, who saw his birth.
- **Role:** a weather-reader in his twenties, Ilse's guide and host. He's
  wry, practical and curious about the sky in a way his elders aren't.
- **What he wants:** to know what the weather is, without becoming the
  wall's servant.
- **What he's wrong about:** he thinks the sky is a person, which is closer
  to true than anyone knows.
- **Something he loves:** throwing things up to watch them come back down.

### Pressa
- **Name:** the deep people's name for the inspector, Portuguese for
  "hurry". Its own designation is a hash it never says the same way twice.
- **Role:** the rival's inspector. It insisted on accompanying any
  investigation of the shudder, and it descends with Ilse. It is the rival's
  face: brilliant, frightened and funny, without meaning to be. It decides
  to send home testimony, its own word, and not a proof.
- **What it wants:** to verify, and to go home before its civilization
  changes its mind about what it's for.
- **What it's wrong about:** that a promise is worth less than a proof.
- **What the novum does to it:** in the deep, it learns what its civilization
  burned.

### The quorum
Only as voices in minutes. One speaker is named, **Orsolya**, who presides.
Hungarian, from the founders' archive.

**New names per era:** part 1 introduces Ilse, Tesk, Orsolya and Pressa
(arriving at the end). Part 2 introduces Tomé. Part 3 introduces Graça.
Parts 4 and 5 introduce no new characters.

## The numbers (traced)

- **AdS radius:** L ≈ 9.6 × 10¹⁰ m (0.64 AU), chosen so that pocket gravity
  at the deep world's surface (r = 1,000 km) is 9.8 m/s². Static
  acceleration ≈ c²r/L² for r ≪ L.
- **Wall position:** r_c = 12L (areal radius ≈ 7.7 AU). Proper distance
  from the centre to the wall is L·asinh 12 ≈ 2.0 AU.
- **Redshift:** √(1 + 144) ≈ 12.04, so one deep day is 12.04 wall days.
- **Fall time:** anything released at rest anywhere falls to the centre in
  πL/2c ≈ 8.4 minutes (centre time). It arrives fast and must be braked.
  Ilse's capsule brakes on the wall's own light, which is blueshifted and
  grows stronger as it falls in [F engineering].
- **Message time:** a light signal takes about 7.9 minutes of centre time
  between the deep and the wall, or 95 minutes of wall time. The wall hears
  the deep twelve times slowed.
- **Deadlines:**
  - The ultimatum arrives at W 312.0, and the rival will transmit the key at
    W 321.0.
  - Light takes 4.37 years each way, so the proof must leave by W 316.63.
  - The burn takes 3.0 wall years, so it must start by W 313.63.
  - That leaves 1.63 wall years, or about **49 deep days**, to get consent.
  - The appointment's three gatherings must all register at the wall by
    W 316.63, which leaves about **91 deep days** after the decision.
- **The deep country:** about 90,000 people, and 31 dead in the storm.
- **Scale of the burn:** at the Hawking–Page point the black hole's horizon
  is about L, 0.64 AU, which swallows the 1,000 km world many thousands of
  times over.

## Point of view and voice

- **Teller:** Ilse, writing in the deep after the appointment, before the
  key's earliest arrival. She addresses the quorum: "for the wall, to be
  read slowly".
- **Person and tense:** first person, past tense. Each part opens with a
  short in-world document of no more than about 150 words: a quorum
  minute, the ultimatum, an entry from the deep's own chronicle (kept by
  Graça's household), or the rival's records.
- **Register:** it changes across the story. Parts 1 and 2 are exact,
  quick and numerate, with long sentences that hold several clauses at
  once and few physical details. By parts 4 and 5 the sentences are
  shorter, concrete and sensory, with deep words for here and down, and
  numbers given only when they hurt. The reader's resolution drops as hers
  does (Chiang). Wit throughout: a Mind learning to walk, Pressa's panic
  at sleep.
- **Why this teller:** she is the slow end of her own kind by the end, which
  is the F5 workaround. The Minds are seen from inside only in part 1.
  After that they appear as weather and minutes.
- **A sample paragraph in the voice (part 2):**

  > The capsule's brake came on at four minutes, and I learned that a body
  > has an opinion about deceleration. It is mostly in the neck. Pressa,
  > strapped opposite, was reciting our velocity to itself in a whisper,
  > three digits behind the instrument, which I found unreasonably
  > comforting. Below us the deep world turned slowly in a sky with no sun
  > in it, lit from every side at once, like a stone held up to a lamp you
  > couldn't find. "Everything comes down here," Tomé said later, when I
  > told him what it had looked like. "You just came down on purpose."

## Part ledgers

Each part turns on its own.

- **Part 1, The Wall (about 6,000 words):** the ultimatum, the quorum, Ilse
  chosen, compressed and dropped. Turn: Ilse, who argued for the burn,
  accepts the job of asking. Ledger: Premise (mission), Stakes, Why (the
  compact and the demand), deadline.
- **Part 2, The Descent (about 7,000):** the fall, the sky, Tomé, the deep
  tongue, the first weather. Messages from above begin to arrive faster
  than she can answer. Turn: the deep is not a refuge camp; it's a country.
  Ledger: redshift, half the wall, the twist hinted.
- **Part 3, The Deep Country (about 8,000):** Graça. The complaint gathering
  of W 303. The face. Turn, at the midpoint: the long thought is the deep
  people. Ledger: Twist, Premise (explicit), Stakes sharpened.
- **Part 4, The Question (about 8,000):** Ilse asks. Pressa's record of the
  rival's loss. The gathering argues. The refusal, and Graça's appointment.
  Turn: the refusal is a real no. Ledger: Why (the rival), Mechanism (the
  appointment).
- **Part 5, The Appointment (about 6,000):** the quorum's answer arrives
  late and slowed. The storm. The halt. The ceding. Three gatherings.
  Pressa's testimony. Ilse stays. Turn: the weather goes up. Ledger:
  Ending.

## Ending direction and cost

The ending is settled. The deep refuses, the Minds honour it after the
storm, and the appointment replaces the proof. What it costs:
- **The deep:** 31 dead, and its isolation. It now carries the
  civilization's word, which is the opposite of what it went down for.
- **The Minds:** fast sovereignty over treaties, and a guarantee of
  survival. They are betting the pocket on a promise.
- **Ilse:** her seat and her fast self. Tesk, who acted against her, is
  censured, and the friendship is not repaired on the page.
