## The one thing

Make the stair rule consistent. Proof step 4 and the cession both say "every use of it lights it end to end". Part 2 shows a cargo use as "a bead of whiter light sliding along the thread", and 5.1 contrasts the storm with "Not the bead of a cargo." The rule is stated twice and contradicted by the story's own pictures. Say "every use is visible, from the deep and from outside", and let the bead count as visible. Step 4's weight rests on this. Structural, but a one-clause fix.

## Notes

1. **Fair play, Part 1 (survived):** every plant is in place and in order.
   - The compact is quoted, with "in a body".
   - Looking spends the key, moving doesn't, and nobody has tested either.
   - The seven reasons, with the slower music, come before 3.4.
   - The 95 minutes and "light is slow down there" are in the Key Office minute.
   - The founding glow ("brighter than a few thousand suns") is the only plant that the stair is loud to outsiders. Keep it.
   - Pressa's "Our sky is warm" and "eleven" re-decisions are planted.
2. **Calendar (survived):** the chain 321.0, 316.63, 313.63 and 49 days checks (1.63 years × 365 / 12 ≈ 49.6). The 33 waking days are re-derived on the cart, and the "six of my days" lag matches half a deep day. Graça's 25.17 × 12.04 gives 303.0. Bálint's eleven years is 132 wall years.
3. **Geometry (line-level):** three figures disagree.
   - Half the circumference is 3.3 light-hours.
   - Pressa gives about two hours near limb to far.
   - Part 1 says "three light-hours of skin away".
   - A reader will not notice, but a Logician would. Add "the long way round" to the 3.3.
4. **Ultimatum paragraph, Part 1 (line-level):** the first reader lost the shudder year against the message year (lost-list 3). One paragraph carries W 303, W 312, 4⅓ years, the key and W 321.0. Split it after "unmakes everything for light-years".
5. **The rival watches the stair (line-level):** step 5 says "the rival watches our heat and our stair". Nothing says the rival sees inside a sealed pocket, except the forty-year glow. Add a clause to step 5 pointing back to that glow.
6. **The "attribution beside the vote" fix (landed):** "That isn't a warning... they would know, past any doubt, whose act it was." The 72-day line is right. This is the cession's real logic, and now it can be felt.
7. **Proof, conclusion first (landed):** "if the whole wall keeps in step, and stays cold, the change came from the deep." The five steps then read as support.

## Is it structurally done?

Yes, once the stair clause (the one thing) is fixed. No further restructuring is needed. The remaining lost-list is hard-SF density, which line edits can thin and structure can't. The real cost is length: the First Reader found 5.3 the dullest stretch. That's a polish decision, not a structural one.
