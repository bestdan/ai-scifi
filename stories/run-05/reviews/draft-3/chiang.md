## Notes
1. **Line-level. "I should say what we were..." (the Mind/wall/pocket paragraph).** This is still the glossary, dropped straight after the best banter. The reader called it a "ball with minds on its surface". Keep two sentences. Fold "frost on a pane" in and cut the rest.
2. **Line-level. The rival paragraph ("The message said that our whole wall had changed state...").** It carries W 303, W 312, 4.33 years and W 321.0 in one block. The reader "lost track of which year the shudder happened". Say "nine years ago" and leave the clock to the single date chain.
3. **Line-level. The long-thought paragraph and the engineering fact.** The plan asked for one clause, and it is still two paragraphs. The 95-minute clause is the only plant needed.
4. **Line-level. "Heat is weather."** The reader couldn't tell whether Tesk was joking, which is right until Part 5. Leave it. But "It's a great deal of weather" is the cleaner beat, so cut the explanatory sentence before it.
5. **Structural, minor. "Part 2, dictated reports ('I said: ...')."** The reader found them dull twice. They are not Part 1, so I leave them to the orchestrator, but they are the same lecture habit.
