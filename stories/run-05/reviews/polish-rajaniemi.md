# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi lens (density and texture)

Blind spots: I favour opacity and density, and can mistake density for depth. The First Reader outranks me on whether a line is clear. I did not check arithmetic or rulings (Asimov's part).

**Overall.** The polish kept the texture and cut slack. The explanations that remain are mostly present, not clumsy: the 3.4 identity paragraph and the 5.3 key-cession paragraph both say the mechanism plainly, as the ledger requires, and they read as Ilse's account rather than a briefing.

**Glossary.** Every glossary term is used consistently: weather, lamp, stair, long thought, gathering, *aqui-fundo*, *desce*. Terms outside the glossary are all defined by use: stay-downs, the patch, the red one, Mind, quorum, Key Office. Two notes:
- "Stay-downs" is a real coinage with a history and is missing from the glossary. Add it there; no change to the text.
- The founding glow is "a few thousand suns" in the story and "a million suns" in the bible. Keep the story's figure and correct the bible.

**Suggestions**

1. Part 5, Bálint's burial. *halottaim* is glossed in full twice, once in part 3 and again here, and the bible says the word appears once.
   `which is Hungarian, from the Varga line, and means *my dead*. The deep has no word of its own for them, so the family keeps that one.` → `*my dead*, in the family's Hungarian, the word the deep has no word for.`

2. Part 5, proof step 5. The relative clause is clumsy.
   `the rival watches our heat and our stair, whose returned light comes out through the wall, as the founding glow did.` → `the rival watches our heat, and our stair, whose light shows through the wall as the founding glow did.`

3. Part 5, the added line. It is correct but flat, and it separates the minute from "the moment the Minds paid".
   `The slowest part of itself was the deep. That was the moment the Minds paid.` → `The slowest part of itself was the deep, and that was what the Minds paid.`

4. Part 2, a familiar detail that one word can make stranger.
   `a long warm sheet that smelled of bread dough` → `a long warm sheet that smelled of proving dough`

No other changes. The prose is dense but swimmable, and I did not find a place where the reader is drowning.
