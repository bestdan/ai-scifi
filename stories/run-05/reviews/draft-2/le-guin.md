## The one thing
Ilse's account of the burning still stops one beat short. The storm kills Bálint, Maria and thirty-one others, and the censure falls on Tesk alone. The Minds then wait ninety-six days. Those 31 names are the price of the Minds' way of deciding. The deep gets Tesk's punishment but no say in it, and Graça's "Good" at the appointment is left unexplained. The story is nearest to Omelas here. Let someone in the deep, Graça or the thatcher, tell Ilse what a censure is worth to a burned family, instead of Ilse telling us. Cut her closing gloss on the cost.

## Notes

1. **Structural, Part 1 opening:** The cold reader says they "would not have kept reading" past page 2. The ultimatum is a block of "W 312.0", "pocket" and "kernel". The definitions that follow are good ("a song happens in the air of a room"). Consider putting the Dita scene first and telling the wall afterwards, as the plan noted. If that is refused, cut the heat arithmetic by half. "Twelve times a hundred is twelve hundred" is enough.
2. **Structural, the cession and consent:** This is much better. The deep votes and the thatcher's "We'd be keeping their gun for them. In our kitchen." is a real objection. One gap remains. The deep took the key "by about twelve hundred stones out of eighty-two thousand", and the stay-downs are bound for good. Ilse's "Offer. Not give." is her only acknowledgement. Show the thatcher paying, even in one sentence at the mill.
3. **Line, Ilse instructs:** "That's the tax. They pay it at every fire now." Graça already said it: "Every quarrel we have from now on, somebody at another star is going to read it." Cut Ilse's gloss. Also cut "I'll say this once, because it's true" before the evening-light paragraph, and the "I think the sky had asked" explanation, which is lovely and stays because it is a guess.
4. **Line, tic:** Rosa is "bored" about nine times. It was a joke, then a character, and by Part 5 it is a tic. Let her be bored twice. The line "I don't believe her, and I think she meant me not to" works because it breaks the pattern.
5. **Line, language:** *Aqui-fundo* is the best invention here, a word the wall lacks and the deep has. "Halottaim" is still glossed twice, once at Part 3 and again at Part 5. Let Rosa say it unglossed.
6. **Line, the long red:** the Part 2 passage about the sky "choosing not to leave yet" and the signature is the best thing in the wall's relation to the deep. Don't touch it. But "We author their days" a paragraph later says it again, so cut that sentence.

## What to protect
- "You're purple," and Dita's "Stay," then running to the other one.
- "Ameixa… That's from up there." The naming by the eldest present.
- Tomé: "To see it come down." And "You know what they meant. I know what it says."
- "They had not reported the sky. They had answered it. The deep tongue had no word for that, and so they bent the nearest one."
- Bálint at the red: "You've got her face wrong at the mouth." Graça: "Don't. Not in that face."
- Maria asleep mid-sentence: "and never sent." And the goat's abstention.
- The ending leaving open whether the plum-coloured light was a reply: "We'll need a word for that."

## Blind spot disclosure
I do not check the physics, the dates or the redshift sums, and I took them on trust. I favour gatherings and domestic detail, so I will overrate Parts 3 and 4 and underrate the wall's logic. My dislike of instruction cuts against a narrator who is meant to teach "the wall". Let Egan and Asimov push back.
