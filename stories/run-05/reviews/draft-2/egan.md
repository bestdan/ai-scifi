## Notes

1. **[structural] 5.3 "We can touch nothing at the centre precisely without running hot. To take the key back, the wall would have to write at the centre, and that is a burn."**
   - Ilse herself, and the key, were written precisely at the centre cheaply, by the stair.
   - So the stated barrier is false in the story's own terms. The wall could send a Mind down in a body, by the compact's own rule that whoever asks "goes down and asks it there", to walk to the mill. Or it could ask the deep to carry the key back up.
   - A retrieval is a stair event. The stair is the brightest line on the wall, and the storm showed it "lit end to end", visible to a watcher. The true barrier is that anything crossing the stair is loud, plus Dita's rule and the deep's refusal. Say that instead of "a burn".
   - The same gap hits proof step 4 (5.2): "the cheapest way known to write at the centre a change that size costs about what the burn costs". Sending ninety thousand bodies down the stair is the obvious cheaper forger. One clause fixes it: stair traffic of that size is visible as light at the stair, and a real gathering shows none. This is the next logical step I wanted.

2. **[line] 5.3 "The rival can't be warned. The light of a gathering and the key it sent would arrive together."**
   - Attribution has landed, and the "watch it build for months" error is gone. But "together" is wrong by about a quarter-year. A six-day gathering is 72 wall days, and the rise starts at the start.
   - Write: "They would see the gathering a quarter-year before the key, and could do nothing with it, since the only answer to a key is a key, and theirs is already four years from us."

3. **[line] 5.3 "To send the key to Alpha Centauri, the deep would have to gather, decide it, and carry it to the foot of the stair."**
   - The key then arrives at the wall, and a Mind must aim it. So the deep's decision is necessary and not sufficient.
   - That is the stronger claim, because both must agree. Say "only with the deep's consent", not "only from a whole people". The attribution point is still true.

4. **[line] 3.5 "I asked how long she'd been here. 'Eleven years.'"**
   - Bálint is 47 and was 24 when she came down, so she was here about 23 years. Eleven is the time since her death.
   - Ask "how long she'd been gone".

5. **[line] 5.2 step 5, "No forgery could be finished inside the window".**
   - I raised this in round 1 and it is unchanged. A slow forger spreads the same heat over years, against a millikelvin-flat baseline. Say "any forgery, at any speed, runs a fever".

6. **[line] 1.4 and 3.4 geometry.** A 2 AU stair is 1,000 s of flat light-time, but the centre-to-wall light takes "ninety-five minutes". Asimov flagged this in round 1 and it is still open. Both can be right if light is slow in the deep. Add four words, "light is slow down there", or the careful reader will trip.
