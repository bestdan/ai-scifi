# Graded ledger rows: run-05, draft 1

| Row | The fact |
|---|---|
| Premise | A machine mind (Ilse) is sent down, in a grown body, to the inhabited world at the centre of a pocket universe her civilization runs from its wall. Her task is to get the people there to consent to a computation that would cook their world. She learns that their lives, seen from the wall, are her own civilization's slow, shared mood (the long thought). So the question becomes whether a mind may burn its slowest part to win an argument. |
| Stakes | If the deep agrees, or is overridden, its world cooks and the Minds lose their long thought. If the deep refuses, the Minds honour it, and the rival isn't satisfied, the rival sends its key and the whole pocket is closed. Ilse personally risks her seat, her return to the wall, and her fast self. |
| Why | The rival pocket demands a proof that the wall can't fail because it saw the whole wall shudder and feared it was failing. Ilse conjectures, from one example, that it also wants a proof because, having burned its own deep, it can no longer keep or trust a promise. The proof needs the whole wall running hot for three years, and the heat falling inward would cook the deep. The Minds must ask consent in person because the founding compact gives the deep self-rule. |
| Twist | The shudder that frightened the rival was the deep's own gathering, complaining that the days were too short for the trees. The Minds' long thought is the deep country seen from the wall. The rival burned its own deep a century earlier. |
| Ending | The deep refuses, narrowly. Tesk heats its sector to drive them up the stair, and the storm kills 31, including Bálint. The Minds halt it, reject striking first with their key, let the burn window pass, and give the pocket's word and the key to the deep. In place of a proof, the deep holds three gatherings on announced dates, as a promise the rival can check. Pressa sends its own testimony. Ilse stays. The rival's answer is unknown. |
