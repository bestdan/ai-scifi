# Interim check, parts 1-3: Asimov lens

Blind spots: I read for rules and arithmetic, not for feel, voice or the body.

1. **[must-fix] Part 1 says the demand can be met; the outline says it can't.** 1.1: "the rival had bounded the demand for us... That could be done, by brute force", and "the rival read it and put the key away." The outline's 4.1 ("Rice... the demand is a trap") then contradicts the page. Fix: in 1.1 keep the burn as real but one-off ("put the key away, that time"). Plant the gap that Pressa half-states in 3.3 ("A proof about your wall. Not about your shudder"). 4.1's argument is then that a proof of the program never answers the fear, so the rival can demand again. Change the outline's "can't be met" to "can't be met for good".

2. **[must-fix] The Key Office rule is stated but its point isn't.** 1.2: "the key may not be examined during a vote about it", and the three fluent reasons are a joke. 5.3's blind vote leans on it. A cold reader doesn't know which key (the rival's, held by the Office) or what examining would permit or reveal. Fix: in 1.2 make one of the three reasons concrete. For example, a key read is a key half-sent, so it can't be looked at while a vote could tempt a Mind to use it. Then say what blindness costs: the quorum must decide on the rival's threat without inspecting anything.

3. **[must-fix] Arithmetic.**
   - 1.1 and 1.2 give half the wall's circumference as "3.3 light-hours", and the far side as "three light-hours of skin away". The stair is 2 AU (2.2), so half the circumference is about 52 light-minutes. Change both figures, and the "3.3 hours" assembly.
   - 3.2: Tesk's "tired of it" was said "a little over two of the wall's months before". Day 9 is about 108 wall days, so write "three and a half".
   - Outline: "W 313.60 (day 47)" is day 48.5, and the 49-day window is really 49.4. Day 74 for W 314.5 should be about 76. Fix the table before part 5 cites any of these.

4. **[should] Bálint's mother's timeline.** 3.5: "I asked how long she'd been here. 'Eleven years.'" But she "came down so I wouldn't come down alone. I was twenty-three", and he is "about fifty" in 3.1. That fits a founding settler who died eleven years ago, though the line reads as if she arrived eleven years ago. It also clashes with 2.1, where settlers "stopped coming before anyone there was born". Fix: "How long has she been under the stone?" Also reconcile 3.1's "This year's had never come" with 3.2's "The trees are in flower again now".

5. **[should] Ilse has no kernel to write her proof against.** 1.4 lists the proofs she leaves behind and the "about forty" she carries. The kernel's 4,106 lines never travel with her, and Pressa carries only the record. Fix: in 1.4, have Ilse take the kernel by knowing it, alongside the forty. Add that she holds it in the slower head only in part. 5.2 can then cost her something real.

Not a problem: day counts (46, 36, 29 left), the lag figures (7.9 and 16 minutes), the Tolman 12.04 and the 24 K to 289 K conversion, and W 303 = 25.17 × 12.04 all check out. The storm's heat and Bálint's grave are planted fairly.
