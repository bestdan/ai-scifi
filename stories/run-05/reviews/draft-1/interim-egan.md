# Interim check, parts 1-3 (Egan lens)

Blind spots: I check numbers and logic, not scene, feeling or readability. Let the First Reader and Chiang weigh the fixes against pace.

Checked and correct: 12.04 redshift; 24 K to 289 K and 100 K to 1,204 K; ~1e18 J/kg; 7.9 min / 95 min; 3.3 h; 2.0 AU (300 million km); 1.8 km horizon; the +0.76% summit figure; the sleep-adjusted day counts; the 4 min to 20 s and 3 h to 15 min conversions.

1. **[must-fix] The midpoint identity contradicts the bible's wedge limit and itself.**
   - Part 3: "every Mind carries the whole deep inside it as a shadow." The line before it says "every *large* region." A Mind is a small patch. Per the bible, only about half the wall or more holds the centre.
   - "They are the same thing, described two ways" also clashes with "about ninety-five minutes later" and Graça's "So it heard." An identity has no latency.
   - Fix: any half of the wall holds the whole deep, and a single Mind holds a blurred share. The change is in the correlations at once, and each patch feels it only when the bulk light arrives, 95 minutes on. Also soften "nobody ever will" to "nobody can afford to": the bible allows it in principle.

2. **[must-fix] The date match fails by Ilse's own tolerance.**
   - Graça: "Day nine or ten" of the third month gives 25.19 deep years, which is W 303.3. Ilse's sum uses only the two months (25.17), and the rival dated the shudder "to a tenth of a year."
   - "Six weeks" before that date also doesn't fit plums that failed in month 2.
   - Fix: "Day two or three" (W 303.1), and "I took six days over it. They still say a season." Check every place that uses the six weeks.
   - Separately, Ilse tells the table "nine of our years ago they had seen our whole wall move." The shudder was nine years ago, and they saw it at W 307.37, about 4.8 years ago. Fix: "nine of our years ago our wall moved, and they saw it four years later."

3. **[should] Pressa's forgery test overreaches.**
   - "Everything you could fake would cost you a fever" is too broad. Lamp changes are cheap, cold and whole-wall, though they point inward. Say "anything written at the centre."
   - "Exponential work" means time or circuit size, not necessarily power. A slow forger stays cool. Give a number: the cheapest known forgery costs the burn's whole wall for years.
   - "In step" is judged against a sixty-day rise, so Pressa should say it means agreement to minutes. The wall is 15.4 AU across, so the near and far limbs differ by about 128 light-minutes, which has to be removed first. A down-and-up route through the stair takes 3.2 h, under the wall's 3.3 h. That undercuts "nothing spreads faster."

4. **[should] Small numbers and the founding energy.**
   - "Three years, which were our first forty" should be three and a third (3 × 12.04 = 36).
   - The pull is "ten and a half" where the bible has 10.6.
   - Unflagged: the world's ~1e22 kg (from the 0.84) returns ~1e39 J as light over forty wall years. That is ~1e30 W, about a million Suns, on a 24 K wall. Either name where the stair's light goes (out of the pocket) or shrink the world.
