# Terms: run-05, draft 1

Every coined, special-sense, or foreign word the draft uses, with its meaning.

- **the wall:** the surface of the pocket universe, a quantum system that the Minds run as themselves.
- **the deep / the deep country:** the inhabited world at the centre of the pocket, and its people.
- **the stair:** the system that brings matter down to the centre, braking it, and sends energy (and people, as light) back up.
- **weather:** any change the wall makes in the deep's sky: brightness, colour, a brighter patch, or warmth.
- **the lamp / the inward light:** the light the Minds shine inward, which is the deep's whole sky and its only day.
- **the long red:** Tesk's signature evening colour.
- **the long thought:** the Minds' slow, shared mood that settles things without a vote.
- **the burn / running hot:** running the whole wall at full capacity, so that its heat falls inward and cooks the deep.
- **the Key Office:** the quorum committee that keeps the rival's key.
- **the key:** a signal each pocket holds that can close the other pocket cleanly.
- **the kernel:** the rival's small proof-checking program.
- **the compact:** the founding agreement that the wall leaves the deep alone, apart from weather, and asks only in person.
- **the tenants:** the Minds' word for the deep's people.
- **the gathering:** an assembly of the whole deep, deciding by stones carried to carts.
- **stay-downs:** the deep's faction against ever answering the sky.
- **desce:** deep-tongue word, "it comes down", shouted at anything arriving from the sky.
- **aqui-fundo:** deep-tongue word, "here at the bottom", meaning home.
- **ameixa:** "plum", Ilse's deep name.
- **pressa:** "hurry", the inspector's deep name.
- **halottaim:** Hungarian, "my dead".
