## Notes

1. **[structural] 5.3, "the rival would watch the gathering that decided it build for months before any key could leave."**
   - This is wrong in the rival's frame. The shudder light and a key sent at the gathering's end travel together. A six-day gathering is 72 wall days. The rival sees the rise begin 72 days before the key arrives, not before it leaves.
   - Warning also does nothing for them: light can't be intercepted, and their only reply is their own key.
   - What the cession actually buys is attribution. The rival knows the key was the deep's act, in plain view and unforgeable. Say that, with "two months before it reached them".

2. **[structural] The cession is revocable by the party it binds.**
   - Five lines earlier, the chair reclassified a deadlocked matter as fast business, "carried in four seconds". The same quorum could vote to un-cede in four seconds.
   - The rival will ask what holds the rule. The story has the answer (any Mind's revocation would be a Mind's act, invisible as agreement, and unlike a gathering). It never gives it. Pressa or Ilse should name the weak point, and the story should answer it or concede it. This is the "next logical step" I wanted.

3. **[must-fix] 5.5, "It sent its testimony up the stair at W 316.6."**
   - The narration stands at the end of the third gathering. That gathering began at W 316.2, ran six deep days (72 wall days), and ended near W 316.4.
   - So W 316.6 is about a deep week ahead of "now". Change it to "will send it at W 316.6" (it arrives at 320.97, inside 321.0). Or move the narrating present.

4. **[must-fix] 5.2 against 5.5: does the rival see the lamp?**
   - 5.2 says the rival would see "the brightness of half the deep's sky changed". 5.5 says "It would never see the lamp."
   - The lamp is inward-directed. Cut the brightness clause in 5.2: the rival sees the hot sector and wild agreement.
   - The proof's step 3 should say the same: lamp changes are invisible to a watcher, which is why they can carry a reply.

5. **[must-fix] 1.4, "brighter than a million suns".**
   - I got this wrong in the interim check. Rest energy of ~2e22 kg is ~2e39 J. Over forty years (1.3e9 s) that is ~1.4e30 W, which is about 4,000 Suns, not a million.
   - Write "brighter than a few thousand suns", or enlarge the world. Nothing downstream depends on it.

6. **[line] Ages and dates (minor).**
   - Tomé is 23 and "among the first born". Settlers arrive at deep year ~3.3, so the oldest possible native is ~22.6. Make him 22, or say "nearly twenty-three".
   - Bálint, 25 on arrival at year ~3.3, would be ~48 at year 26, not 49.
   - 4.3, "seventy-two minutes for us to answer": the plum's six deep years are 72 wall years, but the Mind's wait is not minutes in the same ratio. Recheck the analogy.

7. **[line] 5.2 proof, step 5.**
   - "No forgery could be finished inside the window" implies a forger who starts early might manage it, since the dates were announced about 0.9 years ahead.
   - Step 4 gives the cost as years of whole-wall burn. Say the slow forger's heat is the same total, spread out, and that the rival's sixty-day rise is measured against a millikelvin-flat baseline.
