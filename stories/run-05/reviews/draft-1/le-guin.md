## The one thing
Ask the deep about the cession. The Minds give the pocket's word to "a gathering of the deep" (5.3) in a vote the deep never sits in. Ilse's whole case is that consent must be asked in person, yet the story's final transfer of burden is imposed on ninety thousand people, children included. Graça's "Good" (4.5) shows she half-sees it. Make the story see it too, or make Ilse say aloud that she has done to them again what the thatcher warned of.

## Notes

1. **Structural, Part 1 (the sums and the Key Office wait):** The first reader nearly stopped here ("lots of letters, sums and unfamiliar words"). The people who make this world worth living in don't appear until Dita in Part 2. Cut the deadline arithmetic to one pass and the seven causes to three. Move the "I'm in a body" promise up front, or put one child on page 1.
2. **Structural, Part 5 (the gatherings):** The best material, the carrier-bag thing, is the fourth-day gathering in Part 4: fires, food, who minds children, Maria asleep mid-sentence. The three appointed gatherings in Part 5 are told in recap. The goat gathering deserves a scene at that density. The Minds' vote and the announcement can be shorter.
3. **Structural, who pays:** The cost is visible: the 31 names, Bálint, Maria, Tesk barred from its own craft. But the burden of the key now sits on the deep's children for good. The "tax" paragraph (the stopped quarrel, "Not now. They're reading.") is the right size. Extend it from the cession.
4. **Line, Ilse explains too much:** "I'll say it again here, for the wall, as plainly as I can" (3.4); "That's the tax" (5.4); the closing "I know it was given in their way". The reader had it already from Graça's "So we're your mood." Cut the instruction. This is my own blind spot at work, so weigh it.
5. **Line, Pressa's trap paragraph (4.1):** Dense and abstract, and the first reader did not follow it. Keep "We can't take anyone's word for anything. Not even our own." Break the rest with a gesture or a laundry beat.
6. **Line, language:** The best invented work is what the deep cannot say. Check that "Desce!" and "Pressa" are glossed on first use. "Halottaim" is glossed twice, with a repeated gloss in 5.2. Let Rosa say it unglossed there.

## What to protect
- The bent verb: "They had not reported the sky. They had answered it. The deep tongue had no word for that, and so they bent the nearest one."
- Tomé: "To see it come down," and "You know what they meant. I know what it says. Down here those are two different things."
- Bálint: "You've got her face wrong at the mouth," and Graça: "Don't. Not in that face."
- Naming by the eldest present at a birth, and the vote with stones with its known weighing error.
- Maria asleep mid-sentence, waking to say "and never sent."
- The goat's abstention. Keep it funny.
- The ending refusing to decide whether the plum-coloured light was a reply.

## Blind spot disclosure
I am weak on the physics and the redshift arithmetic. I did not audit the dates and took them as the first reader did, on trust. I favour slow gatherings and domestic detail, so I will overrate Part 4 and underrate the wall's side. My dislike of instruction (note 4) cuts against a first-person narrator who is explicitly teaching "the wall". Let Banks, Egan and Watts push back.
