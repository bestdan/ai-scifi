# Named characters: run-05, draft 1

- **Ilse** (also called **Ameixa** in the deep): the narrator. A machine mind of the governing quorum on the wall, sent down in a grown body.
- **Tesk:** Ilse's oldest friend on the wall, and the main author of the deep's weather.
- **Orsolya:** the Mind who presides over the quorum.
- **Pressa:** the rival pocket's inspector, sent down beside Ilse.
- **Dita (Benedita):** a nine-year-old girl in the deep who finds and names Ilse.
- **Tomé:** a young weather-reader in the deep, Ilse's guide.
- **Graça Almeida:** head of the pond house household.
- **Bálint Varga:** Graça's husband, son of the woman whose face Ilse wears.
- **Rosa:** Graça and Bálint's fifteen-year-old daughter.
- **Maria do Céu:** a plum-seller and orchard-keeper.
