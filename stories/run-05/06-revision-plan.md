## Structural items

### 1. Part 1 is too dense (consensus: First Reader, Chiang, Le Guin, Cartographer, Asimov)

The reader was "on the edge" until Dita appeared, and lost entries 1–5 all
sit in part 1. Rewrite part 1 to about 4,000 words (from 5,986):

- **a. First page, plain words.** Before anything else, establish in plain
  words what a Mind is, what the wall is, what the pocket is, and where the
  deep is. One concrete image each. The reader should know all four by the
  end of the first page.
- **b. The deadline, plainly.** Say once, in two or three sentences, that
  the wall has nine years, that a deep day is twelve of the wall's, and
  that the deep has forty-nine days. Keep W 316.63 and W 313.63 on the
  page, since Asimov needs them planted, but give the derivation in one
  short paragraph without fanfare, so the arithmetic carries the plot
  rather than slowing it.
- **c. The Key Office, down to one short paragraph.** Keep:
  - the 3.3-hour assembly joke;
  - the rule that the key may not be examined during a vote about it;
  - the true reason (the key is a quantum state, examining it spends it,
    so nobody has ever tested either key);
  - one new clause, which sets up item 3: *moving it doesn't spend it,
    only looking.*

  Cut the "the rest… the joke" sentence (lost entry 3).
- **d. The seven causes, down to one sentence** naming two or three of
  them, with Ilse's "slower music". This resolves the conflict between
  Watts, who keeps the confabulation, and the reader, Chiang and
  Cartographer, who found it homework: Watts keeps the point and the list
  goes.
- **e. The long thought gets one concrete early example** (a fashion that
  lasts, a grudge that ends without anyone deciding), so it isn't vague
  for two parts (lost entry 4).
- **f. Tesk's lesson trimmed by about a third.** Keep "coldly" and
  "suddenly", the long red, the joke, the fourth construction, and "There
  won't be a you up here".
- **g. Explain the interruption device** in one sentence at its first use
  (lost entry 5).

### 2. Why (missed), so restate it plainly where it matters (comprehension)

The reader's answer gave the compact and the keys "as a mutual safeguard",
but not why the rival demands a proof. Make three things explicit, each in
plain words, each once, in addition to the opener:
- **a. At the 3.1 table,** Ilse to the household: *they saw our wall move
  as one, thought it meant our wall was failing, and a failing wall would
  kill them, so they want proof it can't fail. The only way to make that
  proof is to run the wall hot for three years, and the heat comes here.*
- **b. In 4.1,** Ilse's conjecture in one plain sentence, marked as hers:
  *they've burned their own deep, and since then they can't keep or trust a
  promise, which is why a proof is the only thing they'll take.*
- **c. In 4.5,** the decision rule as now.

### 3. The cession: consent, logic, mechanism (consensus: Le Guin, Chiang, Asimov, Egan; lost entry 11)

- **a. The deep is asked** (Le Guin). The quorum offers the cession; it
  doesn't impose it. The first appointed gathering (W 314.5) is the deep
  deciding whether to take the key and the pocket's word at all, a full
  scene at part 4's density, with the stay-downs against it ("we came down
  to be left alone"). It passes narrowly. The trees argument moves to the
  third gathering.
- **b. The mechanism** (Egan's revocability note, Asimov's temptation
  note). The key is a quantum state. It can be moved, not copied, and
  looking at it spends it.
  - The quorum offers to send the key itself down the stair to the deep,
    to be held at the bottom, in the mill, by the deep.
  - The wall can't touch anything at the centre precisely without running
    hot, so taking the key back would mean a visible burn. That is why
    the Minds can't simply un-cede.
  - Sending it to the rival would need a gathering to decide it and send
    it up the stair.
- **c. What it buys,** stated plainly and placed beside the first-strike
  vote, not after it (Chiang).
  - The peace never rested on retaliation, because a closed pocket can't
    answer. It rested on neither side striking first.
  - After the cession, our first strike can't come from a fast faction, an
    accident or a mood. It can come only from a whole people, deciding in
    the open, cold and in step, in a form nobody can forge.
  - The rival will know any key is the deep's act. That is attribution,
    not warning. Light and key travel together, so warning is impossible.
    Cut "watch the gathering build for months before any key could leave"
    (Egan).
- **d. The gamble, stated honestly** (Asimov's one thing). The appointment
  doesn't meet the rule, and the page says plainly that it is a gamble,
  then why it's a fair one:
  - Everything in it the kernel can check: the cold-shudder proof, and
    Pressa's inference from its observations.
  - Except one thing: that Pressa saw what it says it saw.
  - So the rival is asked to accept exactly one promise, from its own
    inspector.

  Ilse or Pressa says this in 5.3, in about three sentences.

### 4. Premise (partial): state the question in plain words (comprehension)

The reader framed it as "asking them to leave" and never named the real
question. Put one plain line in 3.4, after the identity, spoken to Graça
and Pressa: *so the question was never whether you'd leave. It was whether
we'd burn the slowest part of ourselves to win an argument.* Don't repeat
it.

### 5. Stakes (partial): say both branches aloud before the climax (comprehension)

At 3.4, say it to Graça on the pond wall, not only in the dictation: if the
wall burns the deep, the Minds lose whatever holds still in them, the long
thought, and become what Alpha Centauri is. If the deep refuses, the Minds
honour it, and the rival isn't satisfied, the key ends everything. Keep the
dictation version short, or cut it, so it isn't said three times.

### 6. Restatements cut (consensus: Chiang, Le Guin, the First Reader's tallies)

- **a. "Was the no free?"** is said once, at the carts in 4.4. At the end,
  cut the restatement paragraph. Ilse may say "I still can't tell you" in
  half a sentence at most.
- **b. The 3.4 identity** gets one plain statement plus its limits. Cut the
  second, "for the wall" restatement. Fold any physics it carried (any half
  holds the whole deep; felt when the light arrives) into the one
  statement.
- **c. "Days left" tallies,** about half of them cut. Keep the ones at 2.2,
  3.4, 4.3 and 4.5.

### 7. The ending's order (Chiang)

Move the arithmetic (W 325.37, nine months), the lost seat and comets, and
Pressa's state to before the third gathering's last evening. Part 5 then
ends with the lamp, Pressa's "That was light", Tomé's "That was a reply…
We'll need a word for that", Ilse's not-knowing (one or two sentences), and
the short final paragraph (palm flat, and stay). Keep "and stay" as the
last words.

### 8. The three gatherings as scenes (Le Guin)

The first gathering is item 3a, a full scene. The children's gathering is
already a scene, so keep it. The third carries the trees, Maria's quarrel
hushed ("Not now. They're reading"), and the light. Part 5 may grow by
about 1,000 words to carry these.
