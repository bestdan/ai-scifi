## Items

### 1. Rewrite part 1 as scenes, in order (Chiang's fix; the consultant's D2 at 75%)

- **a. The first line is a one-sentence frame from the field,** something
  like "I was born on my back in a field of stubble, and before that I was
  a Mind." Then go straight to Tesk. **The ultimatum block is no longer
  page 1.** Quote only its rule clause, where Orsolya asks for the dates,
  and fold the rest into the scene. Part 1 needs no opening document. The
  other parts keep theirs.
- **b. Open on Tesk, teasing, in a fast, present-feeling scene.** Carry the
  definitions of Mind, wall, pocket and deep in the banter and the
  situation, not in a glossary. The quick world is enacted, not reported:
  short exchanges, interruptions, Minds finishing each other's sentences.
  Weather is Tesk's lesson, and it is the scene readers enjoyed both
  rounds, so it leads.
- **c. The ultimatum arrives inside that scene.** The quorum's first
  second is a short burst, with no sums.
- **d. The deadline chain is told once,** in two or three plain sentences:
  W 321.0, then W 316.63, then W 313.63, then forty-nine days.
- **e. The Key Office is cut to the lines the ledger needs:** the 3.3-hour
  joke; the key may not be examined; looking spends it, moving doesn't;
  nobody has ever tested either key.
- **f. The long thought is one clause plus one example.** The seven
  reasons are one sentence, with the slower music.
- **g. Keep the plants:**
  - the compact;
  - Ilse arguing for the burn and being chosen for it;
  - "There won't be a you up here";
  - the fork that can't stay;
  - Pressa's few lines ("Our sky is warm");
  - the stair and its founding glow;
  - the borrowed name and face;
  - the departure ("Show me where").
- **h. Length:** about 3,500–4,000 words. Put the stakes early, in one line
  (Asimov's cost note).

### 2. Terms (missed)

- **a. The W-clock.** At first use, in plain words: the wall counts years
  from its founding, and W 312 is its three hundred and twelfth.
- **b. "Agreement."** At its first use in 3.3, define it in plain words, or
  replace it there and in the 5.2 proof. For example: "how closely
  far-apart parts of the wall keep in step with each other", with "keep in
  step" as the short form afterwards.

### 3. The partial rows

- **a. Premise.** Keep the 3.4 line "whether we'd burn the slowest part of
  ourselves to win an argument". Echo it once at the culmination, in 5.3:
  the quorum's decision, or Ilse's plea, says the Minds won't burn their
  slowest part to win an argument. That is the payoff, not a restatement.
- **b. Stakes.** At 3.4, to Graça, check that both branches are there in
  two plain sentences:
  - if the wall burns the deep, the world cooks and the Minds lose the
    long thought;
  - if the deep refuses and the rival isn't satisfied, the key ends
    everything.
- **c. Why.** At the 3.1 table, check that three things are said plainly:
  - the rival feared the shudder meant failure;
  - the proof needs the whole wall hot for three years;
  - that heat falls here and cooks the deep.

### 4. The stair as the one door (Egan's one thing)

In the cession (5.3) and in proof steps 4–5, make the stair the one
precise door through the wall, and a loud one.
- **Every use lights it end to end,** visible from the deep and from
  outside. So retrieving the key, or forging a gathering, means a visible
  stair event or a burn.
- **Replace** "we can touch nothing at the centre precisely without running
  hot" with that.
- **For forgery:** a gathering's keeping in step can't be written cheaply,
  and the only cheap way to make one is to hold one.
- **Add a clause** inside existing sentences: the deep's decision is
  needed, but a Mind still aims the key from the wall.

### 5. The proof, conclusion first (Asimov)

5.2 states the conclusion first ("if the whole wall keeps in step, and
stays cold, it came from the deep"), then the five steps. Close step 5's
slow-forger gap: no forgery could be made in time without a fever or a
loud stair.

### 6. Attribution beside the vote (Asimov)

In 5.3, put the sentence that a key can now come only from a whole
people, in the open, unforgeable, directly beside the first-strike vote.
Fix "arrive together": the gathering's light reaches the rival about 72
wall days before any key could, and the rival can do nothing with that
warning. Attribution is what counts.

### 7. Who pays for a censure (Le Guin's one thing)

After the censure, Graça or the thatcher says what a censure is worth to
a burned family, in a line or two. Cut Ilse's gloss "That's the tax", and
let the fire scene ("Not now. They're reading") carry it.

### 8. Ending restatements (Chiang)

- Cut the end's "any more than I can tell you whether the no was free".
  The carts in 4.4 carry it.
- Keep only one "I'll mark this as mine" aside.
- Drop days-left tallies beyond the four that are kept.

### 9. Egan's line fixes

- **a.** Bálint's "eleven years" means since she died, not how long she
  was here. Fix the wording.
- **b.** Add one clause on the 95 minutes: light is slow down there, so a
  two-AU fall takes far longer than flat space would say.

## Declined

  consultant (75%) and the orchestrator agreed against it.
  - The flashback would have to split the field-to-door run every reader
    praised, because part 2 spends part 1's plants from its second scene

  - If a rewritten part 1 still loses the reader, the flashback is the
    next option.
- **Chasing the lost-list count.** Most entries are hard-SF content the
  reader "took on trust", and there's no cheap fix for those.
