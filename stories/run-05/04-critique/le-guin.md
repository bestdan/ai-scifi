# Le Guin lens: stage 4 critique of the bible

Blind spots, stated: I'm weak on the physics and the arithmetic, and I lean toward slowness and instruction. Egan and Watts should check the numbers. Lem and Stephenson should push back where I ask for more society than the pace allows.

1. **[must-fix] The deep has no children or old people in it, and the demography is off.** The bible says "most are second generation" among 90,000 people after only 26 deep years. That means most of the country is under 26, mostly children and teenagers. Tomé is "in his twenties", so he can't be second generation, and Graça's "founders in their fifties to eighties" gives a very narrow age band. The only children on the page are the ones shouting *desce*. Fix: put a household in the bible with a count. It needs a grandmother, a toddler, a teenager who is bored by the weather, and one meal. Give the children one job in the plot. For example, a child names Ilse, or the eldest who "saw the birth" must do the naming while the gathering is running. The dead of the storm should come out of that household.

2. **[must-fix] Who pays, and do they consent?** The bible's cost list says the deep gets "31 dead, and its isolation", then carries "the civilization's word, which is the opposite of what it went down for". Nobody asks the deep whether it wants to be the Minds' keeper of promises. The Minds' ceding of the treaty voice is a gift to the deep that it didn't request, and the bible treats it as a clean win. Give the deep a dissenting faction. Some people should say, "We came down to be left alone, not to be your conscience." Let Graça's proposal pass narrowly. Let the chronicle list the 31 by name, with ages and trades, in the part 5 opening document. Currently they are a number.

3. **[should] Tesk is a function.** It has two roles, the voice of the weather and the antagonist, and one love, the weather. The only voice from above that the deep hears is Tesk's. Make Tesk's weather *personal*: a recurring gesture in the sky that Tomé has learned to recognise. Then Tesk's heating of its sector is a betrayal of a relationship the reader has seen. Let Ilse love Tesk as a friend on the page, with a shared joke, before it burns people. The unrepaired friendship in the ending only costs something if we have seen it work.

4. **[should] The language implies less culture than the bible claims.** The consequence ladder says the deep "grows a language built around 'here' and down", and the glossary has two Portuguese words. Two words aren't a language. The stronger move is a gap: what *can't* the deep say? The taboo is "answering the weather". The deep tongue could have no word for "reply" to the sky and only "report". The Minds could have no word for "stay". Put one such gap on the page, shown by someone failing to translate. Also note that the founders are a "Danube-and-Atlantic" mix. Everything in the deep tongue is Portuguese and nothing is Hungarian. Either say why (Graça's household won the argument) or let one Hungarian word survive, for one thing the Portuguese can't name, such as the dead.

5. **[should] Ilse wears a dead woman's face and nobody asks.** The original Ilse Varga died twenty deep years ago, and Graça knew her. The Minds took her body plan from the founders' archive. Was there a descendant, a husband or a child in the deep? If so, Graça's recognition is a grief and a grievance, not just a plot beat. If there is no descendant, say why. This is the respectful-names question: borrowing the name of someone the deep buried needs a scene where the deep answers it. Give Graça one line of anger, and have it unresolved.

6. **[could] The structure is a gathering, which is good, so don't spend it on a fight.** The storm is the only conflict in the bible. Keep it short and let the three appointed gatherings carry the weight. Show one in full as an ordinary, loud, slow day: argument, food, who minds the children, someone falling asleep. That is the carrier bag, and it is also the story's climax. Then the shudder going up reads as a household's voice, not a mechanism.
