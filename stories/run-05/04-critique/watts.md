# Stage 4 critique: Watts lens

**Blind spots:** I underrate ritual and joy (the kept appointment may work better than I credit), I can't audit the AdS arithmetic (Egan's job), and I read for case-study rigor, which can flatten people.

1. **[must-fix] The ending flinches: the refusal is treated as clean consent.** The bible says Tesk has "authored" the deep's weather for three centuries and that the Minds "have been steering their own long thought without knowing it". It also says the first complaint gathering came from failing harvests. So the "no" is partly the Minds' own output, and reactance (Brehm, 1966) is a cheaper explanation for it than judgement. Meanwhile "not puppetry" is asserted, not shown. Don't resolve it. Ilse should be unable to tell authored from free, say so in her last account, and the appointment should be honoured anyway. The model is split-brain work (Sperry, Gazzaniga): the quorum's "for its good" is the left-hemisphere interpreter's confabulation, and the weather is the thin callosal channel. Let Tesk confabulate on the page when asked why the mood changed in W 303.

2. **[must-fix] The long thought has no selective logic, so the twist reads as a pun on "deep".** Why can't a fast civilization keep a promise? Fast minds can precommit with escrow and a proof kernel. Ground it in real work: cortex has a hierarchy of intrinsic timescales (Murray et al., 2014; Hasson et al., 2008), and vmPFC patients such as Damasio's Elliot keep their intelligence but can't commit to a decision. The rival is that patient. Also give Pressa the Blindsight argument, which the draft needs: the deep is overhead, and the rival's science is faster since it shed the tax. The story then argues for the slow layer. It doesn't assume it.

3. **[must-fix] The descent body is wrong.** A fall from r = 12L arrives at about γ = 12. Braking from roughly 0.99c in "four minutes" is on the order of 10⁵ g. The sample paragraph's "mostly in the neck" joke assumes a gentle stop. Either lengthen the braking so the body is plausible, or decant the body at the bottom and cut the capsule. Egan should check the arithmetic. I flag it because the voice sample commits to a human sensation.

4. **[should] No night, no zeitgeber.** The bible says "no night" and "a day is set by work, not light". Constant light makes human rhythms free-run, with households drifting out of phase (Aschoff's bunker studies; Siffre's caves), and orchards need photoperiod and chilling. The weather is therefore the deep's only clock, so Tesk's authored brightening is also their circadian entrainment. That is a domestic detail that makes the authorship bite. Also, 49 deep days is about 33 waking days. Put sleep into the deadline arithmetic, and let Ilse's sleep debt (microsleeps, mood swings after a few bad nights) colour the days before the refusal. She narrates reasons for what her body is doing.

5. **[should] Slow time does not make people patient.** The deep live at one second per second. Their contrariness and patience should come from founder self-selection (people who left benevolent government) and from three centuries of being condescended to, not from the redshift. Check the demographics too. D 25.9 means Tomé, "in his twenties", was born during construction (D 0–3.3), so he is among the first-born. Say so, or age him down.

6. **[could] Show the twist as a mood.** After the 31 deaths, let the quorum minutes record a grief nobody can source. The Minds' long thought has lost 31 parts, and they feel it as weather in themselves. This also makes "neither side is fake" a cost. One deep death is a change in a Mind's mood.

**Citations to verify before use:** Murray et al. 2014 (*Nature Neuroscience*), Hasson et al. 2008 (*J. Neurosci.*), Bechara/Damasio on the Iowa Gambling Task, Aschoff and Wever's isolation studies, Gazzaniga on the interpreter.
