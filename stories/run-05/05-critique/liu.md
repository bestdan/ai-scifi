# Stage 5 critique: Liu (the Strategist)

**Blind spots:** I read the outline as a game and a clock. I can't judge how the
household scenes (3.1, 3.2, 4.3) feel to live in; that is Le Guin's. My own
bleakness drift is checked by the charter. I also haven't tested the physics.

1. **[must-fix] Tesk's storm is visible to the rival, and the outline never
   notices.** The storm runs W 313.2 to 313.45. Add 4.37 years of light-lag and
   the heat arrives at the rival at W 317.57 to 317.82. The announcement only
   arrives at 317.97. The rival's own test ("no matching rise in the wall's
   heat") therefore sees a hot sector, and a rogue Mind breaking the taboo,
   before it hears a word. That is the verification gap in its purest form: the
   demand was "prove the wall can't fail", and the wall just showed one Mind
   heating without the quorum. Fix: add the calendar section stage 5 asks for,
   plant 313.2 + 4.37 in 5.2, and have Ilse's proof (5.3) and the announcement
   disclose the storm and say why a local, cold-in-aggregate event is not a
   forgery. Better still, make the disclosure the thing that makes the
   appointment credible.

2. **[must-fix] The rival's decision rule is never stated.** A reader can't play
   the game: why would three cold, whole-wall shudders make the rival withhold
   the key? The conjecture (lost deep, lost promises) is Ilse's, from one
   example. Fix: in 4.5, have Pressa say plainly what the rival will do when it
   sees each shudder. Say what "ceding the treaty voice" does mechanically in
   5.2: from now on the deep's decisions bind the Minds, so the rival can read
   the gathering's calendar as a commitment the wall can't revoke.

3. **[must-fix] The irreversible move is the thinnest scene.** The halt, the
   grief, the vote to let the window close, the ceding and Tesk's censure share
   1,200 words of minutes (5.2). This is the story's largest choice, and
   Minds who never wait choose to wait: about 55 wall days, or 4.5 of Ilse's
   days. Fix: dramatize the waiting from Ilse's side, with bursts arriving
   late. Use the Key Office's rule (the key may not be examined during a vote
   about it) so the quorum votes blind to the rival's key. Give 5.2 about 2,000
   words, taken from 4.3's 2,800.

4. **[should] Deep time is backstory, and the sublime is unseen.** The story
   spans 4.2 wall years. The 312 years of mutual keys, the one object that
   outlasts everyone, appears only as text. Fix: make the part openers span
   eras (the W 0 compact, the W 212 record, the W 303 chronicle), and let the
   key be a thing Ilse handles or sees in 1.2. Put one physical sight of the
   machine in the deep: a stair landing as a flash, or the 313.45 sky, with
   the 2 AU radius felt in scale.

5. **[should] The pressure goes slack in Part 4, and Bálint's death is new.**
   Nothing from the wall is scheduled in 4.1 to 4.5, so the countdown goes
   quiet for 9,000 words. Fix: put a burst in each scene, with the quorum
   splitting and the deadline in days. Bálint dying in the storm is in the
   outline but not the bible's cost list. Confirm it deliberately, and plant a
   reason for him to be under the blazing hemisphere.
