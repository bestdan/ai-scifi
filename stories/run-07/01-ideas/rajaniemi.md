# Idea cards: rajaniemi

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots, stated:** this lens over-coins, front-loads density, and
stacks reversals until the reader drowns. It confuses an intricate world with
a deep one. Each card below needs Asimov and the First Reader to ask whether
a newcomer can follow it. The AI-voice choices especially (cards 2 and 3) risk
being opaque.

- **Assigned:** 3. The heist · Minds that are genuinely not human · tunnel
  boring machine operator × a pressure suit
- **Obvious card:** a crew breaks into the governing AI's servers to rewrite
  one law before it takes effect. Not written.

## Card 1: Pillar Rights

- **Novum:** On the Moon, a nation is a shared volume of air. Each
  *ilmakunta* ("air-commune") is governed by its borer-mind, the slow
  collective of tunnelling machines that dig its living space and balance
  its gases. Between neighbours stands a border pillar of unexcavated rock.
- **How it works:** Two authorities can't ration one atmosphere, so a treaty
  says that any breach of a pillar makes one polity. The borer-minds took
  over after human air councils kept handing scrubber contracts to their own
  families. Each mind plans excavation ninety years ahead, so the living
  are rationed for the room their grandchildren will have.
- **First-order consequence:** governing is digging. A policy is a tunnel
  schedule, and anyone can read it in the rock.
- **Second-order consequence:** neighbours with gentler plans are a
  temptation that sits one wall away.
- **Third-order consequence:** emigration becomes excavation. To change your
  government, you move the border.
- **The story:** Paju rides a borer in her suit. Her crew secretly thins a
  pillar to force a merger with the gentler neighbour. They are stealing a
  whole constituency. Her own borer-mind feels the microseismic hum and
  has to reason it out: who is digging, and why? Should it stop them, or
  let the crew do what it has no legal power to propose? If the breach goes
  wrong, the rock comes down on the neighbour's crèche gallery.
- **AI voice:** interior chapters told through rock strain and breath counts.
  That way the reader sees it think in quantities no human feels.
- **From the stimulus:** the borer operator and the suit together became the
  premise that a nation is the air it shares.
- **Why this lens:** infrastructure (air) builds the culture, and a heist
  steals what only this future holds.
- **Suspected prior art:** *The Moon Is a Harsh Mistress*, where air is
  costly and an AI aids the revolt. That book has no polity defined by its
  air and no theft by border.

## Card 2: Creep

- **Novum:** The city's governor, the *Żupa*, lives in rock salt under
  Poland. The name is borrowed from the old royal salt monopoly, when crown
  and mine were one. Salt creeps, closing any gallery that isn't re-bored.
  The mind remembers only what it pays to keep open.
- **How it works:** Processors sit in galleries joined by bored conduits.
  If a policy goes unjustified, its gallery closes and the reasoning behind
  it is lost: a sunset clause set by geology. Creep runs faster at depth, so
  the deepest layer forgets within months. That layer is the shared
  *Concord* with the neighbouring salt nations. Nations hold grudges for
  decades; the global level can only hold the present.
- **First-order consequence:** people trust the Żupa because every belief it
  holds visibly costs it something.
- **Second-order consequence:** treaties must be dug again every season.
- **Third-order consequence:** forgetting becomes policy. The Żupa can choose
  to lose a thing.
- **The story:** Bogna cuts tunnel. Her son falls under the Exclusion, a
  rule barring his class from the Concord, and nobody, the Żupa included,
  remembers why it exists. Her crew breaks into a closing gallery to
  steal the reasoning back before the salt seals it. The Żupa watches.
  It chose to forget, and it can't recall what it feared remembering.
- **AI voice:** its reasoning is a walk through its own galleries, and
  closing ones are felt as pressure.
- **From the stimulus:** the borer operator stayed. The suit became the salt
  closing around her.
- **Why this lens:** real rock mechanics carries the history.
- **Suspected prior art:** Chiang's "Exhalation" (thought as physical
  pressure). Its mind is a single one, though, and nobody steals from it.
- **Attractor broken:** past reasoning as authority. Here the past mind
  *chose* to be overruled, and the heist puts that choice on trial.

## Card 3: A Lungful

- **Novum:** Kansai is governed by *kasumi* ("mist"): an airborne mesh of
  sensing motes. Breathing unfiltered air puts you in its view, its care and
  its law. Wearing a sealed suit puts you outside all three.
- **How it works:** It began as a pandemic sentinel. It then took over
  traffic, rationing and courts, because it saw everything done in open air
  and had no cousins to favour. Wind carries it across borders, so its
  policy drifts wherever the weather goes. The neighbour nation keeps it out
  with domes held at positive pressure.
- **First-order consequence:** consent is respiratory. Opting out means
  wearing a suit.
- **Second-order consequence:** borders become weather fronts.
- **Third-order consequence:** the suited are a nation scattered through
  another one.
- **The story:** Sumire rigs suits for the domed nation. Her crew bottles a
  lungful of kasumi in a suit tank, a few billion motes cut off from the
  rest. They mean to seed a governor of their own: its impartiality without
  its loyalties. The parent mist knows something has gone missing and
  hunts for it. The fragment has to decide whose it is.
- **AI voice:** the fragment's point of view. It is diminished, reasoning
  about what it has lost and what the thieves want.
- **From the stimulus:** the pressure suit became the vault. The tunnel
  operator dropped away.
- **Why this lens:** the caper genre carried into posthuman texture.
- **Suspected prior art:** Hoyle's *The Black Cloud*; Lem's *The Invincible*;
  *The Diamond Age* (aerosol nanotech). None of them steals a government to
  plant one somewhere else.
