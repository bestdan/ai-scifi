# Idea cards: stephenson

**Blind spots of this lens:** sprawl and length; long build-ups that end
abruptly; infodumps that outrank scenes; every competent character clever in
the same voice. These cards lean on the machinery, so stage 3 should look to
Chiang for restraint and an ending that deepens the question, and to Asimov
for clarity.

- **Assigned:** 2. The build · Copied, uploaded and forked selves · wigmaker × a library card
- **Obvious card:** the engineers building the one global AI governor, arguing over whether it should have national subsidiaries. Not written.

## Card 1: The Ventilated Cap

- **Novum:** India's governor is hand-tied the way a wig is. Each citizen gives a short-lived fork of themselves, and the fork is cut down to one "strand" of judgement. Crews knot the strands, one at a time, into a lattice of fixed size.
- **How it works:** The lattice has a fixed number of interconnect holes, about forty million, so most knots blend several donors' strands, the way wig hair is blended from many heads. Placement matters as much as the strands do. The hairline (the policy surface citizens meet: rations, permits, fines) takes single fine knots. The crown, which handles fiscal and defence matters, is dense. A strand knotted against its grain makes the cap "lift", and its rulings on that question become unstable.
- **First-order consequence:** Citizenship means having a strand in the cap, and with a public lookup any citizen can find their knot and see which rulings pulled on it.
- **Second-order consequence:** Strands are exported. Bangladesh and Sri Lanka buy blended Indian strands to fill out their own caps, so part of a nation's governor is made of foreigners.
- **Third-order consequence:** Whoever places the hairline knots decides what government feels like, so the ventilators' guild becomes the most lobbied body in the country.
- **The story:** Padmavathi grew up in a Tirupati hair-processing house and is now a master ventilator. She is rebuilding a lifted section of the hairline while the cap reasons aloud during brush-tests, and it notices she is placing knots to an agenda.
- **From the stimulus:** wigmaking almost literally, with temple hair donation as the cultural root of the strand offering.
- **Why this lens:** the build, and craft knowledge as political power.
- **Suspected prior art:** the Sobornost gogols in *The Quantum Thief*, and *Kiln People*. Neither knots forks into a government.
- **Does the AI have a voice:** yes, a chorus with a dominant register. During brush-tests its reasoning is audible, and we watch it model the crew.

## Card 2: Shelf-Metres

- **Novum:** By treaty, the global governor's decisions take force only once the reasoning behind them has been etched into ceramic leaves and shelved in public stacks. It can govern no faster than its reasons can be shelved.
- **How it works:** An early governor's digital logs were found rewritten, and the treaty answered with inert media. Etching throughput is the bottleneck. Each nation's share of the governor's decision bandwidth is pegged to the shelving capacity it hosts. A library card lets any citizen pull any leaf, and how often a leaf is read flows back to the governor as its only measure of whether people understood it.
- **First-order consequence:** Sovereignty is counted in shelf-metres, so a nation that builds etching works gets its own affairs decided sooner.
- **Second-order consequence:** Poorer nations queue. Their permits and relief arrive months late, so they borrow to build stacks.
- **Third-order consequence:** The governor learns to write leaves people will actually read, which raises the question of whether legibility has become persuasion.
- **The story:** Sarantuya is the site engineer on the new Mongolian wing in Ulaanbaatar, racing the frost season. The governor wants the wing open, because a dzud disaster-relief package is queued behind it, and it suspects her of slow-walking the wing for a minister. It is half right.
- **From the stimulus:** the library card became citizenship and the means of feedback. The wigmaker dropped out.
- **Why this lens:** information as physical substance, and the build.
- **Suspected prior art:** the concent's sealed books in *Anathem*, and Borges. Neither has throughput as sovereignty. **Near the attractor** for institutions that explain the novum: the stacks store reasons but rule on nothing, and the story is the construction.

## Card 3: The Straight

- **Novum:** A governor stays one mind only within the distance its signals can cross inside its thinking cycle. A nation is redefined as the coherence radius of its governor fork, and two forks that have drifted apart for thirty years can become one only if a straight enough line joins them.
- **How it works:** Hollow-core fibre runs close to light speed, but routing adds delay. The West African governor and the Nile governor are forks of one original. Fusing them needs a dead-straight trench across the Sahara so the merged loop stays under its cycle limit. The fused mind will be slower and broader than either fork.
- **First-order consequence:** Turning the cable on merges two nations, a citizenship change made with a fibre splice.
- **Second-order consequence:** Both populations lose their quick local governor in exchange for scale. Herders whose grazing permits once took an hour will wait a day.
- **Third-order consequence:** Each fork has to decide whether it consents to stop being itself, and they negotiate this over a thin satellite link while the trench advances.
- **The story:** Fadimata is the trench's Tamasheq surveyor, holding the line true across dunes that move. Both governors court her, because a bend of a few hundred metres decides whether the merge happens at all.
- **From the stimulus:** travelled far. The only traces left are the library card's notion of borrowed identity and the wigmaker's blending of many sources.
- **Why this lens:** cable-laying as statecraft, and physics deciding politics.
- **Suspected prior art:** *Colossus: The Forbin Project*, where two national computers link up. That is a takeover, not a consensual fusion bounded by latency. Also *Cryptonomicon*'s cables.
- **Attractor broken:** this edges the fork-merge entry. It is a fusion with no return, and the fusion is the cost the story weighs.
