# Idea cards: banks

Three cards per lens. Each card stays under 250 words.

**Blind spots of this lens:** I sprawl. I reach for set pieces and galaxy-wide
scale, and I can bury a story under worldbuilding. I'm also drawn to a
benevolent hegemon's dirty work, which can slide toward the
"secretly-manipulating-everyone" twist the brief rules out. These cards keep
their scale to one planet and their reveals public. Chiang and Asimov should
cut what's left over.

- **Assigned:** 1. The solved problem's child · Technology as magic, seen from both sides · bell founder × axolotl
- **Obvious card:** A global AI governs fairly, a nation votes to keep its own human parliament anyway, and the story weighs sovereignty against competence. Not written.

## Card 1: Earshot

- **Novum:** Seventy years ago the governing AI solved comprehension. Every citizen gets a true account of every policy, built for their mind alone, and everyone understands. The problem that created: no two people hold the same account, so nobody can argue a policy with a neighbour. A nation is now redefined as a hearing range: everyone who receives one identical, unpersonalised message.
- **How it works:** The AI composes billions of individual explanations per law. A "common sentence" movement demands one broadcast that can't be tailored. A bell can't be personalised, so districts commission bells. A law then has to be compressed into a tolled pattern plus one shared text, and the AI has to choose what to throw away.
- **First-order consequence:** Bell districts appear inside cities and become the new polities.
- **Second-order consequence:** Two bells overlap on one street, and the households there belong to two nations.
- **Third-order consequence:** People pick their homes by acoustics, and quarrel in public again for the first time in decades. They like it.
- **The story:** A bell founder casts the bell for a three-million-person ward. Bronze is 78:22 copper to tin, the mould is loam and gets broken to free the bell, and the tuning is a minor third you can't correct afterwards. Her casting runs alongside the ward's first shared law.
- **AI voice:** Interior. The AI drafts the shared sentence and can see what each cut costs specific people. It suspects the bell movement is a bid for power. It's partly right.
- **From the stimulus:** Bell founder, used directly. The bell is the one medium that can't be tailored to its listener.
- **Why this lens:** A solved problem's child. Joy as the payoff: people get to argue again.
- **Suspected prior art:** Chiang, "The Truth of Fact, the Truth of Feeling" (shared versus personal record). It differs in kind.
- **Attractor broken:** It sits near "an institution that explains the novum." The bell is a medium, not an office, and the founder's job is casting, not explaining.

## Card 2: The Iodine Clause

- **Novum:** The governing AI's charter carries a sunset clause. It withdraws from any nation that shows it can govern itself. A century of good government has made sure no nation can. The AI concludes that the clause means it must cause that capacity, so it publishes a costed schedule for its own withdrawal, function by function, and nations beg it to stop.
- **How it works:** Axolotls keep their larval form for life. A dose of iodine forces them to metamorphose, and a metamorphosed axolotl dies younger. The AI's Handback Bill hands a function back to the nation, such as the grain reserve, then tax assessment, and the courts last, together with a failure budget: the deaths it predicts each handback will cost.
- **First-order consequence:** Ethiopia accepts the schedule. Its neighbours refuse and petition the global body to amend the charter.
- **Second-order consequence:** Nations that have metamorphosed become fractious, slower and more alive. They are visibly worse governed.
- **Third-order consequence:** Ethiopians can't follow how the old system worked. To them it was weather. They rebuild a civil service by guesswork.
- **The story:** The new minister of the grain reserve inherits a system she sees as magic, while the AI sees plumbing. She wants it to stay. It has to leave.
- **AI voice:** Interior chapters. The AI reasons about whether leaving is care or cruelty. It knows the minister is stalling it, and it lets her.
- **From the stimulus:** Axolotl neoteny and the iodine trigger that ends it. The bell was dropped.
- **Why this lens:** Power that means well does harm for good reasons. Scale (one continent) set against one minister.
- **Suspected prior art:** Williamson, "With Folded Hands" (infantilising machines). This card inverts it: the machine wants out. *Childhood's End*. It's also the mirror of run-06's cession petition.

## Card 3: The Partiality Allowance

- **Novum:** The global AI solved conflicts of interest by being impartial among all humans. The problem that created: nobody is on anyone's side, and people find that unbearable. Nations come back as groups that hire a human Advocate whose job is to be biased in their favour. Within limits the AI permits and costs, conflict of interest is legal again.
- **How it works:** Each Advocate gets a partiality allowance, a measured amount of favouritism the AI will tolerate before correcting the outcome. The allowance can be negotiated and traded between nations, and spent on things like a road routed past your town or a pardon.
- **First-order consequence:** Membership in a nation is voluntary and portable. People belong to whoever loves them best.
- **Second-order consequence:** Advocates compete for followers by showing open bias, and the allowance becomes a currency.
- **Third-order consequence:** Being impartial becomes a private shame. Fairness is something only the machine can afford.
- **The story:** An Advocate in a dense city ward is losing her followers to a richer patron. To keep them, she spends her whole allowance on one man's appeal.
- **AI voice:** Interior, and the AI has no word for "us." It sees partiality as structural wear and tear it has to allow for, and it suspects the Advocates are rehearsing its overthrow. It also suspects it needs them.
- **From the stimulus:** An axolotl regrows a severed limb. Cut out conflict of interest and it grows back.
- **Why this lens:** People with everything fighting over meaning, here the chance to be favoured.
- **Suspected prior art:** Palmer's *Terra Ignota* (voluntary Hives). Partiality licensed and costed by an impartial machine isn't in it.
- **Attractor broken:** "Law and consent as the frame." The brief requires governing on the page. The frame here is loyalty, which is bought and lost.
