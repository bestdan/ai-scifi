# Egan lens: stage 4 critique of the bible

**Blind spots.** I stress the logic and the arithmetic. I can't say whether Segundo's scenes will move anyone, whether the Kichwa and Kayambi detail is respectful, or whether the pacing carries 30,000 words. Let Le Guin and the First Reader judge those.

1. **[must-fix] The humans hold both halves, so the cord needs no question.**
   The bible says "no human reads both halves", but the ledger has Jacinta doing exactly that in P4. The Accord separates the two minds, not the people. Waska's resonance figures are published. The cord is physical ribbon offcut, and a strain reader can cut a coupon and run a cyclic test. Why does the fix cost 71,500 asks?
   *Suggest:* close the cheap route on the page. Batch 7's records and cure logs sit inside Arriba and open only to an ask. The offcut can't be tied to batch 7 without them. An accelerated fatigue test needs the orbital rig. Put one line of this in Jacinta's P3 scene.

2. **[must-fix] The arithmetic needs 100% turnout.**
   74,100 of 78,000 asks is 95%, and the plan gives all of it. That means every adult in Ayora pooling every remaining ask with Segundo, though he currently pools only 2,100 of 7,800. The margin is 2,600, or 3.5%. The leftover of 74,100 also doesn't fit the Sept "market" button, whose lamps, awnings and cold store cost far more than the 3,900 asks spent so far (the 2133 lamps alone were 4,200).
   *Suggest:* either cut the inspection to about 45,000 (roughly 60% turnout) and let the vote count be the tension, or recost the market button at 1,500 or less. Say who the other 5,700 adults' brokers are. Wilmer's clients must come over for the number to work.

3. **[must-fix] The slack season is not examined.**
   The bible says "Waska bears nothing, so it refuses nothing". But power, medicine and Arriba's dispensing all arrive by cable, so for 40 days either they stop or they run unrefused. The roses can't go up either. The 2101 referendum made every law a load, yet nobody wrote a slack clause in 40 years. Why not simply suspend the Asamblea? And does a law passed during slack get weighed afterwards, and is it void if Waska then refuses it?
   *Suggest:* name the rule. Laws passed in slack are submitted when it ends and fall if refused. Show what stops coming down (a clinic generator on diesel). Then "the Asamblea governs alone" is a real constraint and not a freedom.

4. **[must-fix] The cord undercuts the bible's own silence rule.**
   If a cord dispensed under "the market" is a lawful warning, Arriba has always had a free channel, and "silence now has a price" is false. It also doesn't plausibly answer the label. Arriba's stated theory of Segundo (Proaño's buyer) gives it no reason to warn him, or a reason to warn him *against* Proaño.
   *Suggest:* make the cord a stretch of "plausibly answers the label" that costs Arriba something (2098 is why it won't do more). State that it can pad a dispensation only with what the label would plausibly include. Say in Arriba's P2 note why it sends a warning to a man it thinks is Proaño's agent: because Proaño's contracts are the stake.

5. **[should] Internal numbers don't agree.**
   "Seventy years of the Asamblea drafting disguised loads" is 40, from 2101. Arriba bound "for seventy years" is 37, from 2104. Asks can't be held by a firm, yet Ernesto "sold" the parish's asks to Proaño and Proaño "funds" the clinic. The ribbon is "replaced every 75 years in sections", but only 40 km of lower ribbon is replaced.
   *Suggest:* fix the dates. Let a firm hold a pooling contract but never the asks. Say that only the lower 40 km is replaced now.

6. **[should] The next step the bible stops short of.**
   Ask the obvious: why not a button reading "What aren't you telling us?" Because price scales with scope, and a world-wide answer costs a nation's year. The cord narrows the question to batch 7. That derivation is the novel part, but the bible only shows Segundo's arithmetic. Also, Arriba made the flawed batch, and the ward pays Arriba to inspect Arriba's own defect. Nobody in the bible notices.
   *Suggest:* put the catch-all price in P4 as a number Segundo laughs at. Give Jacinta or Proaño the line about who should be billed.
