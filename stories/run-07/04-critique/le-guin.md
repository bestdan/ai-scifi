# Stage 4 critique: Le Guin lens

**Blind spots.** I read for society, consent and language. I'm weak on pacing
(I'd let P3 breathe longer than a 30–40k novella may afford), on the ribbon
and orbital engineering, and I can tip into instruction. Egan and Watts
should check the physics and the arithmetic. I only flag where the arithmetic
touches consent.

1. **[must-fix] "Why not just test the cord?"** Jacinta holds a length of
   batch 7 ribbon in P3, and the strain readers can recognise stock by touch.
   A reader will ask why she can't put it on a bench and pull it. The bible
   makes the answer cost 71,500 asks, but only Arriba can inspect. Give this a
   history the page meets. For example, the 2068 charter bars Ecuador's
   engineers from sampling consortium stock, because Ecuador once demanded
   the same distrust in reverse. Jacinta can say it in P3 as a grievance she
   has lived with for nine years, not as a rule.

2. **[must-fix] Nobody we meet pays, and nobody refuses.** Segundo is 52 and
   has no household: no spouse, no child, no one whose clinic it is. The
   cost lands on "the ward", an abstraction. The ending also needs 74,100 of
   78,000 asks, which is near-unanimity, from people who gave their asks to
   Wilmer and other brokers, not to Segundo. Suggest: give Segundo a daughter
   turning 16 this year, so her first ten asks are the ones he is spending;
   give the clinic a user we have met (Ernesto's care home is the obvious
   one); and let one named neighbour say no and mean it. Dolores, who
   distrusts Arriba, is the natural refuser. Consent is the brief's theme, so
   show someone who withholds it.

3. **[should] Kayambi is in the surnames only.** Cayambe is Kayambi
   homeland, and the bible says "Kayambi and mestizo families" but every
   custom on the page (stalls, band, the Day of the Dead food) is mestizo.
   Kichwa communities there have a comuna with its own assembly and a
   tradition of collective labour, the minga, that pooled asks closely
   resemble. A reader will ask why a broker's word, not a comuna's decision,
   moved the parish in 2110. Fix it by making Ernesto's sale a betrayal of
   that older form (he sold on his own word what the assembly would have
   decided), and by having one Kichwa speaker say who named Waska and why.
   Respect here means the Kayambi act in the story, not decorate it.

4. **[should] Dolores is a function in P3.** Her ledger line, "Everybody had
   two bosses that year and both were thieves. The rope was the only one who
   said no. So we gave it everything.", is the theme delivered aloud. Style
   rule 4 forbids that. She keeps two payslips, so let her act: she hands
   Segundo the one she cashed without explaining, or she is cleaning
   something while she talks and gets the Treasury's name wrong. Also say what
   she is wrong about on the page. The bible says she is wrong that machines
   are kinder than people, but no scene corrects her.

5. **[should] Use the children for the rule's reason.** The most-known rule
   is "Arriba may not warn the unasked", and the bible says children learn it
   first. Yet the only children are a tour group and Jacinta's twins. Put the
   reason in a nine-year-old's mouth at the P1 anchor tour: "Why can't it
   just tell us?" Segundo answers with the bread (2098). That makes the
   history arrive in a scene with a child in it, and it lets the twins
   (who think the climbers are rockets) matter again when the ribbon is
   named in P4.

6. **[could] Glossary and taboo.** The six terms pass the Job test. Two
   risks: "ask" is a coin, a vote and a ration at once, and the first scene
   must show all three or only the coin lands. And the taboo "saying 'Arriba
   thinks'" has no Job unless it appears; either let Ernesto break it
   (funny, in character) or cut it. Names are plausible and respectful as
   they stand.
