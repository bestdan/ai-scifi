# Stage 5 critique: Banks lens

Blind spots: I favour sprawl and set pieces and am not natural at novella-length restraint. Weigh my "add" notes against Chiang's cuts. I haven't tested the arithmetic (Asimov's job).

1. **[must-fix] Scale is stated, never felt.** The cable is 36,000 km and the counterweight lies beyond it. The outline gives it only a 06:40 climber and rattling windows. Nothing makes the reader stand under the vastness. Fix: give 1.2 (Waska) one concrete moment. It feels the whole ribbon at once, the climber's ninety tonnes passing through cloud, then vacuum, then the station, and Ayora is a few grams of pull at the bottom. Then put one body-sized echo in 4.5 or 5.6. Segundo and Jacinta at the fence look up at the line they can't see the end of, with her boys asleep in the car. The people small, the thing enormous.

2. **[must-fix] P2 and P3 spend the same tradeoff twice.** 2.2 teaches that history is worth money, because Arriba reads the asker. 3.1 has Wilmer offering to rent that history and Segundo envying him. That is the same choice again, with a sale attached. By the "What each part asks" rule, this is the repetition. Fix: cut 3.1 and merge its envy beat into 2.4 or 2.6. Give P3 the room for what it really asks, why people trust machines over each other. Put 3.2 first and let Wilmer's offer arrive inside Dolores's stall as a joke she is not impressed by.

3. **[should] Wit is thin where it is needed most.** The humour is in Ernesto, Wilmer and the tuba, and the tuba is only in the bible, not in any scene. P4 runs six scenes of dread with one laugh (the 9 billion). Fix: schedule it. Put the tuba in 1.4 or 5.6, with the brass band rehearsing in the hall before the ward meeting. Let 4.2's labels be absurd as well as exact, since Segundo tries phrasings and Arriba's priced answers are courteous and deadpan. Give Arriba one dry line per note.

4. **[should] Reordering would hit harder, because the reader is ahead and the story is linear.** The cord is planted in 1.5 and seen in 2.1, the reader knows by 2.6, and 4.1's "halves side by side" then re-tells what the reader holds. That is the idea of the form, but it risks a dragging middle (P3 to P4). Fix: braid one forward-pointing fragment. Open P4 or P5 with the Gabon test-line break of 2140 as a short Arriba or Waska note, so the reader sees what a failing section costs (a crew on a climber) before Segundo prices it. Stakes then becomes felt, not just quoted in 4.5.

5. **[should] Who is missing from the view.** There are no climber crews, who are the ones who die, and no one from Gabon or Kalimantan. The seed's national-versus-global question reaches the page only as an Arriba that "answers everyone" and a brief mention. Nobody in the story does harm for good reasons either: Proaño is merely self-interested and Arriba's cord is unresolved. Fix: one crew chief (named by role) in 2.4 or 4.4 who rides the 06:40 and teaches Segundo what "half pay for climber crews" costs in 5.5. Give the consortium's certifier a single line in 4.1 or 5.4, doing the derating for good reasons and sorry about it.
