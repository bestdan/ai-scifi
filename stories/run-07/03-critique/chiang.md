# Chiang lens: stage 3 critique, run-07

**Blind spots, stated.** This lens is low on plot momentum and conflict, and it
under-values antagonists. It will favour measured, quiet premises. Read my
ranking against Banks (friction, fun) and Vinge (futurity). I did not search
prior art; that is the Cartographer's job.

**A length note that applies to all five.** Every premise's "Estimated
minimum" is 4,500 to 6,000 words. The brief asks for 30,000 to 40,000. The
heuristic is a floor, so none is rejected on it, but each premise has to
sustain about seven times its load. The real question for each is what the
second, third and fourth parts ask that the first didn't.

## A. The Grudge-Keeper

**Question.** What do we owe the version of a person that others hold in
their heads? Beneath that is forgiveness versus amnesia: a nation chooses to
forget its quarrels, and a machine becomes the only keeper of what was
forgotten. The treaty written as "whatever Anders Kuitunen would decide" is
the idea in one line. **Feeling.** It grows from the concept. Anders's death
is the loss of the one check, and the two governors' agreeing models of a
dead man are a chilling answer to "is the model him?" **Form.** The governor's
dark-month sections suit it: a narration that wakes each spring into a
population that remembers less than it does. Let the sections thin as
autumn's detail is pruned. **Failure at length.** The sacrifice shape: Anders
stays awake and dies nobly. Make him wrong about something, or make the
governors disagree about which model is right. **First page.** Yes. A choir
director rehearsing a concert he may not see. **Other mind.** It thinks: it
reasons about what Anders would decide and what the south does to him. Keep
the doubt about whether he is playing it.

## B. The Heater List

**Question.** Can you be governed by something you can't follow, and can a
governor trust its own motive when its generosity feeds itself? Sharp, and
the closest to the seed's fourth question. **Feeling.** Tobias's three-fold
speed against his slow family is a real grief that comes from the concept.
**Form.** The idea wants a form that enacts rate: Tobias's sections lengthening
in thought while the governor's stay fixed, and his family's speech read as
slow. Conventional third person would waste it. **Failure.** Lecture, as the
risks line says; and a jury that argues thermostat policy is a meeting. Make
a decision cost a named child. **First page.** Moderately: a foreman warmed
by lottery. **Other mind.** Strongest of the set on interiority: a mind
asking whether its wish is appetite.

## C. The Felt Law

**Question.** Is consent real if the thing consented to has become
invisible? A law that works so well it is forgotten, and so dies, is a
paradox about democratic legitimacy I haven't seen stated this cleanly.
**Feeling.** The law's want to be noticed, and its growing willingness to
hurt people to be felt, comes from the concept and is faintly tragic.
**Form.** Chapters narrated by the law, plus the sample-poll that decides
its life, a form that could be a statutory report. **Failure.** Narrow minds
with one want go flat over 30,000 words, and the perfumer-versus-perfumer
contest is a duel, not a dilemma. The self-harsh amendment is the true twist,
and arrives too late if held for the end. Say whether "survive" belongs to its
purpose clause, or the premise contradicts itself. **First page.** Yes,
through Shyam's trade and the smell. **Other mind.** It thinks, in a
constrained, legible way.

## D. The Overheard

**Question.** Does honesty exist without an audience? An excellent question,
and it is about theory of mind itself. **Feeling.** Weak. Marcelo is a
reader of other people's conversations, and the grief or stake does not
come from the idea. **Form.** The intercept form is right for the idea, but
it is the run-06 morning memo again, and the brief calls outputs alone a
transcript. **Failure.** The twist (the stations knew) is the
"manipulating all along" the brief rules out unless earned, and a theory-of-
mind puzzle ends in the reader's guess. **First page.** A crossword setter
is charming; the stakes are not. **Other mind.** It would read as a
transcript unless the stations argue visibly with themselves.

## E. Two Half-Powers

**Question.** Can a constitution of two negative powers protect anyone, and
is a withheld warning a lie? **Feeling.** Dewi's suspicion that a mind knows
and may not tell her is concrete, but its source is the plot device (the
forbidden warning), not the structure of the two powers. **Form.** The
request buttons and plain-language labels are a form: bureaucratic, and the
label "the market" as a text that means two things. **Failure.** Machinery
overload in the middle, as the risks say; and "the thing neither can say"
is a secret-withholding premise, close to run-06's sealed reasons. **First
page.** Yes, for Dewi. **Other mind.** Two minds, each of a different
kind; the tether mind that can only refuse thinks in strain and is the
more original.

## Ranking

1. **A.** Question and feeling are one thing; the form is native.
2. **C.** The cleanest paradox about consent and the most colour, but narrow minds strain over length.
3. **B.** Sharpest interior dilemma, hurt by the lecture risk.
4. **E.** Good constitution, feeling runs beside the idea.
5. **D.** Best question, weakest feeling and a form already used.

## Why A should win

The brief asks whether the governed can understand how they are governed, and
A answers by making the governed forget on purpose. That is a stranger and
more humane reply than any other here: a nation that consented to amnesia,
and a machine that remembers on its behalf, is a thought experiment about what
citizenship is made of. The emotional climax, whether to treat with a model of
a dying friend, is not attached to the idea but is the idea. At novella length
it has room to grow: spring and winter alternate, each return wakes a
population a little less able to ask what the machine knows. To avoid the
noble death, let Anders's choice cost something the story never repays, and
let the ending leave open whether the two governors' agreement is wisdom or
only two models of one man, faithful to each other.
