# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi lens (density and texture)

**Blind spots:** I over-value opacity and can mistake density for depth. This pass is light on purpose, and the First Reader's bounce risk outranks my taste. I did not check arithmetic (Asimov's job) and did not run the linter.

**Glossary.** Consistent. Waska, Arriba, ask, button, load, strain reader, slack season, minga and the Accord are each clear at first use, and "derate" is gone. Mostly "label" carries the story and "button" appears rarely, but both are glossed and never contradict each other.

**Verdict.** The polish kept the texture. The explanations are mostly present rather than clumsy. Four small fixes:

1. **1.3, the added narration (the payoff-ledger line is Segundo's speech, so this is only the narrator's echo).**
   `So nothing happened in this country unless an ordinary person asked, and the rope agreed.` →
   `So the country moved only when a stranger at a counter spent six words and the rope let them stand.`
   Same explicit link, but it adds a picture and doesn't repeat the speech word for word.

2. **5.3, Waska, double delivery of one fact.** The section's opening sentence already says the new number reaches the rope.
   `The rating of seven comes down by that share, all of it, every section, and the new rating comes to me as every rating does, a number in a certificate.` →
   `The rating of seven comes down by that share, every section of it.`

3. **4.4, the added stakes sentence is flat after the rhythmic protected line.**
   `The bottom of it parts, and every crew on that length dies.` →
   `The bottom parts under a loaded climber, with the crew in it.`
   This keeps the fact and stays inside the story's own vocabulary (*parts*).

4. **1.5, Arriba defines "dispense" like a dictionary.**
   `To dispense is to send down the rope whatever answers a label; it is how I fill every pool. So I look for a label it answers, and this one does.` →
   `Dispensing is how I fill every pool: I send down the rope whatever plausibly answers a label. So I look for a label it answers, and this one does.`
   The term is still defined on the page, since the ledger depends on it. The new version folds the definition into Arriba's practice.

**Familiar detail:** nothing worth a change at this stage. The archaic carbon receipts and the screens held at arm's length already do the work. I'd leave "rang me" (4.4) alone too.
