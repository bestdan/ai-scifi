# Per-part notes: triage

## Part One (Asimov, Tchaikovsky)

Taken, for the drafter to apply before writing Part Two:

1. **The delivery date** (Asimov, must-fix). The pool fills about 7
   September and the descent takes nineteen days. One line in Arriba's
   note: it assigns the goods from stock already in the hold of a climber
   eleven days down. Keep 15 September.
2. **Waska's rule** (Asimov, must-fix). "nothing that rings should be
   borne" becomes "nothing that rings past tolerance should be borne", so
   bearing batch 7 in Part Three is the rule working.
3. **Waska's detector is pattern, not meaning** (Tchaikovsky, must-fix).
   Waska can't know what mornings or goats are. It notices that broker
   0419's loads weigh and fall differently from every other load filed
   under similar labels at this and other counters, and it never knows
   why. Cut "I can feel the school in it" and the mornings and goats
   reasoning; keep the drum kit only as a weight that matches nothing.
4. **Waska's blind spot isn't self-diagnosed** (Tchaikovsky, must-fix).
   Cut "a thing that is not a load is not a thing I can refuse" and "there
   is nothing to say no to". Keep the wear-memory mechanism; let it file
   the ring as noise, with an unease it can't weigh. The irony belongs to
   4.5.
5. **Arriba's note holds the question open** (Tchaikovsky, should; the
   bible's rule). Cut "Of batch seven I have been asked nothing." Trim the
   grievance: "it is very nearly all I am permitted" and "That is the
   rope's choice. I don't make the rope's choices." become flat statements
   of the rule. Arriba infers the two-week hold from the board's history,
   not from knowing the rope.
6. **Luz Caiza** (Asimov, should). Name the Tabacundo girl Luz Caiza on
   the page, and have her take the mornings label.
7. **The adult count** (Asimov, should). One clause in 1.4: seven thousand
   eight hundred adults, six each.
