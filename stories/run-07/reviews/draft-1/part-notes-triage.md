# Per-part notes: triage

## Part One (Asimov, Tchaikovsky)

Taken, for the drafter to apply before writing Part Two:

1. **The delivery date** (Asimov, must-fix). The pool fills about 7
   September and the descent takes nineteen days. One line in Arriba's
   note: it assigns the goods from stock already in the hold of a climber
   eleven days down. Keep 15 September.
2. **Waska's rule** (Asimov, must-fix). "nothing that rings should be
   borne" becomes "nothing that rings past tolerance should be borne", so
   bearing batch 7 in Part Three is the rule working.
3. **Waska's detector is pattern, not meaning** (Tchaikovsky, must-fix).
   Waska can't know what mornings or goats are. It notices that broker
   0419's loads weigh and fall differently from every other load filed
   under similar labels at this and other counters, and it never knows
   why. Cut "I can feel the school in it" and the mornings and goats
   reasoning; keep the drum kit only as a weight that matches nothing.
4. **Waska's blind spot isn't self-diagnosed** (Tchaikovsky, must-fix).
   Cut "a thing that is not a load is not a thing I can refuse" and "there
   is nothing to say no to". Keep the wear-memory mechanism; let it file
   the ring as noise, with an unease it can't weigh. The irony belongs to
   4.5.
5. **Arriba's note holds the question open** (Tchaikovsky, should; the
   bible's rule). Cut "Of batch seven I have been asked nothing." Trim the
   grievance: "it is very nearly all I am permitted" and "That is the
   rope's choice. I don't make the rope's choices." become flat statements
   of the rule. Arriba infers the two-week hold from the board's history,
   not from knowing the rope.
6. **Luz Caiza** (Asimov, should). Name the Tabacundo girl Luz Caiza on
   the page, and have her take the mornings label.
7. **The adult count** (Asimov, should). One clause in 1.4: seven thousand
   eight hundred adults, six each.

Carried forward:

- Zinger closers run at 6.9 per 1,000 words. Cut the reflexive ones.
- The council's December label is already *Ayora's clinic*. In 5.6
  Proaño's workers' pool needs its own label, which he asks Segundo to
  write; it must differ from the council's.
- 27 October 2141 is a Friday and band practice is on Thursdays. In 5.2 the
  band is at an extra rehearsal for the 2 November procession.
- 26 October is a Thursday, 31 October a Tuesday, 1 November a Wednesday,
  15 September a Friday.

## Part Two (Chiang)

Taken, for the drafter to apply before writing Part Three:

1. **Jacinta states the theme** (must-fix). Cut "So it isn't the words.
   It's you. The same label from two of you buys two different things."
   and "That's worth a lot, if you're Wilmer". She reads "Stall size... Bay
   size" and stops; the carbon-copies paragraph carries it.
2. **Proaño states two theses** (must-fix). Cut the committee speech ("We
   don't write laws any more…") and "If there were anything wrong with that
   ribbon, the rope wouldn't bear it." The truck bill dramatises the first;
   keep "It's the one thing in this country I've never had to check."
3. **One refusal, not three** (should). Trim Proaño's offer to a line
   ("Your father didn't say no"), let Wilmer's pitch carry the argument,
   and cut Sisa's repeat of her receipts contempt; keep the road argument
   and Dolores's "She's right about the road."
4. **The market in histories costs a stranger** (should). One concrete
   instance: a client puzzling over a double name on a receipt, or another
   broker signing up with Wilmer.

## Part Three (Le Guin)

Taken, for the drafter to apply before writing Part Four:

1. **Who paid for 2110** (must-fix). Segundo answers Ernesto with one
   remembered image of the water carried up the hill (buckets, his
   mother's shoulders, the years 2110–2118) and uses it. Give the 2110
   fiesta more room: what the 21-year-old with the tuba saw on his
   neighbours' faces as the hands went up. P5 mirrors it.
2. **The bread and the Accord as briefing** (must-fix). Cut "And the
   Accord… They teach it to the children before they can read." Break the
   2097–2098 recital with things in Dolores's hands (the pot lid, the
   ladle, the bag of change); keep Señora Pilataxi's earrings.
3. **Jacinta wants the pass** (should). Her nine years of training ride
   on the replacement; "It's fine" is relief, and "I don't like it" costs
   her. Cut "That's a feeling, not a reading. I don't do feelings."
4. **The Why lands once** (should). Keep "Everybody had two bosses that
   year…" as the one statement; trim the repeats, and trim Waska's recap
   to the flat place.
