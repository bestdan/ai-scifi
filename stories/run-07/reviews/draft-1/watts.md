## The one thing
Price the clinic. Proaño funds it within a week, the man the answer hurt most. That refunds Segundo's sacrifice and turns the harmed elite into a patron. Make Segundo's one firm label, *Ayora's clinic, from the farms*, cost him what the story has already said it would. The story spends 27 years establishing that Arriba reads his history. One farm-funded label by the honest broker should alter that reading. It shows up nowhere afterwards.

## Notes
1. **Structural. Part Five, Proaño at the bench with the clinic label.** A reputation that works as a costly signal (Zahavi's handicap principle) loses value when it is spent. Arriba's last note says "I keep the revision" and never says what the farm label did to it. Show the next label reading differently, or leave the clinic unfunded.
2. **Structural. Arriba's market section in Part One, and its last note.** "Batch seven's offcut can go into no tether now" means Arriba knew in September. The page then rationalises ("plausibly needs it"). The first reader's question, "did it mean to warn?", has only a warm answer. Give the cold one too: a surplus rule fires first and the market rationale is attached afterwards. That is confabulation, which suits a mind that has to explain its actions. Then the page supports two readings.
3. **Structural. The rope's memory.** "My memory is my wear" is a good constraint. It then keeps exact day-counts ("Thirty-four days"), the 27-year pattern for four-one-nine, and the date of the Accord. Those are stored statistics, not creep and fatigue. Either allow the rope a ledger or let it forget things.
4. **Structural. Retaliation.** The supervisor asks the shed "who'd been asking Arriba about the rope", and nothing follows. 1,240 of Segundo's 2,100 clients are tied to the farm, and 42,300 give on an unexplained word. The selective logic says an employer punishes askers. One consequence for Luz would do it.
5. **Line-level. The unasked question.** Lourdes sees "a cutter's hand, opened by the shears to the bone", and the flour answer teaches that harms nobody pays to look at stay unlooked-at. The story has batch eight for this. It never mentions the rose workers' own exposure (fungicides under plastic, a real occupational hazard). One dispensary case that nobody asks about would extend the idea cheaply.
6. **Line-level. The rattle.** An 80 km/h climber four kilometres off shakes "every window". Nothing on the page causes it, and the ending rests on its absence. Give it a source, such as the reel's start-up through the rock, in the first rope section.
7. **Line-level. The refrain.** "Five rings true. Six rings true…" has become a tic by the third repeat. The first reader skimmed it. Vary it, or keep it only where the number changes.
8. **Line-level. Wilmer.** He turns collaborator ("Give it to him direct"). A defector who is also liked deserves one last exploit, not conversion.

## What to protect
- "I find I have weighed something that I did not feel… It came from outside me, and it was right, and I could not have found it." This is the one place the rope's interiority earns its keep. It is a blindsight beat: a correct output with no felt basis.
- The flat place in the reel's bearings as the rope's memory of 2097. It is physically right (brinelling).
- Dolores: "I cashed the first one, and I kept the second one, and I never cashed it."
- Ernesto's "They gave them for a face". It is the story's strongest claim about how trust works (Willis & Todorov 2006 on trust judged from faces in 100 ms).
- "a whole town of people bent over looking for something they'd dropped."
- The public tally, with neighbours watching who touches a screen. It solves the free-rider problem honestly.

## Blind spot disclosure
I undervalue ritual, receipts and bread dolls as forces. They may be doing more work for the reader than my cold accounting allows, and Le Guin or Banks should push back. I did not check the materials science beyond plausibility. A real NDT lab might see the voids. I did not run the linter. I score fear and cost harder than warmth.
