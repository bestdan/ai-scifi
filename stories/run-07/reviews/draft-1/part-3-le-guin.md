# Le Guin lens: Part Three check (draft 1)

**Verdict.** Mostly lived. Dolores's stall and Ernesto's courtyard have hands, objects and weather. The lab scene and the back half of Dolores's history slide toward briefing. The part does raise a new question: whether trust is a method (Ernesto's "a face"), not a receipt.

## Notes

1. **Must-fix, structural. Who paid for 2110 is a shrug.** Ernesto wins the tank argument unopposed. "Eight years of carrying water. I'm not saying nobody paid. I'm saying they got a road." Segundo answers only "They wanted a tank," then changes the subject. He was 21 to 29 and his mother carried that water. Ernesto even says his money roofed her house. Segundo is silent, so the cost is argued away instead of shown. Give Segundo one remembered image of water carried (buckets, the hill, his mother's shoulders) and let him use it. The fiesta itself (the band stopping, the hands going up) gets one narrated paragraph. It is the scene P5 mirrors, so it deserves more room and more of what the tuba-holder saw on his neighbours' faces.

2. **Must-fix, line-level. The bread and the Accord turn into briefing.** "And the Accord." / "Six years later. They tied its tongue. It can't tell anybody anything they haven't asked for, and it can't lie. They teach it to the children before they can read." Segundo knows this, and P1 already told the reader. Cut it. The 2097 and 2098 recitals also run unbroken for about 750 words after the broom handle drops out. Keep Señora Pilataxi's earrings ("She pretended she hadn't seen me"), which is lived, and give Dolores something in her hands during the bread: the pot lid, the ladle, the cloth bag of change.

3. **Should, structural. The lab scene leaves the reader ahead of the page, and Jacinta's turn is stock.** The reader has known the cord is ribbon since P1. "That's a feeling, not a reading. I don't do feelings" is the scientist's-hunch beat. Her bible want is the replacement on schedule, but nothing here costs her to find a problem. Let her want the pass, so that "It's fine" is relief and "I don't like it" is a cost.

4. **Should, line-level. The Why line lands three times.** "Everybody had two bosses that year" (735), "The rope said no" (751), then the bare thesis (755). Waska's section retells the stacked loads and the eleven days. Keep Waska's flat place and trim its recap. Let the bare thesis land once, as the ledger needs.

## Protect

- The three-header device: Dolores, Ernesto and Jacinta each pay an ask into the dispensary's nights, and each reads the receipt through.

## Blind spot
