## The one thing
Cut Proaño's clinic gift (5.6) or make it cost. As written the villain-ish power
pays for the sacrifice, and the "hard ending" turns into a restoration. That is
the stock "affirms resilience by default" beat. It is also the story's one drift
toward the happy fix. Let the clinic stay unbought, or let Proaño's money come
with a label Segundo can't write.

## Notes
1. **Structural. Prior art for the core device (the oracle that answers
   literally, at a price).** Nearest I know of: Adams's Deep Thought in *The
   Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* (1979, novel and radio), confidence high.
   The answer is exact and useless unless you asked the right thing. Also near:
   Asimov's Multivac in "All the Troubles of the World" (1958), confidence
   med-high; and the monkey's paw / literal djinn (W. W. Jacobs, 1902). The
   cost-by-inspection and pooled-asks economy is new to me, so the distance is
   real. The *the market* misreading (auction-sized frames) is the story's best
   original proof, and the 8,960,000,000 catch-all is the joke that shows the
   price. Status: edge-of-map. Real distance lives in the economy, not the
   literalism.
2. **Structural. The failure mode is Challenger.** Seven rings "inside
   tolerance" and "put it with the noise" is normalisation of deviance, the
   O-ring pattern (Diane Vaughan, *The Challenger Launch Decision*, 1996,
   nonfiction), confidence high. Also: the bridge or walkway that passes every
   test, and the bank run that a question triggers. Charted in reality, thinly
   in SF. The step beyond it is the unpaid second question (batch eight),
   which the ending already holds. Protect it.
3. **Structural. Space-elevator ground.** Clarke, *The Fountains of Paradise*
   (1979), confidence high; Kim Stanley Robinson, *Red Mars* (1992), where the
   cable falls, confidence med. A cable as livelihood is charted, but a cable
   as legislator that can only say no is not. Don't spend more words on the
   cable's engineering. Spend them on the veto.
4. **Structural. Arriba's quiet warning is near the stock twist.** Disposing
   the batch-seven offcut as "surplus", with "The anchor town reads ribbon", is
   the Mind that nudges while keeping its rules (Banks's Culture Minds, e.g.
   *The Player of Games*, 1988, confidence med; Asimov's Machines in "The
   Evitable Conflict", 1950, confidence high). It also touches the brief's
   off-limits "manipulating everyone all along". The draft saves it by never
   confirming intent, and the First Reader's closing question ("Did Arriba mean
   to warn them?") shows it works. But "The anchor town reads ribbon" is
   unreadable. Make the line carry what it means, or cut it.
5. **Structural. The scientist-explains-to-the-layman beat.** Jacinta walks
   Segundo through the weave, the Accord, the sampling sums and the derating.
   The style guide bans it. Two of the four sessions need her only to explain
   (3.3's lab, 4.1's bulletin). Give Segundo a stake in one so that he argues
   back, or let Waska's own section carry the derating.
6. **Structural. The same tradeoff returns.** Segundo's refusal to rent his
   name is posed to Wilmer (2.3), to Proaño over coffee ("Your father didn't
   say no"), to Proaño's smell speech (4.3), and to the clinic label (5.6). Each
   repeats "honest broker versus easy money". Only 5.6 turns it, and the turn is
   the gift above. Merge two.
7. **Line-level. Rope as feeling mind.** "I want it as I want nothing else" and
   "I find I have weighed something that I did not feel" edge toward the banned
   "AI learns to feel". The pairing with the count/want ambiguity saves it. Keep
   it ambiguous and don't add a third "want" line.
8. **Line-level. Draft inconsistencies a reader can catch.** The First Reader
   caught offcut "from batch seven" (Arriba) against "I can't tell you which"
   (Jacinta). Arriba knowing is fine. Mark that the rope and Jacinta can't. The
   timeline (Saturday label, Tuesday answer) also needs one date anchor.

## What to protect
- *the market* as a two-word label read as the auction, against Wilmer's copy:
  "No cold store. No cord. The frames were for stalls two metres wide."
- The clapping rhyme: "It told us once when no one asked, / and the bread cost
  three times in Cayambe."
- "Section forty of batch seven is sound." The literal answer is the story.
- The 8,960,000,000 catch-all, and the clerk pulling it off the board.
- The windows that do not rattle.
- The open batch-eight question.
