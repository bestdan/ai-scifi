## Suggestions

1. **4.1, Jacinta:** "The rope bore the plan this morning, with seven in it." → "The rope bore the plan on Friday, with seven in it."
   - The rope bore the plan on Fri 6 Oct, and this scene is Monday 9 Oct.

2. **Rope section after the lab night (3.x):** "Twenty-three days to the first of November." → "Twenty-six days to the first of November."
   - That morning is 6 Oct.

3. **1.3, the narration line that replaced the cut paragraph:** "So nothing happened in this country unless an ordinary person asked, and the rope agreed." → "So nothing came down from Arriba unless an ordinary person asked, and the rope agreed."
   - As written, it is false. The Asamblea passes laws unasked, including every slack-season law.

4. **4.2, Segundo's thought:** "Nothing happened in this country unless somebody asked." → "Nothing came down from Arriba unless somebody asked."
   - This is the same overreach, echoed.

5. **5.1:** "had been for nineteen hours" → "had been for twenty hours"
   - Her asks came at midnight, and the cake is at eight, with the telling after it.
