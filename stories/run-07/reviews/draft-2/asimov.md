## The one thing
Close the cheap question. Jacinta holds the Gabon page: a batch-seven reference piece parted in 2140. "Why did Libreville's batch-seven reference section part on 14 August 2140?" is one section, about 131 asks (0.3% of the clinic's 44,000), and the Accord says an answer to a "not sound" finding carries its basis. A smart reader asks that before Segundo does. It would name the voids and the storm. Make it fail on a stated fact: Libreville scrapped the piece with the old clamp ("rebuilt" is not enough), and Arriba inspects only what it holds. Plant that on page forty-one.

## Notes
1. **3.3, the rig (structural):** The page gives "One a second." At that rate 1.71 million cycles is about twenty days, and she had twenty-five. She also has 2,000 metres of cord for parallel rigs. She stops at 40,000 because "I can't make a night into three years." A reader who does the sum finds a clever character idle. Either make the rig a single, slow machine (heat, creep), or have her start the long run and let the counter be the clock in Part Four.
2. **Arriba's note, 1.5 (structural):** Under "tidy" the cord is an accident. Under "nudge" Arriba must suspect batch seven. Yet 5.3 says only an inspection reveals the voids, and the logs are "inside spec". Say what Arriba holds before inspecting: the storm is in its own log and nobody has asked. Then the nudge reading has a specific basis. The first reader still asks "was it a hint?" because the page supports only the tidy reading.
3. **1.5 / 5.3, the basis rule (line-level):** "A finding of not sound carries its basis under the Accord" first appears in the note that uses it. Add it to the Accord's definition in 1.5.
4. **4.1, Segundo's inference (line-level):** One of four batches gave the cord, so "ring picks out one batch" is a one-in-four link. Jacinta says so. The Gabon page does the real work. Keep that order.
5. **1.1, "button" (line-level):** "Button history" and "fill buttons" are never glossed, and Part One is titled "The Button". The first reader guessed. Say once what a button is.
6. **The "Received:" headers (line-level):** The first reader guessed they were receipts. Have Segundo's first receipt to Señora Quishpe repeat the header's form, so the match is visible.
7. **1.3, the tour (line-level):** The first reader found it repeats the rope's own section. Keep the string demonstration and cut the two-sentence summary.
