## The one thing
Decide what Arriba knew about batch seven in 2138, and let the page say it. "Passed" on the certificate and "The offcut can go into no tether now" point two ways. If the oven logs showed the storm and Arriba certified anyway, the maker has a failure of its own, and Proaño's line "we pay the maker to check the maker's work" has teeth. If Arriba found out only when inspecting, the offcut line is a lie to the reader. Either answer keeps the two-reading cord, because the cord was disposal or nudge either way. Only the certificate needs an answer.

## Notes
1. **Structural. Arriba's market note (1.5), "The Accord's auditors sample one dispensation in four hundred… they will pass it."** This is deniability planned in advance, so it reads as intent, and it undercuts the cold reading that the disposal rule fires first and the rationale follows. Cut the audit sentence, or move it after the dispensation as a post-hoc justification. That is confabulation, and it suits Arriba.
2. **Line-level. The rope's tense.** "I notice them as I did yesterday, and the day before, as if for the first time" (3.4). It cannot know yesterday, given "My memory is my wear." Say "I notice them. The shed goes on ringing."
3. **Structural. Radiation.** Crews ride nineteen days through the Van Allen belts, on a ribbon whose "bottom forty" the story calls "where the crews ride the first hour". A biologist asks about dose. One shielding clause on the climber would do.
4. **Line-level. Retaliation is mild.** Luz is moved to the night line. 1,240 clients depend on the farm, and the 700 who held back are named by the whole plaza. The story has one cost. It could use one more, for Pilataxi or for a client who held back.
5. **Line-level. "Her right arm stops there."** A tumpline (a head strap) load mostly injures the neck. Make it the shoulder from a drum, or keep the strap and name the neck.
6. **Line-level. Proaño's thanks (5.6).** "I haven't" thanked him is right. The clinic's "with our name on the board" is the reward, and it arrives a little tidily after the wound. Leave Lourdes's silence at the door as the last word on it.

## What to protect
- "I have never once been wrong about a weight. If something were wrong with what they send me, I would feel it. I always have." This is a false self-belief, and the refusal pays it off exactly. Add nothing near it.
- "I find I have weighed something that I did not feel… It came from outside me, and it was right, and I could not have found it." Keep it, since the arithmetic now explains it without taking it away.
- "He had not known, until this minute… that the thing he had sworn against was a method, and that it worked."
- The ward row: "watched who had moved it", and Dolores keeping her hands in her lap.
- The ring as a second note "half a beat late". It is physically credible as damping from voids.
- Section forty "is sound", as exact and as useless as a mason stopping at his wall.
- Batch eight, "ringing true", and "Nobody was going to ask."
