## The one thing
The Sahel is still unpaid. Arriba's note now says "the price of millet in
Niamey doubled... more people died there than the drought alone would have
killed. The Accord followed. I keep it." That is an honest fact, and it fixes
the "why did it warn unasked?" question. But it is a mind reciting its own
history. No human being in the story has lost anything to it. Dolores's
"People died there" and Arriba's sentence are the only two mentions, and both
are statements. I asked for a person who is not a Farinango. A cheap version:
the clapping rhyme counts only Cayambe's bread. Let one old woman say what
the rhyme leaves out, in a line.

## Notes
1. **Structural. Part One, from "At eleven Segundo left the bench" to the bus
   home.** Luz was told the mechanism, Waska's section laid out the town, and
   then the tour says it a third time ("It was the whole country in two
   sentences"). The first reader was bored here. Keep the string and the
   brass plate. Cut the cousin's board-reading to the one row the children
   need.
2. **Structural. Language.** The label is a six-word grammar, and the story
   knows it: "A question is a smell", "Arriba is only the shop", the baker's
   flour. What these people can't say is a want without a price. Dolores's
   "I give Lourdes asks. Arriba is the shop" does this well. One more
   courtesy would settle it, such as a custom of not asking a neighbour what
   their label is. That would also make Segundo's "on my word" cost more.
3. **Line-level. Part Five's blank receipt headers.** The reason arrives only
   at the ward meeting, after two blanks. Gloss the first one in the
   sentence before it.
4. **Line-level. Sisa.** She is quick and funny, but her arc is one embarrassed
   gift. Give her one ordinary chore or opinion of her own in Part Five, so
   that "Two" lands as hers.

## What to protect
- Dolores and the payslips, and "Her right arm stops there." The cost of the
  road is a body, and it's told over beans.
- The ward meeting: neighbours watching one another touch a screen, Dolores
  keeping her hands in her lap, the Pilataxis' "hold ours for the farm".
  Gathering, not fight.
- Lourdes at the board: "as you would read the names on a list from a mine."
  Also the cup of maize drink, passed between the women without a word.
- "Ayora's clinic, from the farms." The ambiguity stays.
- "a whole town of people bent over looking for something they'd dropped."
- The rope: "I remember the flat place." Memory as wear.
- "Nobody was going to ask." The ending is a question the page can answer.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh society over plot, so I forgive the slow Part One that the first
reader didn't. I can't check the derating arithmetic or the physics of the
voids. Egan and Watts should. My Sahel demand can tip into instruction, and
Banks may say Arriba's sentence is enough.

Structural problem remains: yes. Part One still tells the mechanism three
times, and the cost abroad is still only stated.
