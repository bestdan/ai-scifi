## Payoff-ledger lines (draft 3's Drafter's notes)

- **Premise, 1.3:** "One of them can only say no, and the other can only say yes when you ask. So if you want anything, you ask, and you hope the rope agrees."
- **Premise, 2.1:** "I asked, for all of you. Arriba answered and sent it. Now the rope has to agree to carry it down… Asking gets it sent. Only the rope gets it here."
- **Stakes, 4.4:** "If I ask, the clinic's gone, and they hang me if I'm wrong. If I don't, the rope comes down in three years with somebody's boys on the climber." and "That's the price, Jacinta. Their December. All of it, the clinic's asks, every one. And I can't tell them what it's for until it's answered."
- **Stakes, 5.2:** "The price is your December. All of it. The clinic's asks… And I can't tell you what it's for until it's answered."
- **Why, 3.1 (Dolores):** "Everybody had two bosses that year and both were thieves. The rope was the only one that said no. So we gave it everything." and her beat ending "So the whole world went to Quito and tied its tongue… And here we gave the rope the laws… you can't pay a rope to push."
- **Why, 4.3 (Segundo):** "Everybody trusted the rope to refuse anything unsound, and so nobody ever asked about what the rope couldn't feel."
- **Ending, 5.3:** the answer, all 132 words; **5.5:** "*Proof climb, 06:40. 1.04 / −0.04. Refused.*"
- **Reach, 4.3 (Proaño):** "You've shown everyone the price of a straight question, Farinango. I can pay it every week. Ayora can pay it once a year." **5.6 (Wilmer):** "A good name's a thing you can sell now, like a slot on the six-forty."
- **Traced rows:** asks (1.1), the button gloss (1.1), "Section forty of batch seven is sound." (4.2), 40,872 (4.2), the tally (5.2), "This is ribbon. New ribbon. Who gave you ribbon?" (3.3), the lab result (3.3), the slack-season notice (5.6), Rule 5 and the derating (3.3, 5.5).

## The First Reader was moved, surprised or delighted (draft 3)

- The clapping rhyme, "*Arriba, up the rope, never lies and never tells…*", and the smallest girl missing the hard part.
- "the only tuba in the province who played every note in the right place and none of them at the right time."
- "Then she'll have no trouble proving it," and Lourdes putting the receipt under the salt.
- Dolores's stall: the broom handle, the blue payslip cashed and the green one never, and "A woman who cashes both is a thief. A woman who cashes neither is a fool. And I had a boy."
- "Her right arm stops there. It has since 2115." and her "bad arm" at the vigil.
- The fiesta memory: the band stopping, Segundo holding one note alone, his mother's hands in her lap, his father seeing it, and "He had not known… that the thing he had sworn against was a method, and that it worked."
- "Receipts are what you give people who don't know you."
- "What aren't you telling us?", the 8,960,000,000 price, Segundo's laugh, Jacinta folding the 40,872 receipt into his pocket.
- Lourdes touching her screen six times; Sisa's "Two"; "as a dam goes".
- Segundo glad to be right and wanting his father to have been wrong.
- Luz Caiza: "I don't need to know. You wrote it down." and "the kindest thing anyone had said to him that month and he didn't want it."
- The board at ten past four; Lourdes reading it "as you would read the names on a list from a mine"; Dolores with the maize drink.
- The rope refusing a climb because of a number it didn't feel, and admitting it couldn't have found it.
- The windows not rattling; the dog barking once.
- "Give her a year. Then give her another one." The bread dolls with Grandmother's face.
- "Dispensed: an answer."
- "Ayora's clinic," stopping, then "from the farms."
