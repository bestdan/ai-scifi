# Terms

From the bible's Glossary:

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Waska | the tether's control mind, which governs Ecuador by refusal; Kichwa for rope |
| Arriba | the global mind at the top of the cable; Spanish for "up there" |
| ask | one unit of a person's yearly claim on Arriba; ten a year |
| load | in law, any bill submitted to Waska to weigh |
| strain reader | an anchor engineer who translates Waska's figures |
| slack season | the 40 days of the lower ribbon's replacement, when Waska bears nothing |
| minga | the parish's communal work day; also the December pool of asks |
| button | a label posted at the counter that people pool their asks behind; a button history is the record of every label and what it bought |
| the Accord | the Quito Accord of 2104, which bound Arriba to answer only, set ten asks a year and licensed brokers |

|---|---|
