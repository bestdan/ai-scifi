## The one thing
Gloss "dispensed", "derate" and "map" in the mouths that first use them
(all line-level). The grader missed all three. "Dispensed: an answer" is the
payoff the first reader loved, but the word is never taught. Arriba's first
note says "disposal by dispensing is lawful wherever the dispensing answers a
label", and a reader can't decode that. Add one plain clause there: to
dispense is to send down against a label.

## Notes
1. **Line-level. Wall text, 3.5 ("Rule 4… Rule 5… the batch is derated").**
   "Map" and "derated" arrive undefined, in a sign. Rewrite the sign in the
   plain words Jacinta uses later ("a list of every section checked"; "rated
   down by the share found bad").
2. **Line-level. Arriba's first note, 1.5.** See the one thing. Also cut "I
   think the son is buying the same thing and declining to write it": it
   explains the choice that Part Two then dramatises.
3. **Structural. Part One, the tour.** "It was the whole country in two
   sentences" (and the sentences before it) is the third telling after Luz
   and the clapping rhyme. The stage cannot take a restructure. The
   line-level version is to cut that narration paragraph and the cousin's
   "That's my job", and keep the string and the plaque.
4. **Structural. The rope's repeated line.** "I notice it again as if for the
   first time" appears in 1.1, 3.5, 4.5 and twice more. The cold reader
   tired of it. Line-level fix: cut it from 2.5 and the last section, where
   it adds nothing.
5. **Line-level. Continuity.** Segundo "kept to it for thirty-one years" (the
   fiesta) against twenty-seven as a licensed broker. Say "kept to it" with
   no count.
6. **Line-level, optional. Language.** The custom of not asking a neighbour
   their label is still missing. One line at the ward meeting would do it,
   where the Pilataxis say "hold ours for the farm" aloud and the hall
   flinches. It would make "on my word" cost more.
7. **Line-level. Cutters under half freight.** After "Half the roses", one
   sentence from Luz or Lourdes's queue about shifts cut. At present the
   crews get half pay by law and the cutters get nothing.

## Blind spot disclosure
I weigh society over plot, so I forgive Part One's slowness, and the first
reader was patient only because of Segundo. I can't check the derating
arithmetic or the physics. Note 7 can tip into instruction, so cut it if
Banks calls it a sermon.

Structural problem remains: yes, but only notes 3 and 4, and both have
line-level cuts. Nothing else is structural.
