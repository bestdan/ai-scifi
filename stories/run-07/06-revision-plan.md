## Comprehension (structural; these come first)

1. **Premise, partial.** The reader had both half-powers and the paid
   question ("the rope itself, can only refuse loads... the mind at the top
   will only answer what it is asked") but not the link: nothing happens
   unless an ordinary person asks *and* the rope bears what comes down.
   Keep 1.3's statement ("So if you want anything, you ask, and you hope the
   rope agrees") and make it land: give it its own paragraph beat, and show
   it once in action early, when the cold store waits at the anchor and a
   stallholder says it plainly (nothing came till somebody asked, and now
   the rope has to agree). Echo it once in Part Four in Segundo's own
   thought when he understands nobody has asked about batch seven. This
   line serves Premise only; Stakes and Ending read as before.
2. **Why, partial.** The reader had 2097 and the bread but was unsure the
   rule was deliberate, and never said the two halves were crippled on
   purpose so no one holds the whole, or that people trust the refuser so
   nobody asks about what it can't feel. (a) Ernesto in 3.2 says the split
   plainly, as a fixer would: they cut the power in half on purpose, so
   nobody would ever hold the whole knife. (b) Proaño's "It's the one thing
   in this country I've never had to check" stays; add one line from
   Segundo in 4.x connecting it: everybody trusts the rope to refuse, so
   nobody asks about what the rope can't feel. (c) The Accord is defined on
   the page (item 4). Dolores's line in 3.1 still carries Why's main
   statement; these support it.
3. **Reach, partial.** The reader had the price-not-wall point and the 40
   days, not that a broker's good history becomes something to trade. Make
   the histories market reach the ending (see 13): in 5.6 and Arriba's last
   note, Segundo's name is now worth more than ever, Wilmer is renting
   names at a new price, and writing Proaño's label changes how Arriba
   reads him. State it once in a character's mouth: Wilmer's, at the
   counter, pricing Segundo's name after the meeting.
4. **Terms, missed.** (a) **the Accord:** a coined name never explained.
   Define it at first use in plain words (the agreement signed in Quito in
   2104 that bound Arriba to answer only, set ten asks a year and licensed
   brokers) and add it to the bible's Glossary. (b) **the Day of the Dead
   items** (bread dolls, the purple maize drink, the graves on 2 November):
   the reader knew them from outside. Make them clear from the page: what
   the day is, what the dolls and drink are for, at first mention. (c)
   **lupin beans:** describe them at first mention (yellow, salted, boiled,
   eaten with toasted maize). (d) **pasillo:** use the plain words ("the
   slow guitar songs", or "a record of old songs"). (e) "to the centavo" can
   stay.
5. **Lost-list, rows touched.**
   - **L1 (Premise): the first rope section's speaker.** Give its opening
     paragraph one orienting clause, so a cold reader knows by the third
     sentence that the "I" is the rope. Tie "four-one-nine" to the card the
     first time it appears.
   - **L2: the header figures.** In the first rope section, gloss its own
     header in its own terms (sixty-two parts of what it will bear, thirty-

   - **L3: "rings" as a sound.** Plant the sound in Part One, in the rope's

   - **L4 (Premise): why it can't act inside tolerance.** One line in the
     rope's terms: it refuses only what pulls past what it bears, and a ring
     inside the rating pulls nothing.
   - **L5 (Premise, Why): Arriba's note.** Orient the speaker in its first
     sentence. Make "The anchor town reads ribbon" parse (see 9).
   - **L6: "from batch seven" against "no batch mark".** See 10.
   - **L7 (Ending): Part Five's days.** Put a weekday and date at the head

## Consensus (two or more lenses)

   Cartographer, Stephenson). Keep Proaño's offer and the label Segundo
   writes, but make it cost and make it late. (a) The association's pool is
   its **December**, so the clinic is a year off, not this Friday; Lourdes's
   loss lasts. (b) The clinic is now the farms' clinic: Lourdes will be
   nursing in a building with Proaño's name on the board. (c) Arriba's last
   note shows the cost Watts names: the honest broker has written a
   grower's label, and Arriba now reads his history as two histories; his
   January pool will be read in that light. (d) Stay on Segundo's hand
   with the slate longer. Ending row: the clinic is "funded by the farms,
   under a label Segundo writes, a year later", which still matches the
   ledger.
7. **The honest-broker-versus-easy-money choice repeats** (Cartographer,
   Stephenson, Chiang, Asimov). Keep Wilmer's pitch (2.3) and the clinic
   label (5.6). Cut Proaño's job offer at the bakery door. Trim Proaño's

   Trim Part Two's second history-rental scene (the fifth-bench widower and

8. **Jacinta explains to the layman** (Cartographer, Stephenson, Asimov,
   Chiang). (a) After the refusal, keep her 0.92 → 1.04 sum (the gate

   knows (labels, prices, how Arriba reads), so they reason together. (c)
   Put the certification rule in the certifier's mouth or a posted notice,

9. **"The anchor town reads ribbon" doesn't parse** (Chiang, Cartographer,
   Stephenson; the First Reader and the grader). Rewrite so it means
   something plainly, while both readings of the cord stand: for example,
   "In the anchor town people know ribbon when they hold it." Keep the cold
   reading on the page too (Watts): the disposal rule fires first, and the
   market rationale is attached after. The page must support both readings:
   tidy disposal, or a nudge.
10. **The offcut's batch** (Asimov, Cartographer; the First Reader). Arriba's
    note calls it offcut of the replacement ribbon, not batch seven. "It can
    go into no tether now" then reads two ways (offcut is too short for any
    tether; or this stock is bad), which is the ambiguity the bible wants.
    The reader then pairs the cord and seven's hum in Part Four, with
    Segundo, not in Part One.
11. **Close the sampling door with a checkable rule** (Asimov; Stephenson on
    who states it). The consortium's certification rule, posted (a notice
    the strain readers keep on the shed wall, or the certifier), says: a
    batch is rated only on a complete map; a single bad section found is
    replaced from spares and the batch keeps its rating. So twenty sections
    and a hit buys one swapped section and leaves thirty-four bad ones in.
    Plant the rule in Part Two or Three (the shed) before 4.2 uses it. Cut
    "thank you, that's one section, we'll leave it out" as the reason.
12. **The rope's refrain** (Watts, Le Guin, Chiang, Stephenson; the First
    Reader). "Five rings true. Six rings true…" appears five times. Keep the
    first, one in the middle where a number changes, and the last.
13. **Wilmer's histories market drops out** (Stephenson, Le Guin). Let it
    reach the ending: at the ward meeting Wilmer's clients give direct, as
    now; after the answer, Wilmer prices Segundo's name at the counter
    (item 3), and the old man and the fifth bench pay off as they do.
14. **Part Five's days and the consortium's pause** (Asimov, Cartographer).
    Dates on every Part Five scene (L7). Say why the meeting is on the 27th:
    the parish's December asks are released only at the council's ward

    consortium splices on Monday though the question is public: a question
    is not a finding, and the schedule stands until a map changes a
    rating (the certifier, one line).

## Conflicts

- **Cut the clinic, or make it cost** (Cartographer against Watts, Le Guin,
  Chiang, Stephenson). Recommendation, taken: make it cost and make it late
  (item 6). Cutting it would remove the First Reader's ambiguity about
  Proaño's motive ("a bribe, a kindness or both"), which the reader liked.

  argument that the cheap routes all fail. Recommendation: decline. The
  plain question at full price is the point; the exploits stay in the
  middle, where they show the system.
- **A Sahel scene** (Le Guin) against length and the risk of instruction.
  Recommendation: the partial fix in item 22.
