Status: uncharted
Scores: N4 F3 R3 Y4 E4 S3 → weighted 29/40

The water, pastoral-village and clerk attractors mostly held off. Only one card (tchaikovsky/Two Hundred Eyes) lands on the hidden-thing functionary, and only Vinge's harbour button brushes water. The convergence moved to new ground:

1. **Time-rate as sovereignty (about 10 cards).** A clock, tempo, lag, creep or throughput is the governing constant:
   - asimov/The Slow Nations (tempo nations);
   - asimov/Base Notes (an eighty-year horizon);
   - watts/Regent (seasonal torpor);
   - watts/Heater List (thermal speed of thought);
