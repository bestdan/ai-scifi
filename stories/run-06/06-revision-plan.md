## Consensus (fix)

1. **The forecaster and the deadline arithmetic stall Part One and lose
   the reader.** Raised by Stephenson, Chiang, Le Guin, the Cartographer,
   Watts and Asimov, and the First Reader skimmed it (lost-list 2 and 3).
   - **Cut 1.3 to two forecaster runs:** the bare numbers, then the
     failure framing. Drop the accurate-naming run, or reduce it to one
     clause.
   - **Make the swing legible as one rule** (Stephenson's one thing, which
     also answers Watts and Asimov on "immune to wording yet moved by
     it"). Article 9(2) asks whether a nation can *no longer* perform the
     function. The instance doesn't weigh adjectives. It reads facts. A
     fact about the present (these people are doing the work tonight)
     reads as capacity. A fact about the future (no successors, median age
     68, nobody trained behind them) reads as "no longer". The failure
     framing works by choosing true facts that face forward. Say it once,
     in a plain line from Davit to his deputy, and let the forecaster
     confirm it.
   - **State the deadline once, plainly, in 1.3:** the 26th is the last
     day, because of the three turns. Remind the reader once in 4.1. Cut
     the other date-counting to what a reader can feel.
2. **The instance acts like a person.** Raised by Watts (his one thing),
   the Cartographer and Asimov.
   - **Cut from 13,515:** "I find that I would have written it without
     having read it." Keep the flat "The order stands." and "This sentence
     has appeared in each memo since 17 February."
   - **Cut from 13,513** the address to a successor: "It will not read
     this sentence as advice. It may not." The bible forbids an instance
     to address its successor.
   - **Check every memo** for introspection or for addressing tomorrow's
     instance. "These are the reasons. They are record. The record does
     not show why I decided" may stay: it is the record's own rule, stated
     flatly. Cut "and after midnight there will be nobody to ask" if it
     reads as wistful.
3. **The theme is stated three times** (Chiang's one thing, with the
   Cartographer). Keep Mariam's "That's what remembering is" as the one
   statement (Watts protects it). Item 2 cuts the memo's version. Akinyi's
   call keeps "So I don't know", which serves the Ending row, but loses
   any line that restates read-versus-remember as a thesis.
4. **Akinyi's not noticing her order stood is implausible** (Asimov). This
   is linked to the Cartographer's one thing: make the inherited standing
   order the real discovery.
   - **Fix:** she did see "does not follow from the log. The order stands."
     every morning from 13,503. She read it as each instance choosing
     priority 5 afresh: an instance that saw the anomaly and agreed with
     her. Her wrong belief, breaking in the 5.3 call, is that it never
     agreed. It inherited. Her one line has been governing Sadakhlo for
     three weeks, carried by minds that each said it didn't follow.
   - **Make that the discovery** in her call, and trim the "clerk is the
     secret memory" reveal in Part Three, the charted shape, by a few
     lines. Keep its explicit Twist line.
5. **Count, date and line slips.** Raised by Chiang, Asimov, Watts and the
   First Reader (lost-list 5, 6 and 7).
   - Fix the names count everywhere: 212, 1,911 and 1,912. See item 7,
     which changes Part C.
   - Rewrite the Zurab "transit lounge at Dubai" sentence in 4.1.
   - Make Ketevan's dates line up on the page with Davit's age and the
     2054 concert.
6. **The Armenian side is dropped after one line.** Raised by Stephenson,
   Asimov and Le Guin (Lilit unseen). Give the referral one consequence on
   the page: a short beat in 5.4 at Bagratashen, where the rostered
   Armenian nurse, Lilit, who took cash and stayed home, has had her own
   letter from the commons hospital administration. It takes two or three
   paragraphs, and it shows the record closing over the ceded side too.
   Use the words freed by item 1.

## Adopted single-lens notes

7. **Davit's final filing is dominated** (Asimov's one thing). As drafted,
   the refusal is already secured by Tamar's filing, and the licence needs
   only Part C's 61 runners, so naming the full 400 in Schedule 4 does no
   work and costs 406 letters.
   - **Fix:** give the names one job. The Carriers' Code s. 14(2) requires
     Part C to name every person who has carried goods for the applicant
     in the twelve months before application. The cool-bag has gone round
     a weekly rota through the whole circle of shift-swap nurses, so Part
     C names all of them, about 400, Eka among them. Schedule 4 then needs
     no separate list of the 400. It names the retired nurses and the
     others, and cross-refers to Part C. Make the total add up on the page
     (Schedule 4 plus Part C), and keep 1,912 as the number of persons
     named across both, or recompute it and fix every mention.
   - **Make Davit's reasoning one sentence in 4.4,** said aloud to his
     deputy: Tamar has already lost us the hospitals. What I can still
     save is the road, and the road costs the names. That keeps the choice
     forced and visible.
   - Memo 13,516's "Schedule 7 lists 61 persons carrying" changes to
     match. The prosecutor's letters, 406, match Part C. **Rows served:**
     Ending, which still reads (licence, persons not carriage, letters),
     and Stakes and Reach, now with one clear number.
8. **Cut the minister scene in 5.1** (Chiang: it repeats stakes the Tamar
   and Zurab's-father scenes carry). Move "drafting something for the 400"
   to the deputy's last text, which already carries it.
9. **The Registry workload is implausible** (Watts): 206,412 filings
   "read whole" by forty clerks. Clerks stamp most filings unread past the
   header. Akinyi reads whole only what her region's flags send her: the
   Caucasus and Central Asia, a few hundred a night. Fix the figure and
   the claim.
10. **The ledger matches the grievance night too neatly** (Watts). Davit
    goes looking for the concert night. He knows the date, and he turns to
    it on purpose, past dozens of other nights. The match is his search,
    not the book's coincidence.
11. **Akinyi is too self-articulate in two lines** (Watts). Trim her two
    most essayistic sentences in Part Three.
12. **The national "pending list" contradicts lag secrecy** (Stephenson).
    Remove it, or make it a list of intake numbers without contents.
13. **The carrier bag is the form** (Le Guin's one thing). There is no new
    scene. The cool-bag, the rota book, Ketevan's ledger and Akinyi's
    exercise books are one image of carrying what the record won't hold.
    Let one line late in Part Five touch two of them together, lightly,
    without naming the pattern.

## Comprehension (structural)

14. **Premise, partial.** The reader had "will refuse to take over the
    hospitals if it sees they cope", but not the memoryless governor or the
    must-refuse rule, and framed Georgia as resisting rather than
    petitioning. Fixes:
    - **First page** (lost-list 1): one plain clause that the governor is a
      new mind each morning and remembers nothing it doesn't read.
    - **1.3:** Davit's bind line follows directly on a plain statement that
      Georgia is asking the commons to take its hospitals, and that the
      charter makes the governor refuse unless the record proves Georgia
      can no longer run them.
    - **Rows served by the 1.3 lines:** Premise and the refusal standard.
      Both still read.
15. **Stakes, partial.** The reader reversed the branches ("If he succeeds
    in showing Georgia copes… nothing improves"). Fixes:
    - **Two branches, two pictures.** Grant: paid nurses, a date that
      doesn't move, the web ended (Zurab's father in a bed). Refuse:
      deaths go on, and named people go to the prosecutor (Eka's letter).
    - **The costing note in 4.4** states both in plain words, and Davit
      says plainly what he wants: cession granted.
    - **His mother's refusal to be written** is named as part of what he
      stakes.
16. **Why, partial.** The reader had memorylessness against bribery, but
    not the grudge and the secret, the refusing of power, or the distrust
    of earlier governments. Fix Tamar's corridor speech (1.4), adding one
    sentence: it can't hold a grudge, a bribe or a secret, and it has to
    say no so it can never swallow the nations that made it. Keep it an
    argument aimed at Davit. The professor clause stays.
17. **Twist, partial.** The reader didn't have the fear that the governor
    secretly remembers, or the nine-year unlawful mercy. Fixes:
    - **2.5:** make Davit's fear explicit in his head. If it remembers,
      the charter is a lie, and every memo for thirty-seven years was a
      lie.
    - **3.3:** Akinyi's explicit line keeps "nine years" and the
      illegality plain: "It doesn't remember. I do. One line a night,
      since the cold in Ulaanbaatar."
    - **Lost-list 4:** the three Ulaanbaatar memos in her sleeve are
      clearly from 2089, by their headers and her line, and repeat one
      order.
18. **Reach, partial.** The reader didn't have the total named or
    sovereignty's edge moving. Fix: Davit's committee statement in 4.5
    carries the number (see item 7) and the sentence that each name is a
    thing Georgia no longer does for itself. The line between Georgia and
    the commons moves by one clause. Keep the bible's explicit line.
19. **Terms, missed:**
    - **supra:** at first use, show the toastmaster standing and a toast
      raised to the dead, so the reader gets "feast with toasts" from the
      page.
    - **tetri:** "she won't take a tetri" becomes "she won't take a tetri,
      not a single coin", or use the plain "a penny".
    - **the turn:** the reader took it as "3am when the batch is sealed".
      At its first use (1.3) and at Akinyi's line (3.1), state it plainly:
      the hour each night when no instance is running at all, between the
      erasure and the boot, when the clerks work.
20. **Lost-list 8, the Carriers' Code logic.** Item 7's single rule (name
    everyone who carried in the last twelve months) replaces the Part Four
    complications. State it once, in plain words, in 4.3.
21. **Lost-list 9 (did it read Davit or Akinyi?) is intended.** It is the
    earned ambiguity, with two specific readings, and the Cartographer
    protects it. Watts calls it rigged, because Schedule 7 fully explains
    the licence. Fix: Akinyi's "I don't know" in 5.3 says both happened
    and she can't weigh them. His schedule made the licence possible. Her
    order kept the ring alive to be written. The question is whose mercy
    it was, not which document was read.

## Conflicts

- **The memo's thesis line.** Chiang would keep the theme once, in the
  memo. Watts, the Cartographer and Asimov would cut the memo's
  introspection. **Resolved:** cut it from the memo (item 2), and keep
  Mariam's line as the single statement (item 3). The instance must not be
  the most human voice in the room. For the human: this trades a lovely
  memo line for the bible's "not human in costume".
- **Mechanism density.** Le Guin would cut the deadline spine. Stephenson
  would make the exploit legible. **Resolved:** compress and clarify with
  one rule (item 1). Le Guin's freed words go to Lilit (item 6).
