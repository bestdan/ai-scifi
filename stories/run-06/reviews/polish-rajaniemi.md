# Polish spot-check: Rajaniemi lens

Blind spots: I favour opacity and can under-weight reader fatigue; I read the changelog and the changed passages in full, not every line of the 32,000 words (several long lines were truncated in my view of Parts 3 to 5).

**Verdict:** the polish is sound. Terms match the bible glossary: "the turn" (04:00 to 05:00 Tbilisi, 00:00 to 01:00 UTC), "unwritten" (now a second speaker, which is the right fix: a word two people use is a culture's word), "instance", "gazetted", "errata". No new coinages. The new payoff-ledger lines are explicit and read as speech, not narration: "That part is mine ... That part is yours." and "The web is over: the old women go home, and the bus stops carrying." are the lines I would not touch. "Schedules 5 and 6 had been reserved in January ... and their numbers stayed empty" is a good small piece of infrastructure texture.

## Suggestions

1. Page 1, the opening gloss is the one clumsy explanation, a dictionary definition in the first paragraph after the scene is set:
   `A schedule of evidence was any set of facts a petitioner filed in support of a petition, statistics or returns or accounts, and a list of persons was only one kind of it, but that kind wanted names.`
   →
   `A schedule of evidence was any set of facts filed in support of a petition, statistics or returns or accounts. A list of persons was only one kind, but that kind wanted names.`

2. Akinyi's call (5.3): "I don't know" now lands three times in four sentences, and the middle one restates "I can't weigh them." Keep the ledger content; cut the echo:
   `I can't weigh them. I don't know whose mercy it was, yours or mine. So I don't know.`
   →
   `I can't weigh them. Whose mercy it was, yours or mine, I don't know.`
   (Keeps "I don't know" word for word as the closer, so the ending's ledger line stays explicit.)

3. Page 1, the radiator: a familiar detail one word can make stranger. The ministry heating "turned down at six" is plain; the sixth-hour cold is already strong. Leave it. But the driver's "net bag of mandarins" is the only domestic detail on the car ride that is not also in a later scene. No change needed.

None other. I looked for places the added Reach and Premise sentences explain clumsily and did not find any: the deputy's text ("Nineteen hundred people who did it off the books are a file now.") is the best phrasing of the Reach row in the story, because it is a bureaucrat's panic, not a narrator's summary.
