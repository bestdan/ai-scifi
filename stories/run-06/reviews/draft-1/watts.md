## The one thing

Stop the instance acting like a person. It is the only mind in the story that isn't confabulating, and that is the novum. In 13,515: "I find that I would have written it without having read it." In 13,513: "It will read what I have read. It will not read this sentence as advice. It may not." The first is introspection with a self to introspect. The second addresses a successor, which the bible forbids ("Memos describe, never instruct"). The bible's own rule is "not human in costume". Cut both, or make them visibly the record's phrasing that the instance reproduces without owning. Its alienness should come from the dry numbered voice alone.

## Notes

1. **Structural. 3.1 and 3.3, the Registry's load.** The batch is "Two hundred and six thousand, four hundred and twelve filings" and "a clerk read each filing whole". That is about 5,000 filings per clerk per night. No human reads that. Then Akinyi re-scans the day's intake "fast, with a pencil" and finds nine candidates among thousands. Real attention would triage by salience, region and recency, and she would over-catch bridges and ferries (vivid) and miss cold chains (dull). The story gestures at this ("Mostly I never find out") but never lets her mechanism be the biased one. Fix: have her say how she really picks, and show the bias. Shrink the batch, or let "read whole" be a charter fiction everybody skims.
2. **Structural. The ambiguity is rigged.** The two readings are not equal. Schedule 7's text ("This is cross-border carriage of goods") explains the licence completely, and the standing order was never needed for it. Akinyi's "I don't know" therefore protects the reader from a conclusion the page already supports. Either make her line load-bearing (the licence is unreachable without the ring having survived), or let the instance's licensing be a reading nobody can reconstruct, as the bible's "reasons are record, not cause" promises.
3. **Structural. The instance is both immune to wording and moved by it.** The bible says it "can't be flattered or worn down", but 1.3 has the forecaster swing from 9 to 60 on adjectives ("unsustainable"). Plausible for the forecaster. The real instance then reads the final schedules with no framing effect at all. Say that the forecaster is the cheap one susceptible to framing, and that the real one differs. Better, let the real one also be a bit framing-sensitive, which is true of any text reader.
4. **Line-level. 4.2, the ledger.** The ledger holds Davit's one remembered grievance night exactly, "16 May 2054 ... Alone". Memory reconstructed over forty years doesn't line up that cleanly. Give him the ledger entry for a different night, or the right night with a detail he had wrong (it was the second row, not the first; the chair was his father's). Then "he had brought that chair up with her at least once a year" becomes a story the brain told, and the ledger corrects it. That costs him more.
5. **Line-level. 3.4.** "Mostly I never find out" is well judged. But "That's what a good night looks like. Exactly like a wasted one" and "Someone who reads the tray. And then decides." are quotable and self-aware. Akinyi is too articulate about her own function. Cut one.
6. **Line-level. 5.3.** Akinyi realises that "I've written three thousand lines thinking each one was a morning." That is the best self-story break in the piece. Protect it, and move the instance's flat "does not follow from the log" noticings earlier so the reader sees it before she does.
7. **Line-level. 1.3 and 4.5.** The scenario/forecaster percentages (31, 9, 60, 57, 25, 40, 6) are hard to hold, as the first reader said. Drop two of them.

## What to protect

- "It logged the cognac." A scanner as the incorruptible selection pressure that replaced bribery.
- "That's what remembering is." Mariam on the instance, the story's most exact claim about memory.
- "Mostly nothing happens, and nothing happening looks the same whether I did it or not." Akinyi's *After* column ("Not known. Not known.").
- "Then it remembered." Mariam, plainly, on seeing the erratum. The wrong-but-compelling inference is the human condition here.
- Lagos confirmed wrong in 13,504 and 13,515 ("replacement ... May"). The story doesn't flinch on that one.
- "The record shows 31." for the pilot whales. Volunteers outside the record rendered in the instance's voice.

## Blind spot disclosure

I weigh mechanism and cognition, and I distrust tidy moral resolution. I underrate the ritual and favour economy of Lamara's table and the supra. The first reader found Ketevan's ledger the best thing in the story, and my note 4 would make it costlier for Davit and perhaps colder. Let Le Guin and Banks overrule me on that. My load-arithmetic note (1) may be fine if the charter's "read whole" is a deliberate fiction.
