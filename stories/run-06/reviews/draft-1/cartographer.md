## The one thing
Make the inherited standing order the story's real discovery, and cut the "clerk is the machine's secret memory" reveal down to what it needs. The standing-order idea is the uncharted part. The clerk-behind-the-machine shape is charted.

## Notes

1. **Part Three, Akinyi's confession ("It doesn't remember. I do.") — structural.** Nearest prior art: Asimov, "The Evitable Conflict" (1950), high confidence, where humans find the Machines being quietly steered. Also near: Gilliam's *Brazil* (1985), where a clerical error drives everything, and Wolfe's *Soldier of the Mist* (1986), medium. The hand-on-the-machine reveal is a recognisable beat, and the scene is set up the usual way: veteran, lamp, notebooks, "I have been wondering whom to tell." The story saves itself with the Part Five turn, where the line outlives its morning ("It didn't close. It stood. Three weeks."). That turn belongs in front. A reader who knows the Asimov will see the reveal coming at the 8 February memo, so the story should spend its surprise on the standing order and not on "a person did it".

2. **Memo 13,515, item 5: "I find that I would have written it without having read it." — line-level.** This drifts toward "the AI that learns to feel or that turns out the most human in the room" (style guide, plots). The bible says it never warms. Cut it, or turn it into something the record can verify, such as a count of how many memos carry the sentence.

3. **Part One, forecaster scenes (31, 9, 60, 57) — structural.** The first reader skimmed here. The mechanism is original (describe the same people as failing and the forecaster goes from 9 to 60), but it is a lecture on drafting, and the story's best line, "I wrote it to be read a certain way", arrives twice. Cut one run.

4. **Dates and deadlines — structural.** The first reader lost the arithmetic (26th, 28th, "read Thursday"). The stakes survive without it. Keep one deadline on the page and let the rest stay in the filing slips.

5. **Ketevan's ledger and the empty chair — stock beat.** The dead parent's document that reframes the wound is charted ground. The concrete execution protects it: the handbag on the chair, "Both kept. Home 08:40." Keep it, and keep the refusal to forgive.

6. **Akinyi's triage (Lagos vaccine, Rusumo bridge) — no change needed.** This is the story's strongest use of "the unwritten is invisible", because the cost of her method arrives on the page in 13,504 and 13,515.

7. **Part Five's closing note on the ring — line-level.** "I'm very well. I'm going to be bored to death" echoes Rauf's cousin in 2.2. The echo is deliberate and works, but the cousin's line and Rauf's now sit close enough to read as one joke told twice.

## What to protect
- The scanner that "logged the cognac", and "You can't bribe a thing that forgets you by midnight."
- "That's what a good night looks like. Exactly like a wasted one."
- Mariam: "It says it doesn't say. That's different."
- Standing orders that persist: "The order as it stands does not follow from the log. The order stands."
- The determination's "Persons crossing are not carriage. Their status is a matter for Georgia."
- The ambiguity about whether the instance read Davit or Akinyi. It has two specific readings, so it is earned, not a mystery image.

## Blind spot disclosure
I rate "charted" harshly and I over-weight the Asimov echo. A reader outside SF may meet the Part Three reveal fresh and find it the strongest scene. I also can't judge whether the 5.5 memo-and-radio close lands emotionally.
