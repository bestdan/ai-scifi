# Part One check: asimov

Rules check: a reader can state the novum's rules by the end of Part One, and the date arithmetic holds. I checked the lag against the calendar (Thursday 30 Jan filing read Sunday, the 26 Feb cutoff, the 13,485 to 13,488 headers). Everything below is a risk for later parts, not a failure here.

1. **Tamar, corridor ("So they built a king that can't remember... Nobody trusted the ones who remembered") (structural, must-fix).** Davit is a deputy minister and a former Registry clerk. Tamar explains the charter's purpose to a man who knows it, and the style guide bans that. It also gives away the Why row's scene early (the bible puts it in P2). Cut it to the dodge ("Ask it") and let Davit supply the thought himself. Or move the speech to a character who has a reason to explain it.

2. **Final beat, "this time he saved it" (structural, must-fix).** The forecaster scenarios give 31 per cent (bare numbers), 9 per cent (accurate persons) and 60 per cent (the failure framing). Lamara refuses outright. The last line then reads as a decision, but the page never says which schedule the name goes into or why he now goes against his own numbers. Later fair play depends on the reader tracking those three schedules and their odds. Say in a line which one it is, or make "saved, not filed" explicit.

3. **"It can count occupied beds. It said so this morning." (line-level, must-fix).** The memo says only "the hospitals are open". It never mentions beds. The rule is that the instance sees nothing national except through the record. Plant in item 9 or the Care Service returns that occupancy is in the record. Otherwise a sharp reader will ask how the instance knows the hospitals are open.

4. **Deputy and Davit on errata (line-level, carry forward).** Davit lectures a former Gazette Office officer on what errata are for. Compress it, or have her already know and push back. The plants are good: the flat errata line and the civics teacher's "long ones are names", which hide the seam well. Keep the 37 count consistent with "forty clerks, one each" in later memos.

## Protect
- The memo item 9 and the bus-stop petition. The reader learns the lag from a small domestic case.
- "He did not want anything from Davit, and that was worse than if he had."
- The insulin pen with Armenian script, plus "Don't."
- The Świecko carrier line in the memo. It is the fair-play plant for the ending.

## Blind spot
I judge rules and fair play. I can't tell you whether the Lamara scene lands emotionally. Let Le Guin and Banks decide that.
