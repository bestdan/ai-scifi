## The one thing
Make Davit's final filing a decision the reader could have reasoned out. As written, Schedule 4 loses him what he wants and adds nothing the licence needs. He moves from 25% to 6% on cession, and from 61 exposed runners to about 406 exposed nurses, and nobody states why.

## Notes
1. **Structural. 4.5, Schedule 4 against Schedule 7.** Tamar's 640 named nurses already secure the refusal. The licence needs only Part C, 61 runners (Code s.14(2)). Yet Schedule 4 also names the ~400 shift-swap nurses "from the directors' lists", and 406 letters go out by noon. Davit's own forecaster put cession at 25% after Tamar and 6% after his filing. He wants cession (2,100 deaths a year), and he trades a 25% chance of it for 6%. The ledger scene changes his mind about his mother, not about this arithmetic. Direction: give the extra names a job. For example, the licence or the refusal fails unless they are in. Otherwise cut them to the 61, and let the cost be only Eka's.
2. **Structural. 5.3, Akinyi.** She "read[s] every memo, every morning, before she slept" (3.2). Item 5 has carried her order daily since 8 Feb, and since 13,503 the memo has flagged it as not following from the log. Davit saw this in the departure hall and "opened his mouth to say so". Her line "I thought it was one morning… It stood. Three weeks" requires her not to read what she reads daily. The best reveal in the story is built on an oversight. Direction: she saw it stand and told herself it was luck, or she did not know what it meant. Do not have her discover it that morning.
3. **Structural. The reading rule is stated two ways.** 13,493 and 13,513 say named persons are capacity whatever the framing. But the forecaster gives 60% for named persons framed as dependent (1.3, 4.1), and Davit builds the plan on it. Armenia's strike files also named unpaid covering nurses, and Armenia was granted. The disanalogy (Armenia's doors had closed) is planted but never put to the deputy's objection. Direction: make the 60% visibly the forecaster's error and Davit's belief ("lying truthfully" fails against a reader of facts). Answer the deputy with the doors.
4. **Line-level. Clarity.** 4.1: "Zurab had met him off the night flight, and in the transit lounge at Dubai he had looked for the next lot." This has no referent, and the first reader lost it. Counts drift: 212 retired nurses, "1,912" said at 10:00, "1,911 names long" at 16:00 (this one is right, since Ketevan is last). The 8 Feb erratum was "the last night before the ninth", but it appears in the memo of the 8th, so that night was the 7th. 13,513 says "ages stated", but Tamar's list has "age not stated" for Dodo.
5. **Structural. Consequence dropped.** The determination refers the Bagratashen shifts to Armenian administration in one flat line. That is the mirror of the whole premise: the commons roster is "filled" by people the record doesn't show. The rostered Armenian nurses are paid for work they don't do, so the referral puts both ends of the arrangement at risk. Nothing follows from it. A line in 13,516 or Eka's last scene would carry it.
6. **Line-level. Rule edges in the memo voice.** 13,513: "It will not read this sentence as advice. It may not." This addresses the successor, which the charter forbids. 13,515: "I find that I would have written it without having read it" is the instance reflecting on itself, against the bible's "not human in costume". Direction: cut the first. Turn the second into a record fact, such as "carried in 22 memos".
7. **Line-level. Pacing.** The first reader skimmed the 1.3 forecaster run, the committee costing and the Part 5 memo's items 1 to 8. The scenarios could be one run showing 31, 9 and 60 with a one-line gloss that high means "granted".

## What to protect
- The planting of carriage: Świecko timber in the first memo, "It logged the cognac", the arch that "was not for people", Part C. The licence is a fair-play solution a reader can reach before Davit does.
- "Orders were inherited": the standing-order mechanism. It is the real twist about Akinyi's lines.
- The ledger entry for 16 May 2054, and "That's what a good night looks like. Exactly like a wasted one."
- "The last entry in the ministry's Schedule 4 is out of alphabetical order. I take the schedule's order as filed." This is the instance's literalness doing a job.
- Prose: "It tasted of nothing" style restraint, as in "He did not want anything from Davit, and that was worse than if he had."

## Blind spot disclosure
I count rules and exits, so I over-weight note 1's arithmetic. The story may mean Davit's choice to be moral rather than optimal. Interiority and the Ketevan material are outside my standing, and Le Guin should overrule me on whether the unexplained 25%-to-6% trade is the point.
