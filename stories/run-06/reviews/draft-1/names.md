# Named characters: draft 1

- **Davit Kiknadze:** deputy minister of health of Georgia, a career civil
  servant, once a trainee clerk at the Night Registry in Nairobi. He drafts
  the petition's schedules.
- **Mariam Kiknadze:** Davit's daughter, 14, who reads the memo every
  morning.
- **Ketevan Kiknadze:** Davit's mother, a nurse, dead before the story
  begins.
- **Lamara Gogiashvili:** 74, a retired ward sister and Ketevan's closest
  friend. She keeps the rota of retired nurses who work unpaid nights.
- **Dr Tamar Lomidze:** a surgeon and member of parliament, chair of the
  Health Committee. She opposes cession.
- **Zurab:** Davit's driver. His father is on a cardiac waiting list.
- **Rauf Mammadov:** a pharmacist in Marneuli who runs a cross-border
  medicine-lending ring.
- **Eka Beridze:** a nurse in Marneuli who works shifts across the border
  in Armenia.
- **Akinyi Odhiambo:** a senior night clerk at the Night Registry in
  Nairobi, who trained Davit long ago.
