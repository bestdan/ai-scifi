# Per-part notes: orchestrator triage

The notes from the lenses that checked each part as it landed. Fixes to a
part already written are applied by the drafter while drafting the next part.
Carry-forward notes go into the brief for every later part.

## Part One (Asimov, Chiang)

**Fix in Part One:**

1. **The final save is unmotivated** (Chiang 1, Asimov 2). Say which
   schedule the name goes into and why: the persons schedule drafted as
   failure, the 60% version. Motivate the save with the Armenian-labelled
   insulin pen. He has just seen what the web holds up, and he saves her
   name into the "unsustainable" framing, which he can stand. Make the save
   provisional: the schedule is saved, not filed. The Part 4 decision must
   stay unspent.
2. **The errata lecture to the deputy** (Chiang 2, Asimov 4). Cut it back.
   She is ex-Gazette Office and knows errata are corrections. He says only
   that errata are for typos and that a clerk who misused one would be in a
   cell. Drop "one a night", "forty clerks" and "one each". Plant "one per
   clerk per shift" in 2.5, as the outline says.
3. **Bed counts** (Chiang 4, Asimov 3). Name the source. The Care Service
   files monthly bed-occupancy returns, aggregate counts with no names,
   which are gazetted. Memo item 9 adds one clause: "Occupied beds, from
   the Service's January return: 81 per cent." Davit's line then cites it.
4. **Tamar's corridor speech** (Asimov 1). Keep it, because the outline puts
   the Why line here (Chiang at stage 5). But make it an argument aimed at
   him, not an explanation: trim the professor anecdote to one sentence and
   keep the dodge "Ask it."

**Carry forward to every later part:**

- **Read versus remember** (Chiang 3). The question must stay complicated,
  not settled. Akinyi must not rule on it, and nobody restates Mariam's "That's what
  remembering is".
- **Lighten the mechanism load per scene.** Let people and place carry
  Part 2.

## Part Two (Le Guin)

**Fix in Part Two:**

1. **Armenia reads as frictionless.** Add one concrete cost on the ceded
   side: the Armenian ward sister, or the rostered nurses paid to stay
   home. That gives Tamar's case weight.
2. **The "helped, then scheduled" thesis is stated three times.** Keep
   the cousin's version. Cut Lamara's abstract version back to the stove
   and her mother's letter. Zurab's "Both." line stays.
3. **Schedule 3 is filed before Davit rings Rauf,** though he promised
   himself he would read it first. Either fix the order or make it a
   visible failure he notices.

**Carry forward:**

- **Part Three must not just confirm the seam.** Davit deduces it in 2.5,
  so Akinyi has to re-teach it with details only she has: her method, the
  exercise books, the texture of Ulaanbaatar, what she passes over. Part
  Three is her knowledge, not his confirmation.
