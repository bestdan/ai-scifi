# Part Three: Lem lens (per-part check)

Blind spots: I favour essay over scene and can undervalue what a reader enjoys. I did not judge the warmth of the Kibera scenes, or whether the Ulaanbaatar telling lands emotionally. Banks, Le Guin and the First Reader should rule on those.

Verdict: the instance stays alien, and the part is nearly the wise clerk. It is not yet "secretly running the world", but it is leaning that way.

1. **Akinyi knows too much (structural).** The bible says she is wrong that the line is small: she thinks it a patch and it is the governor's only memory. In Part Three she is right about everything. She explains standing-order inheritance ("I wrote one line, once. It has been written every morning since."), answers "You knew that when you wrote it" with "Of course I knew", and reads the Caucasus well enough to see Ketevan's absence "as a shape". Give her one wrong belief, held on the page, that the reader can see and she can't. The natural one is that she thinks she is choosing between a bridge and a vaccine, when the standing-order mechanism means her lines outlive the night she chose them for.

2. **Her mercies get confirmed (structural).** She says "Mostly I never find out." Then memo 13,502 prints Rusumo rerouted, 163 vehicles delayed, and she says "Good." The ledger's "After" column and the Seville line carry the doubt. The Rusumo memo undoes it. Cut the confirmation, or make the memo reroute something she can't tell was her doing. She also writes the Sadakhlo fix on Davit's behalf, unasked, which reads as patron more than clerk.

3. **The instance is nearly absent (structural).** It appears only as framing memos, and Davit's phrase "believed them entirely" does the work. Its best alien moment is 13,503's "The notice of 15 February is not yet in the record. The closure stands on the corrected route entry." That is a mind that sees the seam and takes the record's word. Carry more of that. Never let Akinyi interpret it ("It had read it as hers").

4. **Davit's ask (line-level).** He asks her to carry a line, then says it "was not a principle." That is good. Keep him unforgiven for it into Part Four.
