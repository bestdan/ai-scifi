# Part One check: chiang

Per-part note, not a full review. Blind spots: I under-weight pace and plot
momentum, and I may find the Part One meeting scene too dense because I prefer
a single clean idea.

1. **Last line, "this time he saved it" (must-fix, structural).** Davit's goal
   is cession, and his own forecaster says naming the unpaid persons gives
   9 per cent. Lamara refuses to be listed, and nothing in the scene gives him
   a reason to overrule her. So the final act runs beside the idea instead of
   growing from it, and it spends the decision the bible saves for Part 4
   (after the ledger and Eka). The page already holds the seed that could
   motivate it: the Armenian-labelled insulin pen with "a commons batch code"
   in the cool-bag. That is goods crossing a border, which is commons
   business. Let him see that, and let the save be provisional (a draft in
   the template, not a commitment), or end on the unsaved cursor.

2. **Meeting scene, errata exchange (must-fix, line-level).** "Errata are for
   typos... One a night, for a misspelled name." Davit lectures a former
   gazette officer on the seam the ledger holds back for Part 3 ("Errata are
   for typos, Akinyi."). It also makes the later Part 2 inference too easy.
   Cut his speech to what the deadline argument needs. The same scene carries
   the forecaster, the one-machine rule, Armenia and the lag. That is too
   many mechanisms for one conversation.

3. **Carry forward: "That's what remembering is."** Mariam states the
   story's question, and Davit answers "It read." Tamar's "king that can't
   remember" half-answers it aloud. Later parts must test this and not
   confirm it. Akinyi should make "read" and "remember" harder to separate.
   She should not rule on which one is true. Keep the final exchange between
   Davit and Mariam in Part 5 as a deliberate rhyme.

4. **Continuity (line-level).** Davit says the forecaster "can count occupied
   beds", but the same part insists the hospital charts stay on paper so that
   nothing reaches Nairobi. Name where bed counts come from (the Care
   Service's own returns, say) so the premise doesn't leak.

**To protect:** the instance headers and the erasure at four, which enact
the form. "It was the one voice in public life that nobody he knew had ever
caught in a lie... It had nothing to lie for." The 9, 31 and 60 per cent
