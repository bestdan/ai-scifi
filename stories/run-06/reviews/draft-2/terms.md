# Terms: draft 2

- **the memo:** the governor's daily published account of what it
  understands the world to be.
- **instance:** one day's governor, a fresh mind booted each morning and
  erased each night.
- **the turn:** the hour each night when no instance runs and the Night
  Registry applies corrections.
- **gazetted:** entered into the public record, which the governor can
  read, three turns after filing.
- **erratum / errata:** a correction of a clerical error in the record,
  applied overnight.
- **unwritten:** choosing, as a stance, to stay out of the record.
- **cession:** a nation handing a function over to the global commons.
- **supra:** a Georgian feast with toasts.
- **forecaster:** a cheap imitation model that ministries use to guess what
  the governor will decide.
- **intake:** filings received at the Registry but not yet gazetted.
- **schedule of evidence:** a filed document of facts supporting a
  petition.
- **carrier (licence) / manifest:** a licensed cross-border goods carrier,
  and its declaration of the goods carried.
- **standing order:** an order that stays in force from day to day until
  changed.
- **tetri:** the smallest Georgian coin.
- **ger:** a Mongolian felt tent.
- **khinkali:** Georgian dumplings.
