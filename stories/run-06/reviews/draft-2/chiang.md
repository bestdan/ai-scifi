## The one thing
Akinyi's wrong belief contradicts what she has shown. In 3.3 her sleeve holds
three identical Ulaanbaatar memos from 2089, proof that instances inherit
orders. In 3.3 she also says "some instance reads the log fresh and puts the
order back." In 5.3 she has "read that as an instance... choosing mine." A
twenty-seven-year clerk who kept those three sheets for nine years would know
orders carry. Either let her half-know it and refuse to apply it to her own
line, or cut the Ulaanbaatar parallel from her mouth and let Davit notice it.

## Notes

1. **3.3, "Orders were inherited; every instance carried the standing orders...
   He opened his mouth to say so."** The narration states the discovery two
   parts early. Then 5.3 is Akinyi learning what the reader already knows, and
   it lands as a correction of her, not as a find. Cut the sentence. (Structural)
2. **The ending states its ambiguity four times.** Akinyi's "I can't weigh
   them... I never know," Tamar's "I can't tell which," Davit's "I don't
   know if it was right," and the memo's "The record does not show why I
   decided." Keep the memo and Tamar's text, which is short and costs
   nothing. Trim Akinyi to the facts. (Structural)
3. **1.4 hearing, the line-by-line costing (0.9 per cent, 214 posts).** The
   first reader waited for it to end. Keep Tamar's Armenian speech and the
   corridor. Cut the costing. This is the unnecessary scene. (Structural)
4. **2.2 "collapsing" and 1.3 "unable".** One drafting lesson (the facing-forward
   run in 1.3) carries the thought experiment. The reader is ahead of Davit by
   the second. Cut the "collapsing" exchange to two lines. (Structural)
5. **Forecaster numbers:** 31, 60, 57, 60, 25, 0, 40. The 57 does no work; drop it.
   (Line)
6. **5.2, 2.5 and 5.5: "a story with a hero and a wronged boy in it, already
   told, already over."** Davit is narrating his own silence. Trust the closed
   mouth. (Line)
7. **3.3 Rusumo: "Forty lorries would not cross... or they would"** against the
   memo's 163 and "a hundred and sixty." Fix the count. (Line)
8. **Part One opening:** the First Reader nearly stopped in the first
   explanation of instances. The fix is not more exposition; it's moving the
   Nairobi-desk paragraph after the radiator window-shutting. (Line)

## What to protect

  steals it.
- "Errata are for typos, Akinyi." / "Yes."
- "That's what a good night looks like. Exactly like a wasted one."
- Ketevan's ledger, the 16 May 2054 entry, and Davit looking for it on purpose.
- The radio's valves, "everything visible," and Davit's closed mouth.
- The Patagonia echo. It makes Akinyi's choosing concrete without a speech.
- Prose: "It was my week, Davit. I'd do it again. Write that down too."

## Blind spot disclosure
I am low on momentum and prefer the quiet version of every scene, so my cuts
(hearing, drafting) may be what gives a cold reader footing. I also dislike
resolution; Banks and Watts should say whether the ending needs a harder
edge than four hedges.
