## The one thing
Put Davit's cession trade-off on the page. In 4.3 the forecaster says 25% after Tamar. In 4.4 he says "Tamar has already lost us the hospitals". He wants cession, and he drops a 25% chance of ending 2,100 deaths a year without a figure. A reader asks why he can't file Part C (the licence needs it) plus the forward-facing persons schedule. ## Notes
1. **Structural. 5.2 against 5.5.** 13,515 says the 1,912 are "verified against your registry, and doing the work described". 13,516 then finds Ketevan deceased. Cut "verified…" from the determination, or say verification follows.
2. **Structural. 5.3, Akinyi.** She carries the three 2089 sheets, the origin case of an inherited standing order, yet reads "The order stands" as agreement. She also says in 3.4 that "some instance … puts the order back", which Davit's silent "Orders were inherited" contradicts. Give her a reason: the 2089 memos never flagged the conflict, and these do. Also say why 13,503 first notices, after nine days (a threshold, such as nine days of contrary log).
3. **Structural. 1.3, errata.** The deputy: "we find a typo in something and correct it." Nothing says a ministry can't do that. State once that a nation's correction is gazetted like anything else and only a clerk's hand goes in overnight. Davit's whole deduction rests on it.
4. **Structural. Part C to prosecutions.** Part C is shown as names and weeks carried. The determination says shifts are "described in Schedules 4 and 7", and the letters cite practising abroad. Show the line (the Code requiring each carrier's employer) that links Eka to the prosecutor. The determination says the licence comprises "the persons at Part C", but the officer says "Your licence is for the vehicle". Choose one.
5. **Line-level. Counts.** Tamar: "I didn't put Lamara Gogiashvili on it", so Schedule 4 has 641 retired nurses, yet 13,515 says "the same 640. I have counted them once." Let the instance be literal about one extra. "Four schedules from the ministry" leaves no 5 or 6.
6. **Line-level. Clarity.** "And the third reason?" has no first or second. "A clerk is only a crime." The first reader never settled it. "A day apart and nine days apart" (3.5). Lagos: her own reasoning says the vaccine is spoiled either way, so the 41,000 is not her cost. Show what the three days would have bought. Page one packs the Gazette Office, the Registry and the Night Registry into one paragraph.
