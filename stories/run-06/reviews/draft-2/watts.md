## The one thing

Let the deciding instance be uncertain. The bible's rule is that two boots on one record "could decide a close case differently". Memo 13,513 ends "Georgia is capable of performing the function petitioned", and Davit tells Mariam "it will say no on Saturday". The verdict is therefore announced two days early by a mind that is meant to be unable to bind its successor. That is a deterministic, continuous will, which is the thing the charter was built to prevent. It also removes the only tension in Part Five. Cut that last sentence and keep the fact before it, "staffed at night by persons the record now shows". Davit then infers the refusal from the forecaster, and he can be wrong.

## Notes

1. **Structural. 3.4, Akinyi's dots.** Her region is "the Caucasus and everything east of it". Her three candidates are Lagos, the Gulf of Guinea and Rusumo, none of which is in it, and all three arrive in "the three hundred or so filings that had come into her tray". Either her flagged tray is global, or she is reading outside her region. Make it a flaw: she picks Rusumo, a bridge in someone else's region, because it was vivid. Better yet, show that she can't see the dull cold chain precisely because it isn't hers.
2. **Structural. 13,493.** "If they were identified, they would be capacity" is a conditional addressed to the petitioner, which is advice in effect. Davit's gloss that "it had not advised anything" is a rationalisation the narration doesn't contradict. Stop at "Unverifiable counts carry no weight."
3. **Line-level. 3.3.** The Lagos vaccine is measles, and freeze-dried measles vaccine is the heat-tolerant one. Use a liquid or oral product, or say the vial monitors failed.
4. **Line-level. 3.3.** "Those children will be vaccinated next month from another lot" is asserted and then checked by Davit, which is good. Keep it, and add one more unchecked certainty of hers, so the reader sees the confabulation without Davit pointing at it.
5. **Line-level. 4.2.** The ledger confirms Davit's grievance down to the date. Memory that has been rehearsed for forty years gets details wrong. Give the "Alone" entry one fact he had backwards, such as the chair or who was sitting in it. The plan declined this, but "He went looking for it on purpose" only partly solves it.
6. **Line-level. Hypoglycaemia scene.** Glucose gel in the cheek of a delirious man at 2.1 mmol/L risks aspiration. A ward would use IM glucagon. The nurses' speed is right, so keep it and change the treatment.
7. **Line-level.** Rauf's ring has no defector in it: "Everybody pays. Not in money." One small free-rider, such as a pharmacist who is late on the inhalers, would make the economy of favours believable. Do not add misery, only a defector.

## Blind spot disclosure

I weigh mechanism and cognition and distrust tidy resolutions. That makes me undervalue Lamara's table, the supra and the rota as meaning in their own right, and my note 7 could push toward cynicism the tone charter doesn't want. Le Guin and Banks should overrule note 7 if it costs warmth. I can't judge the Georgian and Armenian cultural detail, or the hospital procedure beyond the textbook.
