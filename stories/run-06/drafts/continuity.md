## Form conventions (set in Part 1)

- Title: **Paper Is Private.** Part headings: `## Part One: The Act`, then
  `## Part Two: Schedules`, and so on.
- Each scene opens with an italic header line: weekday, date, local Tbilisi
  time (or local Nairobi time in Part 3, say so), then `· Instance N`. The
  instance is the one running at that local moment (Tbilisi is UTC+4, so
  before 04:00 local it is still the previous UTC day's instance).
- Memos are blockquotes, item numbers kept, bold `**Instance N.**` header.
  Mariam may skip items; narration covers the skips.
- One paragraph per line.
- Em-dashes: one used so far (the ledger's Premise line). Keep it near zero.
- Contrast-shape: 0 used. Budget at ~35k is 5; spend sparingly.

## Clock and arithmetic

- Boot 01:00 UTC = **05:00 Tbilisi** / 04:00 Nairobi. Memo out ~02:00 UTC =
  06:00 Tbilisi, read at 06:10. Erasure 00:00 UTC = **04:00 Tbilisi** /
  03:00 Nairobi. The turn = 00:00–01:00 UTC.
- Instances: 30 Jan 13,485; 31 Jan 13,486; 1 Feb 13,487; 2 Feb 13,488;
  3 Feb 13,489; 4 Feb 13,490; 8 Feb 13,494; 14 Feb 13,500; 15 Feb 13,501;

  3 Feb Mon; 8 Feb Sat; 26 Feb Wed; 1 Mar Sat. (So 1.4 is a Saturday
  sitting; the clerk jokes about it.)
- Gazetting: filed day D, gazetted at the turn beginning D+3, first read by
  D+3's instance. Act passed and filed Mon 27 Jan → gazetted 30 Jan.
  Petition filed Wed 29 Jan → gazetted Sat 1 Feb. Committee transcript of
  Sat 1 Feb → gazetted Tue 4 Feb ("speaking to Tuesday").

## Voice notes

- **Narration:** close third on Davit, past tense, plain and exact. Long
  sentences built with "and", clause by clause, for procedure and memory;
  short flat sentences for the turns ("He didn't." "Nothing happened.").
  Dry comedy comes from institutions (Saturday sittings "only for budgets
  and for funerals", the lift on alternate weeks, the deputy closing her
  tablet "with the whole of her palm"). Specific objects carry feeling: the
  radiator, the mandarins, the cool-bag behind the milk. Davit's feelings
  are named rarely and then precisely ("a clerk's small shameful pleasure
  in a sentence that would bear weight").
- **Davit's speech:** short, corrective, a drafter's. He corrects other
  people's words ("That's a description." "It read." "The period does. The
  reading doesn't."). He evades with plainness ("Like people"). He says the
  true hard thing once, then stops. With Lamara he slips once and says
  something unguarded ("I grew up in the corridor outside it").
- **Memo voice:** numbered, short declaratives, present tense, no emotion
  words, no "you" outside determinations. Hedges only where the record
  hedges. Humour from literalness and scale ("I take the record's word for
  the eleven"). Recurrent formulas: "This is what I understand the world to
  be today." / "The record shows …" / "I take the record's word." / "I
  will not be here tomorrow. The record will."
- **Tamar:** dry, exact, a surgeon's numbers; takes her glasses off to
  attack; respects Davit and needles him. Uses his first name in private.
- **Lamara:** blunt, domestic, lists favours by bed number; never raises
  her voice; ends on hospitality as a weapon (the wine).
- **Mariam:** quick, half-right, argues from prepared positions, says "it
  remembered".
- Watch: zinger closers at ~3.7 per 1,000; trim where a paragraph ends on
  a reflexive short beat. "The way" similes: 3 so far.
