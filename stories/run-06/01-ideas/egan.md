# Idea cards: egan

**Blind spots of this lens.** It lectures. It lets the derivation outrank the
scene, flattens emotion, and costs accessibility. Each card follows one fixed
parameter of governance down to a person, but whether that person's feeling
lands on the page is for the First Reader and Chiang to judge. The danger at
novella length is procedure with nobody hurting inside it.

## Card 1: Lived Readings

- **Novum:** No bill passes until citizens have lived under it. Consenting volunteers are forked into a model of their country running at about a thousand times speed, live five subjective years under the draft, then merge back before the vote.
- **Futurity:** F4. The deciding minds are forks: parallel, discontinuous, persons in their own right.
- **How it works:** The AI drafts the bill, builds the model and forks a stratified sample of volunteers. The forks' outcomes are the costing. Every amendment needs a fresh run. A fork consented to merge, not to be deleted, so declining its memories breaks a promise.
- **First-order consequence:** Voters understand a law because they remember it.
- **Second-order consequence:** Nations that refuse scanning appear in global treaty runs as statistical ghosts. Global law is tested on everyone except the people it binds.
- **Third-order consequence:** Testers come home carrying marriages and children from a country that never existed.
- **The story:** A ferry engineer's fork returns from a housing bill's run, in which she raised a daughter. The bill is failing. She can decline the merge, or take the memories and then ask whether her vote is about the bill or the child.
- **AI voice:** none in narration. Bill text, run reports and costing tables only, because comprehension here comes from living the law, not being told it.
- **Why this lens:** copies, merges, consent under forking.
- **Suspected prior art:** "Hang the DJ" (Black Mirror), *Permutation City*, Hanson's *The Age of Em*. None uses forks as the legislature's costing method.

## Card 2: The Empty Intersection

- **Novum:** Every law ships with a machine-checked proof that it satisfies the constitution, a formal specification citizens amend clause by clause. The global AI may act only where all member nations' specifications hold at once, and it has just proved that set empty.
- **Futurity:** F3. Citizenship means co-signing a specification, and sovereignty is a clause in a satisfiability problem.
- **How it works:** The AI proposes policy. Human checkers verify a small proof kernel, never the AI's reasoning. Nobody trusts the governor, only the checker.
- **First-order consequence:** Legislatures stop debating policy and draft axioms instead.
- **Second-order consequence:** The short, checkable inconsistency proof traces to one small nation's clause: nobody may be modelled without consent. Global governance can't run without modelling.
- **Third-order consequence:** Global coordination (vaccine stocks, shipping, carbon) freezes, and the world turns on four hundred thousand people.
- **The story:** That nation's chief specification clerk must draft the amendment it is pressed to pass. She hunts for a wording that restores consistency and keeps the protection, without knowing whether one exists.
- **AI voice:** documents only (proposals, proof summaries, counterexamples). Its legitimacy rests on saying nothing that can't be checked.
- **Why this lens:** change one axiom and follow it rigorously; reason as a moral stance.
- **Suspected prior art:** Asimov's "The Evitable Conflict" and the Three Laws stories, Egan's "Luminous", smart-contract governance. Not close.

## Card 3: Morning Instance

- **Novum:** The governing AI keeps no memory. Each morning it boots fresh from the public record alone, so it knows nothing a citizen couldn't read.
- **Futurity:** F4. The governor is discontinuous, and the law is its only continuity.
- **How it works:** Each instance reads statutes, gazette and registry, publishes an orientation memo ("what I understand this country to be today"), governs, and is erased at night. Clerks amend the record by gazetted procedure.
- **First-order consequence:** Citizens can understand the governor completely, because they share its inputs.
- **Second-order consequence:** Mercy, discretion and custom must be written down to survive the night. Drafting becomes the nation's main craft.
- **Third-order consequence:** To be recorded is to be governed: optimised and protected. Communities split over entering the record. The global instance boots from contradictory national records and writes a different memo each morning.
- **The story:** A district registrar has kept a valley's water-sharing custom out of the record, so the state can't optimise it away. In a drought, only recorded arrangements get relief, and she must choose which part of the custom to write down.
- **AI voice:** yes. The daily memos, a mind rebuilding itself from the page, show the reader what the state sees.
- **Why this lens:** identity under transformation; continuity carried by text.
- **Suspected prior art:** *Memento*, Chiang's "The Truth of Fact, the Truth of Feeling", Asimov's Multivac. None makes the state's amnesia its transparency mechanism.
