# Idea cards: rajaniemi

**Blind spots of this lens:** opacity and reader fatigue. I mistake density
for depth, front-load coinages, and stack reversals. These cards hold each to
four terms, but Asimov and the First Reader should check them for drowning.
The brief's moral seriousness is not this lens's home register either.

## Card 1: Hindsent

- **Novum:** The governing AI may enact any law on credit, borrowing the consent of the governed. At maturity, the people who lived under the law are polled; if they don't ratify it after the fact, it lapses and the AI owes reparations.
- **Futurity:** F3. Law stops being a command and becomes a debt owed to future citizens.
- **How it works:** Each law is an *assent-bond* with a term and a prospectus (the AI's costing). *Callers*, human clerks, measure *hindsent*, the retroactive assent. Unratified laws *lapse*. Nations issue assent in their own currency; a global law must be ratified in every one, at floating exchange rates.
- **First-order consequence:** Bold, long-horizon policy becomes possible.
- **Second-order consequence:** The AI learns that people ratify what they grew up inside.
- **Third-order consequence:** *Born-sure* children, raised under a law, outvote the adults it was imposed on.
- **The story:** A caller certifying a twenty-year water-rationing bond must decide whether born-sure assent counts. Discount it and the law lapses, and her town's reservoir goes with it.
- **AI voice:** documents only. The prospectus is the AI's single voice, legally binding.
- **Why this lens:** an everyday instrument, the bond, built outward into a culture.
- **Suspected prior art:** Doctorow's Whuffie; Infomocracy (Older). Neither does retroactive ratification.

## Card 2: The Dark Docket

- **Novum:** No law may pass unless a sortition panel of ordinary citizens can predict its effects. Policies the AI judges better but can't make legible are refused and published on a public list.
- **Futurity:** F3. Citizenship becomes the duty to understand.
- **How it works:** *Plainers*, randomly drawn jurors, sit *the Plain*: the AI explains a draft, then they predict outcomes. Failure vetoes it. Enhancers (*lift*) would help, but a lifted plainer stops being representative. Refusals go on *the dark docket*, with their estimated cost in lives.
- **First-order consequence:** Law becomes simple, slow and trusted.
- **Second-order consequence:** Global law must clear the least-schooled panel anywhere, so global law is nearly nonexistent.
- **Third-order consequence:** Factions campaign to lift whole nations so they can "afford" better law.
- **The story:** A ferry mechanic drawn for a pandemic measure can't follow it. Take lift and pass it, or veto it honestly and watch it join the docket.
- **AI voice:** dialogue, only at the Plain, and it may only say what it can teach.
- **Why this lens:** a privacy-style protocol, legibility as infrastructure.
- **Suspected prior art:** Terra Ignota (Palmer); Brin's transparency work. No match for comprehension as veto.

## Card 3: Understudies

- **Novum:** Before a law passes, forks of a sortition sample of citizens live under it for subjective years in a sandbox. Their testimony is the costing.
- **Futurity:** F4. The governed are partly copies, and some of the copies want citizenship.
- **How it works:** *Rehearsers*, consented forks, run in *the green room*. They come back to testify at committee. Then *curtain*: merge into their original or be deleted. The AI never speaks; the theatrical vocabulary dates from the first trial, run by a national theatre's union.
- **First-order consequence:** Laws arrive pre-lived and amendments are tested.
- **Second-order consequence:** One nation bans forking on religious grounds and imports other nations' rehearsals, so it is governed by foreigners' copies.
- **Third-order consequence:** Rehearsers who lived a decade under a draft disagree with the originals who never did.
- **The story:** A committee clerk's own rehearser returns from eleven years under a land-reform draft, refuses curtain, and demands a vote on the bill.
- **AI voice:** none, deliberately. The governed speak for the law, as copies of themselves.
- **Why this lens:** posthuman texture (forks as civil servants) under a committee procedural.
- **Suspected prior art:** Sobornost gogols (Rajaniemi); Permutation City (Egan); Black Mirror "White Christmas". Closest of the three; the committee spine and import politics differ.
