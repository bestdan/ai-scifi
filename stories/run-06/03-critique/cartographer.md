# Cartographer on the stage 3 premises: run-06

**Blind spots.** I judge novelty and prior art. I am not the lens for whether the
feeling lands, whether the prose will sing, or whether a 35,000-word draft
sustains the mechanism. Where I touch those, I say so. My map is strong on
English-language SF and thin on non-Anglophone and small-press work, so
"uncharted" always means "nearest I know of".

**Searches run this stage** (standard WebSearch, three queries): an AI
government whose explanations come from a separate model; a daily-reset
governing AI that rebuilds from a public record; a frozen AI sovereign whose
hardware port flips a ruling by rounding. None returned a fiction match. That is
weak evidence of absence. Confidence on every "uncharted" below is medium.

**Audit attractors still live in the premises.** The audit asked for no water, no
clerk-finds-a-hidden-thing, no island of 400,000 and no Ines. The premises
dodge the names and the water. The protagonist shape did not move: all five are
a mid-level official (deputy minister, commissioner, engineer, drafter,
lawyer-linguist) who holds a fact and must decide what to do with it. That is
attractor 6 in a new coat. A is the only one whose fact is *his to use* rather than
*his to disclose*, which is why it holds.

Scores use the rubric, N and Y doubled, out of 40.

## A. The Morning Record

Nearest: Wolfe, *Soldier of the Mist* (1986), for the self-scripting amnesiac,
and Scott, *Seeing Like a State* (1998, nonfiction), for legibility as the
precondition of state power. Searching for a daily-reset AI ruler found no
fiction (medium). The synthesis adds the clerk's unlawful line and the minister's
drafting; neither brings in new prior art. The collision still holds: "to be
recorded is to be governed" and the refusal standard that punishes recording are
two mechanisms that feed each other, and I know of no work that does that.
Hazard: the clerk's line sits close to the whistleblower attractor. The synthesis
already knows this, and the fix (his fact to use) is the right one. Water is off
the table, and hospitals are fresh ground.

Revised: **N5** F4 R4 Y5 E5 S4 → 37/40. Status: **uncharted.**
Push further: the record's own growth. Each recorded custom makes the next
morning's instance more competent at absorbing the same nation. The memo never
learns, but the page does.

## B. The Speaker's Reasons

Nearest: Gazzaniga's "interpreter" in split-brain work and Nisbett and Wilson's
confabulation studies (1977), both nonfiction, high. The unfaithful-explanation
literature on language models is also close (c. 2023, medium on the details).
In fiction: Asimov, "Liar!" (1941), and Watts, *Blindsight* (2006), where
explanation is cosmetic. Card's *Speaker for the Dead* (1986) is a name-echo
only. The new combination is the *sincere* confabulation, plural across nations,
for one statute. That is real distance. The weak point is the reveal itself. "The
reasons were never real" sits near "it was a simulation all along" and the
off-limits manipulation twist. The synthesis's "honest limit, not deception"
handling is correct, and it must be what the page shows. The "resettled mill town"
is a familiar shape, and the commissioner judging the statute that made him is a
neat loop of the kind the audit flagged.

Revised: **N4** F4 R4 Y4 E5 S5 → 34/40. Status: **uncharted**, one notch below the
collision's 5.
Push further: let the four Speakers *agree* on a reason nobody holds. Consensus
confabulation is stranger than disagreement.

## C. The Port

Nearest: Chiang, "The Lifecycle of Software Objects" (2010), for raising a mind
in households; Vinge, *A Deepness in the Sky* (1999), for software archaeology;
Miller, *A Canticle for Leibowitz* (1959), for ritual preservation of an
unreadable canon. The frozen-sovereign-plus-rounding-flip is the unmatched part
(search found nothing). Two additions from the synthesis cost it. The "flip is
more just" twist is a gift-from-the-flaw turn, a mild cousin of the noble-error
trope. The engineer whose own family archive holds the ledger is the audit's
find-the-hidden-thing shape, wearing a genealogy. Each part is fresh, but each
sits on a close neighbour, so I score it below the collision's 5.

Revised: **N4** F3 R4 Y5 E4 S5 → 34/40. Status: **edge-of-map**, leaning uncharted.
Push further: drop the ancestor link. The flipped ruling traces to a table that
belongs to nobody on the page, and the engineer has no inherited stake, only the
sum.

## D. The Empty Set

Nearest: Egan, "Luminous" (1995), for inconsistency as threat; Arrow (1951,
nonfiction); the 2016 Wallonia veto of CETA (real, high); Leckie, *Ancillary
Justice* (2013), and Vinge's Tines for a mind that changes with membership. The
synthesis's fix has a charted neighbour. "Consent to be modelled once" is
copy-identity ground in a softer form (the audit's cluster 3: *Kiln People*,
"White Christmas"). The shape "one small nation holds up the world" survives the
removal of the island, because "mid-sized landlocked" is just a cleaner island. A
concord whose own forecast says it is better without them gives it its
distinction. That is still the best part.

Revised: **N3** F4 R4 Y4 E4 S4 → 31/40. Status: **edge-of-map.**
Push further: the proof is wrong in a way that matters. The emptiness is a
bug in the specification language, and the world must decide whether to fix the
bug or keep the freeze.

## E. Equally Authentic

Nearest: Waitangi (1840) and Wuchale (1889), both real and exact. The model's
unreadable original is new in fiction. Neighbours: Kuang, *Babel* (2022);
Miéville, *Embassytown* (2011). Single novum, and the twist ("the divergence may
be the model's") is the manipulation-by-AI turn the brief marks as off-limits
unless it is the novum. It would be here, but only if it is a limit of
translation rather than an act. Note the pair relation with B: both turn on
"nobody can ask the machine what it meant". Choosing either one makes the other's
best idea unavailable.

Revised: **N4** F3 R4 Y5 E5 S4 → 34/40. Status: **uncharted in fiction**, charted
in treaty law.
Push further: the court cannot compare renderings without a reference. The
chosen reference language is itself a rendering. The court's own judgment is
equally authentic.

## Clusters flagged

- **Official-with-a-fact protagonist:** all five. A resists it best.
- **Explainer cannot reach the source:** B and E share the core. Stage 3 should
  treat them as one slot.
- **Copy-identity adjacency:** D (modelled once).
- **Reveal-as-twist:** B and E sit near the off-limits manipulation turn.
  Both can be defended as honest limits.
- Water: clear. Island: D only. Ines: clear.

## Ranking (novelty alone)

1. **A.** Only N5, and its parts feed each other.
2. **B.** The sincere, plural confabulation is real distance, with the reveal as
   the hazard.
3. **E.** Fresh in fiction, but the real treaty record is exact and the
   single novum is already mapped by Waitangi.
4. **C.** Two near neighbours, and the twist is a mild trope.
5. **D.** The fix is copy-identity ground and the island shape survives.

## Why A should win

Novelty is the one thing a premise cannot recover later. A has the only
combination where each mechanism creates the other's problem: the page is both
the state's eyes and the proof against it, and that tension stays live through
every scene without the story needing a secret. Amnesia as a transparency
guarantee reverses the usual reading of "forgetting", which I know of nowhere
as a governing principle. It is also the premise that answers all four seed
questions from one device, so the novelty is not bought at the seed's expense.
The warning to carry into the bible is narrow: keep the clerk's line the
minister's tool, not his discovery to reveal, or the whole premise falls back
into the attractor the audit named.
