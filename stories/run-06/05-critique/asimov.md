# Stage 5 critique: Asimov (the Logician)

Blind spots: I read for rules, fair play and who knows what. I don't judge interiority, texture or the Ketevan scenes' feeling. Le Guin and Banks should push back.

The calendar arithmetic checks out: 13,485 to 13,516, the three-turn lag, and 26 Feb as the last useful day all agree.

1. **[must-fix] Davit's 3.5 request has no content, and it opens a loophole.** An erratum carries unread intake. Anything filed 27 or 28 Feb is in the 1 Mar turn's intake, so the "last useful day" is not last for anyone with Akinyi. A reader will ask why Davit doesn't file a late clause for her to carry. On 17 Feb he can't know what to ask, because Tamar's move comes on 23 Feb. Fix: in 3.5 he asks only for the principle. In 4.4 he holds back one clause, on the nurses' prosecution exposure, and files it on 28 Feb. In 5.3 the clause is the Georgia line she did not choose when she chose Patagonia. That puts the one-erratum-a-night limit on the page as a cost.

2. **[must-fix] Akinyi must be clever, and the channel must have been noticed or not noticed for a stated reason.** She wants nobody to know (bible), yet 3.2 and 3.4 have her confirm the crime and write a line while a petitioner's minister watches. Errata also show old and new text publicly, so nine years of oversized ones sit in the record. Fixes:
   - Davit arrives holding the public diff, so denial is pointless. Give her a reason to show him anyway.
   - Make the memo's "one is longer…" line routine in 1.2 (Mariam: "It always says that"). What is new on 8 Feb is the content, which only a filer who withdrew could trace.
   - Add a line in 3.3 on why no one else noticed.
   - Plant "one per clerk per shift, forty clerks" in 2.5, so a reader beats Davit to the channel.

3. **[must-fix] Reading 2 of the seam is too weak to weigh.** A one-day priority change on 8 Feb does not obviously keep the ring "there to be written" on 26 Feb. Fix: the erratum edits a standing entry, which memos inherit as record. Show it in a Part 4 memo excerpt, with Sadakhlo freight still at priority 5. Then "policy continuity through the record" does the work, and the reader can test both readings.

4. **[must-fix] Naming the 400 is unforced, so the cost misattributes.** The 2.3 counts alone moved the memo toward "capable". Tamar already files the web. Schedule 7 can license the ring without naming nurses. Eka's case against filing is therefore stronger than Davit's answer. Fix: state in 4.4 the mechanism that makes names necessary (say, the instance credits only named, attributable work, and Tamar's schedule names the ring but only counts the swappers). Re-run the forecaster after Tamar's filing and show the numbers: the 60% failure route collapses, so the real choice is honest filing or withdrawal. Let Davit consider Schedule 7 alone and reject it for a reason the page gives.

5. **[should] Arithmetic and loading.**
   - Scene words sum to 34,000, with Part 5 at 6,000 rather than 6,500.
   - 1.3 carries five rules in 1,500 words, said by a "deputy" (of a deputy minister) who already knows them. Give the junior drafter a real question, and spread the rules into 1.4.
   - 2.3 packs three days, two filings, a withdrawal and the Armenia exchange into 1,600 words. Move the withdrawal to its own beat.
   - A hearing on 1 Feb about an Act passed on 27 Jan needs a stated reason, such as an oversight session.
   - "Why" is shown in 3.3 by its price only. A show of its benefit (the instance can't be flattered, or a bribe is declined) would balance it.
