# Stage 5 critique: Banks lens

Blind spots: I favour set pieces and scale, and short forms aren't my
natural ground. Cut anything below that the length can't carry.

**1. [must-fix] The mercy is too clean.** Akinyi's harm-for-good-reasons
lives only in 3.4's list of three candidates (cold-chain fault, mislabelled
vessel, bridge closure). She picks one and the others vanish, so nobody
pays for her triage. The mechanism offers a fix. The unchosen filings are
gazetted three days later, so the instance reads them then. In 3.4, or in
the memo that opens Part 4, let one unchosen item surface as a flat line
("the bridge closure, reported late, is recorded as..."). She chose, someone
paid, and nobody signed off. That is the dirty work, and it also makes
Davit's request in 3.5 weightier.

**2. [should] Scale is felt once, in 3.1, and then drops.** The orbital
board is good. But the 5.2 climax is "told entirely as memo" and risks
reading as Georgia's day. Make Georgia a small item. Let the instance
spend its morning on one concrete large thing (a debris avoidance, a
vessel in the deep sea, the Patagonian grid) in more detail than it gives
the determination, and give the determination the same flat weight. The
verdict that decides Davit's life then arrives at the scale of a bus
timetable, which is the joke and the horror together.

**3. [should] The wit thins after Part 2.** Memos, Rauf and the opera carry
Parts 1 and 2. Then Parts 3 and 4 are Ulaanbaatar, the ledger and Eka, with
almost nothing light. Put play where the mechanism offers it. In 3.1, give
the Registry its rituals: the one kettle, the forms' absurd field names,
the clerks' bets on the memo. For the memo excerpts that open Parts 3 and
4, give the instance a dry orbit-versus-pharmacy line like the one in 1.2.
Part 4 needs it most, because it is the grimmest.

**4. [should] Reorder Ulaanbaatar as a document, not a story.** In 3.3
Akinyi tells it. Instead show the three 2089 memos side by side, with the
identical rationing order and the fault report sitting in the lag. The
reader then sees what no instance could, which is the structural trick
this form was made for. Her account then needs only the number 41 and
"it was in my tray." The scene gets shorter and hits harder, which also
helps the word budget.

**5. [should] Who is missing: the view from the ceded side.** The Armenian
hospitals, with their pay and their lost say, are only argued about
(1.4, Tamar). Nobody on the page has lived in them. The nurses of 2.2 ride
toward them and the scene stops at the scanner. Extend 2.2 by a few
hundred words to dawn at the Armenian hospital: clean, standardised, a
protocol screen overriding a nurse's judgement, a nurse who is happy there.
Without it, cession is a theory, and the choice Davit faces is
unevenly weighted. The 39 other clerks are missing too. One glimpse of one
in 3.1, who might be the other suspected hand, costs fifty words.
