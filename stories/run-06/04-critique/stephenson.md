# Stage 4 critique: Stephenson (systems builder)

**Blind spots, stated.** I over-reward machinery and digression, so I will
tend to ask for more procedure than a 35,000-word novella can carry. I don't
judge pacing, restraint or whether the ending deepens the question (Chiang,
Le Guin), or whether the characters sound different from one another. The
cast reads clever in one voice to me, but I'm the lens least able to say so.

1. **[must-fix] The errata channel is under-specified, and the bible
   contradicts itself about what it carries.** The bible says Akinyi
   carries "what that day's instance learned." But the instance's only
   input is the record, so it learns nothing the next instance couldn't
   re-derive. What it lacks is the un-gazetted intake, which only the
   clerks see during the turn. Fix the spec: her line carries something
   from the 72-hour backlog, and reading that backlog is itself unlawful, a
   bigger breach than the erratum. Then close the obvious loophole on the
   page. Every memo is record, so why don't instances leave notes to their
   successors? State the answer (memos are gazetted too, so they are
   delayed; or the instance never sees the backlog it would warn about).
   The Ulaanbaatar scene (P3) is the place. Without it a reader will ask
   "why doesn't the machine just write itself a note?" and the novum
   collapses.

2. **[must-fix] The numbers don't trace.** The closing memo says "Ketevan
   Kiknadze, nurse, 2041–2090, deceased." The bible has her born 2019 and
   dead in December 2096. That line is the last image of the story, so
   correct it to 2019–2096 and check that the ledger years (2058–2071) fit.
   Also, 3,300 lines from January 2089 to January 2098 is more than the
   nights available (about 3,287 days). Say "about 3,100" and let the
   nights off account for the gap. The instance arithmetic (13,486 and
   13,515) checks out.

3. **[must-fix] The ending's exploit has to be legal on the page.** The
   governor licenses Armen's ring as "carriage" while 400 shift-swap nurses
   are prosecuted. A reader will ask why a nurse crossing at Sadakhlo isn't
   the commons' business too. Put one sentence in the world list and the
   determination: the commons licenses carriers of goods, vessels and
   fleets, while a person commuting to a job is a passenger, not a
   carrier, and the bus operators are already licensed. That distinction is
   the exploit the ending turns on, and it is also where the cost to the
   400 comes from. Make it a clause a clever character can quote.

4. **[should] Give the system an adversary.** Everything runs on Davit's
   filings. But the schedule period is open, and anyone with standing can
   file. Let Tamar, or the Prosecutor General, file a counter-schedule
   that reads the same customs as capacity ("here is who runs them"). Then
   the honest-versus-failure choice becomes a schedule war, and the
   72-hour lag becomes a weapon: the last effective filing on 25 February
   is a race, not just a deadline. The 28 February close then has a
   reason to hurt. Davit, an ex-clerk, should also be shown considering
   forging the errata route himself, and rejecting it for a craft reason
   (the one-per-clerk limit is audited).

5. **[should] Make Akinyi's one-line limit the cost of the Patagonia
   reveal.** She had one erratum that night and spent it on a grid fault.
   So she chose Patagonia over Georgia, and the mercy that did arrive
   came from Davit's record. Say it: a scarce line, spent against a
   town, and the bible's "the seam didn't matter" becomes "the seam
   matters every night, in someone else's favour." That is the wittier and
   harder ending, and it costs no words.

6. **[could] Spend one digression on the Registry as an institution.** The
   bible has the lamp at each desk and the 3 a.m. turn, but nothing on how
   forty clerks process errata by hand: the stamp, the second-reader rule,
   the form where "phrase replaced by sentence" can hide. A page on this
   (and on how the record is compressed so that forty minutes reads thirty-seven
   years of every nation's gazette) would be load-bearing, because it is
   where the exploit lives. The reader will enjoy being taught, and the
   teaching sets up note 1. Put it in P3, framed as Akinyi training a
   newcomer, so it is a scene.

Strengths worth keeping: "lying truthfully" as the temptation; the memo
voice ("I take the record's word for the eleven"); the exercise books as a
holdable object.
