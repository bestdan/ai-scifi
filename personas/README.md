# The editorial panel

Each lens has one job on the panel, and its blind spots are there on purpose:
another lens covers them. If every lens agreed, the panel would be useless.

## Author lenses

| Lens | File | Job on the panel | Pulls against |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Asimov**, the Logician | `asimov.md` | Clarity, fair-play logic, ideas as puzzles with rules | Rajaniemi (density), Lem (unknowability) |
| **Banks**, the Mind | `banks.md` | Scale, moral irony, the view from power and from below it | Chiang (restraint) |
| **Watts**, the Biologist | `watts.md` | Neuroscience, evolution, whether consciousness is necessary | Le Guin (meaning) |
| **Egan**, the Mathematician | `egan.md` | Rigor taken to its full logical end; identity, physics, uploads | First Reader (accessibility) |
| **Chiang**, the Philosopher | `chiang.md` | Form shaped by the idea; feeling that comes from the concept | Banks (sprawl) |
| **Le Guin**, the Anthropologist | `le-guin.md` | Lived-in societies, language, ambiguity | Watts (bleak reduction) |
| **Lem**, the Skeptic | `lem.md` | The truly alien, the limits of knowing, satire of human vanity | Asimov (everything solvable) |
| **Vinge**, the Singularitarian | `vinge.md` | Futurity; how to write past the event horizon | Le Guin (the human scale) |
| **Rajaniemi**, the Immersionist | `rajaniemi.md` | Texture, jargon density, trusting the reader | Asimov (plate glass) |

## Functional roles

| Role | File | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **The Cartographer** | `cartographer.md` | Maps prior art and gatekeeps novelty. The most important role here. |
| **The First Reader** | `first-reader.md` | A smart reader from outside the genre: lost, bored, moved? |
| **The Line Editor** | `line-editor.md` | Sentence-level polish; enforces the style guide |

## Adding a lens

Copy an existing file and keep its sections. A new lens earns a seat only
with a **distinct job and a named tension** with an existing lens. One that
would mostly agree with somebody already on the panel doesn't qualify. Lenses
that could be added later: Octavia Butler (power, survival, bodies changed by
the other), Ann Leckie (distributed selves, pronouns, empire), Stross (the
economics of acceleration), Adrian Tchaikovsky (truly non-human cognition, evolutionary scale).
