# Attractors

Ground the generating model keeps returning to, whatever the lens and
whatever the seed. Every lens is the same model underneath, so blind
ideation still converges: the stage 2 audits have found the same water
settings, the same clerk and the same names run after run. Published SF may
have nothing here. The model's own habits have still mapped it, and a card
that lands on it is a repeat of this project's earlier runs.

**Ideators** read this before writing a card, and stay off every entry
unless the card says which entry it breaks and why that serves the story.
**The Cartographer** adds to it at every audit (`process/stages/02-audit.md`)
and treats a card on an entry as edge-of-map at best.

Each entry names the runs whose audits found it. An entry seen in two runs
or more is a hard attractor.

## Settings

- **Water: rivers, dams, floods, droughts, tidal barriers, water rights,
  river treaties.** About 12 cards in every run where it was counted
  (run-02, run-03, run-06), and the pastoral river valley in calibration-01
  and calibration-02. Hard.
- **The small pastoral polity: a valley, a village, an orchard, a harbour
  town.** calibration-01, calibration-02, run-02, run-03. Hard.
- **A nation of 400,000 people.** run-06, three cards.

## Story shapes

- **An institution that certifies, explains or adjudicates the novum:**
  a clerk's office, a tribunal, a reader panel, a comprehension gate, a
  wording office. calibration-01 (the certifier's dilemma, 7 cards),
  calibration-02 (6), run-02 (8), run-03 (5), run-06 (10). The strongest
  attractor there is. Hard.
- **Law, votes, quorums and consent as the frame.** calibration-01 (8),
  calibration-02 (10), run-06 (consent after the fact, 4). Hard.
- **The dead, or a past self, as a continuing authority.** calibration-01
  (9), calibration-02 (9), run-05 (the black hole as a grave, 5). Hard.
- **Elder knowledge that intelligence can't reach,** and its sibling, the
  benevolent deception of an elder who knew all along. run-02, run-03.
  Hard.
- **A role reversal over a parent.** run-02, run-03.
- **The family safe word failed by a grandparent.** run-04, 6 cards.
- **Reversible or uncomputed minds.** calibration-01's top card returned in
  calibration-02 unprompted.
- **Copies or forks who live a life and merge back.** run-06; charted in
  published SF as well.

## Protagonists

- **A mid-level functionary who finds something hidden and must decide
  whether to disclose it,** usually a woman clerk or a teenage girl at a
  tell-or-don't choice. run-02 (21 of 27), run-03 (about 21), run-05,
  run-06 (about 20 of 30). Hard. Run-06's finished story landed on it
  anyway, through a night clerk's secret errata, while this entry was
  already listed: the audit's warning alone didn't hold the line.
- **Ferry mechanics, bus mechanics and translator parents** as the
  ordinary job. run-02, run-04, run-06. Hard.
- **A grandparent with dementia** as the default stake in a gentle story.
  run-04, 6 cards.

## Endings and moves

- **The flawed human beats the smooth machine.** run-04, about 7 cards.
- **The elder knew all along.** run-03.
- **A hidden sacrifice of the Magi's shape.** run-02, three of four
  premises.
- **The founders chose it,** as the explanation of a world. run-05.

## Ground a finished story took

A story that landed has charted its ground for this project, whatever
published SF holds. A card that reuses its devices repeats that story.

- **Run-06, *Paper Is Private*:**
  - a memoryless governor that boots fresh each day;
  - a public-record gazette the governor reads with a lag;
  - a Night Registry, with clerks applying errata to the record;
  - one machine, at a single seat in Nairobi, governing other nations;
  - a nation petitioning to cede functions to that governor;
  - a cross-border carriage licence as the resolution;
  - a Georgia–Armenia border setting.

  ("self, death, place, kin") as their Futurity line. calibration-01,
  calibration-02. Ideators don't rate Futurity; the Cartographer does.

  *Infomocracy* on about nine cards, whatever the nearest work really was.
